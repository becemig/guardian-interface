# DMT and Endogenous Psychedelics

## Abstract

DMT and endogenous psychedelics — the investigation of N,N-dimethyltryptamine as an endogenously produced trace amine present in mammalian brain tissue, cerebrospinal fluid, and blood, synthesized from tryptamine by the enzyme INMT indolethylamine N-methyltransferase, and producing the most intense and rapidly onset psychedelic experience known when administered parenterally, with its possible role as an endogenous neuromodulator in extraordinary states of consciousness including near-death experiences, mystical states, and dreams. DMT biosynthesis in mammals — Dean's 2019 confirmation that rat brain tissue actively synthesizes DMT from tryptamine through INMT enzymatic activity, with DMT detectable in living rat pineal gland and cortex at concentrations sufficient for receptor activity, and with cardiac arrest producing a transient surge in pineal DMT synthesis in rat models — providing the biological substrate for the hypothesis that endogenous DMT mediates extraordinary states of consciousness. Sigma-1 receptor DMT — the discovery that DMT is an endogenous agonist at the sigma-1 receptor, a unique intracellular chaperone receptor at the mitochondria-associated endoplasmic reticulum membrane that governs cellular survival, neuroplasticity, and the cellular stress response, with sigma-1 receptor activation producing neuroprotective and neuroplastic effects that may contribute to the therapeutic and transformative effects of DMT experiences independently of its hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonism. Ayahuasca MAO inhibition — the ethnobotanical genius of combining DMT-containing plant preparations with beta-carboline MAO inhibitors from Banisteriopsis caapi vine in the traditional Amazonian medicine ayahuasca, with the harmine harmaline and tetrahydroharmine MAO inhibitors preventing the intestinal and hepatic first-pass metabolism of DMT that normally renders it orally inactive, producing the 4-6 hour oral psychedelic experience that is the foundation of multiple indigenous healing traditions. The Bardo transition state consciousness correspondence in TCM and Tibetan Buddhist philosophy describes the classical understanding of transitional states of consciousness between ordinary waking awareness and death, with the Bardo teachings describing consciousness navigation through extraordinary states corresponding to the DMT experience phenomenology of entity encounters, life review, and boundary dissolution.

## Endogenous DMT Evidence

- INMT enzyme: present in mammalian brain pineal lung and other tissues.
- Brain DMT: detectable in rat cortex and pineal at trace amine concentrations.
- Cardiac arrest: pineal DMT synthesis surge in rat cardiac arrest model.
- CSF: DMT detectable in human cerebrospinal fluid.

## Sigma-1 Receptor

- Unique receptor: mitochondria-associated ER membrane chaperone — not classical GPCR.
- DMT agonist: nanomolar affinity at sigma-1 receptor.
- Neuroprotection: sigma-1 activation protects against ischemia and oxidative stress.
- Neuroplasticity: sigma-1 governs BDNF secretion and dendritic outgrowth.

## Ayahuasca Pharmacology

- DMT source: Psychotria viridis leaves — oral DMT without MAO inhibitor inactive.
- MAO inhibition: harmine harmaline tetrahydroharmine — beta-carboline alkaloids.
- Interaction: MAO inhibition allows oral DMT bioavailability — 4-6 hour experience.
- Therapeutic evidence: depression PTSD addiction treatment in clinical trials.
