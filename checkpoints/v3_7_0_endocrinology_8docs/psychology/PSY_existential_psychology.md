# Existential Psychology

## Abstract

Existential psychology addresses the fundamental givens of human existence — the ultimate concerns that arise inevitably from the conditions of human life. Irvin Yalom identified four ultimate concerns: death as the inevitable termination of existence, freedom as the groundlessness of human choice without predetermined essence, existential isolation as the unbridgeable gap between self and other, and meaninglessness as the absence of inherent cosmic purpose. These ultimate concerns produce existential anxiety that humans manage through various defensive strategies — some adaptive and some pathological. Terror management theory developed by Greenberg, Pyszczynski, and Solomon demonstrates experimentally that mortality salience activates worldview defense and self-esteem seeking as proximal anxiety buffers. Authentic existence in Heidegger's sense — living with full awareness of one's mortality and freedom — enables a more vital and engaged relationship with life. Zhi courage — the Kidney spirit's capacity to face the deep unknown without collapse — corresponds to the existential psychological capacity for authentic existence in the face of ultimate concerns.

## Four Ultimate Concerns

- Death anxiety arises from awareness of inevitable personal annihilation and is the primary existential concern.
- Freedom anxiety arises from awareness that humans must author their own existence without inherent essence.
- Existential isolation arises from the recognition that no relationship can fully bridge the gap between selves.
- Meaninglessness anxiety arises from the absence of inherent cosmic purpose requiring personal meaning creation.

## Terror Management

- Mortality salience — reminding people of their death — produces predictable defensive responses.
- Cultural worldview defense increases when worldview provides symbolic immortality against death anxiety.
- Self-esteem seeking increases as self-esteem provides anxiety buffering against existential threat.
- These defensive responses demonstrate the pervasive influence of death anxiety on human motivation.

## Authentic Existence

- Authentic existence involves confronting the ultimate concerns rather than fleeing into defensive self-deception.
- Awareness of death intensifies the preciousness of present experience enabling fuller engagement with life.
- Owning one's freedom produces anxiety but also the vitality of genuine self-authorship.
- Authentic relationships acknowledge existential isolation while building genuine intimacy across the gap.
