# Cytokines and Immune Signaling

## Abstract

Cytokines and immune signaling — the diverse family of small secreted proteins including interleukins, tumor necrosis factors, interferons, chemokines, and colony-stimulating factors that mediate intercellular communication between immune cells and between the immune system and other organ systems including the brain, endocrine system, and autonomic nervous system, orchestrating the initiation, amplification, direction, and resolution of immune responses through receptor-mediated signaling that regulates gene expression, cell survival, proliferation, differentiation, and effector function. Pro-inflammatory cytokine networks — the interconnected signaling networks of the primary pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha produced by macrophages in response to LPS that drives the acute phase response, activates endothelium, and induces fever through hypothalamic prostaglandin production, IL-1beta produced by inflammasome activation that amplifies fever and the acute phase response and drives Th17 differentiation, and IL-6 produced by multiple cell types that drives hepatic acute phase protein production including CRP and fibrinogen, promotes B cell differentiation to plasma cells, and drives fever through hypothalamic IL-6 receptor signaling. Cytokine storm pathophysiology — the life-threatening systemic inflammatory syndrome arising from dysregulated and self-amplifying cytokine production in response to severe infection, CAR-T cell therapy, bone marrow transplantation, or autoimmune activation, with the hallmarks of cytokine storm including hyperferritinemia, markedly elevated CRP and IL-6, lymphopenia, and multiorgan failure from cytokine-mediated endothelial activation, coagulopathy, and direct tissue toxicity — with COVID-19 severe disease and macrophage activation syndrome representing prominent recent examples. Neuroimmune cytokine signaling — the bidirectional communication between the immune system and the brain mediated by cytokines, with peripheral cytokines signaling to the brain through the vagus nerve and circumventricular organs to produce the sickness behavior complex of fever, fatigue, social withdrawal, anhedonia, and hyperalgesia that is adaptive during acute infection, and with chronic low-grade cytokine elevation in inflammatory and metabolic disease producing the depressive, fatigue, and cognitive symptoms of neuroimmune dysregulation. The Fire Toxin Heat Pathogen cytokine storm correspondence in TCM describes the classical category of extreme Heat conditions arising from Fire Toxin pathogens that penetrate deeply into the Ying Blood level, producing the high fever, delirium, hemorrhage, and organ failure of severe febrile disease — a classical syndrome corresponding to the cytokine storm pathophysiology of dysregulated inflammatory cytokine amplification producing multiorgan damage.
