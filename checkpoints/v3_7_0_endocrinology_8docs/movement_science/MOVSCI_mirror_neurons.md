# Action Observation and Mirror Neurons

## Abstract

Action observation and mirror neurons — the discovery by Giacomo Rizzolatti and colleagues that a class of neurons in monkey premotor cortex fires both when the monkey executes a goal-directed action and when it observes another individual performing the same action, and the subsequent identification of a human mirror neuron system through neuroimaging and TMS studies — provides a neural mechanism for understanding intentional actions, learning through imitation, empathy, and the therapeutic effects of action observation in motor rehabilitation. Mirror neuron discovery — the serendipitous discovery that F5 premotor neurons in macaque monkeys respond both to the monkey's own grasping actions and to the observation of a human or another monkey performing similar grasping actions, with the mirroring property specific to goal-directed actions and not to the mere movement kinematics, and with the system extending to include audiovisual mirror neurons responding to the sound of actions as well as their visual observation. Action understanding — Rizzolatti's proposal that the mirror neuron system provides the neural substrate for understanding the intentions and goals of others' actions through internal motor simulation — the direct matching hypothesis — in which observation of another's action activates the observer's own motor programs for that action, providing an immediate experiential understanding of the action's goal through the felt sense of what it would be like to perform it. Motor imagery neural overlap — the neuroimaging demonstration that mental imagery of movement activates overlapping regions of premotor cortex, supplementary motor area, and cerebellum as actual movement execution, with motor imagery practice producing measurable improvements in motor performance and cortical reorganization and forming the neural basis of mental rehearsal in athletic and musical training. The Shen resonance observational learning correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding that the Shen can resonate with and absorb the qualities of what it observes — the basis of learning from a master through attentive observation — a correspondence with mirror neuron-mediated action understanding and imitative learning.

## Mirror Neuron Properties

- Execution and observation: fire during own action and observation of same action.
- Goal specificity: code action goal not movement kinematics.
- Audiovisual: some neurons respond to sound of action as well as visual observation.
- Human homolog: IFG BA44 45 premotor cortex — mu rhythm suppression during observation.

## Action Understanding

- Direct matching: observed action mapped onto own motor programs.
- Intentional reading: mirror system activates appropriate motor program for observed goal.
- Broken mirror: reduced mirror system activation in autism — social understanding deficit.
- Empathy: mirror system activation correlates with empathic accuracy.

## Motor Imagery

- Overlapping activation: motor imagery and execution share premotor SMA cerebellar networks.
- Mental rehearsal: imagery practice improves performance in sport music rehabilitation.
- Cortical reorganization: imagery alone produces measurable motor cortex plasticity.
- Action observation therapy: observing movement activates mirror system — rehabilitation tool.
