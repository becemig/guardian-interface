# Ren Mai - Conception Vessel

## Abstract

Ren Mai the Conception Vessel ascends along the anterior midline from the perineum through the abdomen, stomach, chest, throat, and face to the lower lip, constituting the primary yin regulatory channel and the Sea of Yin Channels because every yin meridian intersects through this anterior midline. Governs reproductive function, nourishes all yin organs, and provides the constitutional yin resource all yin organ systems draw from when depleted. Runs as the Great Mother Flow in complementary opposition to Du Mai Great Father Flow, together forming the Central Channel pair - most primary of all extraordinary vessels because all twelve principal meridians exchange energy through the Central Channel. Ren Mai specifically governs conception, gestation, reproductive cycles, and prenatal essence nourishment, making it the primary yin tonic resource in both male and female bodies.

## Channel Pathway

- CV 1 (Hui Yin) perineum between genitals and anus: origin point
- CV 2 (Qu Gu / Curved Bone) pubic symphysis: root anchor - 1st chakra
- CV 6 (Qi Hai / Sea of Chi) 2 fingers below navel: hara center - 2nd chakra
- CV 12 (Zhong Wan / Middle Duct) midpoint sternum-navel: solar plexus - 3rd chakra
- CV 17 (Shan Zhong / Sea of Tranquility) center sternum at heart level: 4th chakra
- CV 22 (Tian Tu / Celestial Chimney) at throat hollow: 5th chakra
- Continues through throat, lower lip, connects to eyes at terminus

## Master Point and Release Pairs

- Lu 7 (Lie Que / Narrow Defile): radial groove 3 fingers above wrist crease
- Primary quick release pair: Lu7 + K6 (Joyful Sleep below medial malleolus)
- Secondary pair: SI3 + B62 (opens Du Mai simultaneously for full Central Channel balance)

## Chakra-Anterior Axis Correspondence

- CV 2 Curved Bone (pubic symphysis): 1st chakra root / grounding

## Clinical Indications

- Respiratory: asthma, coughs, difficult breathing, chest tightness
- Digestive: intestinal pain, stomach disorders, appetite dysregulation
- Reproductive: menstrual irregularity, lactation difficulties, infertility support
- Emotional: grief, depression, emotional anguish, anxiety, panic
- Constitutional: cold patterns, sore throats, night sweating
- Emergency: revival and stabilization after shock or emotional collapse
