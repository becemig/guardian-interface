# Chest and Thorax Acupressure Point Cluster

## Abstract

The chest and thorax acupressure point cluster covers the primary anterior trunk points from the clavicle through the sternum, ribs, and upper abdomen, governing respiratory function, emotional heart processing, grief and depression, breast and chest tension, digestive regulation, and the heart-lung axis of the upper body. This cluster contains some of the most emotionally significant points in basic acupressure practice, including CV 17 Sea of Tranquility at the heart center sternum and Lu 1 Letting Go at the outer chest, reflecting the TCM understanding that unresolved grief, emotional anguish, and suppressed feeling are held in the chest musculature and respiratory apparatus and can be released through sustained pressure at these points.

## Point Inventory

### K 27 - Elegant Mansion (Shu Fu)
- Location: depression between the 1st rib and clavicle lateral to sternum
- Benefits: opens all meridians to run correctly, opens the spirit, revitalizes the back
- Note: K 27 is the convergence point where all meridians are said to run correctly

### St 16 - Breast Window (Ying Chuang)
- Location: between 3rd and 4th rib directly above the nipple
- Benefits: depression, insomnia, chest tension, heartburn, breast disorders

### Lu 1 - Letting Go (Zhong Fu)
- Location: upper outer chest below collarbone lateral pectoral region
- Benefits: difficult breathing, grief, skin disorders, emotional release
- TCM: Lung governs grief - Lu 1 is primary point for held grief and sadness

### Sp 16 - Abdominal Sorrow (Fu Ai)
- Location: below the rib cage at the 8th-9th costal cartilage
- Benefits: indigestion, liver and gallbladder disorders, irritability, ulcers

### CV 17 - Sea of Tranquility (Shan Zhong)
- Location: center of sternum at the level of the heart - 4th chakra
- Benefits: emotional anguish, depression, grief, breathing difficulties, lactation
- Note: primary calming and emotional regulation point of the body

### CV 12 - Middle Duct / Center of Power (Zhong Wan)
- Location: midpoint between sternum and navel on the midline
- Benefits: digestion, stress, appetite dysregulation, overall body tension balance

### B 38 - Vitals Diaphragm (Gao Huang)
- Location: medial border of scapula at T4-T5 level between shoulder blades and spine
- Benefits: heart and respiratory conditions, grief, anxiety, emotional pain
- Note: posterior counterpart to CV 17 - front-back heart release pair

## Clinical Protocols

- Calming Flow: B 38, Lu 1 (bilateral), CV 17, CV 6
- Immune Strengthening: B 38, Lu 1, Lu 7, GB 21
- Increasing Vitality: B 38, K 27, Lu 1
