# Acupressure Clinical Protocols and Assessment

## Abstract

This node documents the complete clinical protocol suite from Basic Acupressure (Gach 2008), comprising six specific acupressure flow sequences for targeted conditions, the assessment methods for reading yin-yang balance through pulse and facial diagnosis, and the lifestyle health care framework integrating daily exercise, dietary balance, and session preparation. Each protocol is a sequenced multi-point treatment designed to activate specific extraordinary vessels and meridian clusters for a defined therapeutic indication, moving systematically through the body from proximal to distal or following the extraordinary vessel release logic of anchor-activate-integrate.

## Protocol 1: Calming Flow (21 points)

Indication: mental-emotional-physical calming, anxiety, mental clarity, headaches
Key points: GB 20, GB 14, St 3, GB 21, Sp 16, Sp 13, GB 31, GB 34, GB 41, B 38, Lu 1 (bilateral), SI 11, TW 5, P 6, GV 20, CV 17, CV 6
Note: shoulder and neck release with final balancing also proven effective for calming

## Protocol 2: Increasing Vitality Flow (16 points)

Indication: headaches, eye and sinus problems, jet lag, digestive disorders, fatigue, insomnia, hypertension, breathing problems, allergy
Sequence: seated start with GB 21, B 10, GB 20 - then supine: GB 20, LI 11, LI 4, B 47, Sp 16, Sp 13, Sp 9, Lv 3, B 38, K 27, Lu 1 - body clock balance: LI 4, Lv 3 alternating sides, St 36 bilateral

## Protocol 3: Immune Strengthening Flow (15 points)

Indication: breathing problems, asthma, respiratory conditions, colds, flu, immune disorders
Key points: B 38, St 13, St 16, Sp 16, Sp 13, B 10, GB 14, GB 21, Lu 1, Lu 7, Lu 11, St 13 (opposite)

## Protocol 4: Woman's Balance Flow (20 points)

Indication: regulates menstrual cycle, blood flow, appetite, and hormones
Key points: LI 11, Sp 16, B 48, Sp 13, Sp 10, Sp 9, Sp 6, Sp 4, GB 29, GB 41, GB 34, GB 31, B 47, K 6, P 6, CV 6, CV 12, GV 26, GV 24.5

## Protocol 5: Lower Back Release (10 points)

Indication: back pain, stress, fatigue - improves circulation and strengthens immune and uroreproductive systems
Key points: B 10, K 27, CV 6, GB 29, B 47, B 48, B 54, B 57, B 60, B 67

## Protocol 6: Expanded Shoulder and Neck Release

Indication: shoulder tension, neck stiffness, headaches, emotional holding in upper body
Sequence: SI 10 and SI 11 (double gates) - GB 21 with opposite scapula-spine point - TW 15 progression - neck lift with GB 14 and St 3 - traction GB 20 - final balance GV 16, GV 24.5, CV 17 - toes and heart center synchronized pulse

## Assessment Methods

### Pulse Reading
- Three positions each wrist: index finger (cun), middle finger (guan), ring finger (chi)
- Right wrist: Lung, Spleen, Pericardium-Triple Warmer
- Left wrist: Heart, Liver, Kidney
- Pulse qualities: floating vs deep, rapid vs slow, full vs empty, wiry vs slippery

### Face Reading - Eye Assessment (Sanpaku)
- Yin sanpaku: white visible below iris - yin excess, hypoglycemic, drugs, extreme diet
- Yang sanpaku: white visible above iris - yang excess, extreme tension, anger, violence
- Balanced: iris centered with no white showing above or below

### Facial Shape Yin-Yang Typology
- Yang face: narrow, angular, defined - tends toward excess and tension patterns
- Yin face: round, soft, full - tends toward deficiency and fluid retention patterns

## Dietary Balance Framework

### Yang Foods (contracting, warming, grounding)
- Salt, meat, fish, fowl, buckwheat, miso, tamari

### Yin Foods (expanding, cooling, dispersing)
- Tropical fruits, sugar, alcohol, coffee, raw foods, spices

### Healthy Foods (balanced)
- Vegetables, whole grains, seeds and nuts in moderation, beans, tofu, miso soup, fish, seaweed, fresh fruits

### Foods to Reduce or Avoid
- Processed foods with sugar, white flour products, excess salt, fried foods, dairy, red meat, chemical additives, coffee

## Health Care Summary - Daily Practice

- Stretching: 5 minutes twice daily keeps muscles and joints loose
- Aerobic exercise: 20-30 minutes daily - swimming, cycling, running, brisk walking
- Exercise regulates whole system - prevents sluggish metabolism and depression tendency
- Diet and acupressure together build resistance and strengthen the body protective capacity
