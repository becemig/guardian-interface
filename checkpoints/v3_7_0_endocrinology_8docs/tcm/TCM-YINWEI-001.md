# Yinwei Mai - Yin Linking Vessel

## Abstract

Yinwei Mai the Yin Linking Vessel - functioning as the yin aspect of the Regulating Channel pair together with Yangwei Mai - runs through the interior of the body linking all yin organ meridians into a unified regulatory network, balancing yin energy distribution across the front of the body, and serving as the primary extraordinary vessel for emotional regulation and internal nourishment of all yin organs. The Yinwei vessel redistributes excess yin from any single meridian to deficient ones, functioning as a yin banking system that prevents gross excess or deficiency while the organs compensate. Clinically governs all conditions involving emotional imbalance, immune deficiency from yin depletion, stress-related chest tightness and insomnia, and patterns involving fears, nightmares, or depression from yin insufficiency.

## Channel Pathway

- Runs through the interior linking all six yin organ meridians
- Intersects Kidney meridian in the lower leg
- Intersects Spleen meridian at Sp 13 (Rushing Door) in the groin
- Passes through chest connecting with Pericardium meridian at P 6
- Ascends to intersect Conception Vessel at CV 22 and CV 23 in the throat region
- Connects with Liver and Lung meridians through interior linking function

## Master Point and Release Pairs

- P 6 (Nei Guan / Inner Gate): anterior forearm 2 fingers above wrist crease between tendons
- Primary quick release pair: P6 + Sp4 (activates Yinwei and Chong Mai simultaneously)
- Secondary pair: TW5 + GB41 (activates Yangwei and Dai Mai for full regulatory balance)

## Anchor Points

- GB 21 (Shoulder Well): releases upper body tension via yang anchor
- B 10 (Heavenly Pillar) and GB 20 (Gates of Consciousness): neck and cranial base
- Sp 13 (Rushing Door) and GB 29 (Dwelling in the Bone): groin and pelvic floor anchor

## 17-Point Regulating Channel Release Sequence

Sequential protocol activating both Yinwei and Yangwei channels:
- Cranial base: GB 20 and B 10 (skull base bilateral)
- Shoulder: GB 21 (top of trapezius)
- Torso: Sp 16 (base of ribs) and Sp 13 (groin)
- Hip: GB 29 (outer groin near hip)
- Lateral leg: GB 31 (outer thigh) GB 34 (below fibula head) GB 41 (dorsal foot)
- Back: B 38 (between scapula and spine)
- Chest: Lu 1 (outer upper chest)
- Wrists: TW 5 (outer gate) and P 6 (inner gate)
- Central axis: GV 20 (crown) CV 17 (heart sternum) CV 6 (hara)

## Clinical Indications

- Immune: immune deficiency, recurrent colds and flu, susceptibility to infection
- Emotional: stress, anxiety, depression, fears, nightmares, emotional imbalance
- Cardiovascular: chest tightness, palpitations, emotional heart disturbance
- Sleep: insomnia from yin deficiency or emotional agitation
- Thermal: fever management, temperature dysregulation, chills
- Digestive: stress-related digestive disruption
