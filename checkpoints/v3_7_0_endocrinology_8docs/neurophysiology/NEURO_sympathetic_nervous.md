# Sympathetic Nervous System

## Abstract

The sympathetic nervous system is the division of the autonomic nervous system governing the fight-flight response and the mobilization of physiological resources for action and defense. Walter Cannon described the fight-or-flight response as the coordinated sympathetic activation preparing the organism for intense physical effort in response to threat. Sympathetic preganglionic neurons originate in the intermediolateral cell column of the thoracic and upper lumbar spinal cord — the thoracolumbar outflow — and synapse on postganglionic neurons in the paravertebral sympathetic chain ganglia and prevertebral ganglia. Postganglionic sympathetic neurons release norepinephrine at target organs while the adrenal medulla releases epinephrine and norepinephrine directly into the bloodstream. Sympathetic activation produces tachycardia, hypertension, bronchodilation, pupil dilation, increased glucose, reduced gut motility, and vasoconstriction of non-essential vascular beds. Yang activation in TCM — the upward ascending warming mobilizing quality of Yang Qi — corresponds to sympathetic nervous system activation as its physiological substrate.

## Fight-Flight Response

- Threat perception activates the hypothalamus which coordinates sympathetic outflow and HPA axis activation.
- Heart rate and contractility increase through beta-1 adrenergic activation improving cardiac output.
- Blood pressure increases through alpha-1 adrenergic vasoconstriction of non-essential vascular beds.
- Bronchodilation through beta-2 adrenergic activation improves oxygen delivery for intense physical effort.

## Thoracolumbar Outflow

- Sympathetic preganglionic neurons occupy the intermediolateral cell column from T1 to L2.
- They synapse on paravertebral chain ganglia adjacent to the spinal cord and prevertebral ganglia.
- The paravertebral chain runs alongside the vertebral column corresponding to the Bladder meridian Back Shu points.
- Each spinal level innervates specific target organs corresponding to the TCM Back Shu point organ relationships.

## Adrenal Medulla

- The adrenal medulla is a modified sympathetic ganglion releasing epinephrine and norepinephrine into blood.
- Epinephrine produces the systemic fight-flight response — tachycardia, glycogenolysis, and bronchodilation.
- Adrenal medulla activation amplifies and prolongs sympathetic effects beyond direct neural innervation.
- Chronic adrenal activation depletes medullary catecholamines corresponding to Kidney Yang depletion.
