# Interoception and the Brain

## Abstract

Interoception is the sense of the internal state of the body — the neural representation of all signals arising from within the body including cardiovascular, respiratory, gastrointestinal, immune, and hormonal states. A.D. Craig's landmark work identified the interoceptive pathway from peripheral C-fiber afferents through the spinal lamina I, brainstem, and thalamus to the posterior insular cortex as the primary neural substrate of bodily self-awareness. The anterior insular cortex integrates interoceptive signals with emotional, cognitive, and social information producing the subjective feeling of bodily states — the material basis of emotion and subjective experience. Lisa Feldman Barrett's predictive processing theory of emotion proposes that the brain constructs emotions by generating predictions about bodily states — allostatic predictions that regulate the body budget — and matching these predictions against actual interoceptive input. Antonio Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis proposes that bodily feeling states mark decision alternatives with emotional valence guiding decision-making through visceral wisdom. High interoceptive accuracy — the ability to accurately sense bodily signals — predicts emotional clarity, decision quality, and therapeutic outcome in somatic therapies. Shen listening in TCM — the Heart-Mind's attentive awareness to the felt signals of the body — corresponds to the neuroscience of interoception as the felt sense of the body's inner life.

## Interoceptive Pathway

- Peripheral C-fiber and A-delta interoceptive afferents signal visceral, thermal, and chemical states.
- These afferents synapse in spinal lamina I and brainstem nuclei then project to the thalamus.
- The thalamus relays interoceptive signals to the posterior insular cortex for primary interoceptive representation.
- The anterior insular cortex integrates interoceptive with contextual information producing subjective feelings.

## Predictive Interoception

- The brain generates predictions about expected interoceptive states based on past experience and context.
- These predictions are continuously compared against actual afferent interoceptive signals.
- Prediction errors update the model and drive allostatic regulatory responses to maintain homeostasis.
- Emotions arise from the brain's best guess about the cause of current interoceptive signals.

## Somatic Markers

- Damasio proposed that bodily feeling states mark decision alternatives with positive or negative valence.
- Somatic markers from previous experiences with similar situations bias decision-making below conscious awareness.
- Patients with insular damage lose somatic marker function producing impaired real-world decision-making.
- Gut feelings and intuition correspond to somatic marker access to visceral wisdom from accumulated experience.
