# Mesolimbic Reward System

## Abstract

The mesolimbic dopamine system — projecting from the ventral tegmental area VTA to the nucleus accumbens NAcc, prefrontal cortex, amygdala, and hippocampus — is the primary neural substrate of reward, motivation, and reinforcement learning. Wolfram Schultz's landmark recordings demonstrated that VTA dopamine neurons signal reward prediction errors — firing when rewards are better than expected, pausing when rewards are worse than expected, and showing no response when rewards match expectations exactly. This prediction error signal drives reinforcement learning by updating predictions about future rewards. Kent Berridge's distinction between wanting and liking dissociated the dopaminergic incentive salience system from the opioid and endocannabinoid hedonic hotspot system. Wanting — the motivated urge to pursue a reward — depends critically on mesolimbic dopamine. Liking — the hedonic pleasure of consuming the reward — depends primarily on mu-opioid and endocannabinoid systems in hedonic hotspots within the NAcc shell and ventral pallidum. Addiction involves a pathological escalation of wanting without corresponding liking — the addict craves intensely but experiences diminishing pleasure. Anhedonia — the loss of pleasure and motivation characteristic of depression — reflects hypofunction of both wanting and liking systems. The NAcc serves as the limbic-motor interface — translating emotional and motivational signals into motor action. Heart Shen desire in TCM — the Heart's natural movement toward what nourishes the Shen and away from what depletes it — corresponds to the mesolimbic system's guidance of motivated behavior toward rewarding and away from aversive outcomes.

## Reward Prediction Error

- VTA dopamine neurons fire when rewards exceed predictions signaling better-than-expected outcomes.
- They pause when rewards are worse than expected signaling negative prediction error.
- They show no response when rewards exactly match predictions indicating the expected outcome.
- This prediction error signal updates the value assigned to stimuli and actions through reinforcement learning.

## Wanting vs Liking

- Wanting is the motivated urge to pursue a reward — incentive salience — mediated by mesolimbic dopamine.
- Liking is the hedonic pleasure of consuming the reward mediated by opioid and endocannabinoid systems.
- Dopamine depletion eliminates wanting motivation without eliminating liking of consumed rewards.
- Addiction escalates wanting through sensitization while liking decreases through tolerance.

## Nucleus Accumbens

- NAcc core receives dopamine from VTA and glutamate from prefrontal cortex, hippocampus, and amygdala.
- NAcc shell contains hedonic hotspots where opioid activation produces intense pleasure.
- NAcc serves as the limbic-motor interface translating motivational signals into motor approach behavior.
- NAcc dysfunction underlies both addiction excessive wanting and depression reduced wanting.
