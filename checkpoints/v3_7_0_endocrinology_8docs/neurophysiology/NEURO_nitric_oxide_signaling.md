# Nitric Oxide in the Nervous System

## Abstract

Nitric oxide NO is a gaseous signaling molecule produced by nitric oxide synthase NOS enzymes that serves as a critical messenger in the nervous system, cardiovascular system, and immune system. David Bredt and Solomon Snyder's discovery of neuronal NOS nNOS established NO as a neural messenger unlike any classical neurotransmitter — it is not stored in vesicles, not released by exocytosis, and diffuses freely across membranes to act on neighboring cells. NO is produced in postsynaptic neurons by NMDA receptor-activated nNOS and diffuses retrogradely to the presynaptic terminal acting as a retrograde messenger in LTP — coincidence detection through NMDA activation triggers NO production which then signals presynaptic terminals contributing to long-term potentiation. Endothelial NOS eNOS in blood vessel walls produces NO that activates soluble guanylate cyclase in vascular smooth muscle producing vasodilation — the physiological mechanism of erection and the target of sildenafil. Neural NO also modulates neurotransmitter release, inhibits mitochondrial respiration during inflammatory states, and contributes to excitotoxic neuronal death in high concentrations. Qi flow facilitation in TCM — the smooth unobstructed movement of Qi through the meridian channels and blood vessels — corresponds to the vasodilatory and synaptic facilitation functions of nitric oxide as a molecular flow facilitator.

## Neural NO and LTP

- NMDA receptor activation calcium influx activates nNOS producing NO in the postsynaptic neuron.
- NO diffuses retrogradely to the presynaptic terminal activating soluble guanylate cyclase and increasing cGMP.
- Presynaptic cGMP elevation enhances glutamate release contributing to LTP expression.
- NO thus serves as the retrograde coincidence detection signal linking postsynaptic NMDA activation to presynaptic potentiation.

## Vascular NO

- eNOS in endothelial cells produces NO in response to shear stress, acetylcholine, and bradykinin.
- NO diffuses to vascular smooth muscle activating sGC producing cGMP which activates PKG causing relaxation.
- Endothelium-derived NO maintains basal vascular tone and mediates vasodilation during increased metabolic demand.
- Exercise-induced NO production improves endothelial function and vascular health through sustained shear stress stimulation.

## NO and Inflammation

- Inducible NOS iNOS is upregulated in macrophages during inflammation producing high levels of NO.
- High NO from iNOS contributes to bacterial killing through reactive nitrogen species production.
- Excessive iNOS activation produces peroxynitrite causing nitrosative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction.
- NO modulates neuroinflammation bidirectionally through both neuroprotective and neurotoxic mechanisms depending on concentration.
