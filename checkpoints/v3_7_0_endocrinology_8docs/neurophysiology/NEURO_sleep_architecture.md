# Sleep Architecture

## Abstract

Sleep — far from being a passive state of neural inactivity — is a highly organized and metabolically active process essential for memory consolidation, metabolic waste clearance, immune regulation, emotional processing, and the restoration of the cognitive and physiological systems that waking life depletes. Human sleep is organized into 90-minute cycles that alternate between non-REM NREM sleep and REM rapid eye movement sleep, with each cycle containing lighter NREM stages N1 and N2 followed by deep slow-wave sleep SWS in stage N3, followed by REM sleep. The proportion of deep SWS and REM sleep within each cycle shifts across the night — early cycles are SWS-dominant serving memory consolidation and physical restoration, while late cycles are REM-dominant serving emotional processing, creative integration, and procedural skill consolidation. Matthew Walker's synthesis of sleep research established that SWS consolidates declarative memory through hippocampal-cortical dialogue — the hippocampus replays the day's experiences to the neocortex during slow oscillations, gradually transferring memories from the hippocampal temporary store to the neocortical permanent store. Lulu Xie's landmark 2013 discovery of the glymphatic system revealed that during sleep the brain's interstitial space expands by approximately 60%, allowing cerebrospinal fluid to flush through the brain tissue clearing metabolic waste products including beta-amyloid and tau protein — the pathological accumulations of Alzheimer's disease. This glymphatic clearance occurs almost exclusively during sleep and is severely impaired by sleep deprivation, providing a direct mechanistic link between chronic sleep disruption and neurodegeneration. Hun return during sleep in TCM describes the Liver Hun returning to the Liver during sleep — the Hun that wanders during waking consciousness in creative thought, planning, and vision returns to the Liver Blood during sleep for restoration, and the quality of this return determines the quality of dream life and emotional processing.

## NREM-REM Architecture

- N1 light sleep: hypnagogic imagery, muscle jerks, theta waves replacing alpha.
- N2: sleep spindles and K-complexes, consolidation of motor memories.
- N3 SWS: delta waves, declarative memory consolidation, growth hormone release, immune restoration.
- REM: rapid eye movements, skeletal muscle atonia, vivid dreaming, emotional memory processing.

## Glymphatic Clearance

- Interstitial space expands 60% during sleep enabling CSF flushing of metabolic waste.
- Beta-amyloid and tau clearance during sleep — chronic sleep deprivation accelerates Alzheimer pathology.
- Glymphatic flow is driven by the slow oscillations of SWS — deep sleep is essential for brain cleaning.
- This corresponds to the TCM concept of the Liver filtering and cleansing the Blood during sleep.

## Circadian Regulation

- SCN suprachiasmatic nucleus in the hypothalamus is the master circadian clock.
- Light entrains the SCN through the retinohypothalamic tract suppressing melatonin during the day.
- Adenosine accumulates during waking as a sleep pressure signal — caffeine blocks adenosine receptors.
- Disruption of circadian rhythm through shift work and artificial light produces metabolic and psychiatric pathology.
