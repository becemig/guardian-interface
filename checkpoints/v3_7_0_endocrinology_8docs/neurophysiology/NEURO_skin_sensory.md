# Skin as Sensory Organ

## Abstract

The skin is the largest sensory organ providing discriminative touch, thermal, and pain information through diverse mechanoreceptor subtypes. C-tactile afferents are unmyelinated fibers tuned to gentle stroking at 1 to 10 centimeters per second corresponding to affiliative social touch. These fibers project to insular cortex linking touch to interoceptive and emotional processing. Wei Qi circulates at the body surface governing defensive immunity and sensory alertness at the body boundary.

## Cutaneous Mechanoreceptors

- Meissner corpuscles detect low-frequency flutter and grip slip signals in dermal ridges.
- Pacinian corpuscles detect high-frequency vibration and rapid indentation in deep dermis.
- Merkel discs detect sustained pressure and fine spatial detail at the epidermis-dermis junction.
- Ruffini endings detect skin stretch and contribute to proprioceptive and thermal sensing in deep dermis.
- Free nerve endings detect temperature, pain, itch, and through C-tactile fibers affiliative touch.

## C-Tactile Afferents

- CT afferents are tuned to gentle stroking at 1 to 10 cm per second corresponding to affiliative touch.
- CT fibers project to posterior insular cortex linking gentle touch to social-emotional processing.
- Oxytocin release triggered by CT fiber activation mediates bonding and calming effects of gentle touch.
- Slow Tai Chi partner practice activates CT afferent pathways through gentle contact and weight sensing.

## TCM Correspondence

- Wei Qi circulates between skin and muscles governing defensive surface immunity and thermal regulation.
- The Lung opens to the skin governing Wei Qi distribution and sweat pore opening and closing.
- Triple Burner governs defensive Qi distribution to the body surface corresponding to systemic skin innervation.
