# Reiki Attunement Transmission

## Abstract

The Reiki attunement or initiation — called Reiju in Japanese meaning spiritual blessing or empowerment — is the transmission ceremony through which a Reiki master opens the student's Ki pathways to channel Reiki energy for healing and self-cultivation. This transmission mechanism distinguishes Reiki from other healing modalities where skills are taught conceptually — in Reiki, the capacity to channel Reiki energy is transmitted directly from teacher to student through the Reiju ceremony, corresponding to the empowerment transmission lineages found across multiple Asian spiritual traditions including Tibetan Buddhist tantric initiation, Taoist inner alchemy transmission, and Hindu Shaktipat. The three-level Reiki training system in the Western tradition — Reiki I Shoden First Teaching, Reiki II Okuden Inner Teaching, and Reiki III Shinpiden Mystery Teaching — corresponds to the traditional Japanese Reiki levels, with each level receiving attunements that open increasingly deep Ki pathway levels. Reiki I attunement opens the physical-level Ki pathways enabling hands-on self-healing. Reiki II attunement opens the mental-emotional Ki pathway level and activates the three primary symbols. Reiki III master attunement opens the spiritual-level pathways and activates the master symbol enabling the student to transmit attunements to others. The mechanics of attunement in the Western tradition involve the master working around the seated student — opening the crown, third eye, throat, and heart energy centers; placing the Reiki symbols into the student's Ki pathways; and sealing the transmission through specific hand placements. The extraordinary vessel opening model proposes that attunement corresponds to the sequential opening of the eight extraordinary vessels — the pre-natal constitutional channels — enabling the free flow of universal Ki through the practitioner's fundamental Ki structure.

## Reiju Mechanics

- The Reiju ceremony creates a transmission connection between teacher and student Ki fields.
- Crown opening activates the upper Dan Tian and Governing Vessel spiritual connection.
- Heart opening activates the Heart-Pericardium axis and the capacity for compassionate Ki transmission.
- Palm activation opens the laogong PC-8 points enabling Ki projection and reception through the hands.

## Three-Level Opening

- Reiki I: physical-level Ki pathway opening enabling self-healing and basic hands-on practice.
- Reiki II: mental-emotional Ki pathway opening and symbol activation enabling deeper treatment.
- Reiki III: spiritual-level Ki pathway opening enabling master transmission and attunement giving.
- Each level corresponds to deepening access to the extraordinary vessel constitutional Ki structure.

## Extraordinary Vessel Model

- Reiki I attunement corresponds to opening Du Mai governing vessel Yang foundation.
- Reiki II attunement corresponds to opening Ren Mai and Chong Mai Yin and Blood constitutional vessels.
- Reiki III attunement corresponds to opening all eight extraordinary vessels enabling full constitutional Ki flow.
- This model connects Reiki attunement directly to the TCM extraordinary vessel constitutional treatment system.
