# Zero Balancing

## Abstract

Zero Balancing ZB developed by Fritz Frederick Smith MD integrates osteopathic structural principles with acupuncture energy principles to address the relationship between energy and structure at the level of the bones and joints — the foundation fields of the body. Smith proposed that the bones and joints carry the deepest and most fundamental energy fields of the body — foundation fields — as distinct from the more superficial energy fields of the soft tissues and meridians. ZB uses finger pressure and traction through fulcrums — specific held positions that simultaneously engage bone structure and energy field — to release tension at the bone-joint interface. Interface is the key concept — the practitioner maintains a clear boundary between their own energy field and the client's while making direct contact at the bone level. Working signs — client responses including spontaneous breath changes, eye flutter, swallowing, and body tremors — signal that the session is engaging the client's deeper organizational levels. Zero Balancing consistently produces altered states of consciousness in recipients corresponding to the depth of contact with the foundation energy fields. Jing-bone resonance in TCM — the Kidney Jing stored in the bones and marrow as the constitutional essence — corresponds to the Zero Balancing understanding of bone as the carrier of the deepest constitutional energy.

## Foundation Fields

- Bones and joints carry the foundational energy fields of the body underlying all other energy expression.
- These fields are denser, slower-moving, and more constitutional than the meridian Qi fields.
- Foundation field imbalances persist through soft tissue treatment until addressed directly at the bone level.
- ZB addresses foundation fields through specific bone-contact fulcrums that engage both structure and energy.

## Fulcrum

- A fulcrum is a held position of gentle traction or compression through a joint or bone segment.
- The fulcrum simultaneously engages the structural and energetic dimensions of the contacted area.
- Holding the fulcrum creates a still point of tension that enables spontaneous reorganization.
- The practitioner holds the fulcrum until working signs signal that reorganization has occurred.

## Interface

- Interface is the maintenance of clear practitioner-client energetic boundary during bone-level contact.
- Without interface the practitioner merges with the client's field losing the therapeutic leverage.
- Skilled interface enables deep contact with the client's foundation fields while maintaining practitioner clarity.
- Interface is a learned skill requiring practitioners to cultivate their own energetic boundary and clarity.

- Kidney governing the bones and marrow corresponds to the constitutional depth of ZB foundation field work.
- Jing-bone resonance corresponds to the ZB principle that the bones carry the deepest constitutional energy.
