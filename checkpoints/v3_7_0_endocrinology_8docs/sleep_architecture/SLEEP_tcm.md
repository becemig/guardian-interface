# Sleep in TCM

## Abstract

Traditional Chinese Medicine has a sophisticated multi-dimensional framework for sleep that integrates the Wei Qi diurnal circulation cycle, the Yinqiao-Yangqiao extraordinary vessel sleep-wake axis, Heart Shen anchoring in Blood during sleep, Liver Blood storage governing the Hun soul, and the twelve organ clock governing the sequential activation of organ-meridian systems through the 24-hour cycle. Sleep is understood as the yin phase of the fundamental yin-yang alternation of activity and rest, requiring adequate Blood to house the Shen, Liver Blood to anchor the Hun, Kidney Yin to cool and moisten, and the Yinqiao vessel to govern the descent into rest. Insomnia in TCM is never a single pattern but requires differentiation across at least six distinct pathological mechanisms.

## Wei Qi Diurnal Cycle

- Day cycle: Wei Qi circulates through the yang meridians and body surface during waking - 25 cycles
- Night cycle: Wei Qi enters the interior yin organs during sleep - 25 cycles
- Entry point: Wei Qi enters the interior at B 1 (Jing Ming) at the inner canthus at sleep onset
- B 1 convergence: both Yinqiao and Yangqiao terminate at B 1 - extraordinary vessel sleep gate
- Sequence: Wei Qi traverses Kidney, Heart, Lung, Liver, Spleen - organ restoration sequence
- Morning: Wei Qi re-emerges at B 1 and B 10 as waking initiates
- Disruption: Wei Qi failing to enter interior produces superficial restless sleep

## Yinqiao-Yangqiao Sleep-Wake Axis

- Yinqiao (K 6): governs the yin rest dimension - descent into sleep, fluid and yin nourishment
- Yangqiao (B 62): governs the yang waking dimension - alertness, muscular activity, rising yang
- Balance: healthy sleep requires Yinqiao to dominate at night, Yangqiao to dominate by day
- Insomnia from yang excess: Yangqiao hyperactive - cannot descend into sleep - B 62 treatment
- Insomnia from yin deficiency: Yinqiao insufficient - no yin container for rest - K 6 treatment
- Release pair: K 6 + Lu 7 activates Yinqiao and Ren Mai - primary insomnia combination

## Heart Shen and Blood

- Shen during sleep: Shen returns to Heart and is housed in Blood during sleep
- Blood deficiency insomnia: insufficient Blood cannot house Shen - Shen floats - restless sleep
- Symptoms: difficulty falling asleep, waking easily, vivid disturbing dreams, palpitations
- Heart Fire insomnia: excess Heat agitates Shen - cannot settle into rest
- Symptoms: difficulty falling asleep, feeling hot at night, bitter taste, dark urine
- Acupressure: CV 17 Sea of Tranquility, HT 7 Spirit Gate, B 10 Heavenly Pillar, GV 20

## Liver Blood and Hun Soul

- Liver stores Blood during sleep: Liver Blood volume increases as body is horizontal
- Hun soul: Ethereal Soul - stored in Liver Blood during sleep, active during dreaming in REM
- Healthy dreaming: Hun travels during sleep producing meaningful creative dreams
- Liver Blood deficiency: Hun inadequately anchored - excessive or disturbing dreams, nightmares
- Liver Qi stagnation: Heat disturbs Hun - anger-themed dreams, waking at 1-3 AM
- Organ clock: Liver time is 1-3 AM - waking at this hour indicates Liver pathology

## Twelve Organ Clock and Sleep

- 11 PM - 1 AM: Gallbladder time - Yang descends, decision energy consolidates
- 1 AM - 3 AM: Liver time - Blood stored, Hun active in dreaming
- 3 AM - 5 AM: Lung time - Wei Qi begins to surface, Po stirs
- 5 AM - 7 AM: Large Intestine time - Yang rises, elimination, awakening
- Waking at specific hours indicates corresponding organ imbalance

## TCM Insomnia Pattern Differentiation

- Heart Blood deficiency: difficulty falling asleep, vivid dreams, palpitations
- Heart and Kidney not communicating: insomnia, palpitations, low back weakness, night sweats
- Liver Fire rising: difficulty falling asleep, anger, headache, red eyes, waking 1-3 AM
- Phlegm-Heat disturbing Heart: insomnia, restlessness, nausea, chest oppression
- Kidney Yin deficiency: waking during night, night sweats, five-center heat, tinnitus
- Stomach disharmony: difficulty falling asleep after eating, bloating, reflux

## Acupressure Sleep Protocols

- Calming protocol: CV 17, B 10, GV 20, GB 20, GV 24.5
- Heart Shen anchoring: HT 7, PC 6, CV 17
- Yang excess descending: B 62, GB 20, GV 16
