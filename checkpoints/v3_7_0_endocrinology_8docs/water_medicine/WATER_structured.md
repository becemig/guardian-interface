# Structured Water and Exclusion Zone Water

## Abstract

Structured water and exclusion zone water — the controversial but experimentally documented phenomena of water adjacent to hydrophilic surfaces forming a gel-like exclusion zone EZ with distinct physical and chemical properties from bulk water, first systematically characterized by Gerald Pollack at the University of Washington — represents one of the most contested frontiers of water science, with potential implications for understanding cellular function, biological energy, and the physical basis of the Qi field in Chinese medicine if the more speculative aspects of the theory are validated. Exclusion zone water — the layer of water extending hundreds of microns from hydrophilic surfaces including biological membranes and gels, which excludes solutes including microspheres and dyes, absorbs strongly in the ultraviolet at 270 nm, has a charge of approximately -200 mV relative to bulk water, and has a honey-like viscosity and liquid crystalline ordering — was characterized by Pollack using microsphere exclusion experiments and infrared spectroscopy, demonstrating that interfacial water adjacent to biological surfaces has fundamentally different properties from bulk water. Infrared water structuring — Pollack's demonstration that infrared radiation in the range emitted by the body and by therapeutic infrared sources dramatically expands the exclusion zone, with infrared causing the EZ to grow by hundreds of microns through a mechanism involving the absorption of infrared energy to drive the ordering of water molecules at the interface — provides a potential biophysical mechanism for the effects of infrared therapeutic modalities including near-infrared photobiomodulation, far-infrared saunas, and moxibustion. Biological water organization — the controversial but experimentally supported idea advanced by Gilbert Ling that intracellular water is not bulk liquid water but is organized in a gel-like polarized multilayer state associated with intracellular proteins and membranes, with this organized water having different solvent properties that govern the differential distribution of potassium over sodium inside cells — provides an alternative or complementary framework to the standard pump-leak model of cellular ion distribution. The Qi field water structuring correspondence in TCM describes the highly speculative but conceptually stimulating correspondence between the Qi field as a coherent biological organizational field and the exclusion zone water as a measurable physical substrate of biological organization at the cellular and intercellular scale — a correspondence that remains at the speculative frontier of both traditional and scientific understanding.

## Exclusion Zone Properties

- Extends hundreds of microns from hydrophilic surfaces.
- Excludes microspheres dyes and solutes from EZ region.
- Strong UV absorption at 270 nm — distinct from bulk water spectrum.
- Negative charge approximately negative 200 mV relative to bulk water.

- Hydration shell water around proteins differs from bulk water in dynamics and structure.
- Dehydration effects on protein function partly through loss of structured hydration shell.
