# Orchestrated Objective Reduction

## Abstract

Orchestrated Objective Reduction Orch OR — the theory of consciousness proposed by mathematical physicist Roger Penrose and anesthesiologist Stuart Hameroff — proposes that consciousness arises from quantum computations occurring in the microtubules of neurons, with each conscious moment corresponding to an objective reduction — a quantum state collapse — that is orchestrated by biological processes and connected through quantum gravity to the fundamental spacetime geometry of the universe. Penrose's argument, developed in The Emperor's New Mind and Shadows of the Mind, begins with the mathematical insight that certain aspects of mathematical understanding — particularly the recognition of Godelian truths that cannot be derived by any formal algorithm — cannot be replicated by classical computation, suggesting that the brain performs non-algorithmic quantum computation. Hameroff proposed that microtubules — the cylindrical protein polymers that form the structural skeleton of neurons and coordinate cellular dynamics — are the biological quantum computers proposed by Penrose, with the tubulin protein subunits of microtubules serving as qubits whose quantum superposition states are orchestrated by microtubule-associated proteins and biological signaling. Objective reduction is Penrose's proposed mechanism for quantum state collapse — rather than the decoherence model in which quantum states collapse through interaction with the environment, objective reduction proposes that quantum superpositions become unstable when they involve sufficient spacetime curvature and spontaneously collapse to a definite state at a rate determined by quantum gravity. Gamma synchrony — the 40 Hz and higher frequency neural oscillations associated with conscious awareness, attention, and cognitive binding — corresponds in the Orch OR theory to the frequency of orchestrated objective reduction events in neuronal microtubules. The anesthetic action of diverse anesthetic agents through microtubule disruption provides indirect evidence for the microtubule consciousness theory — if microtubule quantum computation is essential for consciousness, then anesthetics that disrupt microtubule quantum states would be expected to suppress consciousness. Shen as quantum field describes the TCM cultivation understanding of Shen — the luminous awareness that witnesses and guides all experience — as the macroscopic quantum coherent field of consciousness that Orch OR proposes arises from orchestrated quantum processes in the microtubule networks of the brain.
