# Somatic Experiencing

## Abstract

Somatic Experiencing SE developed by Peter Levine is a naturalistic body-based approach to healing trauma that draws on ethological observations of wild animals — who routinely survive life-threatening events without developing trauma — to understand the biological basis of trauma and its healing. Levine observed that wild prey animals regularly face life-threatening predator encounters and discharge the mobilized survival energy through shaking, trembling, and spontaneous movement after the threat passes — completing the biological defensive response and returning to normal function. Human trauma occurs when this completion is prevented — by overwhelming experience, by social constraints on expression, or by the addition of shame and judgment to the defensive response — leaving the survival energy undischarged and the nervous system frozen in incomplete defensive activation. The felt sense — Eugene Gendlin's term for the holistic bodily knowing of a situation — is the primary therapeutic medium of SE. Working with the felt sense enables access to the implicit procedural and somatic encoding of traumatic experience below the level of verbal narrative. Titration — working with very small amounts of traumatic activation rather than cathartic flooding — is the fundamental SE principle distinguishing it from abreactive trauma approaches. Pendulation — the therapeutic use of oscillation between activation and resource, between the trauma vortex and the healing vortex — enables gradual expansion of the window of tolerance. Kidney fear release in TCM — the release of frozen constitutional fear from the Kidney spirit through the completion of the incomplete freeze response — corresponds precisely to the SE healing process.

## Trauma Physiology

- Trauma occurs when the biological survival response is overwhelmed and cannot complete its cycle.
- Fight and flight activation mobilizes massive physiological resources — when blocked these remain undischarged.
- Freeze is the last-resort survival response — immobility, analgesia, and metabolic shutdown.
- Human freeze differs from animal freeze in that cognitive shame and judgment prevent spontaneous completion.

## Felt Sense

- The felt sense is the holistic bodily knowing of a situation — not an emotion or a thought but a body sense.
- It is the implicit procedural intelligence of the nervous system made available to awareness.
- Working with the felt sense accesses trauma encoding below the level of verbal and explicit memory.
- The felt sense shifts naturally when given attention — following its shifts is the SE therapeutic movement.

## Titration and Pendulation

- Titration works with tiny amounts of traumatic activation — a drop at a time rather than flooding.
- This prevents re-traumatization while enabling the nervous system to process what it previously could not.
- Pendulation oscillates between activation and resource — trauma vortex and healing vortex.
- Graduated oscillation gradually expands the window of tolerance enabling more activation to be processed.
