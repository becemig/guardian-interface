# Somatic Experiencing Advanced

## Abstract

Somatic Experiencing SE advanced practice develops the foundational principles of titration and pendulation into sophisticated clinical skills for tracking and completing the incomplete defensive responses frozen in the nervous system by overwhelming experience. The SIBAM model provides the clinical tracking framework: Sensation, Image, Behavior, Affect, and Meaning — the five channels through which trauma is encoded and through which healing occurs. The trauma vortex describes the pull of traumatic activation drawing the system toward overwhelm, while the healing vortex represents the resources and regulated states that provide counterbalance. Renegotiation is the core SE process — rather than cathartic re-experiencing, SE facilitates the gradual renegotiation of the traumatic experience through small steps of controlled approach and retreat. Completion of the thwarted defensive response — the freeze, flight, or fight impulse that was prevented from completing — is the central biological mechanism of SE healing. This completion occurs not through behavioral enactment but through the subtle internal movement and nervous system discharge that completes the biological action sequence. Trembling, shaking, temperature changes, spontaneous breath, and involuntary movement are all forms of nervous system discharge. Kidney fear resolution in TCM — the restoration of the Kidney spirit's capacity for grounded fearlessness after traumatic depletion — corresponds to the SE process of completing frozen fear responses and restoring the nervous system's regulatory capacity.

## SIBAM Model

- Sensation tracking follows the body's felt sense including temperature, pressure, movement, and visceral sensation.
- Image includes visual imagery, memories, and other sensory representations arising during processing.
- Behavior includes observable and micro-movement patterns including posture, gesture, and facial expression.
- Affect includes emotional states and their somatic textures while Meaning includes cognitive interpretations.

## Trauma and Healing Vortex

- The trauma vortex is the pull of traumatic activation threatening to overwhelm the system.
- The healing vortex represents resources, safety, and regulated states that provide counterbalance.
- SE works by alternating attention between the trauma and healing vortices — pendulating between activation and resource.
- Gradually increasing the time in the trauma vortex while maintaining access to the healing vortex builds capacity.

## Completion and Discharge

- Incomplete defensive responses — frozen fight, flight, or freeze — maintain the trauma in active unresolved state.
- Completion occurs through the subtle internal completion of the interrupted biological action sequence.
- Discharge manifests as trembling, shaking, temperature change, spontaneous breath, and involuntary movement.
- Completion produces a spontaneous shift in tissue tone, breathing, and subjective sense of settledness.

## TCM Correspondence

- Kidney fear resolution corresponds to SE completion of frozen fear responses restoring Kidney Zhi groundedness.
- Kidney governing survival and constitutional fear corresponds to the dorsal vagal freeze response of overwhelming trauma.
- Governing Vessel governing the spinal axis corresponds to the SE attention to spinal and postural patterns in trauma.
