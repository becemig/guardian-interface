# Dance Movement Therapy

## Abstract

Dance movement therapy DMT is a psychotherapeutic modality that uses movement and dance as the primary medium for emotional expression, integration, and healing. Marian Chace pioneered DMT at St. Elizabeths Hospital in Washington DC demonstrating that structured movement groups produced significant therapeutic benefits for psychiatric patients. Chace's primary technique of kinesthetic empathy — the therapist mirroring the patient's movement qualities to communicate attuned understanding — became foundational to the field. Laban Movement Analysis LMA provides the systematic vocabulary for describing movement qualities across four categories: body, effort, shape, and space. Authentic Movement developed by Mary Whitehouse involves moving from inner impulse with eyes closed while a witness holds the space — a moving meditation that bridges somatic and depth psychological dimensions. Body image disturbance in eating disorders, trauma, and psychosis is directly addressed through DMT's grounding in lived movement experience. Liver Hun vision — the Liver spirit's projective creative imagination that envisions possibility and drives creative action — corresponds to the improvisational creative movement intelligence cultivated through dance movement therapy.

## Kinesthetic Empathy

- The therapist observes and mirrors the patient's movement qualities communicating non-verbal attunement.
- Mirroring activates mirror neuron systems enabling direct kinesthetic resonance between therapist and patient.
- Gradual introduction of movement variations introduces therapeutic alternatives while maintaining connection.
- Group mirroring creates kinesthetic cohesion — the sense of being seen and moving together.

## Laban Movement Analysis

- Body category describes what body parts are moving and how they are organized.
- Effort category describes the dynamic qualities of movement — weight, space, time, and flow.
- Shape category describes how the body changes form in relation to itself and the environment.
- Space category describes directional pathways and levels of movement in the kinesphere.

## Authentic Movement

- The mover follows inner impulse with eyes closed moving spontaneously without aesthetic or expressive agenda.
- The witness holds focused attention witnessing without interpretation or direction.
- After moving, mover and witness share their experiences — the mover's inner experience and the witness's observations.
- This practice develops the inner witness — the capacity to observe one's own somatic experience from within.
