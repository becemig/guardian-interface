# Dance Movement Therapy and Authentic Movement

## Abstract

Dance Movement Therapy DMT is a psychotherapeutic approach that uses movement and dance to support emotional, cognitive, social, and physical integration. Authentic Movement developed by Mary Whitehouse from Jungian active imagination principles involves a mover moving with closed eyes following inner impulse while a witness observes with full attention. Laban Movement Analysis provides a comprehensive descriptive vocabulary for movement qualities through the categories of body, effort, shape, and space. DMT demonstrates clinical effectiveness for trauma, depression, anxiety, and psychosis through its embodied non-verbal processing pathway. Shen emergence — the spontaneous expression of genuine inner life through movement — corresponds to the authentic movement principle and the HeartMind expressing freely through the body.

## Authentic Movement

- The mover closes their eyes and follows inner movement impulse without directing or shaping the movement.
- The witness observes with full non-judgmental attention holding the space for the mover's exploration.
- The witnessing function corresponds to the internalized observing ego — the capacity to be simultaneously mover and witness.
- Developing the inner witness transforms impulsive automatic movement into conscious embodied expression.

## Laban Movement Analysis

- Body category describes which body parts initiate, lead, and follow in the movement.
- Effort category describes the movement qualities of weight, space, time, and flow.
- Shape category describes how the body changes form through growing, shrinking, enclosing, and spreading.
- Space category describes the pathways and levels of movement in the kinesphere and general space.

## Clinical Applications

- DMT reduces trauma symptoms through non-verbal processing of pre-verbal and body-held traumatic memory.
- Depression responds to DMT through activation of approach motivation systems through expansive movement.
- Psychosis benefits from DMT through grounding, boundary establishment, and reality-oriented movement.
- Parkinson disease shows significant motor and quality of life improvement from dance-based interventions.

## TCM Correspondence

- Shen emergence through authentic movement corresponds to the Heart expressing its genuine nature freely.

- Hun the ethereal soul governs imagination, vision, and projective creative movement corresponding to authentic movement impulse.
- The witness function cultivates Shen clarity — the capacity to observe experience without identification or suppression.
