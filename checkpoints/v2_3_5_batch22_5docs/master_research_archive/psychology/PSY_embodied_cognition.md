# Embodied Cognition

## Abstract

Embodied cognition is the theoretical framework proposing that cognitive processes are fundamentally shaped by the body's sensorimotor capacities and its interactions with the environment. George Lakoff and Mark Johnson's conceptual metaphor theory demonstrated that abstract concepts are systematically grounded in bodily experience — understanding is grasping, time is spatial, emotional warmth derives from physical warmth. Lawrence Barsalou's perceptual symbol systems proposes that conceptual knowledge is stored as multimodal simulations in sensorimotor cortices rather than as amodal symbols. James Gibson's ecological psychology introduced affordances — the action possibilities that environments offer organisms — as the fundamental units of perception-action coupling. Francisco Varela's enactivism proposes that cognition arises through the structural coupling of organism and environment rather than internal information processing. Yi — the Spleen's applied intelligence — as the intelligence that arises from direct bodily engagement with the world corresponds to the embodied cognition understanding of thought as grounded sensorimotor simulation.

## Conceptual Metaphor

- Abstract concepts are systematically grounded in concrete bodily experience through conceptual metaphor.
- Understanding is grasping — we talk of getting a grip on ideas, holding a thought, grasping a concept.
- Emotional warmth derives from physical warmth — warm people, cold shoulder, heated argument.
- These are not mere linguistic conventions but reflect the sensorimotor grounding of abstract thought.

## Perceptual Symbol Systems

- Barsalou proposes that conceptual knowledge is stored as multimodal sensorimotor simulations.
- Understanding chair activates visual, tactile, and motor simulations of chair-related experience.
- Thinking about running activates motor cortex corresponding to running movements.
- Abstract concepts activate embodied simulations of the concrete experiences they are grounded in.

## Affordances and Enactivism

- Gibson's affordances are the action possibilities that environments offer to organisms with specific capacities.
- Perception is directly of affordances — of what can be done — not of neutral physical properties.
- Varela's enactivism proposes cognition as the enacted bringing-forth of a world through structural coupling.
- Knowledge is not stored representation but the capacity for skilled embodied action in a domain.

## TCM Correspondence

- Yi as the Spleen intelligence arising from direct bodily engagement corresponds to embodied cognitive knowing.
- The body-based concepts of TCM — Qi flowing, Blood nourishing, organs housing spirits — are embodied metaphors.
- Qigong and Tai Chi as embodied practices cultivate direct sensorimotor knowledge rather than abstract understanding.
- Heart Shen directing and Spleen Yi applying together correspond to the perception-action coupling of enactivism.
