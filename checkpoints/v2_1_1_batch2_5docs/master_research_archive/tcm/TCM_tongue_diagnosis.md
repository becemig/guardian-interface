# Tongue Diagnosis

## Abstract

Tongue diagnosis is one of the primary diagnostic methods in Traditional Chinese Medicine providing a direct visual window into the state of the internal organs, Qi, Blood, and body fluids. The tongue body color reflects the state of Blood and Yin, the tongue coat reflects the state of the Stomach and digestive function, and the tongue shape reflects the state of Qi and fluid metabolism. Different regions of the tongue correspond to different organ systems enabling topographic diagnosis. Tongue diagnosis provides objective physical signs that complement the subjective pulse findings and patient history in the TCM diagnostic process.

## Tongue Body Color

- Pale tongue indicates Blood deficiency or Yang deficiency with insufficient Blood or warmth to color the tongue.
- Red tongue indicates Heat — either excess Heat from pathogenic invasion or deficiency Heat from Yin deficiency.
- Deep red or scarlet tongue indicates severe Heat in the nutritive or Blood level in febrile disease.
- Purple tongue indicates Blood stasis with impaired circulation producing cyanotic discoloration.
- Blue-purple tongue indicates severe cold or blood stasis with Yang deficiency.

## Tongue Coat

- White coat is normal or indicates cold and exterior conditions when thick.
- Yellow coat indicates Heat and interior conditions — the deeper the yellow the more severe the Heat.
- Thick coat indicates excess pathogenic factor — Dampness, Phlegm, or food stagnation.
- Thin coat is normal indicating good Stomach function and absence of significant pathogenic factor.
- Absent coat or peeled tongue indicates Stomach Yin deficiency with loss of fluid production.
- Greasy coat indicates Dampness or Phlegm accumulation in the middle Jiao.

## Tongue Shape

- Swollen tongue indicates Dampness or Phlegm with fluid accumulation and Spleen Qi deficiency.
- Thin tongue indicates Blood or Yin deficiency with insufficient nourishment.
- Scalloped edges indicate Spleen Qi deficiency with teeth impressions from soft swollen tongue against teeth.
- Stiff tongue indicates Wind or Heat disturbing the Heart and Shen.
- Flaccid tongue indicates severe Qi and Blood deficiency or Kidney Yin exhaustion.
- Deviated tongue indicates Wind stroke or internal Wind.

## Topographic Regions

- Tip of tongue corresponds to the Heart and Lung reflecting upper Jiao conditions.
- Middle tongue corresponds to the Spleen and Stomach reflecting middle Jiao digestive function.
- Root of tongue corresponds to the Kidney reflecting lower Jiao and constitutional conditions.
- Sides of tongue correspond to the Liver and Gallbladder reflecting Wood element states.
