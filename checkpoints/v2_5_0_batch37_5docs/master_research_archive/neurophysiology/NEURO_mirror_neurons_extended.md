# Mirror Neuron System Extended

## Abstract

Mirror neurons were discovered in the macaque premotor cortex by Giacomo Rizzolatti and colleagues — neurons that fire both when the monkey performs a goal-directed action and when it observes the same action performed by another. In humans the mirror neuron system MNS comprises a network of areas including the inferior frontal gyrus Broca's area and the inferior parietal lobule that shows matching activity during action observation and execution. The MNS provides a neural mechanism for direct action understanding — the observed action is understood by simulating it in one's own motor system rather than through abstract inference. Marco Iacoboni proposed that the MNS is the neural substrate of empathy — we understand others' emotions by resonating with them motorically and viscerally rather than through cold cognitive inference. The broken mirror theory of autism proposes that impaired MNS function underlies the social and empathy deficits of autism spectrum disorder. Language evolution theories propose that the MNS in the inferior frontal gyrus provided the neural substrate for the evolution of language from manual gesture through a mirror mechanism. Embodied simulation — the brain's use of its own motor, emotional, and sensory systems to simulate others' experience — provides the foundation for intersubjectivity. Heart resonance in TCM — the direct Shen-to-Shen communication between Heart-Minds that underlies genuine empathy and therapeutic rapport — corresponds to the mirror neuron embodied simulation as its neurobiological substrate.

## Action Understanding

- Mirror neurons in the premotor cortex fire for both action execution and observation of the same action.
- They respond to goal-directed actions — the goal matters more than the precise movement kinematics.
- Occluded actions activate mirror neurons if the goal can be inferred from context — simulation fills the gap.
- Mirror neuron activity during observation is stronger for actions within the observer's own motor repertoire.

## Empathy and Simulation

- Observing pain in others activates the affective pain matrix — anterior cingulate and anterior insula — in the observer.
- Facial expression observation activates the observer's own facial motor cortex through mirror mechanisms.
- Emotional contagion — the automatic transmission of emotional states — operates through embodied simulation.
- Simulation theory proposes that we understand others by running simulations using our own neural resources.

## Language Evolution

- Broca's area in the inferior frontal gyrus contains mirror neurons responding to both manual gestures and speech.
- This suggests language evolved from manual gesture through a mirror mechanism enabling gesture-to-speech transition.
- Speech observation activates motor speech areas in Broca's area through mirror mechanisms.
- Sign language uses the same inferior frontal mirror system as spoken language in the deaf.
