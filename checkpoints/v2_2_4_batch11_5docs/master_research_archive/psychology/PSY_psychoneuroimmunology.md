# Psychoneuroimmunology

## Abstract

Psychoneuroimmunology PNI is the scientific field investigating the bidirectional communication between psychological processes, the nervous system, and the immune system. Robert Ader and Nicholas Cohen's 1975 discovery that immune responses could be classically conditioned established the field by demonstrating that the nervous system directly regulates immunity. Chronic psychological stress produces consistent and clinically significant immunosuppression through HPA axis cortisol elevation, sympathetic nervous system activation, and reduced vagal tone. Social support, positive emotion, laughter, and spiritual practice all produce measurable immune enhancement. The Heart Shen governing psychological well-being and the Lung Wei Qi governing defensive immunity are linked in TCM through the Metal-Fire relationship and the Heart-Lung Qi axis.

## Conditioned Immune Responses

- Ader and Cohen demonstrated that saccharin paired with cyclophosphamide produced conditioned immunosuppression.
- This classical conditioning of immune responses proved the nervous system directly regulates immunity.
- Placebo immune enhancement and nocebo immune suppression operate through conditioned neural-immune pathways.
- Expectation and belief about treatment efficacy directly modulate immune outcomes through these pathways.

## Stress Immunosuppression

- Acute stress produces transient immune enhancement redistributing immune cells to sites of potential injury.
- Chronic stress produces sustained immunosuppression through cortisol, epinephrine, and reduced vagal tone.
- NK cell activity, antibody production, and wound healing are all impaired by chronic psychological stress.
- Marital conflict produces slower wound healing corresponding to reduced immune regenerative capacity.

## Positive Emotion and Immunity

- Social support produces measurable NK cell enhancement and reduced stress-induced immunosuppression.
- Laughter increases salivary IgA, NK cell activity, and reduces cortisol providing immune enhancement.
- Spiritual practice and meaning-making reduce inflammatory cytokines and enhance cellular immunity.
- Qigong and Tai Chi produce immune enhancement through both autonomic and neuroendocrine mechanisms.

## TCM Correspondence

- Heart Shen governing psychological well-being directly influences Wei Qi defensive immunity.
- The Lung-Heart relationship governs the connection between emotional state and surface immune defense.
- Joy nourishes Heart Fire which supports Lung Metal through the Fire-Metal controlling cycle.
- Prolonged emotional constraint producing Liver Qi stagnation impairs Wei Qi distribution and immune function.
