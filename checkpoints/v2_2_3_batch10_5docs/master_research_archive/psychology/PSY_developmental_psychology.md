# Developmental Psychology

## Abstract

Developmental psychology examines the psychological growth of individuals across the lifespan from prenatal development through aging. Attachment theory describes the bond between infant and caregiver as the foundational template for all subsequent relationship and self-regulatory capacity. Adverse childhood experiences ACEs including abuse, neglect, and household dysfunction produce dose-dependent increases in adult disease risk through epigenetic and neurobiological mechanisms. Erikson's psychosocial stages describe the developmental challenges of each life phase while Piaget's cognitive stages describe the development of logical reasoning. Constitutional patterning in TCM describes how prenatal and early childhood experiences shape the fundamental Five Element constitutional type and its characteristic vulnerabilities and strengths.

## Attachment Theory

- Secure attachment develops when caregivers consistently respond to the infant's distress with attuned soothing.
- Secure attachment provides the safe base from which the child can explore and the safe haven to return to.
- Insecure attachment patterns — anxious, avoidant, and disorganized — arise from inconsistent or frightening caregiving.
- Adult attachment patterns replicate early templates in romantic partnerships and therapeutic relationships.

## Adverse Childhood Experiences

- ACEs include physical, emotional, and sexual abuse, neglect, and household dysfunction including violence and substance abuse.
- The ACEs study demonstrated dose-dependent relationships between ACE score and adult disease risk.
- High ACE scores predict cardiovascular disease, cancer, diabetes, autoimmune disease, and mental health disorders.
- Epigenetic reprogramming of HPA axis stress reactivity mediates the long-term health effects of early adversity.

## Psychosocial Development

- Erikson's eight stages from trust versus mistrust in infancy through integrity versus despair in late life.
- Each stage presents a developmental challenge whose resolution shapes character and adaptive capacity.
- Unresolved developmental challenges create constitutional vulnerabilities corresponding to Five Element imbalances.
- Guardian Interface knowledge design supports adult developmental tasks of generativity and integrity.
