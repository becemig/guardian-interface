# Prefrontal Cortex and Executive Function

## Abstract

The prefrontal cortex is the anterior frontal lobe region governing the highest order cognitive functions of inhibitory control, working memory, and cognitive flexibility collectively termed executive functions. The dorsolateral prefrontal cortex supports working memory and cognitive control while the ventromedial prefrontal cortex governs emotional regulation and value-based decision making. The anterior cingulate cortex monitors conflict and errors triggering executive control when automatic responses are insufficient. Executive function development continues into the mid-twenties through protracted prefrontal myelination. Shen governance in TCM — the Heart-Mind's capacity to direct, regulate, and integrate all psychic functions — corresponds to the executive function of the prefrontal cortex governing the ordering of mental life.

## Core Executive Functions

- Inhibitory control suppresses automatic responses enabling deliberate chosen action over habitual reaction.
- Working memory maintains and manipulates information over seconds enabling complex reasoning and planning.
- Cognitive flexibility shifts between mental sets, perspectives, and strategies enabling adaptive response to change.
- These three core executive functions underlie all higher cognitive capacities including academic achievement and social success.

## Neural Substrates

- Dorsolateral PFC supports working memory through sustained neural firing maintaining representations online.
- Ventromedial PFC integrates emotional signals into decision making through connections with amygdala and insula.
- Anterior cingulate cortex detects conflict between competing response tendencies triggering executive control.
- Orbitofrontal cortex encodes reward value and governs impulse control through inhibition of limbic drives.

## Practice Effects

- Mindfulness meditation produces structural and functional changes in prefrontal cortex and anterior cingulate.
- Tai Chi improves executive function in older adults through its dual-task coordinative and attentional demands.
- The inhibition training of Alexander Technique directly cultivates inhibitory control as a core practice principle.
- HRV coherence states enhance prefrontal function through improved heart-brain coupling.
