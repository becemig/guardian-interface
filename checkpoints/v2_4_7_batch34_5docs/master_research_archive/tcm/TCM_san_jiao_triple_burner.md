# San Jiao Triple Burner

## Abstract

The San Jiao Triple Burner is the most enigmatic of the twelve organ systems in TCM — described as having a name but no form, governing water metabolism throughout the three warming spaces of the body without being a discrete anatomical organ. The Triple Burner serves three primary functions: governing the transformation and transportation of fluids throughout the body as the official in charge of irrigation and the controller of the water passages, distributing Yuan Qi original Qi from the Kidney to all organs through the Triple Burner network, and governing the three Jiao warming spaces — upper Jiao like a mist governing Heart and Lung function, middle Jiao like a maceration chamber governing Spleen and Stomach transformation, and lower Jiao like a drainage ditch governing Kidney and Bladder excretion. The ministerial fire Xiang Huo is housed in the Triple Burner and Pericardium as the functional expression of Kidney Yang warming and activating all organ systems from the constitutional root. Modern anatomical correlates for the Triple Burner include the fascial network — the connective tissue system permeating all body compartments distributing fluids and providing structural continuity — and the mesentery as the connective tissue organizing the abdominal organs.

## Three Warming Spaces

- Upper Jiao like a mist governs the distribution of fluids and Qi through the Heart and Lung — the chest and head.
- Middle Jiao like a maceration chamber governs the transformation of food and fluids through the Spleen and Stomach.
- Lower Jiao like a drainage ditch governs the separation of clear and turbid fluids through the Kidney and Bladder.
- The three Jiao together constitute the complete water metabolism system of the body.

## Yuan Qi Distribution

- The Triple Burner distributes Yuan Qi from the Kidney to all twelve organs through its network.
- This distribution function makes the Triple Burner the functional connection between the constitutional Kidney root and all organ activity.
- Impaired Triple Burner Qi distribution produces deficiency patterns in organs despite adequate constitutional Yuan Qi.
- TB-5 Waiguan is the confluent point of the Yang Linking Vessel and the primary Triple Burner treatment point.

## Fascial Network Correspondence

- The fascial network permeating all body compartments distributes fluids and provides structural continuity.
- Fascial planes correspond to the Triple Burner passages through which water and Qi distribute throughout the body.
- The mesentery as the connective tissue organizing all abdominal organs corresponds to the middle Jiao organizing function.
- Fascial restriction corresponds to Triple Burner fluid obstruction producing regional Qi and fluid stagnation.

## TCM Correspondence

- Ministerial fire as the functional Yang warmth activating all organs corresponds to the distributed metabolic heat of oxidative phosphorylation.
- Triple Burner governing the water passages corresponds to the lymphatic and interstitial fluid systems.
- Pericardium paired with Triple Burner as its Yin counterpart corresponds to the cardiac and vascular protective functions.
- Triple Burner transformation of fluids corresponds to the kidney filtration and fluid redistribution of modern physiology.
