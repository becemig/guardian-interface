# Interpersonal Neurobiology

## Abstract

Interpersonal neurobiology IPNB developed by Daniel Siegel is an interdisciplinary framework integrating neuroscience, developmental psychology, attachment theory, and mindfulness practice around the central concept of integration — the linking of differentiated parts into a functional whole. Siegel proposes that mental health is characterized by integration while mental illness involves either chaos from insufficient integration or rigidity from excessive constraints on the system. The triangle of well-being encompasses mind, brain, and relationships as three facets of a single reality — each can be used to influence the others. Mindsight is Siegel's term for the capacity to see the mind clearly — to observe the internal workings of one's own mind and the minds of others — enabling intentional integration. The window of tolerance describes the optimal arousal zone within which integration is possible — above the window lies hyperarousal and below lies hypoarousal. Coherent narrative — the ability to tell a cohesive, emotionally integrated life story — is the strongest predictor of secure attachment transmission from parent to child and corresponds to high levels of brain integration. Reflective function — the capacity to understand behavior in terms of mental states — develops through attuned interpersonal experience and mediates the intergenerational transmission of attachment security. Heart-Shen resonance in TCM — the mutual attunement of Heart-Mind between practitioner and patient enabling direct Shen to Shen communication — corresponds to the IPNB principle of resonance circuits enabling interpersonal neural integration.

## Window of Tolerance

- The window of tolerance is the optimal arousal zone within which integration and learning are possible.
- Hyperarousal above the window produces sympathetic flooding — fight, flight, and fragmented experience.
- Hypoarousal below the window produces dorsal vagal shutdown — dissociation and collapsed experience.
- Therapeutic work widens the window of tolerance enabling integration of previously overwhelming experience.

## Mindsight

- Mindsight is the capacity to see the mind clearly — both one's own mind and the minds of others.
- It includes insight — seeing one's own internal states — and empathy — sensing the states of others.
- Mindsight is a trainable skill developed through mindfulness practice and attuned interpersonal experience.
- The wheel of awareness — attending to different rim elements while resting in the hub of awareness — is Siegel's mindsight practice.
