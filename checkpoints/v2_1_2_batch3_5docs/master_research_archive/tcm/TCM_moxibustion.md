# Moxibustion

## Abstract

Moxibustion is the application of burning Artemisia vulgaris mugwort to acupoints and meridian pathways producing thermal stimulation with additional pharmacological effects from the combustion products. Moxa is the primary Yang-tonifying treatment modality in Chinese medicine indicated for Cold, Deficiency, and Stagnation patterns. Direct moxa involves burning moxa cones directly on the skin while indirect moxa interposes a medium such as ginger or garlic between the moxa and skin. Modern research demonstrates that moxibustion produces far-infrared radiation, stimulates local immune and circulation responses, and modulates autonomic function. ST-36 Zusanli moxibustion is the most studied point for immune enhancement and general Yang tonification.

## Moxa Material and Combustion

- Artemisia vulgaris mugwort leaves are processed into moxa wool and formed into cones or sticks.
- The aging and processing of moxa increases its combustion quality and reduces irritating volatile oils.
- Moxa combustion produces far-infrared radiation in the 2 to 6 micron range penetrating several centimeters into tissue.
- Combustion products including borneol, cineole, and artemisinin are absorbed through the skin producing pharmacological effects.

## Direct Moxibustion

- Scarring direct moxa burns small cones directly on the skin producing a therapeutic scar.
- Non-scarring direct moxa removes the cone before it burns to the skin producing warmth without scarring.
- Direct moxa produces the strongest thermal and immunological stimulation of any moxa technique.
- Classical Japanese moxa uses very small precise cones on specific points for constitutional tonification.

## Indirect Moxibustion

- Ginger moxa places a slice of fresh ginger between the moxa cone and the skin warming and dispersing cold.
- Salt moxa fills the navel CV-8 with salt before applying moxa for emergency Yang restoration.
- Warming needle moxa attaches moxa to the handle of an inserted needle conducting heat into the acupoint.
- Moxa stick indirect moxa holds a burning moxa stick 2 to 3 cm from the skin for comfortable warming.

## Clinical Applications

- ST-36 Zusanli moxibustion tonifies Spleen and Stomach Qi, boosts immunity, and supports general vitality.
- CV-4 Guanyuan moxibustion tonifies Kidney Yang and original Qi for deep constitutional deficiency.
- CV-6 Qihai moxibustion tonifies Qi and Yang for fatigue, prolapse, and constitutional weakness.
- GV-4 Mingmen moxibustion tonifies Kidney Yang and the Gate of Vitality for cold limbs and low libido.

## TCM Correspondence

- Moxibustion is the primary treatment for Cold and Yang deficiency patterns where acupuncture alone is insufficient.
- The warming action of moxa moves Qi and Blood stagnation caused by cold invasion or constitutional Yang deficiency.
- Regular ST-36 moxibustion as a health maintenance practice is recommended in classical texts for longevity.
- Mingmen Gate of Vitality moxibustion directly tonifies the constitutional Yang fire governing all warming functions.
