# Innate Immunity and Pattern Recognition

## Abstract

Innate immunity and pattern recognition — the ancient evolutionarily conserved arm of the immune system that provides immediate non-specific defense against pathogens through the recognition of conserved molecular patterns shared by broad classes of microorganisms but absent from host cells, operating through germline-encoded pattern recognition receptors including the toll-like receptors, NOD-like receptors, RIG-I-like receptors, and C-type lectin receptors that detect pathogen-associated molecular patterns and damage-associated molecular patterns to trigger rapid innate immune responses within minutes to hours of pathogen encounter. Toll-like receptors and PAMPs — the family of 13 human toll-like receptors expressed on macrophages, dendritic cells, neutrophils, and epithelial cells that recognize specific PAMPs including TLR4 recognizing bacterial LPS, TLR2 recognizing bacterial peptidoglycan and lipoteichoic acid, TLR3 recognizing double-stranded viral RNA, TLR7 and TLR8 recognizing single-stranded viral RNA, and TLR9 recognizing bacterial CpG DNA, with TLR signaling through MyD88 and TRIF adaptors activating NF-kB and IRF transcription factors to produce pro-inflammatory cytokines, type I interferons, and antimicrobial peptides. Inflammasome activation — the intracellular multi-protein complexes assembled in macrophages and other innate immune cells in response to cytosolic danger signals including bacterial toxins, crystalline substances, mitochondrial DNA, and ATP, with the NLRP3 inflammasome being the most clinically relevant, assembling in response to cholesterol crystals, uric acid crystals, and amyloid beta to activate caspase-1 which cleaves pro-IL-1beta and pro-IL-18 into their mature pro-inflammatory forms and initiates pyroptotic cell death — with NLRP3 inflammasome activation implicated in gout, atherosclerosis, type 2 diabetes, and Alzheimer's disease. Innate immune memory trained immunity — the paradigm-shifting discovery that innate immune cells including monocytes and NK cells can develop a form of immunological memory through epigenetic reprogramming following initial stimulation, with beta-glucan from fungal cell walls inducing trained immunity in monocytes through H3K4me3 histone methylation at the promoters of cytokine genes enabling enhanced responses to subsequent stimulation — challenging the dogma that memory is exclusively an adaptive immune phenomenon. The Wei Qi defensive fire first response correspondence in TCM describes the classical Wei Qi as the fast-moving defensive Qi circulating in the exterior of the body providing the first line of defense against external pathogenic factors, distributed by the Lung to the skin surface and responding immediately upon pathogen encounter — a classical framework corresponding to the innate immune system's rapid non-specific first-line defense against pathogens.

## Toll-Like Receptors

- TLR4: LPS gram-negative bacteria — primary sepsis trigger receptor.
- TLR2: peptidoglycan lipoteichoic acid — gram-positive bacteria recognition.
- TLR3: dsRNA — viral infection detection — type I interferon induction.
- TLR9: CpG DNA — bacterial and viral DNA — plasmacytoid dendritic cells.

## Inflammasome

- NLRP3: cholesterol crystals uric acid ATP — sterile inflammation.
- Caspase-1: activated by inflammasome — cleaves pro-IL-1b pro-IL-18.
- Pyroptosis: inflammatory cell death — IL-1b IL-18 release amplifies inflammation.
- Disease: gout atherosclerosis T2D Alzheimer — NLRP3 implicated.
