# Somatic Modulation of Selective Attention

## Abstract

This knowledge node connects selective attention, somatic regulation, and Guardian Interface telemetry.

## Key Ideas

- Attention can be modeled as both cognitive filtering and embodied regulation.
- Somatic practices such as Tai Chi and Qigong may influence attention through breath, posture, rhythm, and autonomic state.
- Guardian Interface can connect written research to telemetry streams such as HRV, respiration rate, and confidence state.
