# Creativity and Insight

## Abstract

Creativity involves novel idea generation through deliberate analytical processes and spontaneous insight. The default mode network supports associative thinking during internally directed cognition. Insight is preceded by right temporal alpha power reflecting internal attentional focus followed by a gamma burst at the moment of solution. Shen clarity corresponds to the neural state optimal for creative insight.

## Default Mode Network

- DMN comprising medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate, and angular gyrus supports associative ideation.
- Creative individuals show stronger DMN-executive network coupling enabling controlled associative thinking.
- Meditation training enhances flexible attention between focused and open awareness improving creative DMN use.

## Insight and Alpha Waves

- Right temporal alpha power increases before insight reflecting internal attention and sensory suppression.
- The aha moment shows a gamma burst in right temporal cortex marking novel association integration.
- Incubation allows unconscious spreading activation to generate associations outside conscious awareness.
- Sleep incubation is particularly powerful through REM memory network reorganization.
