# Binaural Beats and Auditory Entrainment

## Abstract

Binaural beats — the auditory phenomenon produced when two slightly different frequencies are presented separately to each ear through headphones, causing the brain to perceive a third beat frequency equal to the difference between the two tones — are the most widely studied and clinically applied form of auditory brainwave entrainment, with a growing body of EEG research demonstrating measurable effects on brainwave activity, cognitive function, mood, and physiological parameters. Gerald Oster's landmark 1973 Scientific American paper on auditory beats in the brain established the scientific foundation for binaural beat research by demonstrating the neurophysiological basis of the binaural beat percept — the frequency-following response — and proposing its potential applications in neurology and consciousness research. The frequency-following response — the tendency of cortical EEG activity to synchronize with the frequency of an external auditory stimulus — is the neurophysiological mechanism underlying binaural beat entrainment: when a binaural beat frequency is presented at 10 Hz the brain's EEG alpha activity increases as the auditory cortex and associated networks synchronize with the 10 Hz stimulus. Specific binaural beat frequency protocols target different brainwave states: delta 0.5-4 Hz protocols for deep sleep and pain reduction, theta 4-8 Hz protocols for meditation, creativity, and hypnagogic states, alpha 8-12 Hz protocols for relaxed alert focus and stress reduction, beta 12-30 Hz protocols for focused attention and cognitive performance, and gamma 30-100 Hz protocols most notably 40 Hz for cognitive enhancement, memory consolidation, and the promising Alzheimer's disease research demonstrating that 40 Hz gamma entrainment reduces amyloid plaque burden in mouse models. Hemispheric synchronization — the Robert Monroe Institute's hemisync technology — uses binaural beats to produce interhemispheric coherence, promoting states of expanded awareness and altered consciousness through the synchronization of left and right hemisphere activity. The Shen entrainment correspondence describes the TCM understanding that specific acoustic frequencies and rhythmic sound patterns can entrain the Heart Shen — the governing consciousness — producing specific states of awareness corresponding to the different brainwave frequency domains targeted by binaural beat protocols.
