# Phytocannabinoids: THC, CBD, CBG, CBC, THCV

## Abstract

Phytocannabinoids are lipophilic plant-derived compounds that interact with the endocannabinoid system through partial or full agonism, antagonism, allosteric modulation, or indirect mechanisms. Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) is the primary psychoactive compound acting as a partial CB1 and CB2 agonist. Cannabidiol (CBD) exerts complex polypharmacology with minimal direct CB1/CB2 binding, acting instead as a negative allosteric modulator of CB1, a TRPV1 agonist, a GPR55 antagonist, and a serotonin 5-HT1A agonist. Minor cannabinoids CBG, CBC, and THCV each demonstrate distinct receptor profiles with emerging clinical relevance. The entourage effect describes synergistic interactions between cannabinoids and terpenoids that modulate the overall pharmacological response beyond individual compound activity.

## THC — Delta-9-Tetrahydrocannabinol

- Primary receptor: CB1 partial agonist (Ki ~10 nM), CB2 partial agonist
- Psychoactive effects: euphoria, altered sensory perception, memory impairment via CB1-mediated suppression of glutamate release in hippocampus
- Analgesic: CB1 activation in PAG, spinal dorsal horn, peripheral nociceptors
- Antiemetic: CB1 activation in brainstem dorsal vagal complex
- Appetite stimulation: hypothalamic CB1 activation increases ghrelin sensitivity
- Anxiety biphasic: low dose anxiolytic via PFC CB1, high dose anxiogenic via amygdala CB1
- Tolerance: rapid CB1 receptor desensitization and downregulation with chronic exposure

## CBD — Cannabidiol

- CB1: negative allosteric modulator - reduces THC efficacy without direct binding
- CB2: weak partial agonist or inverse agonist depending on assay
- TRPV1: agonist at high concentrations - contributes to analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects
- GPR55: antagonist - anti-proliferative, modulates intracellular calcium
- 5-HT1A: agonist - anxiolytic, antidepressant-like, neuroprotective
- FAAH inhibition: indirect - elevates anandamide by reducing degradation
- Anticonvulsant: multiple mechanisms including sodium channel modulation, GPR55 antagonism
- Anti-inflammatory: inhibits adenosine reuptake, suppresses TNF-alpha and IL-6

## Minor Cannabinoids

### CBG — Cannabigerol
- Precursor molecule: CBGA is biosynthetic precursor to THC, CBD, CBC
- CB1 and CB2: weak partial agonist at both receptors
- Alpha-2 adrenoceptor agonist: antihypertensive potential
- 5-HT1A antagonist: distinct from CBD serotonin profile
- TRPM8 antagonist: potential application in prostate conditions
- Antibacterial: effective against MRSA strains in vitro

### CBC — Cannabichromene
- Non-psychoactive: negligible CB1 affinity
- TRPA1 and TRPV1 agonist: anti-inflammatory and analgesic via TRP channels
- Neurogenesis: promotes neural progenitor cell viability in vitro
- Synergistic with CBD in antidepressant and anti-inflammatory models

### THCV — Tetrahydrocannabivarin
- CB1 antagonist at low doses, partial agonist at high doses
- CB2 partial agonist: anti-inflammatory
- Appetite suppression: CB1 antagonism reduces food intake - opposite of THC
- Anticonvulsant: reduces seizure frequency in animal models
- Metabolic: improves insulin sensitivity in type 2 diabetes models

## Entourage Effect

- Definition: synergistic modulation of ECS activity by combined cannabinoid-terpenoid action
- Terpenoid contributions: myrcene (sedative, CB1 synergy), limonene (anxiolytic, 5-HT1A), linalool (GABA modulation), beta-caryophyllene (CB2 agonist - only dietary CB2 ligand known)
- CBD-THC interaction: CBD reduces THC anxiety and memory impairment via CB1 allosteric modulation
- Clinical implication: whole-plant preparations may outperform isolated compounds for complex conditions
