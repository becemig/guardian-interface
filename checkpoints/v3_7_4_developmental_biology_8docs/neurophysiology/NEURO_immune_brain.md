# Immune-Brain Axis

## Abstract

Psychoneuroimmunology PNI is the scientific discipline studying the bidirectional communication between the immune system, nervous system, and endocrine system — established by Robert Ader and Nicholas Cohen's 1975 demonstration that immune responses can be classically conditioned. The immune system communicates with the brain through multiple pathways: cytokines crossing the blood-brain barrier or signaling through vagal afferents and circumventricular organs, direct sympathetic innervation of lymphoid organs, and shared receptor systems for neurotransmitters and hormones. The brain regulates immune function through the HPA axis via glucocorticoids, the sympathetic nervous system via norepinephrine, and the vagal anti-inflammatory reflex. Chronic psychological stress produces predictable immune dysregulation — suppressed NK cell activity, reduced vaccine responsiveness, enhanced pro-inflammatory cytokine production, and accelerated wound healing impairment. Social support, positive affect, and mindfulness meditation all produce measurable improvements in immune function. Telomere length — a marker of cellular aging — is shortened by chronic stress and psychological adversity corresponding to immune cell aging. Wei Qi intelligence in TCM — the defensive Qi that circulates at the body's surface detecting and responding to external pathogenic threats — corresponds to the immune surveillance function of the innate immune system.

## Conditioned Immunity

- Ader and Cohen demonstrated that saccharin paired with cyclophosphamide produced immunosuppression on saccharin re-exposure alone.
- This classical conditioning of immune responses demonstrated direct nervous system control of immune function.
- Placebo immune effects and nocebo immune suppression operate through conditioned immunity mechanisms.
- The conditioned immune response involves learning-induced changes in autonomic and neuroendocrine regulation.

## Stress and Immune Function

- Acute brief stress enhances immune surveillance — NK cell activity and lymphocyte trafficking increase.
- Chronic stress suppresses cellular immunity — NK cell cytotoxicity, T cell proliferation, and vaccine responses.
- Chronic stress enhances innate inflammatory responses — IL-6, CRP, and TNF-alpha increase.
- The pattern of stress-induced immune dysregulation matches the TCM Lung Wei Qi deficiency pattern.

## Vagal Anti-Inflammatory Reflex

- The vagus nerve provides direct neural control of peripheral immune responses through the cholinergic pathway.
- Vagal efferent activation suppresses macrophage TNF-alpha release through alpha-7 nicotinic receptors in the spleen.
- This reflex provides rapid neural regulation of acute inflammatory responses.
- VNS is being investigated therapeutically for sepsis, rheumatoid arthritis, and inflammatory bowel disease.
