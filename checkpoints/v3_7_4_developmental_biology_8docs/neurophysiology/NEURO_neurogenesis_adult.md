# Adult Neurogenesis

## Abstract

Adult neurogenesis — the birth of new neurons in the mature brain — occurs continuously in the subgranular zone of the hippocampal dentate gyrus and the subventricular zone of the lateral ventricles throughout the adult human lifespan, though the functional significance and rate of human hippocampal neurogenesis remain actively debated. Fred Gage's laboratory established the existence of adult neural stem cells and their capacity to generate new neurons, glia, and oligodendrocytes under appropriate conditions. New neurons generated in the dentate gyrus integrate into the hippocampal circuitry over a period of weeks to months, initially showing enhanced synaptic plasticity before maturing to adult-like properties. Young immature neurons show lower activation thresholds and enhanced LTP corresponding to superior pattern separation capacity — the ability to distinguish between similar inputs. Exercise is the most potent promoter of hippocampal neurogenesis — aerobic exercise increases BDNF, IGF-1, and VEGF in the hippocampus driving increased stem cell proliferation and new neuron survival. All major antidepressant treatments increase hippocampal neurogenesis — this effect requires weeks corresponding to the lag period before antidepressant clinical efficacy. Chronic stress and glucocorticoids suppress hippocampal neurogenesis through GR activation reducing BDNF and increasing apoptosis of new neurons. The neurogenesis hypothesis of depression proposes that reduced hippocampal neurogenesis contributes to depression pathophysiology and that neurogenesis restoration mediates antidepressant effects. Kidney Jing renewal in TCM — the capacity of the constitutional Essence to renew and replenish through appropriate cultivation — corresponds to adult neurogenesis as the literal cellular renewal of the brain's most Kidney-governed structure.

## Neurogenic Niche

- The subgranular zone contains radial glia-like neural stem cells that generate new dentate granule cells.
- New neurons require 4-6 weeks to develop axons and dendrites and integrate into hippocampal circuits.
- Young neurons at 2-3 weeks post-mitosis show enhanced LTP corresponding to superior learning capacity.
- Local GABAergic interneurons, astrocytes, and blood vessels constitute the neurogenic niche supporting new neuron survival.

## Exercise and Neurogenesis

- Aerobic exercise increases hippocampal BDNF, IGF-1, and VEGF driving neurogenic stem cell proliferation.
- Running wheel access in rodents doubles dentate gyrus neurogenesis within weeks.
- Exercise-induced neurogenesis corresponds to improved pattern separation, spatial memory, and antidepressant effects.
- The minimum effective exercise dose for neurogenesis is approximately 20-30 minutes of moderate aerobic activity.

## Stress and Suppression

- Chronic stress and elevated glucocorticoids suppress hippocampal neurogenesis through GR-mediated BDNF reduction.
- Acute stress can temporarily increase neurogenesis while chronic stress produces sustained suppression.
- Early life adversity produces lasting reductions in hippocampal neurogenesis corresponding to vulnerability to depression.
- Adrenalectomy removing glucocorticoids produces dramatic increases in hippocampal neurogenesis.
