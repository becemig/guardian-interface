# Methyl Pharmacology

## Abstract

Methylation is one of the most fundamental biochemical processes in the body — the transfer of a methyl group CH3 from a donor molecule to an acceptor molecule, regulated by a network of enzymes, cofactors, and genetic variants that collectively constitute the one-carbon metabolism cycle. The methylation cycle underlies monoamine neurotransmitter synthesis and degradation, DNA methylation epigenetic regulation, histione methylation gene expression, phospholipid synthesis, and dozens of other critical biochemical processes. SAMe S-adenosylmethionine is the universal methyl donor — the molecule that provides the methyl group for the majority of methylation reactions in the brain and body. SAMe is synthesized from methionine and ATP, and its production depends on the folate-B12 methylation cycle. MTHFR methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase is the key enzyme converting folate to its active methylating form 5-methyltetrahydrofolate which provides the methyl group for converting homocysteine to methionine — the step that regenerates the SAMe pool. Common MTHFR genetic variants C677T and A1298C reduce enzyme activity by 30-70 percent, impairing SAMe production and producing elevated homocysteine, impaired monoamine synthesis, and increased psychiatric vulnerability. COMT catechol-O-methyltransferase degrades dopamine, norepinephrine, and epinephrine through methylation — COMT activity determines how quickly catecholamines are cleared from the synapse, directly governing dopamine levels in the prefrontal cortex. The Val158Met COMT polymorphism produces either warrior-type low COMT high dopamine or worrier-type high COMT low dopamine prefrontal phenotypes with characteristic cognitive and psychiatric profiles. TCM kidney governing constitutional depth corresponds to the constitutional methylation capacity encoded in the MTHFR and COMT genetic variants.

## SAMe and Methylation Cycle

- SAMe is the universal methyl donor synthesized from methionine and ATP.
- Its production depends on adequate folate, B12, and B6 for the methylation cycle.
- SAMe methylates monoamine neurotransmitters, DNA, histones, phospholipids, and hundreds of other substrates.
- SAMe supplementation has demonstrated antidepressant efficacy comparable to tricyclic antidepressants.

## MTHFR Variants

- MTHFR C677T reduces enzyme activity 30-70 percent impairing active folate production.
- Impaired MTHFR produces elevated homocysteine, reduced SAMe, impaired monoamine synthesis.
- MTHFR variants increase risk for depression, anxiety, cardiovascular disease, and neural tube defects.
- L-methylfolate supplementation bypasses the MTHFR defect providing the active folate form directly.

## COMT and Dopamine

- COMT degrades dopamine and norepinephrine in the prefrontal cortex through methylation.
- Val158Met polymorphism: Met allele low COMT activity high dopamine — worrier phenotype with anxiety but better fine motor.
- Val allele high COMT activity low dopamine — warrior phenotype with stress resilience but lower baseline cognition.
- COMT inhibitors like quercetin and EGCG from green tea slow dopamine degradation increasing prefrontal dopamine.
