# African Traditional Medicine

## Abstract

African traditional medicine — encompassing the diverse healing practices of thousands of distinct ethnic groups across the African continent — is unified by a common cosmological understanding that health is fundamentally a state of harmony between the living, the ancestors, the community, and the natural world, and that disease arises from the disruption of these relationships through spiritual imbalance, social conflict, ancestral displeasure, or the malevolent action of others. Ubuntu — the southern African philosophical concept usually translated as I am because we are — expresses the foundational ontological principle of African healing: the individual self is constituted through its relationships with others, and health and illness are always simultaneously individual and communal phenomena. Sangoma healing — the healing practice of the diviner-healer in southern African Nguni traditions — is initiated through the thwasa calling: an illness-as-initiation in which the ancestral spirits call a person to become a healer through the experience of profound physical and psychological disruption that can only be resolved through acceptance of the healing vocation and training under an established Sangoma. The Sangoma works through divination — using bones, shells, or other tools to communicate with the ancestor spirits — to identify the spiritual and relational causes of illness, prescribe plant medicines and ritual actions to restore harmony, and facilitate the community healing ceremonies that address the social dimensions of individual illness. African herbalism is among the most pharmacologically sophisticated in the world — the African continent contains an estimated 5,000 plant species used medicinally, with a growing body of pharmacological research confirming the efficacy of traditional preparations for conditions ranging from malaria to HIV to diabetes. Vital force as communal Qi describes the African traditional understanding of life force as inherently social — unlike the individual cultivation focus of Chinese and Indian traditions, African vital force is maintained and restored through the quality of one's relationships with the living, the ancestors, and the natural world.

## Ubuntu Philosophy

- I am because we are: individual identity and health are constituted through communal relationships.
- Health is the harmonious functioning of the person within their web of relationships.
- Disease is always simultaneously a personal and communal event requiring communal healing response.
- This corresponds to the TCM understanding that Shen health requires appropriate social and ancestral connection.

## Sangoma Healing

- Thwasa calling: illness-as-initiation through which the ancestors call a person to the healing vocation.
- Divination communicates with ancestor spirits to identify the spiritual causes of illness.
- Plant medicines and ritual actions restore harmony between the living, the ancestors, and the spirit world.
- Community healing ceremonies address the social dimensions of illness that individual treatment cannot reach.

## African Herbalism

- An estimated 5,000 African plant species used medicinally with growing pharmacological confirmation.
- Artemisia afra widely used for respiratory conditions — related to the Chinese Qing Hao with similar chemistry.
- Sutherlandia frutescens adaptogenic immune tonic showing HIV and cancer clinical trial promise.
- African plant medicine knowledge is the foundation of emerging ethnopharmacology and drug discovery.
