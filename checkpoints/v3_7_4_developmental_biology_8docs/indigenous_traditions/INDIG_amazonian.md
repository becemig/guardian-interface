# Amazonian Plant Medicine

## Abstract

Amazonian plant medicine traditions — centered on the extraordinary pharmacological and spiritual sophistication of the indigenous peoples of the Amazon basin — represent one of the world's most complex and pharmacologically innovative healing systems, developed over thousands of years of intimate relationship with one of the most biodiverse ecosystems on Earth. Vegetalismo is the mestizo shamanic tradition of the Peruvian Amazon in which the healer develops relationships with plant spirits through extended dietas — periods of dietary restriction, isolation, and ceremonial ingestion of specific plants — during which the plant spirits teach the healer their healing songs icaros, their medicinal properties, and their diagnostic and healing capacities. Ayahuasca — the vine of the soul — is the central sacrament of Amazonian plant medicine, a brew combining Banisteriopsis caapi vine containing harmine and harmaline MAO inhibitors with Psychotria viridis leaves containing DMT N,N-dimethyltryptamine, producing a powerful and prolonged visionary experience used for healing, divination, and spiritual development. The pharmacological sophistication of the ayahuasca preparation is remarkable — the MAO inhibitors in the vine are precisely what is needed to make the orally ingested DMT bioavailable, as DMT is normally broken down by MAO enzymes in the gut before reaching the brain. This specific combination was discovered through empirical exploration of thousands of plant species by peoples without biochemical knowledge — a fact that indigenous practitioners attribute to the plant spirits themselves as teachers. Icaro healing songs are the primary therapeutic tool of the Amazonian healer — melodic patterns that encode plant spirit knowledge, direct the movement of healing energy, purify the ceremonial space, and guide the visionary experience of ceremony participants. The neuroscience of ayahuasca reveals profound effects on default mode network activity, serotonergic signaling, neuroplasticity through BDNF upregulation, and the dissolution of rigid cognitive patterns — mechanisms that correspond to the traditional understanding of ayahuasca as a teacher that shows what needs to be seen and healed.
