# Blood Stasis

## Abstract

Blood stasis Xue Yu is one of the most clinically significant pathological products and causative factors in TCM representing the obstruction of Blood flow producing fixed stabbing pain, dark discoloration, and palpable masses. Unlike Qi stagnation which produces moving distending pain Blood stasis produces fixed stabbing pain that is worse at night and with pressure corresponding to the immovable nature of obstructed Blood. The tongue in Blood stasis is purple or has purple spots indicating impaired peripheral circulation. The complexion is dark dusky or has spider nevi reflecting chronic Blood stagnation in the skin and subcutaneous vessels. Blood stasis arises from trauma, chronic Qi stagnation failing to move Blood, Cold congealing Blood, Heat drying and thickening Blood, or Qi and Blood deficiency failing to propel circulation. Dan Shen salvia miltiorrhiza and San Qi notoginseng are the two flagship herbs for Blood stasis — Dan Shen activating Blood and clearing Heart stasis, San Qi stopping bleeding while transforming stasis. Modern cardiovascular medicine recognizes Blood stasis in coronary artery disease, stroke, deep vein thrombosis, and endometriosis.

## Diagnostic Criteria

- Fixed stabbing pain that is worse at night, worse with pressure, and better with warmth.
- Purple tongue body or purple spots indicating impaired peripheral microcirculation.
- Choppy or wiry pulse reflecting the obstructed quality of Blood movement.
- Dark dusky complexion, spider nevi, or visible superficial vessels indicating chronic stasis.

## Etiological Pathways

- Trauma produces localized Blood stasis through direct vessel damage and hematoma formation.
- Chronic Qi stagnation fails to move Blood — since Qi is the commander of Blood, Qi stagnation produces Blood stasis.
- Cold congeals Blood reducing its fluidity and promoting stasis in cold-exposed regions.
- Heat dries and thickens Blood increasing viscosity and promoting stagnation.

## Treatment Principles

- Activate Blood and transform stasis is the primary treatment principle for Blood stasis patterns.
- SP-10 Xuehai Sea of Blood is the primary acupoint for activating Blood and transforming stasis.
- Dan Shen activates Blood, clears Heart stasis, and calms Shen — addressing both circulatory and cardiac dimensions.
- San Qi simultaneously stops bleeding and transforms existing stasis — unique among hemostatic herbs.

## TCM Correspondence

- Liver governing the smooth flow of Qi and Blood makes Liver Qi stagnation the most common cause of Blood stasis.
- Heart governing Blood and vessels makes the Heart the primary organ affected by severe Blood stasis.
- Spleen governing the holding of Blood in vessels makes Spleen deficiency a contributing factor.
- The Conception Vessel as the sea of Yin Blood corresponds to gynecological Blood stasis patterns.
