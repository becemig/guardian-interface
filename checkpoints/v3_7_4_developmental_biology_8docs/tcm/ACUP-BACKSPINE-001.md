# Back and Spine Acupressure Point Cluster

## Abstract

The back and spine acupressure point cluster covers the primary posterior trunk, shoulder, and spinal points from the sacrum through the thoracic and cervical spine to the occiput and shoulders, governing kidney vitality, back pain, fatigue, shoulder and neck tension, reproductive function, digestive organ back-shu correspondence points, and the full Governing Vessel spinal axis. The Bladder meridian runs two parallel lines on either side of the spine carrying the back-shu points that directly influence all internal organs, making this cluster essential for systemic organ tonification. The GV spinal points simultaneously govern the chakra-spine correspondence documented in the extraordinary vessel system.

## Point Inventory

### GB 21 - Shoulder Well (Jian Jing)
- Location: top of trapezius muscle 3 fingers from the base of the neck
- Benefits: irritability, fatigue, shoulder tension, headaches, neck stiffness
- Caution: use light pressure during pregnancy - can stimulate labor

### SI 10 - Shoulder Correspondence (Nao Shu)
- Location: directly below the acromion in the shoulder socket
- Benefits: stiff neck, shoulder tension, cold hands, hypertension

### B 42 - Soul Door (Po Hu)
- Location: below inferior angle of scapula at T9 level 3 fingers lateral to spine
- Benefits: digestive disorders, stomach ulcers, jet lag, emotional processing

### B 47 - Sea of Vitality (Zhi Shi)
- Location: between L2-L3 3 fingers lateral to midline at waist level
- Benefits: fatigue, low backache, kidney tonification, impotence, fear
- Note: primary kidney back-shu region - directly tonifies kidney essence

### B 48 - Womb and Vitals (Bao Huang)
- Location: outside the widest part of the sacrum lateral sacral foramen
- Benefits: pelvic tension, hemorrhoids, hip pain, prostate, menstrual cramps

### B 54 - Equilibrium Middle (Wei Zhong)
- Location: center of the knee crease posterior
- Benefits: back pain, sciatica, arthritis of hips and knees

### GV 4 - Gate of Life (Ming Men)
- Location: posterior midline between L2 and L3
- Benefits: constitutional yang tonification, kidney yang, sexual vitality, lower back
- TCM: 2nd chakra correspondence - the root of all yang energy in the body

### GV 9 - Utmost Yang (Zhi Yang)
- Location: posterior midline at T7 level
- Benefits: 3rd chakra solar plexus correspondence, liver and gallbladder back region

### GV 12 - Body Pillar (Shen Zhu)
- Location: posterior midline at T3 level
- Benefits: 4th chakra heart back correspondence, lung and heart back region

### GV 14 - Reunion of Yang (Da Zhui)
- Location: posterior midline at C7 below the prominent cervical vertebra
- Benefits: clears heat, strengthens immunity - all six yang meridians meet here
- TCM: 5th chakra throat correspondence - supreme yang convergence point

### GV 16 - Wind Palace (Feng Fu)
- Location: posterior midline at the occiput base in the hollow below skull
- Benefits: clears the mind, internal wind, pathogenic entry point, brain function
- TCM: 6th-7th chakra bridge - consciousness gateway point
