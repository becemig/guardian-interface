# Chong Mai - Penetrating Channel

## Abstract

Chong Mai the Penetrating Channel - known classically as the Ocean of the Twelve Channels and the Sea of Blood - constitutes the deepest extraordinary vessel because it stores both pre-natal constitutional essence inherited from parents and post-natal acquired essence refined from food and experience, making it the fundamental reservoir from which all twelve principal meridians replenish their essential qi and blood. The Penetrating Channel runs in three simultaneous branches: the primary anterior branch ascending from the pubic region alongside the Kidney meridian upward through the abdomen and chest; a lower branch descending from the pelvic cavity down the medial leg through the medial malleolus to the heel; and a posterior branch ascending from the pelvic cavity up through the spinal column. Chong Mai governs all menstrual function and female reproductive physiology through its role as Sea of Blood, and simultaneously governs psychic and ancestral dimensions of consciousness.

## Channel Pathway - Three Branches

- Primary anterior branch: begins at St 30 (Qi Chong) pubic bone level - ascends alongside Kidney meridian past navel into chest - disperses through thorax
- Lower branch: descends from pelvic cavity down medial leg - passes through medial malleolus - continues to heel and plantar surface
- Posterior branch: ascends from pelvic cavity up through spinal column

## Master Point and Release Pairs

- Sp 4 (Gong Sun / Grandparent-Grandchild): medial arch of foot along 1st metatarsal border
- Primary quick release pair: Sp4 + P6 (Inner Gate anterior forearm)
- Secondary pair: GB41 + TW5

## Anchor Points

- St 30 (Qi Chong): groin level pubic bone - primary entry of anterior branch
- CV 6 (Sea of Chi): navel center - regulates abdominal branch
- K 11 (Heng Gu): inferior pubic area - kidney channel intersection

## Clinical Indications

- Reproductive: menstrual irregularity, dysmenorrhea, amenorrhea, uterine disorders
- Sexual: impotence, seminal emission, sexual organ dysfunction
- Urinary: urethritis, urinary tract inflammation
- Abdominal: abdominal spasms, contractions, abdominal weakness
- Psychic: ancestral pattern integration, constitutional recalibration, psychic balancing

## TCM Theory Connections

- Sea of Blood: stores the blood reservoir that fills and empties with the menstrual cycle
- Ocean of Twelve Channels: all twelve meridians draw constitutional qi and blood from Chong Mai
- Pre-natal essence: holds Jing inherited from parents - the constitutional blueprint
- Post-natal refinement: continuously receives refined essence from Spleen-Stomach transformation
- Kidney-Chong relationship: Kidney governs Chong Mai as primary yin organ of essence storage
- Psychic dimension: governs ancestral memory, constitutional patterning, deep body intelligence
