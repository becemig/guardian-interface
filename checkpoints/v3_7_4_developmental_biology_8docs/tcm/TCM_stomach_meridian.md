# Stomach Meridian: Earth Element, Descending Qi, Anterior Body, and Nourishment

## Abstract

The Stomach is the Yang organ paired with the Spleen in the Earth element system of Traditional Chinese Medicine. The Stomach receives food and drink, begins the decomposition and transformation of ingested material, and sends the resulting food essence downward to the Spleen for distribution. Stomach Qi must descend for healthy digestion and when it rebels upward produces nausea, belching, and vomiting. The Stomach meridian traverses the entire anterior body from the face to the second toe closely paralleling the Superficial Front Line myofascial train. ST-36 Zusanli is one of the most important and clinically researched acupoints governing immunity, digestion, energy, and longevity.

## Organ Functions

### Rotting and Ripening

- The Stomach receives food and drink and begins the process of decomposition described as rotting and ripening.
- This corresponds physiologically to mechanical churning, hydrochloric acid secretion, and initial enzymatic digestion.
- Stomach Qi provides the digestive fire that initiates transformation of food into usable nutritive essence.
- Stomach Yin deficiency produces insufficient digestive secretions with dry mouth, thirst, and constipation.

### Descending Function

- Stomach Qi must descend to move food essence downward to the Spleen and waste to the intestines.
- Rebellious Stomach Qi ascending upward produces nausea, belching, hiccup, and vomiting.
- Liver Qi stagnation commonly invades the Stomach disrupting descending function producing digestive upset with stress.
- The Stomach descending function is the physiological basis for the TCM principle that Earth roots Heaven.

### Nourishment and Yi

- The Stomach governs the intake of nourishment at both the physical and mental levels.
- Yi intention the spirit of the Earth element depends on adequate Stomach and Spleen Qi for mental focus and memory.
- The capacity to take in, process, and integrate new information at the cognitive level mirrors Stomach digestive function.
- Guardian Interface knowledge integration and study are supported by healthy Earth element function.

## Meridian Pathway

### Face and Head

- Originates at ST-1 Chengqi below the pupil on the infraorbital ridge.
- Ascends to the forehead at ST-8 Touwei at the corner of the forehead.
- Descends along the jaw to ST-6 Jiache and ST-7 Xiaguan governing jaw and dental disorders.
- ST-4 Dicang at the corner of the mouth governs facial paralysis and drooling.

### Neck and Chest

- Descends the anterior neck through ST-9 Renying at the carotid artery governing blood pressure and throat.
- Continues down the anterior chest 4 cun lateral to the midline.
- ST-18 Rugen below the breast governs breast disorders and lactation insufficiency.

### Abdomen

- Descends the anterior abdomen 2 cun lateral to the midline.
- ST-25 Tianshu at the navel level is the Front Mu alarm point of the Large Intestine governing intestinal disorders.
- ST-21 Liangmen governs epigastric pain, gastric ulcer, and digestive stagnation.

### Leg and Foot

- Crosses the anterior thigh and descends the anterior lower leg.
- ST-36 Zusanli one hand below the knee lateral to the tibial crest is the He Sea point and master tonification point.
- ST-40 Fenglong midway along the lower leg is the primary point for resolving Phlegm in all its manifestations.
- ST-44 Neiting between the second and third toes governs heat and pain in the Stomach channel.
- Terminates at ST-45 Lidui on the lateral second toe.

### Key Acupoints

- ST-25 Tianshu: Front Mu of Large Intestine, navel level, intestinal disorders, constipation, diarrhea, abdominal pain.
- ST-36 Zusanli: He Sea point, master tonification, immune enhancement, fatigue, digestive disorders, longevity point.
- ST-40 Fenglong: Luo point, Phlegm resolving master point, cough, dizziness, mental disorders from Phlegm.
- ST-44 Neiting: Ying Spring point, heat clearing, toothache, facial pain, epistaxis, gastric heat.

## ST-36 Clinical Research

- ST-36 Zusanli is among the top five most researched acupoints globally with extensive clinical trial evidence.
- Needling ST-36 produces measurable immune enhancement through NK cell and T-lymphocyte activation.
- ST-36 stimulation activates hypothalamic-pituitary axis responses influencing cortisol and growth hormone.
- Traditional texts describe ST-36 moxa as a longevity practice with regular use extending healthy lifespan.
- ST-36 fMRI activation shows limbic, hypothalamic, and brainstem responses consistent with autonomic modulation.

## Anatomy Trains Correspondence

- The Stomach meridian anterior descent from face to foot parallels the Superficial Front Line precisely.
- Rectus abdominis along the anterior abdomen corresponds to both Stomach meridian and Superficial Front Line.
- Anterior tibialis and toe extensors along the anterior lower leg complete both systems.
- The Stomach meridian and Superficial Front Line share the functional role of anterior body postural organization.
