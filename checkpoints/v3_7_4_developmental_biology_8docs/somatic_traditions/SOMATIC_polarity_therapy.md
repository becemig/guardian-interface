# Polarity Therapy

## Abstract

Polarity Therapy developed by Randolph Stone DO DC ND is a comprehensive energy medicine system synthesizing principles from Ayurveda, TCM, osteopathy, naturopathy, and Western hermetic philosophy into a unified model of health based on the movement of life energy between positive and negative poles. Stone proposed that the human energy field is organized as a series of bipolar electromagnetic relationships — every energy flow has a positive pole, a negative pole, and a neutral point — and that health consists in the free balanced flow of energy between poles while disease arises from energy blocks and distortions. The wireless anatomy describes the invisible energy fields that precede and organize the physical body corresponding to the Ayurvedic concept of the subtle body. Five element energy fields — ether, air, fire, water, and earth — correspond to different anatomical zones, chakras, and tissue qualities. Polarity bodywork uses three types of touch — sattvic neutral touch, rajasic stimulating touch, and tamasic dispersing touch — to address different energetic conditions. The five elements of polarity therapy — ether corresponding to the causal field, air to the respiratory and nervous system, fire to the digestive system, water to the pelvic and reproductive domain, and earth to the lower extremities — correspond meaningfully to the five elements of TCM and the three Doshas of Ayurveda.

## Wireless Anatomy

- Stone proposed that invisible energy fields organize and precede the physical body — the wireless anatomy.
- These fields correspond to Ayurvedic subtle body concepts and Chinese meridian theory.
- Physical symptoms reflect disturbances in the energy field that have condensed into material expression.
- Polarity treatment addresses the field disturbance rather than only the physical symptom.

## Five Element Fields

- Ether element governs the causal field and corresponds to the throat, ears, and the space principle.
- Air element governs the nervous system and breath corresponding to the shoulders, ankles, and movement.
- Fire element governs digestion and transformation corresponding to the solar plexus, knees, and eyes.
- Water element governs fluid metabolism and the pelvis corresponding to the sacrum, feet, and emotional depth.
- Earth element governs structure and elimination corresponding to the lower GI, rectum, and physical foundation.

## Three Touch Qualities

- Sattvic touch uses neutral light contact that creates space for the energy to reorganize without direction.
- Rajasic touch uses stimulating contact that activates and disperses stagnant or blocked energy.
- Tamasic touch uses deep grounding contact that disperses excess energy and grounds the system.
- Skilled practitioners alternate between touch qualities based on the energetic assessment of each area.

## TCM Correspondence

- Five element energy fields of polarity correspond to the Wu Xing Five Elements of TCM through analogous correspondence.
- Positive-negative pole organization corresponds to the Yin-Yang polarity governing all TCM physiological relationships.
- Governing and Conception Vessels as the primary Yang-Yin axes correspond to the primary polarity axis of the wireless anatomy.
- Water element governing the pelvis and sacrum corresponds to the Kidney-Water constitutional ground of TCM.
