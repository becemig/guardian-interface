# Breathing and Movement Integration

## Abstract

The diaphragm is both the primary muscle of respiration and a key spinal stabilizer through intra-abdominal pressure generation. IAP from co-contraction of diaphragm, pelvic floor, and abdominal wall creates a pneumatic cylinder reducing spinal compressive loading. Dan Tian breathing deliberately cultivates this diaphragmatic-pelvic floor coordination as a foundation of internal arts practice.

## Diaphragm Dual Function

- Diaphragm descent during inhalation expands the thoracic cavity and simultaneously increases IAP.
- Anticipatory diaphragm activation precedes limb movement by 20 to 30 ms providing pre-movement spinal stability.
- Impaired IAP generation from pelvic floor weakness increases spinal injury risk.

## Breath-Movement Coordination

- Exhaling during exertion exploits natural IAP rise for spinal protection.
- Tai Chi coordinates inhale with opening movements and exhale with closing movements.
- This coordination optimizes spinal stability and HRV through respiratory sinus arrhythmia amplitude.

## Dan Tian Breathing

- Dan Tian breathing directs breath awareness to the lower abdomen below the navel.
- Natural Dan Tian breathing produces visible lower abdominal expansion on inhalation.
- Reverse abdominal breathing contracts the abdomen on inhalation for stronger IAP and Qi activation.

## TCM Correspondence

- Lung governs Qi descent and the diaphragm as the primary muscle of Lung Qi descending movement.
- Kidney receives Qi from the Lung grasping it into the lower Dan Tian during deep inhalation.
- Conception Vessel governs the Dan Tian as the sea of Qi on the anterior midline.
