# Embryology and Development

## Abstract

Developmental biology describes how a single fertilized egg gives rise through precise, coordinated, and self-organizing processes to the extraordinary complexity of a multicellular organism. The core processes — cell division, differentiation, migration, apoptosis, and morphogenesis — are governed by a conserved set of developmental signaling pathways (Wnt, Hedgehog, Notch, BMP, FGF) that have been repurposed across evolution from the simplest organisms to humans. Epigenetic programming during embryonic development establishes organ-specific gene expression patterns that persist throughout the lifespan and can be disrupted by maternal stress, nutrition, toxin exposure, and infection. The developmental origins of health and disease (DOHaD) hypothesis — pioneered by David Barker — demonstrated that intrauterine conditions program adult risk of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, obesity, and psychiatric conditions. Waddington's epigenetic landscape provides a powerful conceptual model of how developmental trajectories are canalized and how perturbations produce persistent phenotypic consequences. In TCM, the quality of prenatal Jing — determined by ancestral vitality and maternal conditions during gestation — establishes the constitutional foundation that shapes health across the entire lifespan.

## Early Development

- Fertilization: fusion of sperm and egg - diploid zygote formed - totipotent
- Cleavage: rapid cell division without growth - morula to blastocyst
- Blastocyst: inner cell mass (pluripotent embryonic stem cells) and trophoblast
- Implantation: day 6-10 - blastocyst embeds in endometrium
- Gastrulation: three germ layers formed - ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm
- Ectoderm: nervous system, skin, sensory organs
- Mesoderm: muscle, bone, cardiovascular, kidney, gonads
- Endoderm: gut, liver, pancreas, lung epithelium

## Developmental Signaling Pathways

- Wnt: cell fate, axis patterning, stem cell maintenance, cancer when dysregulated
- Hedgehog (Shh): neural tube patterning, limb development, tissue polarity
- Notch: lateral inhibition - ensures neighboring cells adopt different fates
- BMP: bone and cartilage, neural crest, dorsal-ventral patterning
- FGF: branching morphogenesis, limb outgrowth, wound healing
- Hox genes: master body plan - anterior-posterior axis identity
- Reuse in adult: same pathways active in adult stem cell niches and tissue repair

## Stem Cell Hierarchy

- Totipotent: zygote and first few divisions - can form entire organism including placenta
- Pluripotent: inner cell mass - can form any tissue but not placenta
- Multipotent: tissue-specific stem cells - hematopoietic, neural, mesenchymal
- Unipotent: committed progenitors - one cell type only
- Waddington landscape: differentiation as ball rolling downhill into valleys
- iPSC: induced pluripotent stem cells - Yamanaka factors reverse differentiation
- Niche: stem cell niche maintains stem cell identity - signals from surrounding cells

## Epigenetic Programming in Development

- Methylation reset: genome-wide demethylation at fertilization - then re-methylation
- Imprinting: parent-of-origin-specific methylation - Igf2, H19 - established in gametes
- X-inactivation: one X chromosome silenced in female cells - Barr body
- Organ-specific: each cell type has unique methylation and histone modification pattern
- Stability: epigenetic patterns established in development persist through all subsequent divisions
- Vulnerability: greatest epigenetic vulnerability during rapid division - first trimester

## DOHaD - Developmental Origins of Health and Disease

- Barker hypothesis: intrauterine growth restriction predicts adult cardiovascular disease
- Dutch Hunger Winter: famine during pregnancy programs offspring for obesity, diabetes, schizophrenia
- Thrifty phenotype: undernourished fetus programs for food scarcity - maladaptive in abundance
- Maternal stress: glucocorticoids cross placenta - program offspring HPA axis reactivity
- Epigenetic mechanism: maternal environment alters offspring methylation patterns
- Windows: each organ has sensitive period when environmental programming most potent
- TCM: prenatal Jing quality - mother's health, diet, emotional state during pregnancy
