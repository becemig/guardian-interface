# Wei Qi Diurnal Cycles

## Abstract

Wei Qi defensive Qi follows a precise diurnal circulation pattern cycling 25 times through the Yang surface during the day and 25 times through the Yin interior organs during the night completing 50 daily cycles. During waking hours Wei Qi circulates in the skin, muscles, and superficial layers defending against external pathogenic invasion and regulating pore opening and closing. At nightfall Wei Qi retreats from the surface to the interior cycling through the five Yin organs — Kidney, Heart, Lung, Liver, and Spleen — in sequence nourishing the constitutional Yin and enabling the deep restoration of sleep. The transition of Wei Qi from surface to interior at dusk corresponds to the onset of drowsiness and the physiological preparation for sleep. Insomnia from Wei Qi failing to enter the interior produces hypervigilance, inability to settle, and surface hypersensitivity. The diurnal Wei Qi cycle corresponds to the circadian immune rhythm — immune surveillance is enhanced during the day while inflammatory responses peak during nighttime Wei Qi interior circulation.

## Day Circulation

- Wei Qi exits the Kidney at dawn through the inner canthus of the eye beginning its Yang surface circulation.
- It circulates through the six Yang meridians — Bladder, Gallbladder, Stomach, Triple Burner, Small Intestine, Large Intestine.
- Surface Wei Qi warms the skin, controls pore opening, and maintains the defensive barrier against external pathogens.
- Yang surface Wei Qi produces the alert waking state through its activating warming presence in the exterior.

## Night Circulation

- At dusk Wei Qi retreats from the surface entering the Kidney to begin its Yin interior circulation.
- It cycles through Kidney, Heart, Lung, Liver, and Spleen in sequence nourishing each Yin organ.
- Interior Wei Qi supports the deep restoration and regeneration that occurs during sleep.
- Failure of Wei Qi to enter the interior produces hypervigilance, spontaneous sweating, and insomnia.

## Circadian Immune Rhythm

- Immune cell trafficking, cytokine production, and inflammatory responses follow circadian patterns.
- NK cell activity peaks during the day corresponding to Wei Qi surface defensive function.
- Inflammatory cytokines peak at night during the interior Wei Qi circulation phase.
- Circadian disruption from shift work and jet lag produces immune dysregulation corresponding to Wei Qi cycle disruption.

## TCM Correspondence

- Governing Vessel governing Yang and Conception Vessel governing Yin correspond to the two poles of Wei Qi circulation.
- Kidney as the starting and ending point of Wei Qi corresponds to its role as the constitutional root of defensive Qi.
- Insomnia from Wei Qi failing to enter the interior corresponds to chronic sympathetic hyperarousal and cortisol elevation.
- Spontaneous daytime sweating from insufficient Wei Qi consolidation corresponds to autonomic dysregulation.
