# Sensorimotor Psychotherapy

## Abstract

Sensorimotor Psychotherapy developed by Pat Ogden is a body-centered approach to trauma treatment that addresses the somatic symptoms of trauma — posture, gesture, movement, and sensation — as primary treatment targets alongside cognition and emotion. Traumatic experiences are encoded in the body as fixated action tendencies — incomplete defensive responses frozen at the moment of overwhelm. Treatment involves tracking somatic indicators, slowing down and mindfully completing the interrupted defensive movements, and integrating the completed action with verbal narrative. Resourcing through grounding, centering, and boundary exercises builds the somatic foundation for trauma processing. The window of tolerance framework guides titration of trauma processing to maintain optimal arousal for integration.

## Fixated Action Tendencies

- Traumatic overwhelm interrupts the defensive fight-flight or freeze response before completion.
- The incomplete defensive movement is encoded as a fixated action tendency in procedural memory.
- Somatic trauma symptoms including bracing, collapse, startling, and dissociation reflect these fixated tendencies.
- Treatment involves mindfully tracking and completing the interrupted defensive movement in present safety.

## Somatic Completion

- Somatic completion involves slowing down and following through the interrupted defensive movement to its natural end.
- Completing the movement discharges the survival energy mobilized at the time of trauma.
- The completion integrates the traumatic experience from fragmented procedural memory into narrative memory.
- Patients often report spontaneous insight and emotional release accompanying somatic completion.

## Resourcing

- Resourcing builds somatic foundations of safety, grounding, centering, and boundary through body-based exercises.
- Grounding establishes contact with the ground through the feet and legs providing physical foundation.
- Centering locates awareness in the center of the body corresponding to the lower Dan Tian.
- Boundary establishment through arm gestures and postural orientation defines the body's personal space.
