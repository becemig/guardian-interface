# Process Work and Process-Oriented Psychology

## Abstract

Process-Oriented Psychology Process Work developed by Arnold Mindell is an interdisciplinary approach integrating Jungian psychology, physics, Taoism, and shamanic traditions that follows the process — the flow of experience as it unfolds moment to moment — rather than imposing a pre-determined therapeutic agenda. The foundational concept is the dreambody — Mindell's term for the body-dream unity, the observation that dreams and body symptoms speak the same symbolic language and are expressions of the same underlying process. Process Work amplifies signals — the subtle communications constantly arising in body movement, symptoms, sounds, relationship dynamics, and the environment — following them toward their meaningful message. Primary process is the identity the person consciously identifies with and promotes, while secondary process is the communication arising from outside this identity — from body symptoms, accidents, relationship conflicts, and disturbing dreams. The edge is the boundary between primary and secondary process — the point where the person stops, reverses, or becomes confused as the secondary process begins to emerge. Therapeutic work follows the secondary process over the edge allowing its message and energy to integrate. World work extends Process Work to group dynamics and social conflict — applying the same principles to cultural, political, and social processes. Lucid dreaming — becoming aware within the dream — is the Process Work model for becoming aware within any process including symptoms, relationships, and social conflict. Liver-Hun dream intelligence in TCM — the Liver Hun's capacity to generate meaningful dream experiences that carry wisdom from the deep self — corresponds to the Process Work dreambody as the unified body-dream intelligence field.

## Dreambody

- The dreambody is the unified field in which dreams and body symptoms express the same underlying process.
- Body symptoms are dreaming processes expressing themselves in the body rather than in sleep.
- Following the body symptom amplifying its quality and movement reveals the dreambody message.
- The dreambody bridges the sleeping and waking states as a continuous expressive intelligence.

## Signals and Edges

- Signals are the subtle communications constantly arising in movement, posture, sound, and environment.
- Primary process is the identity promoted and secondary process arises from outside this identity.
- The edge is the boundary where the person reverses or becomes confused as secondary process emerges.
- Following the secondary process over the edge integrates its energy and message into the primary process.

## World Work

- World work applies Process Work principles to group, cultural, and social conflicts.
- Collective secondary processes — the marginalized, disturbing, or disowned — appear as social conflict and symptoms.
- Deep democracy values all voices including the most disturbing as carrying important information for the whole.
- Following the conflict process rather than resolving it prematurely allows the deeper message to emerge.
