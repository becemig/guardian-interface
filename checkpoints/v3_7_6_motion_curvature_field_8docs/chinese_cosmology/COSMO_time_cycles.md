# Chinese Cosmological Time

## Abstract

Chinese cosmological time — organized through the interlocking cycles of the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches — provides a multi-scale temporal framework that governs everything from the two-hour periods of the daily organ clock to the 60-year grand cycle of Wu Yun Liu Qi cosmological medicine. The twelve Earthly Branches divide the 24-hour day into twelve two-hour periods each associated with a specific organ network and the peak of its Qi circulation: Zi midnight to 1am governs the Gallbladder, Chou 1-3am the Liver, Yin 3-5am the Lung, Mao 5-7am the Large Intestine, Chen 7-9am the Stomach, Si 9-11am the Spleen, Wu 11am-1pm the Heart, Wei 1-3pm the Small Intestine, Shen 3-5pm the Bladder, You 5-7pm the Kidney, Xu 7-9pm the Pericardium, Hai 9-11pm the Triple Burner. This organ clock system predicts that symptoms will be most prominent during the two-hour period of the affected organ's peak circulation and most relieved during the opposite period — a prediction that modern chronobiology has partially validated through studies of symptom timing in cardiovascular, respiratory, and digestive diseases. Zi Wu Liu Zhu — Midnight-Noon Ebb and Flow — is the classical acupuncture timing system that selects treatment points based on the organ clock period of treatment, with specific open points at each time period determined by the complex interaction of the daily Heavenly Stem with the organ clock circuit. Ling Gui Ba Fa — the Eight Methods of the Spiritual Turtle — is the extraordinary vessel timing system that selects the eight extraordinary vessel opening points based on the day and hour of treatment, providing constitutional-level treatment timed to the cosmological moment. Modern chronobiology — the science of biological time — has validated the existence of tissue-level clocks in every organ of the body governed by circadian genes, with each organ having its own rhythmic peak of metabolic activity that corresponds remarkably to the TCM organ clock predictions.

## Organ Clock

- Gallbladder 11pm-1am: peak Gallbladder Qi — bile production, decision processing, Hun dreaming.
- Liver 1-3am: peak Liver Qi — Blood storage, detoxification, emotional processing, Hun return.
- Lung 3-5am: peak Lung Qi — deepest sleep, Wei Qi consolidation, breath regulation.
- Heart 11am-1pm: peak Heart Qi — mental clarity, Shen presence, cardiovascular peak.

## Chronobiological Validation

- Circadian genes CLOCK BMAL1 PER CRY govern tissue-level biological clocks in every organ.
- Cardiac events peak in the morning hours corresponding to the Heart peak at 11am-1pm and the transition period.
- Asthma attacks peak at 4am corresponding to the Lung organ clock period of 3-5am.
- Cortisol peaks at 8am corresponding to the Stomach-Spleen period of digestive metabolic activity.

## Zi Wu Liu Zhu

- Open points change every two hours according to the organ clock period of treatment.
- The daily Heavenly Stem interacts with the organ clock to determine which specific points are open.
- Treatment during the open period of the affected organ produces stronger therapeutic effects.
- This timing system requires the practitioner to know both the patient's pattern and the cosmological moment.

## TCM Correspondence

- Organ clock two-hour periods correspond to the chronobiological tissue-level clocks validated by modern research.
- Zi Wu Liu Zhu timing system corresponds to chronopharmacology — optimizing treatment timing for maximum efficacy.
- Liver 1-3am sleep period for Blood storage and detoxification corresponds to the glymphatic clearance peak during deep sleep.
- Lung 3-5am deepest sleep corresponds to the circadian nadir of cortisol and immune activity supporting restoration.
