# Circadian Photobiology

## Abstract

Circadian photobiology — the science of how light entrains the endogenous circadian clock to the 24-hour solar cycle — is the foundation of all light medicine, establishing light as the primary zeitgeber time-giver that synchronizes the master circadian clock of the suprachiasmatic nucleus to the environmental light-dark cycle and through it synchronizes the peripheral clocks of every organ and tissue in the body to the appropriate phase relationship with the solar day. The suprachiasmatic nucleus SCN — the master circadian pacemaker located in the anterior hypothalamus above the optic chiasm — generates an endogenous approximately 24-hour rhythm through the interlocking transcription-translation feedback loops of the CLOCK BMAL1 PER and CRY genes, and is reset daily to exactly 24 hours by the photic signal conveyed from the retina through the retinohypothalamic tract. The retinohypothalamic tract RHT — the dedicated neural pathway from the intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells ipRGCs to the SCN — conveys the photic entrainment signal using the neurotransmitters glutamate and PACAP, with the ipRGCs expressing the photopigment melanopsin that provides maximal sensitivity to blue wavelength light at approximately 480 nm, explaining why blue-enriched morning light most powerfully advances the circadian phase. Phase response curves PRC for light — the systematic characterization of how light exposure at different circadian phases shifts the clock — demonstrate that light exposure in the early subjective night produces phase delays shifting the clock later while light exposure in the late subjective night produces phase advances shifting the clock earlier, providing the theoretical basis for the clinical use of light to treat jet lag shift work disorder and delayed sleep phase disorder. The Yang rising at dawn cosmological correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding of the dawn as the moment when Yang Qi begins its daily ascent, with the cultivation practice of rising with the sun and facing the east at dawn to receive the first Yang Qi of the day — a practice that corresponds to the modern understanding of morning light exposure as the most potent zeitgeber signal for circadian entrainment.

## SCN Master Clock

- Located in anterior hypothalamus above optic chiasm — 20000 neurons bilaterally.
- CLOCK-BMAL1 heterodimer: drives transcription of PER1 PER2 CRY1 CRY2.
- PER-CRY complex: accumulates and inhibits CLOCK-BMAL1 — negative feedback loop.
- Free-running period: approximately 24.2 hours in humans — requires daily light entrainment.

## Retinohypothalamic Tract

- ipRGCs project directly to SCN through optic nerve and optic chiasm.
- Melanopsin peak sensitivity: 480 nm blue wavelength — dawn sky spectral peak.
- Glutamate and PACAP: primary neurotransmitters of RHT photic signal to SCN.
- High-intensity sustained light required: brief flashes less effective than sustained exposure.

## Phase Response Curve

- Early subjective night light: delays clock — used to treat advanced sleep phase.
- Late subjective night light: advances clock — used to treat delayed sleep phase and jet lag.
- Morning light: most potent advancing signal for most people — aligns with natural dawn.
- Light intensity and duration: dose-response relationship for phase shifting magnitude.
