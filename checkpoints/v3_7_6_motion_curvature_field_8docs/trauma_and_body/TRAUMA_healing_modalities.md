# Somatic Healing Modalities

## Abstract

Trauma treatment has shifted decisively toward body-based approaches following decades of evidence that talk therapy alone cannot resolve the subcortical, somatic, and autonomic dimensions of traumatic experience. The most evidence-supported somatic modalities include Somatic Experiencing (SE), EMDR, Trauma Release Exercises (TRE), yoga, pranayama breathwork, and acupuncture. Each works through distinct but overlapping mechanisms: completion of incomplete defensive responses, bilateral hemispheric integration, vagal tone restoration, GABA upregulation, interoceptive recalibration, and restoration of the felt sense of safety in the body. All effective trauma therapies ultimately operate through the autonomic nervous system - restoring flexible movement between arousal states within the window of tolerance.

## Somatic Experiencing (SE)

- Developer: Peter Levine
- Core mechanism: titrated completion of thwarted defensive responses
- Key techniques: tracking body sensation, pendulation, titration, discharge facilitation
- Felt sense: proprioceptive-interoceptive body awareness as primary therapeutic vehicle
- SIBAM model: Sensation Image Behavior Affect Meaning - full experience integration
- Evidence: RCTs showing reduction in PTSD symptoms and autonomic dysregulation

## EMDR - Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing

- Developer: Francine Shapiro
- Mechanism: bilateral stimulation (eye movements, tapping, tones) during trauma recall
- Proposed mechanism: bilateral stimulation mimics REM sleep memory consolidation
- Orienting response: lateral eye movement activates orienting reflex - dampens amygdala
- Working memory hypothesis: dual attention reduces vividness of traumatic memory
- Evidence: WHO and VA recommended first-line PTSD treatment

## TRE - Trauma Release Exercises

- Developer: David Berceli
- Mechanism: neurogenic tremoring through fatigued psoas and leg muscles
- Psoas: primary trauma muscle - involuntary tremoring releases chronic psoas holding
- Discharge: tremoring completes the incomplete defensive response discharge cycle
- Self-administered: can be practiced independently after learning - scalable

## Yoga for Trauma

- van der Kolk yoga study: 10-week trauma-sensitive yoga RCT reduced PTSD more than talk therapy
- Mechanism: interoceptive recalibration through sustained body awareness
- Insula normalization: yoga restores insula activation patterns disrupted by trauma
- GABA: yoga increases brain GABA levels - anxiolytic via GABAergic tone restoration
- HRV: regular yoga practice increases HRV and vagal tone
- Embodiment: yoga restores relationship with body as safe - reverses dissociative avoidance

## Breathwork and Pranayama

- Vagal afferents: slow deep breathing activates vagal afferents - ventral vagal shift
- 4-7-8 breathing: extended exhale activates parasympathetic - reduces hyperarousal
- Coherent breathing: 5-6 breaths per minute maximizes HRV and RSA amplitude
- Holotropic: hyperventilation shifts CO2 - non-ordinary states for trauma processing
- TCM: breath is the governor of Lung Wei Qi and regulates Qi movement through all channels

## Acupuncture and Acupressure

- Mechanism: point stimulation activates vagal afferents and modulates limbic system activity
- fMRI evidence: acupuncture deactivates amygdala and activates mPFC - reverses PTSD circuit
- PTSD protocol: battlefield acupuncture (auricular) widely used in VA system
- Acupressure self-care: GB 20, CV 17, B 10, GV 20 - calming point cluster for ANS regulation
- EFT (tapping): acupressure-based bilateral stimulation - evidence for anxiety and PTSD
