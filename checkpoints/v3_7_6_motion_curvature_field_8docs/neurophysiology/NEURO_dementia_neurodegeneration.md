# Dementia and Neurodegeneration

## Abstract

Dementia represents the clinical syndrome of progressive cognitive decline severe enough to impair daily function, most commonly caused by Alzheimer's disease 60-70 percent of cases or Parkinson's disease dementia and Lewy body dementia sharing alpha-synuclein pathology. Alzheimer's disease is characterized by two pathological protein accumulations: extracellular amyloid-beta plaques and intracellular neurofibrillary tangles of hyperphosphorylated tau. The amyloid cascade hypothesis proposed by John Hardy and Dennis Selkoe posits that amyloid-beta accumulation is the initiating event triggering downstream tau pathology, neuroinflammation, and neuronal death — though recent failures of anti-amyloid therapeutics have complicated this linear model. Tau staging by Braak and Braak maps the predictable spread of tau pathology from the entorhinal cortex through the hippocampus to the neocortex — explaining the characteristic progression from episodic memory loss to semantic memory loss to executive and eventually global impairment. Parkinson's disease involves alpha-synuclein misfolding and aggregation into Lewy bodies in dopaminergic neurons of the substantia nigra — the protein pathology that also characterizes Lewy body dementia and is found in the gut enteric nervous system years before brain symptoms in many patients. The gut-brain connection in Parkinson suggests alpha-synuclein pathology may spread from the gut to the brain via the vagus nerve — corresponding to the TCM Spleen-Stomach axis governing the relationship between gut and brain. Glymphatic clearance during sleep is essential for removing amyloid-beta and tau from the brain — chronic sleep deprivation dramatically accelerates Alzheimer pathology. TCM herbs with documented neuroprotective effects against Alzheimer and Parkinson pathology include He Shou Wu stilbene glycosides, Ren Shen ginsenosides, and Ling Zhi triterpenes.

## Alzheimer Pathology

- Amyloid-beta accumulates extracellularly forming senile plaques that disrupt synaptic function.
- Tau hyperphosphorylation produces neurofibrillary tangles destroying the microtubule cytoskeleton.
- Braak staging maps tau spread from entorhinal cortex through hippocampus to neocortex.
- Neuroinflammation amplifies both amyloid and tau pathology through microglial activation.

## Parkinson and Alpha-Synuclein

- Alpha-synuclein misfolding and Lewy body formation destroys dopaminergic neurons in substantia nigra.
- Loss of 60-80 percent of substantia nigra neurons before motor symptoms appear — large reserve capacity.
- Gut alpha-synuclein pathology precedes brain pathology — potential prion-like spread via vagus nerve.
- Lewy body dementia involves alpha-synuclein pathology throughout the cortex producing dementia with parkinsonism.

## TCM Neuroprotective Herbs

- Yuan Zhi Polygala tenuifolin reduces amyloid-beta aggregation and activates BDNF-TrkB neuroprotective signaling.
