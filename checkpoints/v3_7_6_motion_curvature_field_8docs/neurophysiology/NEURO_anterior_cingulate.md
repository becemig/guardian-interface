# Anterior Cingulate Cortex

## Abstract

The anterior cingulate cortex ACC is a critical integrative region forming the anterior part of the cingulate gyrus on the medial surface of the frontal lobe — a hub at the intersection of cognitive control, emotional processing, pain affect, and autonomic regulation. Matthew Botvinick and colleagues proposed the conflict monitoring theory of dorsal ACC dACC function — the dACC monitors for conflict between competing response tendencies signaling the need for increased cognitive control when conflict is detected. This conflict signal recruits prefrontal executive resources to resolve the competition. Pierre Rainville's hypnosis study demonstrated that ACC encodes the affective unpleasantness of pain independently of somatosensory cortex — hypnotic suggestions that reduced pain unpleasantness without changing pain intensity reduced ACC activation while leaving S1 unchanged. The rostral ACC rACC is more involved in emotional processing and its activation during emotional regulation tasks predicts antidepressant response. ACC is a primary regulator of autonomic function — ACC stimulation produces heart rate and blood pressure changes and ACC activity correlates with HRV, linking cognitive and emotional states to autonomic output. The ACC receives strong input from the amygdala enabling emotional salience to influence cognitive processing and autonomic regulation. ACC dysfunction is implicated in depression, anxiety, OCD, schizophrenia, and chronic pain. Heart-Mind integration in TCM — the Heart Shen's capacity to integrate emotional awareness with cognitive clarity and embody their unity in autonomic coherence — corresponds to the ACC as the neural hub of this integration.

## Conflict Monitoring

- The dACC monitors for conflict between simultaneously active competing response representations.
- When conflict exceeds threshold the dACC signals prefrontal cortex to increase cognitive control.
- Error-related negativity ERN is an EEG component reflecting ACC activation within 100ms of error commission.
- ACC conflict monitoring operates continuously and automatically outside conscious awareness.

## Pain Affect

- ACC encodes the affective unpleasantness of pain — how much the pain bothers the person.
- Somatosensory cortex encodes the sensory qualities of pain — location, intensity, and quality.
- Hypnotic reduction of pain affect reduces ACC activation without affecting S1.
- Chronic pain produces structural changes in the ACC corresponding to persistent affective pain processing.

## Autonomic Regulation

- ACC is a primary cortical regulator of autonomic function through projections to the hypothalamus and brainstem.
- ACC activity correlates with HRV — higher ACC activation associated with greater vagal tone and HRV.
- Emotional regulation through ACC reduces sympathetic activation and increases parasympathetic tone.
- Mindfulness meditation increases rACC thickness and activity corresponding to improved autonomic regulation.
