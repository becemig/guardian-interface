# The Inflammasome and Sterile Inflammation

## Abstract

The inflammasome — the intracellular multiprotein complex that senses danger signals and activates the inflammatory caspase cascade producing IL-1beta and IL-18 — is the central molecular platform of sterile inflammation, the chronic low-grade inflammatory state that underlies metabolic syndrome, atherosclerosis, neurodegeneration, and the accelerated aging phenotype called inflammaging, corresponding in TCM to the constitutional accumulation of Heat and Toxin that produces systemic inflammatory pathology across multiple organ networks. The NLRP3 inflammasome — the most extensively studied inflammasome complex, consisting of the sensor protein NLRP3, the adaptor protein ASC, and the effector protease caspase-1 — is activated by a remarkably diverse range of sterile danger signals including ATP crystals of uric acid and cholesterol, reactive oxygen species, lysosomal rupture, and mitochondrial damage products, producing caspase-1-mediated cleavage of pro-IL-1beta and pro-IL-18 to their active inflammatory forms. Pyroptosis — the inflammatory form of programmed cell death executed by gasdermin D pores activated by inflammasome-cleaved caspase-1 — releases the entire intracellular contents including mature IL-1beta and IL-18, DAMPs, and the inflammasome complex itself into the extracellular space, amplifying the inflammatory signal and recruiting additional immune cells in a feed-forward inflammatory cascade. Chronic low-grade inflammation — the persistent subclinical elevation of inflammatory markers including CRP IL-6 TNF-alpha and IL-1beta seen in metabolic syndrome, obesity, aging, and chronic stress — is driven by constitutive low-level NLRP3 inflammasome activation by the metabolic danger signals of Western lifestyle: excess glucose producing glycation end products, excess saturated fat producing ceramide and LPS, excess uric acid from fructose metabolism, and mitochondrial dysfunction producing reactive oxygen species. Inflammaging — the chronic low-grade sterile inflammation of aging described by Claudio Franceschi — is driven by the accumulation of cellular damage products, senescent cell SASP factors, and the progressive failure of autophagy to clear inflammasome-activating debris, representing the common inflammatory mechanism underlying the convergence of age-related diseases. The Heat Toxin accumulation constitutional pattern in TCM describes the classical understanding of the pathological accumulation of pathogenic Heat and Toxin in the body that produces systemic inflammatory pathology corresponding to the chronic low-grade inflammasome activation of modern chronic disease.

## NLRP3 Inflammasome

- Sensor: NLRP3 detects diverse danger signals — crystal ATP ROS lysosomal rupture.
- Adaptor: ASC bridges NLRP3 to caspase-1 through CARD domain interaction.
- Effector: caspase-1 cleaves pro-IL-1beta and pro-IL-18 to active inflammatory forms.
- Potassium efflux: common proximal signal for NLRP3 activation across diverse stimuli.

## Pyroptosis

- Gasdermin D cleaved by caspase-1 — N-terminal fragment forms plasma membrane pores.
- Pyroptotic pores release IL-1beta IL-18 and intracellular contents into extracellular space.
- Amplifies inflammation by releasing DAMPs and inflammasome complexes.
- Distinct from apoptosis: inflammatory cell death versus anti-inflammatory programmed death.

## Inflammaging

- Senescent cells: secrete SASP factors that activate NLRP3 in neighboring cells.
- Accumulated mtDNA: activates cGAS-STING and NLRP3 as cytosolic danger signal.
- Failed autophagy: allows accumulation of protein aggregates and organelle debris that activate inflammasome.
- Caloric restriction and autophagy induction: suppress inflammaging through NLRP3 inhibition.
