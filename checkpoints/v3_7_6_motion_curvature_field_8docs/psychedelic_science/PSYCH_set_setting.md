# Set and Setting in Psychedelic Therapy

## Abstract

Set and setting in psychedelic therapy — Timothy Leary's foundational insight that the psychological set including expectations, intentions, beliefs, and emotional state of the person, and the setting including the physical environment, social context, and relational container, are as important as the pharmacological properties of the psychedelic compound in determining the nature, quality, and therapeutic value of the psychedelic experience — has been validated by clinical research demonstrating that the quality of the therapeutic relationship, the completeness of preparation, the design of the session environment, and the quality of integration support are major determinants of therapeutic outcome, corresponding to the TCM and traditional healing understanding that the ritual sacred space and the healer's presence constitute the therapeutic field within which medicine acts. Expectation and mindset effects — the powerful influence of pre-session expectations and intentions on psychedelic experience phenomenology and therapeutic outcome, with positive intentions and well-prepared psychological set associated with more complete mystical-type experiences and better therapeutic outcomes, and with anxiety and ambivalence associated with challenging experiences and reduced therapeutic benefit — establishing intentional preparation as a clinical necessity rather than optional enhancement. Therapeutic container design — the clinical protocol elements governing the physical and relational environment of psychedelic sessions including the careful selection and preparation of guides and therapists, the design of the session room to feel safe and homelike rather than clinical, the provision of eye shades and carefully selected music to facilitate inner focus, the establishment of clear agreements about the therapeutic relationship, and the provision of experienced guide presence throughout the session. Music in psychedelic sessions — the remarkable power of carefully selected music in psychedelic therapy sessions to guide emotional processing, support difficult passages, facilitate peak experiences, and provide a temporal arc for the session, with the Johns Hopkins and Imperial College groups developing curated playlists of classical, world, and ambient music that have become de facto clinical standards for psilocybin-assisted therapy sessions. The ritual sacred space Yi Jing correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding that the treatment space and ceremony constitute the ritual container within which the healing field of the physician's Yi intention and the patient's receptivity can operate — a direct correspondence with set and setting as the ritual container determining psychedelic therapeutic outcomes.

## Mindset Preparation

- Intention setting: explicit articulation of therapeutic goals and openness.
- Preparation sessions: 2-3 therapeutic sessions before psychedelic session.
- Anxiety reduction: addressing fears and building therapeutic alliance.
- Mystical experience predictor: prepared positive mindset predicts complete mystical experience.

## Therapeutic Container

- Physical environment: homelike comfortable safe — not clinical or institutional.
- Eye shades: facilitate inner focus reduce external distraction.
- Guide presence: continuous presence without intrusion — attentive witness.
- Trust establishment: therapeutic relationship quality major outcome predictor.

## Music Protocols

- Johns Hopkins playlist: classical world ambient — 6-8 hour arc.
- Emotional guidance: music selected to support emotional processing not suppress.
- Difficult passages: specific music for challenging emotional territory.
- Peak facilitation: transcendent music for mystical experience support.
