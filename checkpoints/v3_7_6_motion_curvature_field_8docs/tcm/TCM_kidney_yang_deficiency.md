# Kidney Yang Deficiency

## Abstract

Kidney Yang deficiency is the depletion of the warming, activating, and transforming power of the Kidney corresponding to constitutional decline of the metabolic, hormonal, and thermogenic functions. Mingmen Fire the gate of vitality housed between the two Kidneys is the constitutional root of all Yang Qi in the body. Kidney Yang deficiency produces a characteristic cold pattern with cold limbs, lumbar weakness, urinary frequency, low libido, and profound fatigue. It corresponds to adrenal insufficiency, hypothyroidism, and the constitutional Yang decline of aging. Treatment tonifies Kidney Yang and Mingmen Fire through warming herbs, moxibustion, and constitutional practice.

## Pathomechanism

- Kidney Yang deficiency arises from constitutional depletion, excessive cold exposure, overwork, and aging.
- Mingmen Fire declining fails to warm the Spleen producing Spleen Yang deficiency and digestive cold symptoms.
- Kidney Yang failing to transform water produces fluid accumulation edema and urinary dysfunction.
- Kidney Yang failing to grasp Lung Qi produces dyspnea on exertion and chronic respiratory weakness.

## Clinical Presentation

- Profound fatigue with desire to rest and lie down reflecting Yang deficiency failing to activate.
- Cold limbs and aversion to cold reflecting Mingmen Fire failing to warm the extremities.
- Lumbar and knee weakness and soreness as the Kidney governs the lumbar region and knees.
- Urinary frequency especially at night with pale copious urine from Kidney failing to consolidate fluids.
- Low libido and sexual dysfunction from Kidney Yang and Jing insufficiency.
- Pale puffy face with a blue-gray hue and swollen pale tongue with white moist coat.
- Deep slow weak pulse especially in the right chi position corresponding to Mingmen Fire.

## Western Correlates

- Adrenal insufficiency with low cortisol, DHEA, and aldosterone corresponds to Kidney Yang deficiency.
- Hypothyroidism with low T3 and T4 producing cold intolerance and fatigue corresponds to Kidney Yang depletion.
- Testosterone deficiency in aging males producing fatigue, low libido, and muscle loss corresponds to Kidney Yang decline.
- The constitutional Yang decline of aging corresponds to the progressive Mingmen Fire diminishment through the life cycle.

## Treatment

- You Gui Wan restore the right pill is the classical formula tonifying Kidney Yang with Aconite and Cinnamon.
- GV-4 Mingmen and CV-4 Guanyuan moxibustion directly tonifies the constitutional Yang fire.
- Qigong practice focusing on lower Dan Tian cultivation builds Kidney Yang through internal heat generation.
- Dietary warming foods including lamb, ginger, cinnamon, and walnuts support Kidney Yang recovery.
