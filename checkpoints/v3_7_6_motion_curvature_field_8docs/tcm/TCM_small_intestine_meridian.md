# Small Intestine Meridian: Fire Element, Separation of Pure and Impure, and Shoulder

## Abstract

The Small Intestine is the Yang organ paired with the Heart in the Fire element system of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Its primary physiological function is the separation of pure from impure at every level of digestion, absorption, and mental discrimination. The Small Intestine receives the partially digested food from the Stomach, separates usable nutrients from waste, sends the pure upward to the Spleen and the impure downward to the Large Intestine and Bladder. At the mental level the Small Intestine governs discernment and the capacity to sort clear from unclear thoughts and genuine from false feelings. The Small Intestine meridian traverses the posterior shoulder making it the primary channel for posterior shoulder and scapular disorders.

## Organ Functions

### Separation of Pure and Impure

- The Small Intestine separates food and fluids into usable pure substances and waste impure substances.
- Pure food essence is sent upward to the Spleen for transformation and distribution as nutritive Qi.
- Pure fluids are sent to the Bladder for reabsorption or excretion.
- Impure solids descend to the Large Intestine for final elimination.
- Small Intestine dysfunction produces muddled digestion with bloating, loose stools, and poor nutrient absorption.

### Mental Discernment

- At the mental level the Small Intestine separates clear from unclear thoughts and genuine from false perceptions.
- Small Intestine Qi weakness produces mental confusion, difficulty making decisions, and inability to distinguish what is true.
- The capacity to study, analyze, and extract meaning from complex information is a Small Intestine function.
- Guardian Interface knowledge graph navigation and research synthesis are expressions of Small Intestine discernment.

### Heart Support

- The Small Intestine supports Heart function by keeping the mental field clear of confusion and false information.
- When the Small Intestine fails to separate pure from impure mental content the Heart Shen becomes disturbed.
- Heat from the Heart can transfer to the Small Intestine producing urinary symptoms including dark burning urine.

## Meridian Pathway

### Hand and Arm

- Originates at SI-1 Shaoze on the ulnar side of the little finger tip.
- Ascends along the ulnar aspect of the forearm and posterior upper arm.
- SI-3 Houxi at the ulnar hand is the Shu Stream point and opening point of the Governing Vessel.
- SI-6 Yanglao at the posterior wrist governs visual disorders and acute lower back pain.
- SI-8 Xiaohai at the medial elbow olecranon groove is the He Sea point.

### Shoulder and Scapula

- Crosses the posterior shoulder in a complex pathway through the scapular region.
- SI-9 Jianzhen posterior and inferior to the shoulder joint governs shoulder pain and frozen shoulder.
- SI-10 Naoshu superior to SI-9 governs shoulder and scapular disorders.
- SI-11 Tianzong in the suprascapular fossa governs scapular pain, breast disorders, and respiratory conditions.
- SI-12 Bingfeng in the supraspinatus fossa governs shoulder and upper back pain.

### Neck and Face

- Ascends the lateral neck to SI-16 and SI-17 governing throat and jaw disorders.
- SI-17 Tianrong governs tinnitus, sudden deafness, and throat disorders.
- Terminates at SI-19 Tinggong anterior to the tragus of the ear governing hearing disorders and tinnitus.

### Key Acupoints

- SI-3 Houxi: Shu Stream point and Du Mai opening point, posterior hand, occipital headache, neck rigidity, back pain, malaria.
- SI-11 Tianzong: Suprascapular fossa, scapular pain, breast disorders, difficulty breathing.
- SI-17 Tianrong: Lateral neck, tinnitus, deafness, throat swelling, goiter.
- SI-19 Tinggong: Anterior ear, tinnitus, deafness, ear disorders, jaw pain.

## Governing Vessel Connection

- SI-3 Houxi opens the Governing Vessel Du Mai as one of the eight extraordinary vessel opening points.
- Through this connection Small Intestine treatment accesses the entire posterior midline and spinal axis.
- SI-3 combined with BL-62 Shenmai treats acute and chronic spinal disorders through the Du Mai pathway.
- This connection links Small Intestine discernment function with Governing Vessel central nervous system governance.

## Shoulder Clinical Significance

- The Small Intestine meridian pathway through the posterior shoulder makes it essential for scapular and rotator cuff disorders.
- SI-9, SI-10, SI-11, and SI-12 form the primary point cluster for posterior shoulder pain treatment.
- The pathway correspondence with the posterior shoulder also links to the Superficial Back Line and spiral line fascial trains.
- Frozen shoulder adhesive capsulitis responds to Small Intestine and Large Intestine combined treatment in clinical practice.
