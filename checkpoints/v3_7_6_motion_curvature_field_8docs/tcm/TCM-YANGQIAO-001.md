# Yangqiao Mai - Yang Motility Vessel

## Abstract

Yangqiao Mai the Yang Motility Vessel - functioning as the yang aspect of the Bridge Channel pair together with Yinqiao Mai - governs the yang qi driving muscular activity and physical movement, regulates the yang contribution to the yin-yang balance each meridian uses in functional activity, controls lateral and exterior fluid distribution, governs the wake and alertness dimension of the sleep-wake cycle, and connects to hypertension and excess yang rising patterns. Runs along the lateral and posterior aspects of the body from the lateral heel through the lateral ankle and leg, through the hip and lateral flank, up the lateral arm and shoulder to the neck and occiput, continuing to the inner canthus of the eye where it converges with Yinqiao Mai, the two vessels meeting at the eye to jointly govern visual and consciousness function. Psychologically governs healthy assertiveness and the aggressive-defensive response, with excess manifesting as hyperactivity, aggressiveness, hypertension, and irritability, and deficiency manifesting as fatigue and inability to mount appropriate yang assertion.

## Channel Pathway

- Begins at B 62 (Shen Mai / Calm Sleep) below the lateral malleolus
- Ascends lateral ankle and leg through Bladder meridian territory
- Passes through lateral hip (GB 29 region)
- Continues up lateral body to shoulder via SI 10 and adjacent points
- Ascends neck to GB 20 and B 10 at the cranial base
- Continues to inner canthus of eye (B 1 / Jing Ming) - convergence with Yinqiao
- Connects with Governing Vessel at GV 16 region

## Master Point and Release Pairs

- B 62 (Shen Mai / Calm Sleep): directly below the lateral malleolus in the groove
- MASTER POINT of the Bridge Channel
- Primary quick release pair: SI3 + B62 (activates Yangqiao and Du Mai simultaneously)
- Secondary pair: Lu7 + K6 (activates Yinqiao and Ren Mai for full Bridge balance)

## Clinical Indications

- Sleep: insomnia from yang excess failing to descend, hyperactive sleep patterns
- Neurological: epileptic seizures, convulsions (primary indication alongside Yinqiao)
- Pain: backaches, sciatica along lateral leg and hip, migraines
- Psychological: aggression, irritability, hypertension pattern from excess yang rising
- Behavioral: hyperactivity, difficulty calming
- Fatigue: paradoxical fatigue from yang depletion despite outward activation
- Urogenital: urinary and reproductive dysfunction (lateral pathway)
- Ocular: eye disorders, visual disturbances

- Yang activity driver: Yangqiao provides the yang motivational force for muscular exertion

- Hypertension pattern: excess Yangqiao yang rising upward equals hypertension headache agitation
- Eye convergence: Yangqiao and Yinqiao meet at inner canthus - explains K6 and B62 both treating eye conditions
- Lateral fluid governance: Yangqiao governs lateral body fluid as complement to Yinqiao medial fluid governance
