# Abdomen and Pelvis Acupressure Point Cluster

## Abstract

The abdomen and pelvis acupressure point cluster covers the primary lower trunk and pelvic girdle points governing the hara center, reproductive function, pelvic tension, menstrual regulation, sexual energy, digestive processing, and the root of constitutional vitality. This cluster anchors the lower extraordinary vessel system - CV 6 Sea of Chi at the hara is the primary tonic anchor for the Conception Vessel, St 30 is the entry point of the Chong Mai Penetrating Channel, and GB 29 is a key anchor for both the Belt Channel and Bridge Channel. The hara (CV 6) holds special significance in both TCM and Japanese bodywork traditions as the physical and energetic center of the body - the seat of original chi and the root of all vitality.

## Point Inventory

### CV 6 - Sea of Chi / Hara (Qi Hai)
- Location: 2 fingers directly below the navel on the midline
- Benefits: energy development, physical power, centeredness, reproductive health
- Note: primary tonic point of the entire body - anchor of Conception Vessel
- TCM: corresponds to 2nd chakra sacral center and Dan Tian lower energy field

### CV 2 - Curved Bone (Qu Gu)
- Location: superior border of pubic symphysis on the midline
- Benefits: 1st chakra root grounding, pelvic floor activation, urogenital function

### Sp 13 - Rushing Door (Fu She)
- Location: groin region 1 finger above the inguinal crease lateral to midline
- Benefits: pelvic tension, menstrual tension, sexual imbalances, groin cramping
- Caution: press gently - femoral artery is nearby

### St 30 - Qi Thoroughfare (Qi Chong)
- Location: pubic bone level 2 fingers lateral to midline
- Benefits: entry point of Chong Mai - regulates Penetrating Channel function
- Note: primary anchor of the Sea of Blood / Ocean of Twelve Channels

### K 11 - Curved Bone (Heng Gu)
- Location: superior pubic area on the Kidney meridian
- Benefits: Chong Mai intersection - urogenital function, reproductive regulation

### GB 29 - Dwelling in the Bone (Ju Liao)
- Location: hip region between ASIS and greater trochanter
- Benefits: sciatica, hip pain, lower back, sinew and tendon release
- Note: anchor point for Dai Mai Belt Channel and Yangwei Yang Linking Vessel

## Clinical Protocols

- Lower Back Release: B 10, K 27, CV 6, GB 29 (opening sequence)
- Woman's Balance Flow: Sp 13, CV 6, CV 12, GB 29, GB 41
- Increasing Vitality Flow: Sp 16, Sp 13, Sp 9, Lv 3

## Indications by Condition

- Menstrual disorders: Sp 13, CV 6, St 30, K 11
- Pelvic tension: Sp 13, GB 29, CV 2
- Fatigue and low vitality: CV 6 (primary tonic)
- Digestive: CV 12, Sp 16, CV 6
