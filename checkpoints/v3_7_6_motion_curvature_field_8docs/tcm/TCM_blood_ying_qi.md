# Blood and Ying Qi

## Abstract

Blood Xue in Chinese medicine is a dense nutritive substance closely related to Qi that nourishes and moistens the body tissues and provides the material foundation for the mind and spirit. Ying Qi nutritive Qi flows within the blood vessels as the nutritive layer of Qi most closely associated with Blood. Blood is produced through the transformation of food essence by the Spleen which sends it to the Heart to be transformed into Blood proper. The Heart circulates Blood through the vessels while the Liver stores and regulates Blood volume. The four levels of Ying Wei Qi Blood describe pathogenic invasion from surface to deep in febrile disease. Blood deficiency and Blood stasis are the two primary Blood pathologies with distinct clinical presentations and treatment principles.

## Blood Production

- The Spleen transforms food essence and sends the refined product upward to the Lung and Heart.
- The Heart with the assistance of Heart Fire transforms this essence into Blood proper.
- Kidney Jing provides the constitutional substrate for Blood production through bone marrow hematopoiesis.
- Ying Qi and Blood travel together in the vessels as the interior nutritive circulation.

## Blood Functions

- Blood nourishes all tissues providing the material substrate for organ function and somatic vitality.
- Blood moistens the sinews, skin, and sense organs maintaining their pliability and sensory acuity.
- Heart Blood houses the Shen providing the material foundation for consciousness, sleep, and mental stability.
- Liver Blood nourishes the eyes, sinews, and menstrual Blood providing the storage reservoir for Blood.

## Blood Pathologies

- Blood deficiency produces pale complexion, dry skin and hair, poor memory, insomnia, and menstrual irregularity.
- Blood stasis produces fixed stabbing pain, dark complexion, purplish lips and tongue, and masses.
- Heat in Blood produces reckless movement of Blood with hemorrhage, rashes, and agitation.
- Wind in Blood produces skin disorders with itching, movement, and spreading corresponding to allergic inflammation.

## TCM Correspondence

- Heart governs Blood and the vessels providing the pumping force for Blood circulation.
- Liver stores Blood and regulates its distribution to the periphery during activity and rest.
- Spleen produces Blood and holds Blood in the vessels preventing extravasation.
- Conception Vessel is the sea of Blood governing menstrual Blood and uterine function.
