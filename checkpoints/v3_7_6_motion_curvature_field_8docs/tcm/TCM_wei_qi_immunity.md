# Wei Qi and Defensive Immunity

## Abstract

Wei Qi defensive Qi is the Yang Qi that circulates at the body surface between the skin and muscles governing defensive immunity against external pathogenic invasion. Wei Qi is produced from the Lung and distributed by the Triple Burner to the exterior surface. During the day Wei Qi circulates in the Yang channels at the exterior while at night it circulates through the Yin organs. Wei Qi governs the opening and closing of sweat pores regulating perspiration and temperature. Seasonal transitions are the periods of greatest Wei Qi vulnerability as the body adjusts to changing environmental Qi. External Wind as the primary vehicle for pathogenic invasion penetrates through Wei Qi deficiency at the nape of the neck and upper back.

## Wei Qi Production and Distribution

- Wei Qi arises from the transformation of food essence by the Spleen and is dominated by the Lung.
- The Lung distributes Wei Qi to the exterior surface through its dispersing and descending functions.
- Triple Burner provides the pathway for Wei Qi distribution throughout the three body cavities and surface.
- During the day Wei Qi completes 25 cycles through the Yang channels protecting the exterior.

## Sweat Pore Governance

- Wei Qi governs the opening and closing of the sweat pores Cou Li regulating perspiration and temperature.
- Wei Qi deficiency allows pathogenic Wind to penetrate through poorly regulated pores.
- Spontaneous sweating from Wei Qi deficiency indicates failure to hold the pores closed at rest.
- Night sweats from Yin deficiency reflect a different mechanism — deficiency Heat forcing fluids out.

## Seasonal Vulnerability

- Spring Wind, Summer Heat, Late Summer Damp, Autumn Dryness, and Winter Cold are seasonal pathogens.
- Seasonal transitions require Wei Qi adaptation to changed environmental conditions increasing vulnerability.
- Strengthening Wei Qi before seasonal transitions through Lung tonics and practice reduces illness susceptibility.
- BL-12 Fengmen Wind Gate and BL-13 Feishu Lung Back Shu are primary Wei Qi protection points.

## TCM Correspondence

- Wei Qi corresponds to innate immune function at the body surface including mucosal immunity and skin barrier.
- Lung opening to skin and nose governs the primary Wei Qi defense zones against airborne pathogens.
- Triple Burner systemic Wei Qi distribution corresponds to the systemic innate immune surveillance network.
- Governing Vessel Yang Qi governs the posterior body surface corresponding to the primary Wind invasion pathway.
