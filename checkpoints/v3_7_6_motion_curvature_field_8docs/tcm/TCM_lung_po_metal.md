# Lung Po Metal

## Abstract

The Lung Po corporeal soul is the most somatic and instinctual of the five spirits — the aspect of the psyche most closely identified with the physical body itself, inhabiting the body boundary and registering all physical sensation as the ground of somatic knowing and instinctual response. Po is often translated as corporeal soul or animal soul — it is the soul that comes with the body at birth and returns to the earth with the body at death, distinguished from the ethereal Hun that survives death. The Po governs the body's instinctual intelligence — the automatic somatic responses of defense, feeding, mating, and territory that precede conscious deliberation. It inhabits the skin and body surface — the outermost boundary between self and world — and governs the tactile and proprioceptive sensing that establishes the body's edge. The Lung governs the Po, and the Lung governs Qi and breathing, the skin, the body hair, and the diffusion of Wei Qi to the surface — all functions of the outermost boundary. The Metal element correspondence of the Lung-Po axis emphasizes the qualities of clarity, boundary, letting go, and the cycle of taking in the new and releasing the old. Grief is the emotion associated with the Lung — appropriate grief honors what has been lost and enables letting go, while unresolved grief contracts the chest, inhibits breathing, and depletes Lung Qi. The seven Po correspond to seven somatic instincts — the seven deadly sins of Chinese medicine — that operate autonomously through the body when the Shen does not consciously guide them. Clinical treatment of Po disturbance addresses the Lung network, the Wei Qi surface, and the grief and loss dimension.

## Corporeal Soul Functions

- Po governs the instinctual somatic intelligence that operates below conscious deliberation.
- It inhabits the skin and body surface registering all sensation as the primary somatic sensing faculty.
- Po governs the automatic defensive responses — the flinch, recoil, and startle that protect the body boundary.
- It carries the somatic memory of all physical experience including trauma encoded in the tissues.

## Grief and Letting Go

- Grief is the appropriate emotional response to loss — the Lung-Po's recognition of what has been taken from the field.
- Appropriate grief flows and resolves through its natural cycle enabling letting go and renewal.
- Unresolved grief contracts the chest, inhibits breathing, and depletes Lung Qi over time.
- The Metal element cycle of taking in and letting go corresponds to the breath cycle as its moment-to-moment expression.

## Wei Qi and Boundary

- Lung diffusing Wei Qi to the surface corresponds to Po inhabiting the body boundary as its energetic guardian.
- Strong Wei Qi with clear Po boundary enables appropriate discernment of what to take in and what to exclude.
- Po boundary weakness produces susceptibility to external invasion — both pathogenic and psychic.
- The skin as the largest sense organ corresponds to Po as the most body-identified of the five spirits.

## TCM Correspondence

- Po somatic instinct corresponds to the insula interoceptive body mapping and somatic marker system.
- Lung governing the skin corresponds to the skin as the primary sensory organ of Po body-boundary awareness.
- Grief depleting Lung Qi corresponds to the immunosuppressive and physiological effects of complicated grief.
- The seven Po as somatic instincts corresponds to the subcortical instinctual systems below cortical regulation.
