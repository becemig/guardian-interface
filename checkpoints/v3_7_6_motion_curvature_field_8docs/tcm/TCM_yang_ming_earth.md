# Yang Ming Stage

## Abstract

The Yang Ming stage in Zhang Zhongjing's Six Stage system represents the interior Yang stage where pathogenic heat has penetrated from the Taiyang exterior into the interior combining with the body's Yang to produce intense interior heat. Yang Ming is called Bright Yang because it is the stage of maximum Yang — the full confrontation between pathogenic heat and the body's interior defensive resources. Two Yang Ming patterns exist — Yang Ming channel pattern with the four bigs (big fever, big sweat, big thirst, big pulse) and Yang Ming organ pattern with dry bound stool from heat drying the intestinal fluids. The Stomach and Large Intestine as the Yang Ming organs govern the interior Earth domain where heat accumulates. Modern inflammatory conditions including high fever, sepsis, and acute inflammatory disease correspond to Yang Ming excess heat patterns.

## Four Bigs

- Big fever — high persistent fever reflecting interior heat at maximum intensity.
- Big sweat — profuse sweating reflecting heat forcing fluids outward through the surface.
- Big thirst — intense thirst reflecting heat consuming fluids and requiring urgent replacement.
- Big pulse — flooding surging pulse reflecting maximum Yang Qi mobilization against interior heat.

## Yang Ming Organ Pattern

- When Yang Ming heat combines with existing food stagnation dry bound stool results.
- The three承 Qi formulae — minor, major, and regulate Qi — are the classical Da Huang rhubarb purgative formulae.
- Dry stool produces secondary heat accumulation creating a vicious cycle of increasing interior heat.
- Successful purgation breaks the cycle rapidly reducing fever and restoring normal Qi movement.

## Modern Correlates

- Yang Ming channel pattern corresponds to systemic inflammatory response with high fever and sepsis physiology.
- The four bigs correspond to the clinical signs of bacteremia — fever, diaphoresis, tachycardia, and vasodilation.
- Yang Ming organ pattern corresponds to ileus, obstruction, and constipation in febrile illness.
- Purgative treatment in Yang Ming organ pattern corresponds to early enteral feeding and bowel management in ICU.

## TCM Correspondence

- ST-44 Neiting clears Yang Ming channel heat and is the primary cooling point of the Stomach meridian.
- LI-4 Hegu clears Yang Ming heat through the Large Intestine and disperses heat from the face and head.
- Governing Vessel points GV-14 Dazhui and GV-12 Shenzhu clear Yang Ming heat from the posterior axis.
- The Yang Ming stage represents the TCM understanding of acute inflammatory fever physiology.
