# Microdosing

## Abstract

Microdosing — the practice of taking sub-perceptual doses of psychedelic substances typically one-tenth to one-twentieth of a full dose on a regular schedule — has become one of the most widespread and culturally significant developments in the contemporary psychedelic renaissance, driven by widespread anecdotal reports of enhanced creativity, focus, emotional regulation, and wellbeing, and now the subject of growing scientific investigation. James Fadiman systematized the microdosing protocol through years of self-experiment reports, establishing the most common schedule of one day on followed by two days off, cycling over four to eight weeks, with careful journaling of effects. The hypothesized mechanisms of microdosing include sub-threshold 5-HT2A activation producing enhanced BDNF and neuroplasticity without full receptor activation, sub-threshold dopamine modulation enhancing motivation and focus, and the psychological effects of intentional practice and heightened self-observation producing benefits independent of direct pharmacological action. Vince Polito's longitudinal study documented increased openness, decreased depression and mind-wandering, and improved neuroticism over one month of microdosing, with some participants also reporting headaches, anxiety, and physiological discomfort — establishing that microdosing effects are real but heterogeneous. The self-blinding citizen science study by Szigeti and colleagues used an innovative at-home placebo-controlled design to separate pharmacological from expectancy effects, finding that both active and placebo microdosing produced improvements in psychological wellbeing, with active microdosing producing additional improvements in psychomotor performance. The placebo response in microdosing research is substantial — expectancy, intention, and the practice of self-observation may account for a significant portion of reported benefits. Yi clarity cultivation describes the TCM framework for microdosing: the sub-threshold activation of Shen clarity through minimal quantities of potent Shen-opening substances, analogous to the graduated dose escalation of TCM tonic herb protocols that build constitutional strength incrementally.

## Fadiman Protocol

- Dose one day on two days off cycling over 4-8 weeks — the most studied microdosing schedule.
- Sub-perceptual dose: one-tenth to one-twentieth of a full psychedelic dose.
- Careful journaling tracks cognitive, emotional, and somatic effects across the cycle.
- The off days prevent tolerance buildup and allow integration of subtle effects.

## Cognitive and Emotional Effects

- Enhanced focus and flow state access — reduced mind-wandering and increased task absorption.
- Increased emotional openness and decreased emotional reactivity in many participants.
- Enhanced creativity through increased associative thinking and novel connection formation.
- Decreased depression and anxiety in consistent responders with heterogeneous response overall.

## Placebo and Expectancy

- Substantial placebo response in microdosing research suggests expectancy is a significant active ingredient.
- The practice of self-observation, intention-setting, and journaling may produce benefits independent of pharmacology.
- Active microdosing adds psychomotor enhancement and possibly greater psychological flexibility beyond placebo.
