# Meditation Neuroscience

## Abstract

Meditation practices produce measurable and lasting changes in brain structure, functional connectivity, and neural oscillatory dynamics. Neuroimaging studies consistently show that experienced meditators exhibit reduced default mode network activity during practice, thickened prefrontal and insular cortex, enhanced anterior cingulate cortex function, and self-induced high-amplitude gamma synchrony during open presence practices. Three primary meditation styles recruit distinct neural networks: focused attention practices train the dorsal attention network and anterior cingulate; open monitoring practices train metacognitive awareness and reduce DMN reactivity; loving-kindness practices activate insula, putamen, and medial OFC. Long-term practice produces trait-level changes persisting outside formal meditation, including reduced mind-wandering, enhanced interoception, faster attentional recovery, reduced amygdala reactivity, and reduced inflammatory gene expression. In TCM, meditation practices cultivate Shen clarity, gather scattered Yi, and progressively unify the five spirits.

## Default Mode Network

- DMN: medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate cortex, angular gyrus, hippocampus
- Function: self-referential processing, mind-wandering, autobiographical memory, future simulation
- Meditation effect: experienced meditators show reduced PCC activity during practice
- Mind-wandering: DMN activation predicts unhappiness - Killingsworth and Gilbert 2010
- PCC: posterior cingulate - key node - tracks self-referential engagement
- Deactivation: meditation reduces PCC coupling - less rumination and self-narrative
- TCM parallel: quieting Yi (intellect) and Hun (wandering mind) allows Shen to clarify

## Gamma Synchrony in Advanced Meditators

- Lutz 2004: Tibetan monks with 10,000-50,000 hours show sustained 40 Hz gamma
- Amplitude: gamma bursts 30 times larger than novice meditators
- Coherence: long-range fronto-parietal gamma coherence during open presence
- Interpretation: gamma reflects binding and integration of conscious content
- Novice meditators: do not show gamma - requires years of practice to emerge
- Non-dual states: highest gamma coherence during non-referential compassion meditation

## Cortical Thickness Changes

- Lazar 2005: meditators show thicker prefrontal cortex and right anterior insula
- Insula: interoceptive awareness - body sensation - maps to somatic self-awareness
- Anterior cingulate: attentional control, error monitoring, conflict detection
- Age-related thinning: meditators show less age-related cortical thinning
- MBSR 8 weeks: increases hippocampal, PCC, temporo-parietal junction gray matter density
- Amygdala: MBSR reduces amygdala gray matter density - less reactive stress response

## Focused Attention Meditation

- Object: sustained attention on breath, mantra, or visual object
- Neural: dorsal attention network - IPS and FEF - sustained engagement
- Error detection: ACC detects mind-wandering - triggers reorienting
- Stages: novice - effortful redirection; intermediate - fewer distractions; advanced - effortless stability
- EEG: alpha increases over frontal midline - theta at Fz in deep concentration
- TCM: Yi concentration - gathering and stabilizing the intention

## Open Monitoring Meditation

- Object: non-reactive awareness of all arising phenomena without selection
- Neural: reduced DMN reactivity, enhanced metacognitive awareness networks
- Decentering: observing thoughts as events rather than truths
- EEG: widespread alpha increase - reduced beta - receptive open state
- Long-term: reduces automatic emotional reactivity - greater response flexibility
- TCM: Shen as witness - pure awareness prior to content

## Loving-Kindness Meditation

- Object: systematic cultivation of warmth and goodwill toward self and others
- Neural: insula, putamen, medial OFC, anterior cingulate activation
- Empathy: increases both affective and cognitive empathy scores
- Oxytocin: loving-kindness increases oxytocin and prosocial behavior
- Inflammation: MBSR and LKM reduce CRP and IL-6 gene expression
- TCM: cultivating Heart Shen - expanding the Heart field of awareness
