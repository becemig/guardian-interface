# States of Consciousness

## Abstract

Consciousness is not binary but exists as a spectrum of qualitatively distinct states, each with characteristic phenomenology, neural oscillatory signatures, neurochemical profiles, and functional capacities. The primary states — waking, NREM sleep, REM dreaming — are supplemented by transitional and altered states including hypnagogic imagery, flow, trance, absorption, dissociation, and contemplative states. Each state represents a different configuration of the same underlying neural hardware, selected by neuromodulatory tone (norepinephrine, serotonin, acetylcholine, dopamine), thalamocortical gating, and cortico-cortical connectivity patterns. The EEG provides a real-time window into state transitions through characteristic oscillatory signatures. Guardian Interface models state as a continuous variable influencing somatic node activation weighting and telemetry interpretation.

## Primary States and EEG Signatures

- Alert waking: beta (13-30 Hz) and gamma (30-100 Hz) dominant - high-frequency low-amplitude
- Relaxed waking: alpha (8-12 Hz) dominant - eyes closed, posterior - idling rhythm
- Drowsy: theta (4-8 Hz) increasing - slowed cognition, imagery onset
- NREM stage 1: theta dominant - hypnagogic - threshold consciousness
- NREM stage 2: sleep spindles (12-15 Hz) and K-complexes - consolidation
- SWS: delta (0.5-4 Hz) dominant - slow oscillations - thalamocortical down-states
- REM: mixed frequency - wake-like EEG with muscle atonia - dreaming
- Meditation: theta and alpha increase - gamma bursts in advanced practitioners

## Hypnagogic State

- Definition: threshold between waking and sleep - Stage N1
- Phenomenology: vivid imagery, auditory hallucinations, body sensations, ego dissolution
- Neural: reduced norepinephrine, rising acetylcholine - loosens associative constraints
- Creative use: Edison and Dali used hypnagogic state for problem solving
- NSDR: non-sleep deep rest protocols target hypnagogic-adjacent states
- TCM: transition between Hun activity (dreaming) and Shen clarity (waking)

## Flow State

- Definition: optimal experience - complete absorption in challenging activity
- Conditions: skill-challenge balance, clear goals, immediate feedback
- Phenomenology: effortless action, time distortion, loss of self-consciousness, intrinsic reward
- Neural: transient hypofrontality - reduced prefrontal activity - reduced self-monitoring
- Neurochemistry: norepinephrine, dopamine, anandamide, serotonin, endorphins all elevated
- EEG: alpha-theta border - relaxed yet focused - creative problem solving zone
- TCM: Wu Wei - effortless action - alignment of Shen and Jing in movement

## Trance and Absorption

- Trance: narrowed attentional focus with reduced peripheral awareness
- Hypnotic trance: heightened suggestibility - default mode suppression - theta dominant
- Shamanic trance: rhythmic drumming at 4-7 Hz entrains theta - facilitates non-ordinary states
- Absorption: total immersion in internal or external object - high hypnotizability
- Dissociation: detachment from self or environment - theta and delta intrusions into waking

## Neuromodulatory State Control

- Norepinephrine (LC): alert, focused, narrow attention - high in stress and flow
- Serotonin (raphe): contentment, broad attention, reduced rumination
- Acetylcholine (BF, PPT): REM, learning, attention gating - high in dreaming and focused attention
- Dopamine (VTA): motivation, prediction error, reward anticipation - drives seeking
- Adenosine: sleep pressure - builds with waking duration - cleared during SWS
- State transitions: shifts in neuromodulatory balance drive consciousness state changes
