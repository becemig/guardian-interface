# Somatic Trauma Integration

## Abstract

Somatic trauma integration encompasses a family of body-inclusive trauma therapies that address the somatic encoding of traumatic experience through specific techniques targeting the nervous system, body posture, eye position, and bilateral brain stimulation. Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing EMDR developed by Francine Shapiro uses bilateral stimulation — alternating eye movements, taps, or tones — to facilitate the adaptive processing of traumatic memories reducing their distress and integrating them into the normal memory network. The adaptive information processing AIP model proposes that trauma produces maladaptively stored memories isolated from adaptive neural networks and that bilateral stimulation facilitates their processing and integration. Brainspotting developed by David Grand identifies specific eye positions gaze points that correlate with activation of traumatic material in the subcortical brain — the brainspot is both a diagnostic tool and a processing position. Sustained attention to the brainspot with bilateral stimulation enables processing of subcortically encoded trauma that may be inaccessible through verbal or cognitive approaches. Pat Ogden's sensorimotor psychotherapy addresses the somatic resources and postures that were present during trauma directly through mindful body attention, movement, and resource development. All three approaches share the understanding that trauma is somatically encoded below the level of verbal narrative and requires somatic as well as cognitive integration. Jing restoration in TCM — the rebuilding of depleted constitutional essence through appropriate cultivation after severe depletion from trauma — corresponds to the somatic trauma integration project of restoring constitutional regulation after traumatic disruption.

## EMDR

- Bilateral stimulation during trauma memory activation facilitates adaptive reprocessing through uncertain mechanisms.
- Proposed mechanisms include working memory taxation reducing memory vividness, REM sleep simulation, and orienting response activation.
- Eight-phase protocol moves from history taking through desensitization, installation, and body scan to closure.
- EMDR is among the most extensively researched trauma treatments with strong meta-analytic support.

## Brainspotting

- Brainspotting identifies the eye position that most strongly activates subcortical trauma encoding.
- The therapist guides the client to find the brainspot through attunement to somatic indicators of activation.
- Sustained attention to the brainspot with bilateral sound enables subcortical processing without verbal narrative.
- The body serves as the compass guiding toward and through the subcortically encoded material.

## Sensorimotor Psychotherapy

- Sensorimotor psychotherapy works directly with posture, movement, and physical sensation as therapeutic media.
- Trauma resources — movements, postures, and sensations associated with safety and competence — are identified and developed.
- Trauma-related postures and movements are explored mindfully enabling completion and integration.
- The body's intelligence is privileged alongside verbal narrative as a valid source of therapeutic information.
