# Upledger CranioSacral Therapy

## Abstract

John Upledger's CranioSacral Therapy CST is a light-touch manual therapy system derived from William Sutherland's cranial osteopathy but significantly expanded through Upledger's clinical research and teaching at Michigan State University. Upledger's primary contributions include the energy cyst concept, SomatoEmotional Release SER, and the therapeutic imagery and dialogue techniques that emerged from his clinical practice. An energy cyst is an area of disrupted energy and tissue mobility resulting from trauma where the body has isolated the foreign energy to protect normal function — palpable as an area of altered tissue texture, temperature, and mobility. SomatoEmotional Release is the spontaneous discharge of emotional and somatic information stored in tissues when an energy cyst is contacted and released — the person may re-experience sensations, emotions, images, or memories associated with the original trauma. Therapeutic imagery and dialogue involves the practitioner verbally facilitating the person's exploration of the images and material arising during SER. Unwinding is the spontaneous movement of a body part as tissue releases during CST — the limb or head moves through the positions held at the time of the original injury retracing the tissue memory. Hun unwinding in TCM — the release of the Liver Hun from its frozen holding patterns in the fascia and joints through somatic and imaginal dialogue — corresponds to the SER process of releasing trauma held in tissue memory.

## Energy Cysts

- Energy cysts form when the body isolates traumatic energy that it cannot process and discharge immediately.
- They are palpable as areas of altered tissue texture, temperature, and mobility persisting after the initial injury resolves.
- Energy cysts disrupt the normal rhythm and mobility of the craniosacral system in their vicinity.
- CST releases energy cysts through sustained contact that enables the tissue to complete the interrupted discharge.

## SomatoEmotional Release

- SER occurs when contact with an energy cyst enables the stored somatic and emotional content to surface.
- Spontaneous movement, imagery, emotions, and memories may arise during SER.
- The therapist facilitates the process through verbal dialogue while maintaining the tissue contact.
- Successful SER produces lasting changes in tissue mobility, pain, and psychological processing.

## Unwinding

- Unwinding is the spontaneous movement of a body part retracing the positions held during original injury.
- The tissue guides the movement following its own memory without therapist direction.
- Unwinding is facilitated by the therapist following the movement rather than directing it.
- Completion of the unwinding sequence corresponds to the tissue processing and releasing the stored trauma.

## TCM Correspondence

- Hun unwinding as releasing Liver Hun from frozen holding patterns corresponds to SER tissue memory release.
- Liver governing the sinews and their memory of trauma corresponds to the energy cyst in fascial tissue.
- Governing Vessel governing the spinal axis corresponds to the primary CST treatment axis.
- The therapeutic dialogue in SER corresponds to the TCM principle of conversing with the Shen through the body.
