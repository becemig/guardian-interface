# Nine Palaces of the Brain

## Abstract

The nine palaces of the brain are the upper Dan Tian cultivation centers mapped in the Nei Jing Tu and elaborated in Taoist inner landscape traditions — nine specific locations within the brain and skull that are cultivated in sequence during advanced Neidan practice to refine and clarify the Shen spirit toward its ultimate return to emptiness. The Niwan Palace Ni Wan Gong is the most important of the nine palaces — located at the geometric center of the brain approximately at the level of the pineal gland, it is the primary residence of the Yuan Shen original spirit and the culminating point of the microcosmic orbit as it reaches the upper Dan Tian. The nine palaces are distributed through the brain from front to back and correspond to the nine stars of the Big Dipper in the outer cosmos — the body's inner cosmos mirrors the stellar arrangement above. The Yellow Court Huang Ting is a related inner landscape center located variously in the heart or spleen region depending on the tradition — the field of yellow earth where the central cultivation of middle Dan Tian work occurs. Upper Dan Tian cultivation begins with the third eye Yin Tang GV-24.5 and progresses inward and upward through the nine palaces culminating in the Niwan. The jade pillow gate at the occiput is the third of the three gates on the Governing Vessel — its opening is required for the River Cart to circulate Qi into the upper Dan Tian and the nine palaces. Celestial pool Tian Chi at the roof of the mouth collects the nectar of refined Shen as it descends from the upper Dan Tian down the Conception Vessel — the practitioner swallows this nectar to distribute the refined spirit through the body.

## Niwan Palace

- The Niwan Palace at the brain center is the primary residence of Yuan Shen original spirit.
- It corresponds approximately to the pineal gland as the physical correlate of the original spirit seat.
- Cultivation of the Niwan produces the experience of luminous awareness — clear light consciousness.
- The Niwan is the culminating point of upper Dan Tian work and the gateway to returning to emptiness.

## Nine Palace Sequence

- Palace 1 Mingdang at the forehead — the hall of illumination, entry point of upper Dan Tian cultivation.
- Palace 2 Dongfang between the eyebrows and one inch in — the cave of penetrating brightness.
- Palace 3 Niwan at the brain center — the mud ball palace, residence of original spirit.
- Palaces 4-9 extend through the brain to the occiput — each a progressively deeper cultivation center.

## Jade Pillow Gate

- The jade pillow gate at the occiput is the third of the three Governing Vessel gates.
- Its opening allows the River Cart to circulate refined Qi from the spine into the upper brain.
- Physical tension at the occiput-atlas junction corresponds to this gate being closed.
- Opening the jade pillow through cultivation and sometimes manual treatment enables upper Dan Tian work.

## TCM Correspondence

- Nine brain palaces correspond to the TCM concept of the brain as the sea of marrow filled by Kidney Jing.
- Niwan Palace as original spirit seat corresponds to the TCM upper Dan Tian as the residence of Yuan Shen.
- Jade pillow gate at the occiput corresponds to the GV-17 Naohu Brain Door acupoint.
- Celestial pool nectar descending corresponds to the TCM concept of Heart fire descending to warm Kidney water.
