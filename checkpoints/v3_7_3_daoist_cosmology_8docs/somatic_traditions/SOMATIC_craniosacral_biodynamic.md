# Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy

## Abstract

Biodynamic craniosacral therapy BCST is a refined development of William Garner Sutherland's original cranial osteopathy that emphasizes the practitioner's perception of and attunement to the primary respiratory mechanism and the biodynamic forces that shape and sustain the living body. Sutherland discovered the primary respiratory mechanism — the rhythmic motion of the cranial bones, sacrum, and cerebrospinal fluid independent of breathing and heartbeat — which he called the Breath of Life. Rollin Becker deepened this work by focusing on the potency — the biodynamic force carried within the fluid body — as the primary therapeutic agent rather than the practitioner's mechanical intervention. Biodynamic practice cultivates the practitioner's perceptual sensitivity to three rhythmic tidal expressions of primary respiration: the cranial rhythmic impulse at 8-12 cycles per minute, the mid-tide at 2.5 cycles per minute, and the long tide at 1 cycle per 100 seconds. The stillpoint — a moment of complete suspension of primary respiratory motion — enables deep system reorganization and healing. Original breath as the primordial animating force of the living body in BCST corresponds directly to the TCM concept of Yuan Qi constitutional original Qi animating all life functions.

## Primary Respiratory Mechanism

- Cranial bone motion, sacral motion, and CSF pulsation constitute the primary respiratory mechanism.
- This motion is independent of lung breathing and cardiac pulsation representing a third physiological rhythm.
- The primary respiratory mechanism expresses the Breath of Life — the animating potency within the fluid body.
- Skilled practitioners palpate primary respiratory motion through extremely light touch below 5 grams of pressure.

## Three Tidal Expressions

- The cranial rhythmic impulse CRI at 8-12 cycles per minute reflects local tissue and fluid rhythms.
- The mid-tide at 2.5 cycles per minute reflects the deeper fluid body expression of primary respiration.
- The long tide at approximately 1 cycle per 100 seconds reflects the most fundamental biodynamic expression.
- Deepening perceptual attunement progresses from CRI to mid-tide to long tide as practice matures.

## Stillpoint

- The stillpoint is a suspension of primary respiratory motion enabling deep system reorganization.
- It can arise spontaneously during a session or be facilitated by gentle practitioner compression.
- During the stillpoint the fluid body reorganizes around its inherent health and ordering principle.
- The emergence from stillpoint is often accompanied by a deepening and clarification of primary respiratory expression.

## TCM Correspondence

- Yuan Qi constitutional original Qi as the primordial animating force corresponds to the Breath of Life potency.
- Governing Vessel governing the brain-spinal axis corresponds to the primary respiratory mechanism craniosacral axis.
- Kidney Jing as the constitutional essence corresponds to the potency within the CSF fluid body.
- Stillpoint as the threshold of deep reorganization corresponds to the TCM concept of the pivot point in transformation.
