# Microbiome-Immune-Brain Axis

## Abstract

The gut microbiome houses 70-80 percent of the body's immune tissue and is the primary interface between the external environment and the immune system. The gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) — including Peyer's patches, mesenteric lymph nodes, and lamina propria immune cells — is in constant dialogue with the 38 trillion microorganisms of the gut microbiome. Commensal bacteria educate and calibrate immune responses, produce short-chain fatty acids that nourish colonocytes and regulate systemic inflammation, and maintain the integrity of the intestinal barrier. Dysbiosis — disruption of microbial community structure — impairs immune regulation, increases intestinal permeability, and allows LPS (lipopolysaccharide) translocation from gram-negative bacteria to trigger systemic low-grade inflammation. This metabolic endotoxemia drives neuroinflammation, HPA activation, and mood dysregulation. Vagal afferents relay microbiome-derived immune signals to the brainstem within minutes. Psychobiotics — specific probiotic strains with documented neuropsychiatric effects — represent an emerging therapeutic target.

## Gut-Associated Lymphoid Tissue

- GALT: largest immune organ in the body - 70-80 percent of immune cells
- Peyers patches: organized lymphoid follicles in small intestine - antigen sampling
- M cells: specialized epithelial cells overlying Peyers patches - transport antigens to immune cells
- Lamina propria: IgA-secreting plasma cells, T cells, macrophages, dendritic cells
- Secretory IgA: primary mucosal antibody - coats bacteria - prevents epithelial adhesion
- Tolerance: GALT must tolerate commensal bacteria while responding to pathogens
- Microbiome education: germ-free animals have immature immune systems - microbiome required

## Intestinal Permeability and LPS

- Tight junctions: ZO-1, occludin, claudin proteins seal epithelial gaps
- Leaky gut: tight junction disruption allows bacterial products into bloodstream
- LPS: lipopolysaccharide from gram-negative bacteria - potent TLR4 agonist
- Metabolic endotoxemia: low-grade LPS elevation - chronic systemic inflammation
- Drivers: high-fat diet, alcohol, stress, antibiotics, glyphosate, sleep deprivation
- LPS-brain: LPS activates microglia, increases IL-1 and TNF, produces depression-like behavior
- Measurement: serum LPS-binding protein as clinical proxy for intestinal permeability

## Short-Chain Fatty Acids

- Production: fermentation of dietary fiber by anaerobic bacteria
- Types: butyrate, propionate, acetate - each with distinct receptor profiles
- Butyrate: primary fuel for colonocytes - promotes tight junction integrity - anti-inflammatory
- Butyrate-brain: crosses BBB - inhibits histone deacetylase - epigenetic anti-inflammatory
- Propionate: liver and muscle fuel - regulates satiety via PYY and GLP-1
- SCFA depletion: low-fiber diet reduces SCFA producers - impairs gut barrier and immune regulation
- Fiber diversity: diverse plant fiber feeds diverse microbiome - more SCFA production

## Vagal Immune Signaling

- Afferent vagus: 80 percent of vagal fibers are sensory - gut to brain
- Cytokine sensing: vagal sensory neurons detect IL-1, IL-6, serotonin from gut
- Speed: vagal route reaches brainstem within minutes - faster than bloodstream
- Efferent anti-inflammatory: vagal efferents release ACh in spleen - suppress TNF-alpha
- Vagal tone and immunity: higher HRV predicts lower IL-6 and CRP
- Vagus nerve stimulation: reduces RA and Crohn inflammation - anti-inflammatory reflex

## Psychobiotics

- Definition: live organisms that when ingested produce mental health benefit
- Lactobacillus rhamnosus: reduces anxiety and GABA receptor expression in germ-free mice
- Bifidobacterium longum: reduces cortisol and anxiety in humans - RCT evidence
- Mechanisms: SCFA production, vagal signaling, tryptophan metabolism, cytokine modulation
- Limitations: strain-specific, dose-dependent, microbiome context-dependent
- Prebiotics: FOS and GOS increase Bifidobacterium - reduce cortisol awakening response
- TCM: Spleen-Stomach harmony governs microbiome health - Earth element as ecosystem
