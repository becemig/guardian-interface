# Lung Wei Qi and Defensive Immunity

## Abstract

The Lung governs Qi and respiration distributing the refined Qi of air throughout the body and directing the descending and dispersing movement that circulates Wei Qi to the surface. Wei Qi defensive Qi is produced from the interaction of Spleen-derived Gu Qi and Lung-dispersed Zong Qi and circulates in the skin and muscles protecting the body from external pathogenic invasion. The Po corporeal soul housed in the Lung provides the instinctual animal vitality, somatic sensation, and reflexive self-protection of the body. Grief and sadness as the emotions of the Lung injure Lung Qi when prolonged, depleting Wei Qi and reducing resistance to external pathogens. The skin as the outer boundary governed by Lung Wei Qi provides the primary physical interface between self and environment. Treatment supports Lung Qi, strengthens Wei Qi, and resolves Lung-related emotional patterns.

## Lung Governance

- Lung governs Qi and respiration extracting Zong Qi from air and combining it with Spleen Gu Qi.
- Lung governs descent and dispersion — Qi descends from Lung to Kidney while Wei Qi disperses to the surface.
- Lung opens to the nose and governs the skin and body hair as the outermost surface expressions.
- Lung regulates water passages directing the downward movement of fluids to Kidney and Bladder.

## Wei Qi

- Wei Qi circulates outside the channels in the skin and muscles providing defense against external pathogens.
- Wei Qi warms the skin, controls the opening and closing of pores, and moistens skin and body hair.
- During the day Wei Qi circulates on the Yang surface while at night it retreats to the Yin interior.
- Lung Qi deficiency failing to disperse Wei Qi produces susceptibility to Wind Cold invasion and frequent illness.

## Po Soul

- Po is the corporeal soul housed in the Lung providing instinctual somatic vitality and reflexive sensation.
- Po governs the basic animal functions of breathing, instinctual movement, and somatic self-protection.
- Po is the most Yin and earthly of the five souls — it enters with birth and returns to earth at death.
- Unresolved grief holds Po in contraction preventing the natural process of letting go that Po governs.

## TCM Correspondence

- Autumn season corresponds to the Lung resonance of harvest, descent, and the beginning of storage.
- Pungent flavor gently disperses Lung Qi while white color and metal element correspond to Lung nature.
- Sadness and grief directly affect Lung Qi — shallow breathing and chest tightness are somatic expressions.
- Skin disease and respiratory conditions share the Lung-Skin axis as their common TCM substrate.
