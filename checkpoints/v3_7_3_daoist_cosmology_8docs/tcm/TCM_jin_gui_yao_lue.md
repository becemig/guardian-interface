# Jin Gui Yao Lue

## Abstract

The Jin Gui Yao Lue — Essential Prescriptions of the Golden Cabinet — is the companion volume to the Shang Han Lun, also written by Zhang Zhong Jing, addressing the miscellaneous diseases Za Bing that lie outside the acute febrile disease framework of the Shang Han. Together the Shang Han Lun and Jin Gui Yao Lue constitute Zhang Zhong Jing's complete clinical system — the Shang Han addressing acute febrile and epidemic disease, the Jin Gui addressing the full range of chronic constitutional and miscellaneous disease that constitutes the majority of clinical practice. The Jin Gui's approach to constitutional medicine establishes the Jing Fang framework for treating the deep constitutional patterns underlying chronic disease — the deficiency patterns of Xu, the accumulation patterns of Ji, the painful obstruction patterns of Bi, the phlegm-fluid patterns of Tan Yin, and the gynecological conditions of obstetrics and women's disease that Zhang Zhong Jing addresses with remarkable clinical sophistication. The Bi syndrome chapter — painful obstruction from Wind-Cold-Dampness invasion of the channels and joints — provides the classical framework for all rheumatological conditions in Chinese medicine, with formulas that remain the foundation of TCM rheumatology practice today. The Xu labor chapter addresses the wasting and exhaustion syndromes that correspond to modern chronic fatigue, autoimmune, and constitutional deficiency conditions. The phlegm-fluid chapter Tan Yin establishes the TCM framework for the retention of pathological fluids in different body cavities producing diverse clinical manifestations depending on the location of retention. The gynecological chapters address pregnancy, postpartum, and women's diseases with formulas that remain central to modern TCM gynecological practice.

## Constitutional Patterns

- Xu deficiency labor patterns: wasting and exhaustion from constitutional Kidney-Spleen deficiency.
- Bi painful obstruction: Wind-Cold-Dampness in channels and joints producing pain, swelling, limited range.
- Tan Yin phlegm-fluid: pathological fluid retention in chest, abdomen, limbs, or underneath the hypochondrium.
- Ji accumulation: fixed masses from Blood stasis and Phlegm congealing in the abdomen.

## Bi Syndrome Framework

- Wind Bi Xing Bi: migrating joint pain from Wind predominance — pain moves location.
- Cold Bi Tong Bi: fixed severe joint pain from Cold predominance — pain is intense and fixed.
- Damp Bi Zhuo Bi: heavy fixed swollen joints from Dampness predominance — pain is dull and constant.
- Heat Bi: red hot swollen joints from Wind-Cold-Damp transforming to Heat — corresponds to inflammatory arthritis.

## Gynecological Classics

- Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan: Blood stasis in the uterus — the foundational formula for gynecological Blood stasis.
- Wen Jing Tang: Cold-deficiency with Blood stasis in the Chong and Ren vessels — menopausal and menstrual disorders.
- Dang Gui Shao Yao San: Blood deficiency with Dampness in pregnancy — the key prenatal formula.
- These formulas address the constitutional and pathological patterns of women's physiology with extraordinary precision.
