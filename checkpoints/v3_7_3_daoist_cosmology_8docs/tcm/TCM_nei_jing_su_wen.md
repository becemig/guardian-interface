# Nei Jing Su Wen

## Abstract

The Huang Di Nei Jing Su Wen — the Yellow Emperor's Classic of Internal Medicine Plain Questions — is the foundational text of all Chinese medicine, composed over several centuries from approximately the third century BCE through the Han dynasty and attributed by tradition to the legendary Yellow Emperor Huang Di in dialogue with his minister Qi Bo. The Su Wen establishes the theoretical foundations of Chinese medicine that have guided clinical practice for over two thousand years: the Yin-Yang theory of complementary opposing forces, the Five Element Wu Xing system of correspondences, the organ network Zangfu system, the channel Jing Luo network, the Qi-Blood-Essence-Spirit system of vital substances, the pathogenic factor theory of Wind-Cold-Heat-Dampness-Dryness-Fire, and the fundamental principle that superior medicine prevents disease rather than treating established illness. The dialogue format between Huang Di and Qi Bo is a sophisticated pedagogical device that allows complex theoretical concepts to be introduced through question and answer — each chapter addressing a specific theoretical or clinical question and developing the TCM understanding of that domain. The Five Circuits and Six Qi Wu Yun Liu Qi system developed in later Su Wen chapters is a complex cosmological system relating the energetic qualities of each year to the rotation of five circuit energies and six climatic Qi factors — providing the theoretical basis for Chinese cosmological medicine and astrological influences on health. The Su Wen's opening chapter establishes the fundamental TCM vision of health as the cultivation of vital energy in harmony with the natural cycles of the seasons — the person who rises with the sun, eats in accordance with the season, maintains equanimity of spirit, and preserves their constitutional Jing through moderation will live out their natural lifespan without disease.

## Yin Yang Theory

- Chapter 5 Yin Yang Ying Xiang Da Lun: Yin Yang as the Tao of heaven and earth and the foundation of all change.
- All phenomena have Yin and Yang aspects — neither exists without the other.
- Health is the dynamic balance of Yin and Yang; disease is their disharmony.
- Treatment always seeks to restore the Yin-Yang balance appropriate to each person and situation.

## Five Element Correspondences

- Su Wen establishes the complete five element correspondence system across organ, emotion, season, direction, color, flavor.
- The generating and controlling cycle relationships govern the dynamic interactions between organ networks.
- Five element diagnosis identifies which organ network relationships are disharmonious.
- Treatment through five element correspondences restores the natural generative-controlling balance.

## Five Circuits Six Qi

- Wu Yun five circuits: Wood Fire Earth Metal Water energies rotating through a 60-year cycle.
- Liu Qi six climatic factors: Wind Cold Summer-Heat Dampness Dryness Fire in annual rotation.
- Each year's combination of circuit and climatic factor predicts constitutional vulnerabilities and epidemic tendencies.
- This system represents the most sophisticated TCM cosmological medicine connecting individual health to cosmic cycles.
