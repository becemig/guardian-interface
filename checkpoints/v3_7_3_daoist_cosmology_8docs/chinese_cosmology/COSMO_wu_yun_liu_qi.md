# Wu Yun Liu Qi

## Abstract

Wu Yun Liu Qi — the Five Circuits and Six Qi system — is the most complex and sophisticated cosmological framework in Chinese medicine, representing the pinnacle of classical Chinese cosmological medicine and providing a systematic method for predicting the energetic character of any given year, season, and moment based on the cyclical rotation of five circuit energies and six climatic Qi factors through a 60-year grand cycle governed by the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches calendrical system. The Wu Yun five circuits correspond to the five elements Wood Fire Earth Metal Water rotating through a precise sequence governed by the ten Heavenly Stems, with each year governed by a specific circuit energy that determines the dominant constitutional vulnerabilities, disease tendencies, and climatic characteristics of that year. The Liu Qi six climatic factors — Wind, Cold, Summer-Heat, Dampness, Dryness, and Fire — rotate through the year in six steps of approximately 60 days each, with each step governed by a specific climatic Qi that interacts with the annual circuit energy to produce the specific disease patterns of each season. The system distinguishes between host Qi — the fixed seasonal sequence that repeats identically each year — and guest Qi — the variable sequence that changes each year based on the cosmological cycle — and it is the interaction between host and guest Qi that produces the specific disease patterns of each year and season. When the guest Qi harmonizes with the host Qi the year is balanced; when they clash or fail to follow the proper order the year is characterized by specific climatic and epidemic pathologies. The system was used historically for epidemic prediction — the prediction of which diseases would predominate in a given year based on the Wu Yun Liu Qi analysis — and modern researchers have investigated correlations between Wu Yun Liu Qi predictions and actual epidemic patterns with suggestive but not yet conclusive results.

## Five Circuits

- Wood circuit years governed by Jia and Yi Heavenly Stems: Wind predominant, Liver-Gallbladder vulnerable.
- Fire circuit years governed by Bing and Ding: Heat predominant, Heart-Small Intestine vulnerable.
- Earth circuit years governed by Wu and Ji: Dampness predominant, Spleen-Stomach vulnerable.
- Metal circuit years governed by Geng and Xin: Dryness predominant, Lung-Large Intestine vulnerable.
- Water circuit years governed by Ren and Gui: Cold predominant, Kidney-Bladder vulnerable.

## Six Qi Steps

- Step 1 Jue Yin Wind-Wood: Wind predominant — Liver diseases, respiratory conditions.
- Step 2 Shao Yin Sovereign Fire: Warm conditions — Heart diseases, febrile conditions.
- Step 3 Shao Yang Ministerial Fire: Hot conditions — Triple Burner diseases, summer heat.
- Step 4 Tai Yin Damp-Earth: Dampness predominant — Spleen diseases, digestive conditions.
- Step 5 Yang Ming Dry-Metal: Dryness predominant — Lung diseases, skin conditions.
- Step 6 Tai Yang Cold-Water: Cold predominant — Kidney diseases, cold-type conditions.

## Epidemic Prediction

- Wu Yun Liu Qi analysis predicts which organ systems are vulnerable in each year and season.
- Historical use for epidemic prediction corresponds to modern syndromic surveillance systems.
- The 60-year cycle allows retrospective correlation with historical epidemic records.
- Modern research investigating Wu Yun Liu Qi correlations with actual epidemic data is ongoing.
