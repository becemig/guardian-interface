# Circadian Biology

## Abstract

Circadian rhythms are approximately 24-hour biological cycles generated by a self-sustaining molecular clock operating in virtually every cell of the body. The suprachiasmatic nucleus SCN of the hypothalamus functions as the master circadian clock entraining peripheral clocks throughout the body through neural, hormonal, and behavioral signals. The molecular clock mechanism involves transcription-translation feedback loops of CLOCK, BMAL1, PER, and CRY genes producing self-sustaining approximately 24-hour oscillations. Light through retinal photoreceptors is the primary zeitgeber entraining the SCN to the environmental light-dark cycle. Cortisol, melatonin, core body temperature, and autonomic tone all follow robust circadian rhythms coordinated by the SCN. The TCM organ clock assigns each of the twelve primary meridians a two-hour period of peak Qi activity corresponding to the circadian optimization of each organ system's function.

## SCN Master Clock

- The SCN contains approximately 20,000 neurons with intrinsic circadian oscillation synchronized by light.
- SCN neurons maintain synchrony through gap junctions and neuropeptide signaling producing coherent rhythms.
- The SCN coordinates peripheral clocks through cortisol pulses, autonomic neural signals, and body temperature cycles.
- SCN lesions abolish circadian rhythms demonstrating its master clock role in circadian organization.

## Molecular Clock Mechanism

- CLOCK and BMAL1 proteins form a dimer activating transcription of PER and CRY genes.
- PER and CRY proteins accumulate, dimerize, and translocate to the nucleus inhibiting CLOCK-BMAL1 activity.
- This negative feedback loop produces approximately 24-hour oscillations in gene expression.
- Peripheral clocks in liver, heart, muscle, and other tissues run the same molecular loop entrained by the SCN.

## Circadian Health

- Social jetlag — misalignment between social schedule and chronotype — produces metabolic and cardiovascular risk.
- Time-restricted eating aligned with circadian biology improves metabolic health through peripheral clock optimization.
- Shift work disrupting circadian alignment produces increased cancer, cardiovascular disease, and metabolic syndrome risk.
- Morning light exposure and consistent sleep-wake timing are the most powerful circadian health interventions.
