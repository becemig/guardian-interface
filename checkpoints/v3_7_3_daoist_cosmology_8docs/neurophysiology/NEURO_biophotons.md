# Biophotons

## Abstract

Biophotons — ultra-weak photon emissions from living cells — represent one of the most intriguing frontiers in biophysics, with implications for understanding cell communication, neural signaling, and potentially consciousness itself. Fritz-Albert Popp's systematic research established that all living cells emit a continuous stream of ultra-weak light — far weaker than the threshold of human visual detection but measurable by sensitive photomultiplier equipment — that originates primarily from oxidative metabolic processes and DNA-associated photon emission. The coherence properties of biophoton emission are what distinguish them from random thermal radiation — Popp demonstrated that biophotons show quantum coherence characteristics suggesting they may function as a coherent light field analogous to a biological laser system. Neural biophoton signaling research demonstrated by Tang and Dai using light-sensitive proteins showed that neurons emit and absorb biophotons during activity — raising the possibility that biophoton propagation along neural processes contributes to information processing beyond classical electrochemical signaling. DNA is a primary biophoton source and absorber — DNA excited states relax through biophoton emission and the coherence of this emission may contribute to the coordination of gene expression across cells. Oxidative stress increases biophoton emission — diseased, stressed, and cancerous tissue shows higher and less coherent biophoton emission while healthy tissue shows lower and more coherent emission. The meridian system has been proposed as a biophoton waveguide network — tissue along meridian pathways shows different optical properties from surrounding tissue, potentially conducting biophotons preferentially. Shen as light in the Taoist inner landscape tradition — the luminous awareness of the Niwan Palace, the inner light of cultivation — may correspond to the coherent biophoton field of the brain and pineal region.

## Biophoton Properties

- Ultra-weak photon emission at 10-1000 photons per second per square centimeter from biological tissue.
- Emission spectrum spans UV to near-infrared corresponding to oxidative metabolic processes.
- Coherence properties distinguish biophotons from random thermal emission — biological laser analogy.
- Higher organisms show lower, more coherent emission — less metabolic noise, more ordered signaling.

## Neural Biophotons

- Neurons emit biophotons during electrical activity — stimulated neurons show increased photon emission.
- Neural processes may conduct biophotons as waveguides providing a photonic signaling channel.
- The speed of light signaling could enable neural coordination faster than electrochemical transmission alone.
- Anesthetic gases reduce biophoton emission corresponding to their suppression of consciousness.

## DNA Coherence

- DNA is a primary biophoton source — excited base pairs relax through coherent photon emission.
- The coherence of DNA biophoton emission may coordinate gene expression across cells without chemical signaling.
- Cancer cells show disrupted biophoton coherence corresponding to the loss of organized biological information.
- Healing practices that restore coherence may work through biophoton field normalization.
