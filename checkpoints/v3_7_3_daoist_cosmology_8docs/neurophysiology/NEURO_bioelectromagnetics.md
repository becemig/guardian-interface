# Bioelectromagnetics

## Abstract

Robert Becker's pioneering research established that the body generates and uses endogenous direct current DC electric fields as a fundamental signaling system — distinct from and complementary to the classical neural action potential system — that governs tissue regeneration, wound healing, morphogenesis, and the body's orientation in space. The perineural DC current system runs along peripheral nerves and the spinal cord, generating the steady-state electric fields that Becker proposed as the physical substrate of the acupuncture meridian system. Acupuncture points and meridian pathways show significantly lower electrical resistance than surrounding tissue — a finding replicated across multiple independent research groups — corresponding to preferential current flow along these channels. Endogenous electric fields at wound sites direct cell migration through electrotaxis — cells migrate toward the cathode in response to electric field gradients, explaining the orderly sequential healing of wounds. Bioelectric morphogenesis — the role of endogenous electric fields in guiding embryonic development — is emerging as a fundamental developmental biology principle. Michael Levin's research demonstrated that bioelectric gradients establish the body plan during development and can be manipulated to regenerate missing structures. Galvanotaxis is the directed migration of cells in electric fields — white blood cells, epithelial cells, and fibroblasts all show galvanotactic responses corresponding to the organizing role of endogenous electric fields in tissue maintenance and repair. Qi as bioelectric field proposes that what TCM describes as Qi circulation through meridians corresponds to the DC current flow through the perineural system and the tissue electrical gradients of the fascial matrix.

## DC Body Current System

- The perineural DC current system runs along peripheral nerves generating steady-state electric fields.
- Becker measured consistent DC potentials along meridian pathways with acupuncture points as high-conductance nodes.
- The DC system operates independently of neural impulse transmission — it is a separate signaling layer.
- Anesthesia and sleep alter DC potentials corresponding to the altered Qi state of unconsciousness.

## Wound Healing Currents

- Skin battery wound currents drive the directed migration of healing cells to the wound site.
- Disruption of wound electric fields impairs healing — restoration accelerates it.
- Exogenous electric field application accelerates wound healing through enhanced electrotaxis.
- This mechanism explains acupuncture's wound-healing and tissue-repair effects through local bioelectric stimulation.

## Meridian Electrical Properties

- Acupuncture points show 10-100 fold lower electrical resistance than surrounding skin.
- Meridian pathways show continuous low-resistance channels along their classical routes.
- The acupuncture point low-resistance property is reproducible and correlates with clinical point location.
- This electrical mapping of meridians provides physical evidence for the channel system.
