# Siberian Shamanism

## Abstract

Siberian shamanism — the indigenous healing traditions of the peoples of Siberia, Central Asia, and the circumpolar north — is considered by scholars the original and archetypal form of shamanism from which shamanic practices spread across the globe during the Paleolithic era. Mircea Eliade's foundational scholarly work identified the universal structural features of shamanism across cultures: the shaman as specialist in altered states of consciousness, the three-world cosmology of upper world, middle world, and lower world connected by the cosmic axis or world tree, the drum as the primary technology for inducing shamanic trance, soul retrieval as the primary healing intervention, and power animals as spirit helpers in the shamanic journey. The shamanic drum — typically a single-headed frame drum beaten at a steady rhythm of approximately 4-7 beats per second — induces theta brainwave states through auditory driving, producing the altered state of consciousness in which the shaman perceives the spirit world and performs healing work. Michael Harner's core shamanism identified the universal cross-cultural shamanic techniques underlying the culturally specific forms, making shamanic practice accessible to non-indigenous practitioners through a stripped form preserving the functional technology without appropriating specific cultural content. Soul retrieval is the primary shamanic healing intervention — the shaman journeys to the spirit world to locate and return soul parts lost through trauma, shock, or negotiation with spirit beings, restoring the wholeness of the client's soul and resolving the symptoms of soul loss including depression, dissociation, immune deficiency, and chronic illness. Power animals are spirit helpers in animal form that accompany and assist the shaman in the spirit world — each power animal has specific qualities and capabilities that complement the shaman's own gifts. The cosmic axis — the world tree or world mountain connecting the three worlds — corresponds to the TCM Governing Vessel as the central axis of the body connecting the constitutional root below to the crown above.

## Three Worlds

- Upper world: celestial realm of sky spirits, ancestors, and cosmic intelligences — accessed by ascending.
- Middle world: the spirit layer of the ordinary physical world — journeyed for lost objects and local spirits.
- Lower world: the underground realm of power animals and earth spirits — accessed by descending.
- The shaman navigates all three worlds through the cosmic axis while maintaining middle world awareness.

## Drum Journey

- Steady drumbeat at 4-7 Hz induces theta brainwave state through auditory driving.
- The theta state corresponds to the hypnagogic state between waking and sleeping — the shamanic trance.
- The shaman maintains dual awareness — present in both ordinary and non-ordinary reality simultaneously.
- This corresponds to the TCM cultivation state of Yi remaining present while Shen extends into subtle perception.

## Soul Retrieval

- Soul loss occurs through trauma, shock, surgery, grief, or loss of vital relationships.
- Symptoms of soul loss: depression, dissociation, immune deficiency, chronic illness, feeling incomplete.
- The shaman retrieves the lost soul part from the spirit world and returns it through the breath.
- This corresponds to the TCM understanding that severe trauma can scatter the Shen requiring reconsolidation.
