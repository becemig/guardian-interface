# Biodanza

## Abstract

Biodanza is a system of human integration developed by Chilean psychologist Rolando Toro that uses music, movement, and group encounter to stimulate vivencia — the integration of felt bodily experience. Toro identified five lines of vivencia corresponding to five fundamental human capacities: vitality, sexuality, creativity, affectivity, and transcendence. Each Biodanza session uses specifically selected music to evoke particular qualities of movement and feeling, creating opportunities for genuine felt experience rather than performance. The biocentric principle — life as the supreme value — grounds Biodanza in biological vitality and the preservation of the conditions for flourishing. Research documents immune enhancement, reduced cortisol, improved affect, and social connection outcomes from regular Biodanza practice. Kidney Jing as the constitutional vitality underlying all five lines of vivencia corresponds to the biocentric principle of Biodanza as the primary reservoir of life expression.

## Vivencia

- Vivencia is the lived felt bodily experience that integrates rather than the verbal description or analysis of experience.
- It is the direct somatic knowing arising in the body during movement with music in the group field.
- Toro distinguished vivencia from the conceptual overlay that typically distances people from direct experience.
- Biodanza cultivates the capacity for vivencia as the primary vehicle for integration and growth.

## Five Lines

- Vitality corresponds to the primal life force, vigor, and the joy of physical existence and movement.
- Sexuality corresponds to the erotic life force — pleasure, desire, sensuality, and embodied aliveness.
- Creativity corresponds to the expressive drive toward novelty, play, and aesthetic creation.
- Affectivity corresponds to the capacity for love, tenderness, empathy, and genuine human connection.
- Transcendence corresponds to the experience of unity, ecstasy, and dissolution of ordinary self-boundaries.

## Biocentric Principle

- The biocentric principle places life itself as the supreme value and reference point for all human activity.
- This corresponds to the Taoist principle of following the natural vitality of life as the primary guide.
- Biodanza practice restores contact with the biocentric ground when culture and psychology have alienated it.
- Group encounter in Biodanza creates a field of shared vitality that amplifies individual biocentric reconnection.
