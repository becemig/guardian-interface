# Authentic Movement

## Abstract

Authentic Movement is a somatic practice developed by Mary Starks Whitehouse and elaborated by Janet Adler that cultivates the capacity to move from inner impulse while being witnessed by a compassionate other. The mover closes their eyes and waits for movement to arise from inner sensation rather than imposing movement from will or performance. The witness holds compassionate non-judgmental attention on the mover, tracking their own inner responses as data. After the movement period both mover and witness share their experiences using precise first-person language. The inner witness — the mover's own capacity for self-observation — is cultivated through the practice enabling increasingly refined self-knowledge through embodied experience. Authentic Movement corresponds to the Taoist practice of following natural impulse and the TCM cultivation of listening to the body's inherent intelligence.

## Mover Practice

- The mover closes eyes and allows movement to arise from inner impulse, sensation, and image.
- The mover follows the body's intelligence rather than imposing conscious intent or choreography.
- Authentic movement includes stillness, micro-movement, and full expression as equally valid responses.
- Over time the mover develops the capacity to be moved — to follow impulse with increasing trust and precision.

## Witness Practice

- The witness holds sustained compassionate non-judgmental attention on the moving person.
- The witness tracks their own inner responses — images, sensations, emotions — as data about the relational field.
- Witnessing cultivates the capacity for loving presence that is the foundation of therapeutic relationship.
- The witness shares their experience using first-person language distinguishing what they observed from their responses.

## Inner Witness

- The inner witness is the mover's internalized capacity for self-observation that develops through the practice.
- It is the part of the mover that can observe experience without judgment, identification, or suppression.
- The inner witness corresponds to the meta-cognitive capacity cultivated through mindfulness meditation.
- A developed inner witness enables the practitioner to have and observe experience simultaneously.

## TCM Correspondence

- Following inner impulse in Authentic Movement corresponds to the Taoist principle of Wu Wei and Ziran.
- The mover-witness dyad corresponds to the Heart Shen capacity for both full engagement and clear witnessing.
- Liver Hun as the projective imagination corresponds to the imagery and gesture arising in authentic movement.
- The practice cultivating embodied self-knowledge corresponds to the TCM internal cultivation tradition.
