# Chinese Astrology and Health

## Abstract

Chinese astrology — the system of cosmological timing and constitutional typing based on the Heavenly Stems Tian Gan and Earthly Branches Di Zhi — is the calendrical and cosmological foundation that underlies both the Wu Yun Liu Qi medical cosmology and the Bazi four pillars destiny analysis system. The ten Heavenly Stems correspond to the five elements in their Yang and Yin expressions: Jia Yang Wood, Yi Yin Wood, Bing Yang Fire, Ding Yin Fire, Wu Yang Earth, Ji Yin Earth, Geng Yang Metal, Xin Yin Metal, Ren Yang Water, Gui Yin Water. The twelve Earthly Branches correspond to the twelve animal signs of the Chinese zodiac — Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig — each associated with specific two-hour periods of the day, months of the year, and years in the twelve-year cycle. The combination of ten Heavenly Stems and twelve Earthly Branches generates the 60-year sexagenary cycle that governs Chinese cosmological time across all scales from hours and days to years and decades. The Bazi four pillars system uses the Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch of the year, month, day, and hour of birth to construct a constitutional chart that reveals the five element balance of the individual constitution, the constitutional strengths and vulnerabilities, the timing of significant life transitions, and the environmental and relational factors that best support the individual's health and flourishing. Constitutional astrology in the Chinese medical context uses the birth chart to identify the predominant constitutional element type — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, or Water predominance — that corresponds to specific organ vulnerabilities, emotional tendencies, dietary needs, and optimal lifestyle practices. The 12-year animal cycle influences constitutional health through the energetic quality of the birth year — a person born in a Wood year has Wood Qi stamped into their constitution, predisposing them to Liver-Gallbladder strengths and vulnerabilities.

## Heavenly Stems

- Yang Wood Jia and Yin Wood Yi: Liver-Gallbladder constitution, creative, vision, spring energy.
- Yang Fire Bing and Yin Fire Ding: Heart-Small Intestine constitution, expressive, joy, summer energy.
- Yang Earth Wu and Yin Earth Ji: Spleen-Stomach constitution, nurturing, stable, late summer energy.
- Yang Metal Geng and Yin Metal Xin: Lung-Large Intestine constitution, precise, grief, autumn energy.
- Yang Water Ren and Yin Water Gui: Kidney-Bladder constitution, deep, fear, winter energy.

## Bazi Constitutional Analysis

- Four pillars: year stem-branch, month stem-branch, day stem-branch, hour stem-branch.
- The day stem represents the self — the core constitutional element.
- The balance or imbalance of the five elements across the four pillars reveals constitutional strengths and vulnerabilities.
- Ten-year luck pillars map the major constitutional cycles through the lifespan.

## Animal Signs and Health

- Rat Water: Kidney constitution, endurance, adaptability, cold vulnerability.
- Dragon Earth-Yang: mixed constitution, vitality, digestive strength, dampness tendency.
- Tiger Wood-Yang: Liver constitution, courage, tendon health, Wind vulnerability.
- Each animal sign carries specific five element constitutional implications for health assessment.

## TCM Correspondence

- Heavenly Stems five element correspondences directly map onto TCM organ constitutional types.
- Bazi constitutional analysis corresponds to TCM constitutional assessment identifying the predominant organ pattern.
- Cosmological health cycles correspond to the Wu Yun Liu Qi annual vulnerability predictions.
- Ming Li destiny analysis corresponds to the TCM understanding that constitutional nature is the foundation of all health assessment.
