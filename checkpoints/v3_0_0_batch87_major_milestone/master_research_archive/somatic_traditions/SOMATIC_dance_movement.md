# Dance Movement Therapy Advanced

## Abstract

Dance Movement Therapy DMT advanced practice integrates Laban Movement Analysis LMA as its primary observational and clinical framework — a comprehensive system for describing, analyzing, and interpreting human movement developed by Rudolf Laban that provides the vocabulary for precise movement observation and therapeutic intervention. The kinesphere is the personal movement space surrounding the body — the sphere of reachable space defined by the outstretched limbs — and its habitual use patterns reveal character structure and psychological states. LMA effort qualities describe the dynamic qualities of movement across four motion factors: weight ranging from strong to light, space ranging from direct to indirect, time ranging from sudden to sustained, and flow ranging from bound to free. The combination of effort qualities produces the characteristic movement signature of an individual and their shifts in emotional and psychological state. In group DMT the movement interactions between members become the primary therapeutic medium — movement mirroring, movement dialogue, and movement synchrony develop attunement, empathy, and relational capacity. The body-level processes of DMT access implicit and procedural memory that may be inaccessible through verbal approaches — this is particularly valuable in trauma treatment where traumatic memory is encoded somatically. DMT research demonstrates effects on depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, autism, dementia, and chronic pain through movement-based intervention. Liver-Hun creative expression in TCM — the Liver's governing of the sinews and the Hun soul's creative projective capacity that finds form through movement and art — corresponds to the DMT therapeutic process of accessing and expressing the Hun's creative intelligence through movement.

## Laban Movement Analysis

- Effort qualities describe the dynamic how of movement across weight, space, time, and flow dimensions.
- Strong weight effort conveys assertion and impact while light weight conveys sensitivity and delicacy.
- Direct space effort focuses on a clear spatial pathway while indirect explores the surrounding space.
- Sudden time effort conveys urgency while sustained time conveys patience and deliberate process.

## Kinesphere

- The kinesphere is the personal movement bubble surrounding the body within reach of the limbs.
- Its habitual use reveals psychological patterns — small kinesphere use with depression, large with mania.
- Kinesphere expansion and contraction reflect shifts in emotional openness and self-protective closure.
- Working with kinesphere boundaries therapeutically develops embodied sense of personal space and agency.

## Group Process

- Movement mirroring reflects back the mover's movement quality building attunement and felt recognition.
- Movement dialogue develops turn-taking, responsiveness, and relational embodied communication.
- Group movement synchrony produces social bonding effects through entrainment and shared rhythm.
- The group movement field carries collective therapeutic process beyond what individual work can access.
