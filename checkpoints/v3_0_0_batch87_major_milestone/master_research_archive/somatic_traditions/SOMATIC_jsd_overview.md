# Jin Shin Do Foundation

## Abstract

Jin Shin Do Bodymind Acupressure was developed by Iona Marsaa Teeguarden in the 1970s as a synthesis of classical Japanese acupressure Jin Shin Jyutsu, TCM theory, Taoist philosophy, Wilhelm Reich's segmental armoring theory, and Western body-centered psychotherapy. The name means The Way of the Compassionate Spirit — reflecting the therapeutic philosophy that combines deep physical work with verbal body focusing to address the inseparable body-mind dimension of held tension, trauma, and emotional armoring. Jin Shin Do's unique contribution to the somatic traditions field is the systematic integration of psychological body-centered awareness with the precision point work of the acupressure tradition — creating a modality where physical release and psychological insight occur simultaneously through the tsubo release process. Strange flows is the Jin Shin Do term for the eight extraordinary vessels — the constitutional channels that carry the deepest level of Jing essence and govern the fundamental body organization established before birth. JSD theory holds that the strange flows are the primary level at which deep constitutional patterns are held in the body, and that working the master-coupled point pairs of the extraordinary vessels produces the deepest and most transformative releases. Segmental armoring in JSD synthesizes Wilhelm Reich's concept of body armoring organized in horizontal segments across the body — eye, oral, cervical, thoracic, diaphragmatic, abdominal, and pelvic segments — with the TCM meridian and organ network framework. Each segment corresponds to specific organ networks and emotional holding patterns that can be systematically released through point work combined with verbal body focusing. The verbal focusing component of JSD distinguishes it from purely physical acupressure — the therapist guides the client to attend to sensations, images, and emotions arising during point release, allowing psychological processing to accompany the physical work.

## Strange Flows in JSD

- Strange flows govern the constitutional body organization and hold the deepest pre-natal patterns.
- Master-coupled point pairs open each strange flow: SP-4 and PC-6 for Chong Mai, LU-7 and KI-6 for Ren Mai.
- Working strange flows produces constitutional releases affecting the fundamental body-mind organization.
- JSD strange flow work corresponds to the TCM eight extraordinary vessel treatment for constitutional conditions.

## Segmental Armoring

- Reich identified seven horizontal body segments where emotional holding concentrates in muscular armor.
- JSD maps each segment to specific organ networks and their associated emotional holding patterns.
- Thoracic segment armoring corresponds to Heart and Lung patterns of grief, sadness, and closed heart.
- Diaphragmatic segment armoring corresponds to Liver patterns of frustration, anger, and blocked expression.

## Verbal Body Focusing

- The JSD practitioner invites the client to attend to sensations, images, and emotions during point work.
- Physical release through tsubo pressure creates an opening for psychological awareness and processing.
- The combination of physical and verbal work produces more complete resolution than either alone.
- This synthesis corresponds to the TCM understanding that Shen and Jing are inseparable dimensions of the same reality.

## TCM Correspondence

- Strange flows as constitutional extraordinary vessels corresponds to the TCM eight extraordinary vessel system.
- Segmental armoring corresponds to the TCM pattern of Qi and Blood stagnation accumulating in specific organ network territories.
- Verbal body focusing corresponds to the TCM cultivation of Yi intention and Shen awareness during treatment.
- Bodymind acupressure unity corresponds to the TCM non-dual understanding of body and mind as expressions of the same Qi.
