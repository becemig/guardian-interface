# I Ching Sixty-Four Hexagrams

## Abstract

The sixty-four hexagrams of the I Ching constitute a complete symbolic map of all possible situations of change — every human experience, every natural process, every organizational dynamic can be found somewhere in the sixty-four. The King Wen sequence — the traditional ordering of the hexagrams in thirty-two complementary pairs — reveals the philosophical narrative of the I Ching as a whole: beginning with Heaven and Earth, proceeding through creation, relationship, conflict, community, and culminating in the themes of completion and incompletion in hexagrams 63 and 64. The thirty-two pairs are organized so that each hexagram is paired with its complement — either the hexagram produced by turning it upside down or, when that produces the same hexagram, the hexagram produced by reversing all lines. This pairing reveals the complementary opposite of every situation — the paired hexagram shows what lies on the other side of the present experience. Body correspondence maps specific hexagrams to body regions, organ networks, and constitutional types — providing a somatic reading system where the hexagram reflects both the psychological situation and its physical constitutional correlate. Clinical application of the hexagram system in TCM-informed practice uses the received hexagram as an additional diagnostic layer illuminating the constitutional pattern underlying the presenting symptoms. The constitutional hexagram — derived from birth date and time — provides a Bazi-adjacent system for reading the fundamental constitutional pattern of an individual.

## King Wen Sequence

- The traditional sequence begins with Qian Heaven and Kun Earth — the primary creative and receptive poles.
- Hexagrams 3-4 Difficulty at Beginning and Youthful Folly follow — the first manifestations of creation.
- The sequence proceeds through the major themes of human experience in philosophical progression.
- Hexagrams 63-64 After Completion and Before Completion complete the cycle returning to the threshold of new creation.

## Hexagram Pairs

- Each of the thirty-two pairs reveals the complementary dynamic of a situation and its opposite.
- Hexagram 1 Qian Heaven pairs with Hexagram 2 Kun Earth — creative force pairs with receptive yielding.
- Hexagram 11 Peace pairs with Hexagram 12 Standstill — harmonious flow pairs with obstruction.
- Reading a hexagram alongside its pair reveals the full polarity of the present situation.

## Body Correspondence

- Heaven Qian hexagrams correspond to the head, brain, and Yang constitutional types.
- Earth Kun hexagrams correspond to the abdomen, spleen-stomach, and Yin constitutional types.
- Water Kan hexagrams correspond to the kidneys, ears, and constitutional fear-depth patterns.
- Fire Li hexagrams correspond to the heart, eyes, and constitutional clarity-agitation patterns.

## TCM Correspondence

- Sixty-four hexagrams as complete map of change corresponds to the comprehensive TCM pattern recognition system.
- Constitutional hexagram derived from birth corresponds to the Bazi constitutional analysis of the Four Pillars.
- Hexagram pairs as complementary opposites corresponds to the TCM Yin-Yang organ network pairing system.
- Clinical hexagram application corresponds to the integration of cosmological diagnosis with somatic treatment.
