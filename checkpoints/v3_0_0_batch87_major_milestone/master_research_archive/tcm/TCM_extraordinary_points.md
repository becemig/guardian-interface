# Extraordinary Acupoints

## Abstract

Extraordinary acupoints are acupuncture locations that do not belong to the fourteen primary meridians but have specific clinical functions documented in the classical literature. The extra points supplement the primary meridian system addressing specific conditions not fully covered by the regular points. Ah Shi points are tender or reactive points that emerge at sites of local pathology and serve as treatment points regardless of their meridian location. The Huatuo Jiaji points flanking the spine provide segmental access to spinal nerve roots and their corresponding organ systems. Scalp acupuncture uses points on the scalp corresponding to cortical motor and sensory projections for neurological rehabilitation.

## Key Extraordinary Points

- Yintang EX-HN3 between the eyebrows calms the Shen, clears the mind, and treats insomnia and anxiety.
- Taiyang EX-HN5 at the temples clears head heat, relieves headache, and benefits the eyes.
- Huatuo Jiaji EX-B2 bilateral points 0.5 cun lateral to each spinous process T1 to L5 treat spinal and organ conditions.
- Shiqizhui EX-B8 below L5 spinous process treats lumbar pain and dysmenorrhea.
- Yaoyan EX-B7 lateral lumbar depression treats kidney deficiency and lumbar weakness.
- Zigong EX-CA1 lateral to CV-3 treats gynecological conditions and uterine prolapse.

## Ah Shi Points

- Ah Shi literally means oh yes that is it describing the patient exclamation when the tender point is found.
- Any tender reactive or indurated point becomes an Ah Shi treatment point regardless of meridian location.
- Trigger points of Western myofascial medicine correspond largely to Ah Shi point locations.
- Needling Ah Shi points directly addresses local Qi and Blood stagnation at the site of pathology.

## Huatuo Jiaji Points

- The 34 Jiaji points flank each vertebral level from C1 to L5 providing segmental spinal access.
- Each Jiaji level corresponds to the spinal nerve root and its innervated organ through segmental reflex pathways.
- T3-T5 Jiaji points treat heart and lung conditions through thoracic sympathetic segmental pathways.
- L2-L4 Jiaji points treat kidney, bladder, and reproductive conditions through lumbar segmental pathways.

## Scalp Acupuncture

- Scalp acupuncture zones correspond to cortical motor and sensory projection areas.
- Motor area needling treats contralateral paralysis and motor deficits in stroke and neurological disease.
- Sensory area needling treats contralateral pain and sensory deficits.
- Scalp acupuncture produces rapid neurological effects through direct cortical stimulation via scalp afferents.
