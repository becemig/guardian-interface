# Positive Psychology Deep Dive

## Abstract

Positive psychology — founded by Martin Seligman as a scientific counterweight to psychology's historical focus on pathology — systematically investigates the conditions and processes that contribute to human flourishing, optimal functioning, and the good life. Seligman's PERMA model identifies five pillars of well-being: Positive emotions, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, and Accomplishment — proposing that each contributes independently to flourishing and that sustainable well-being requires cultivation across all five dimensions rather than maximizing any single one. The VIA Classification of Character Strengths developed by Christopher Peterson and Seligman identifies 24 universal character strengths organized under six virtues — wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance, and transcendence — that have been found across cultures and historical periods, providing a positive taxonomy of human excellence complementing the DSM's taxonomy of pathology. Post-traumatic growth PTG — the phenomenon of positive psychological change emerging from the struggle with highly challenging life circumstances — was systematically documented by Richard Tedeschi and Lawrence Calhoun, who identified five domains of growth: greater appreciation for life, warmer relationships, recognition of personal strength, new possibilities, and spiritual or existential change. PTG does not mean trauma was beneficial but that the person rebuilt a more complex and comprehensive understanding of life through confronting the shattering of their prior assumptive world. Eudaimonia — the Aristotelian concept of flourishing through living in accordance with one's highest capacities and virtues — is distinguished from hedonia — the pursuit of pleasure and avoidance of pain — as the deeper foundation of sustainable well-being. Five Shen flourishing maps PERMA onto the TCM five spirit system: Positive emotions correspond to Heart Shen joy and presence, Engagement corresponds to Liver Hun purposeful action, Relationships correspond to Pericardium appropriate intimacy, Meaning corresponds to Kidney Zhi deep purpose, and Accomplishment corresponds to Spleen Yi effective thinking and action.

## PERMA Framework

- Positive emotions: broaden-and-build theory — positive emotions expand attention and build psychological resources.
- Engagement: flow states of complete absorption in challenging activity — the Liver Hun expression state.
- Relationships: high-quality connections with others as a fundamental human need — Heart Shen social field.
- Meaning: belonging to and serving something larger than oneself — Kidney Zhi deep purpose.
- Accomplishment: pursuing goals for their own sake regardless of whether they produce pleasure or meaning.

## VIA Character Strengths

- Wisdom virtues: creativity, curiosity, judgment, love of learning, perspective — Liver Hun vision.
- Courage virtues: bravery, perseverance, honesty, zest — Kidney Zhi willpower.
- Humanity virtues: love, kindness, social intelligence — Heart Shen and Pericardium connection.
- Justice virtues: teamwork, fairness, leadership — Spleen Yi communal thinking.
- Transcendence virtues: gratitude, hope, humor, awe, spirituality — Lung Po reverence.

## Post-Traumatic Growth

- PTG requires the shattering of the prior assumptive world — mild stressors do not produce growth.
- The rebuilding of a more complex worldview integrating the traumatic experience produces the growth.
- Narrative processing — making meaning of the trauma — is essential for PTG to occur.
- This corresponds to the TCM understanding that the most profound Shen cultivation often follows the deepest challenges.
