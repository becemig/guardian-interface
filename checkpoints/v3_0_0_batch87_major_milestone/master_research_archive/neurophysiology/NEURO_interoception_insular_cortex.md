# Interoception and the Insular Cortex in Bodily Self-Awareness

## Abstract

Interoception is the sensing of the internal physiological state of the body. The insular cortex serves as the primary cortical region for interoceptive processing, integrating signals from visceral organs, cardiovascular system, respiratory tract, and musculoskeletal structures. Interoceptive accuracy predicts emotional regulation capacity, pain sensitivity, and the quality of embodied awareness during somatic practices.

## Physiological Mechanisms

### Interoceptive Pathways

- Interoceptive signals travel via lamina I spinothalamic tract and vagal afferents to the thalamus.
- From the thalamus signals project to the posterior insular cortex for primary interoceptive representation.
- The anterior insular cortex integrates these signals with prefrontal and limbic input to generate subjective bodily awareness.
- This anterior re-representation creates the felt sense of physiological state underlying emotion and self-awareness.

### Insular Cortex Architecture

- The posterior insula maps raw physiological signals from the body with somatotopic organization.
- The anterior insula generates meta-representations combining bodily state with cognitive and emotional context.
- Right anterior insula activation correlates with heartbeat detection accuracy and interoceptive precision.
- Insular thickness predicts individual differences in interoceptive awareness and emotion recognition.

### Predictive Interoception

- The brain generates continuous predictions about incoming interoceptive signals based on prior experience.
- Prediction errors between expected and actual bodily state drive updates to the internal body model.
- Chronic prediction error accumulation underlies conditions such as chronic pain, anxiety, and somatic symptom disorders.

## Relevance to Internal Movement Arts

- Tai Chi and Qigong practice systematically cultivate interoceptive attention through directed awareness of breath, weight, and internal movement.
- Slow deliberate movement generates high-resolution interoceptive signal with minimal prediction error, supporting accurate body mapping.
- HRV coherence during practice reflects stable interoceptive prediction aligned with actual cardiac rhythm.
- The cultivation of Yi or intention in internal arts may correspond to deliberate modulation of anterior insular predictive processing.
