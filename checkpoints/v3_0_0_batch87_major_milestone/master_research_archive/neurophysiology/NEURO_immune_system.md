# Immune System Physiology

## Abstract

The immune system is the body defense network comprising innate and adaptive branches that identify and eliminate pathogens, cancer cells, and damaged tissue. Psychoneuroimmunology documents the bidirectional communication between nervous, endocrine, and immune systems through shared receptor systems and signaling molecules. The vagal anti-inflammatory pathway through the cholinergic reflex provides a direct neural mechanism for autonomic regulation of immune function. Wei Qi in TCM governs the defensive surface immunity and corresponds to innate immune function at the body exterior. Practice-induced HRV enhancement directly improves vagal anti-inflammatory tone reducing systemic inflammation.

## Innate Immunity

- Pattern recognition receptors including Toll-like receptors detect conserved pathogen molecular patterns.
- Natural killer cells provide rapid cytotoxic response against virus-infected and cancer cells.
- Neutrophils and macrophages perform phagocytosis and release inflammatory cytokines at infection sites.
- The complement system provides rapid opsonization and membrane attack complex formation.

## Adaptive Immunity

- T lymphocytes provide cell-mediated immunity through cytotoxic and helper T cell subsets.
- B lymphocytes produce specific antibodies providing humoral immunity and immunological memory.
- Regulatory T cells suppress excessive immune activation preventing autoimmunity.
- Adaptive immunity develops immunological memory enabling faster stronger responses to repeat exposures.

## Vagal Anti-Inflammatory Pathway

- The vagus nerve provides direct neural regulation of immune function through the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway.
- Vagal efferents activate alpha-7 nicotinic receptors on macrophages suppressing TNF-alpha and cytokine release.
- HRV as a measure of vagal tone is inversely correlated with systemic inflammation markers including CRP and IL-6.
- Qigong and Tai Chi enhance vagal tone thereby reducing inflammatory cytokine levels through this pathway.

## TCM Correspondence

- Wei Qi defensive Qi circulates at the body surface governing resistance to external pathogenic invasion.
- The Lung governs Wei Qi distribution corresponding to upper respiratory mucosal immunity.
- The Spleen governs production of Qi and Blood providing the substrate for immune cell generation.
- Triple Burner governs systemic Qi and fluid distribution corresponding to lymphatic immune surveillance.
