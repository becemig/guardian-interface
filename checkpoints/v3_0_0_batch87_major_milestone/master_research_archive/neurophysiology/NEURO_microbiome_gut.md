# Microbiome and Gut Health

## Abstract

The gut microbiome comprises approximately 38 trillion microorganisms with metabolic capacity rivaling the liver. The enteric nervous system containing 500 million neurons communicates bidirectionally with the CNS through the vagus nerve. Short-chain fatty acids including butyrate serve as primary colonocyte energy substrates and influence brain function. The Spleen and Stomach govern digestion and transformation corresponding to the microbiome environment and metabolic function.

## Gut Microbiome

- Firmicutes and Bacteroidetes are dominant phyla with their ratio influencing metabolic phenotype.
- Diversity is the primary marker of gut health — reduced diversity correlates with disease risk.
- Diet is the primary modifiable determinant with fiber intake most influential on microbiome composition.
- Butyrate from fiber fermentation promotes intestinal barrier integrity through tight junction expression.

## Enteric Nervous System

- The ENS contains 500 million neurons governing motility, secretion, and immune function.
- Serotonin is predominantly produced in the gut regulating ENS function and gut-brain signaling.
- Vagal afferents carry ENS signals to the brain while efferents modulate ENS and gut function.

## TCM Correspondence

- Spleen governs transformation and transportation corresponding to digestive microbiome function.
- Spleen Qi deficiency with Dampness corresponds to dysbiosis with impaired fermentation and leaky gut.
- Large Intestine governs final transformation and elimination corresponding to colonic microbiome.
- Triple Burner governs systemic Qi distribution corresponding to microbiome metabolite signaling.
