# Autonomic Regulation and HRV Biofeedback

## Abstract

Heart rate variability biofeedback is a technique that trains resonance frequency breathing to maximize HRV amplitude and baroreflex gain. At the individual resonance frequency of approximately 0.1 Hz respiratory sinus arrhythmia and baroreflex oscillations synchronize producing maximum amplitude HRV and heart-brain coherence. Regular HRV biofeedback training produces lasting autonomic improvements including enhanced vagal tone, improved baroreflex sensitivity, and reduced sympathetic reactivity. Heart-brain coherence as described by the HeartMath Institute refers to the ordered rhythmic coupling between heart rhythm patterns and brain activity. Guardian Interface telemetry uses HRV as the primary continuous autonomic health indicator.

## Resonance Frequency Breathing

- Each individual has a unique resonance frequency typically between 4.5 and 7 breaths per minute.
- At resonance frequency RSA and baroreflex oscillations synchronize producing maximum HRV amplitude.
- Paced breathing at resonance frequency is the core technique of HRV biofeedback training.
- Regular practice at resonance frequency shifts the autonomic set point toward greater vagal dominance.

## Baroreflex Gain

- Baroreflex sensitivity BRS reflects the cardiovascular system responsiveness to blood pressure perturbations.
- Higher BRS indicates more robust and rapid autonomic regulation of blood pressure.
- HRV biofeedback training significantly increases BRS in healthy and clinical populations.
- Improved BRS reduces cardiovascular risk through more effective blood pressure regulation.

## Heart-Brain Coherence

- Heart-brain coherence refers to ordered coupling between heart rhythm and brain electrical activity.
- During coherence states the heart rhythm pattern entrains brain alpha rhythms through baroreceptor afferents.
- Coherence states are associated with improved cognitive performance, emotional stability, and intuitive access.
- Qigong and Tai Chi practice naturally induces coherence states through resonance frequency breathing.
