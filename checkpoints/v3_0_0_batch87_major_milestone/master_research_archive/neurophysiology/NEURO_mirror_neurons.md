# Mirror Neurons and Embodied Simulation

## Abstract

Mirror neurons are neurons in the premotor and parietal cortex that fire both when an individual performs an action and when they observe another individual performing the same action. Discovered by Giacomo Rizzolatti in macaque monkeys these neurons provide a direct neural mechanism for action understanding through motor resonance — understanding another's action by internally simulating it in one's own motor system. Vittorio Gallese proposed embodied simulation as the general principle by which we understand others' actions, emotions, and sensations through automatic unconscious simulation in our own sensorimotor systems. Mirror neuron dysfunction has been proposed as a mechanism in autism spectrum disorder affecting social understanding. Shen-to-Shen transmission in TCM — the direct resonant communication between practitioners and patients through presence and intention — corresponds to the mirror neuron system as the neural substrate for embodied interpersonal resonance.

## Mirror Neuron Discovery

- Rizzolatti discovered mirror neurons in macaque premotor area F5 firing during both action execution and observation.
- Human mirror neuron areas include premotor cortex, inferior frontal gyrus, and inferior parietal lobule.
- Mirror neurons provide a direct neural link between self and other through shared motor representations.
- This system enables rapid intuitive understanding of others' intentions from their motor behavior.

## Embodied Simulation

- Gallese proposed that understanding others requires simulation of their actions, emotions, and sensations in our own systems.
- Observing someone in pain activates the observer's pain matrix — the same regions activated by experienced pain.
- Observing facial expressions activates corresponding facial muscle representations in the observer.
- This automatic unconscious simulation provides the felt sense basis of empathy and social understanding.

## Social Brain

- The mirror neuron system integrates with the mentalizing system, limbic system, and autonomic nervous system.
- Skilled practitioners synchronize physiologically with clients — heart rate, respiration, and HRV entrain.
