# Psychedelic Integration

## Abstract

Psychedelic integration — the process of making meaning of, embodying, and incorporating the insights, experiences, and openings of a psychedelic session into everyday life — is increasingly recognized as essential for translating the temporary neuroplastic window of the psychedelic experience into lasting therapeutic change and personal growth. The psychedelic experience itself, however profound, does not automatically produce lasting benefit — the integration work done in the days, weeks, and months following the session determines whether the insights remain as interesting memories or become transformative changes in belief, behavior, relationship, and somatic organization. Narrative construction — making a coherent story of the psychedelic experience and its relationship to one's life history, current challenges, and future direction — is among the primary integration tasks. The psychedelic experience often presents material in symbolic, non-linear, or emotionally overwhelming forms that require active interpretive work to be made therapeutically useful. Somatic integration addresses the body-level changes produced by psychedelic sessions — the release of chronic muscular tension, the emergence of previously unfelt emotions, the altered sense of body boundaries, and the changed relationship to physical sensation that many participants report. Bodywork, yoga, qigong, and other somatic practices support the integration of these body-level changes by providing a container for continued somatic processing. Community support through integration circles — regular group meetings where participants share their experiences, receive witness from others who understand the territory, and support each other's integration — provides the relational dimension of integration that individual therapy cannot fully provide. Shen consolidation practices — the TCM framework for post-session integration — include the cultivation practices of qigong, meditation, and gentle movement that stabilize and deepen the Shen openings produced by the psychedelic session, preventing the gradual closure of the opened state through habitual patterns.

## Narrative Construction

- Making a coherent story of the experience connects it to life history, current challenges, and future direction.
- Journaling immediately after and in the days following the session captures material before it fades.
- Working with a skilled integration therapist helps translate symbolic and overwhelming content into usable insight.
- This corresponds to the TCM Heart Shen function of constructing the coherent narrative of one's life.

## Somatic Integration

- Bodywork, yoga, and qigong support integration of body-level changes produced by the session.
- Chronic muscular tension released during the session needs somatic practices to prevent re-establishment.
- Altered body boundary experiences integrate through practices that clarify and stabilize body awareness.
- This corresponds to the TCM post-treatment cultivation practices stabilizing the Qi changes produced by treatment.

## Integration Timeline

- First 24-48 hours: rest, gentle nature contact, journaling, minimal social demands.
- First week: begin gentle somatic practices, continue journaling, first integration session with therapist.
- First month: regular integration sessions, community support, behavioral changes aligned with insights.
- First year: ongoing integration as insights continue to unfold and deepen through lived experience.
