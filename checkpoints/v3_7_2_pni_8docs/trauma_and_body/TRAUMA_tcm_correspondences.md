# Trauma in TCM

## Abstract

Traditional Chinese Medicine does not use the term trauma but has a sophisticated framework for understanding shock, fright, grief, fear, and their lasting effects on organ systems, vital substances, and extraordinary vessel integrity. The seven emotions are recognized as pathogenic factors capable of damaging specific organ-meridian systems when experienced with sufficient intensity or chronicity. Acute traumatic shock primarily disrupts the Heart Shen and scatters Qi. Chronic fear depletes Kidney Jing and Yang. Unresolved grief damages the Lung and Po corporeal soul. Anger and chronic stress stagnate Liver Qi and cause Blood stasis. At the extraordinary vessel level, trauma disrupts the Du Mai spine-brain axis, disturbs the Chong Mai sea of blood and Shen residence, and collapses the Ren Mai yin nourishing container.

## Shock and Fright - Heart Shen Disruption

- Sudden fright: scatters Heart Qi - classic TCM description of acute shock response
- Heart Shen: governs consciousness, spirit, and integrated awareness
- Acute trauma: Shen cannot reside in Heart - becomes untethered, restless, dissociated
- Symptoms: palpitations, insomnia, anxiety, poor memory, dissociation, startle response
- Polyvagal parallel: Heart Shen disruption maps to loss of ventral vagal social engagement capacity
- Treatment: CV 17 Sea of Tranquility, HT 7 Spirit Gate, PC 6 Inner Gate, GV 20

## Kidney Jing Depletion from Chronic Fear

- Fear is the emotion of the Kidney water element
- Chronic unresolved fear: depletes Kidney Jing (constitutional essence) and Kidney Yang
- Zhi (will): Kidney spirit - chronic fear collapses Zhi - loss of will, motivation, direction
- Symptoms: low back weakness, bone pain, hearing loss, fatigue, sexual dysfunction, depression
- HPA parallel: Kidney Jing depletion maps to allostatic load and adrenal exhaustion
- Treatment: B 47 Sea of Vitality, GV 4 Gate of Life, CV 6 Hara, K 3, K 6

## Liver Qi Stagnation from Shock and Anger

- Liver governs smooth flow of Qi and emotion - trauma disrupts this flow
- Acute shock: sudden Qi disruption causes Liver Qi to stagnate and rebel
- Chronic anger or suppressed rage: Liver Qi stagnates over time causing heat
- Blood stasis: prolonged Qi stagnation eventually congeals Blood - fixed pain, dark complexion
- Symptoms: hypochondriac tension, irritability, depression alternating with anger, menstrual irregularity
- Sympathetic parallel: Liver Yang rising maps to sympathetic hyperactivation

## Lung and Po - Grief and Boundary Collapse

- Lung governs grief and the Po corporeal soul
- Po: the animating somatic instinct - boundary, physical presence, embodied contact
- Unresolved grief: depletes Lung Qi, collapses Wei Qi defensive boundary
- Trauma boundary collapse: loss of felt sense of self-boundary maps to Po dissolution
- Symptoms: chronic respiratory weakness, skin sensitivity, grief that cannot complete, dissociation
- Treatment: Lu 1 Letting Go, B 38, CV 17, LI 4, Lu 7

## Extraordinary Vessel Trauma Patterns

### Du Mai - Spine-Brain Axis Disruption
- Du Mai governs the spine and brain - the posterior yang axis of consciousness
- Trauma disrupts Du Mai flow: rigidity or collapse along the spinal column
- SI3 + B62 release pair: restores Du Mai and Yangqiao - posterior yang axis reset
- Spinal armoring: TCM spine rigidity maps to Reich cervical-thoracic-lumbar armor segments

### Chong Mai - Sea of Blood Disruption
- Chong Mai is the sea of blood and the residence of the Shen in deep essence
- Trauma disrupts Chong Mai: blood stasis, menstrual irregularity, Shen untethered
- Sp4 + P6 release pair: restores Chong Mai and Yinwei - interior yin nourishment

### Ren Mai - Yin Container Collapse
- Ren Mai is the anterior yin container governing nurturing and self-care
- Trauma collapses Ren Mai: inability to self-nurture, yin deficiency, front-body holding
- Lu7 + K6 release pair: restores Ren Mai and Yinqiao - yin nourishment and sleep
