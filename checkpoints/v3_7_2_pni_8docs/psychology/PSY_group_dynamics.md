# Group Dynamics

## Abstract

Group dynamics — the psychological and social processes that emerge when individuals come together in groups — reveals that groups are more than the sum of their individual members, generating emergent properties including collective intelligence, shared emotional states, group coherence, and the distinctive intersubjective field that shapes every participant's experience. Henri Tajfel and John Turner's social identity theory proposes that people derive significant aspects of their self-concept from their group memberships — the social identity — and that this social identity motivates in-group favoritism and out-group discrimination as mechanisms for maintaining positive group identity. Anita Woolley's research on collective intelligence demonstrated that groups have a general collective intelligence factor analogous to individual g that predicts group performance across diverse tasks — this collective intelligence factor is best predicted by the social sensitivity of group members, the equality of turn-taking in conversation, and the proportion of women in the group. Group coherence — the degree to which group members' physiological states become synchronized — has been documented through HRV synchronization studies showing that people in close proximity and social engagement develop coordinated autonomic rhythms. Mirror neuron systems provide the neurological substrate for the automatic resonance between nervous systems in social contexts — the felt sense of another person's emotional state that underlies empathy, social coordination, and the intersubjective field. Therapeutic groups harness these group dynamic principles for healing — the universality of shared experience, the social microcosm of the group, the interpersonal learning from feedback, and the cohesion that develops over time all contribute to therapeutic outcomes that exceed individual therapy for many conditions. Collective Shen field is the TCM framework for the shared energetic field that emerges in group practice — the enhanced Qi field generated by synchronized group qigong, group meditation, or group healing work.
