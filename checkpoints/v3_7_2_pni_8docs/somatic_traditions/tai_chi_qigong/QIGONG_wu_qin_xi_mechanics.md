# Neurovegetative Regulation via Wu Qin Xi Movement Profiles

## Abstract

This module details the biomechanical and physiological impacts of the Wu Qin Xi, or Five Animal Frolics, focusing on their capacity to modulate autonomic nervous system tone.

## Biomechanical Correlates

- Deer routines emphasize lateral spinal rotation and structural tension release.
- Crane routines emphasize thoracic expansion, unilateral balance, breath regulation, and heart rate variability.

## Intersection with Cognitive Topography

The somatic grounding required during complex kinematic sequences may support selective attention by reducing physiological hyperarousal and stabilizing breath, posture, and rhythm.
