# Interoception Deep Dive

## Abstract

Interoception — the sensing of the internal state of the body — is now recognized as the neurological foundation of emotional experience, self-awareness, and the sense of being a living body rather than merely inhabiting one. A.D. Craig's landmark research mapped the interoceptive pathway from peripheral C-fiber afferents through the lamina I spinothalamic tract to the posterior insula and then the anterior insula — establishing that the anterior insula is the primary cortical representation of the body's physiological state and the substrate of subjective feeling states. The lamina I spinothalamic tract carries information about temperature, pain, itch, metabolic state, visceral sensations, and the general condition of the body's tissues — this is not classical somatosensory information about external touch and pressure but a continuous broadcast of the body's internal homeostatic state. The anterior insula integrates this interoceptive information with information from memory, prediction, and context to produce the felt sense of the body's current state — what Craig calls the sentient self. Lisa Feldman Barrett's predictive processing model of emotion proposes that the brain is fundamentally a prediction machine that is constantly generating predictions about the body's internal state based on past experience — interoceptive inference — and that what we call emotions are the brain's best guesses about the causes of interoceptive signals. This model has profound implications: emotions are not read from the body but constructed by the brain as explanatory frameworks for interoceptive signals. Allostasis extends homeostasis — rather than merely reacting to internal disruptions, the allostatic brain proactively anticipates and prepares for upcoming metabolic demands, managing the body budget of energy, resources, and homeostatic stability. Poor allostatic regulation corresponds directly to TCM patterns of Qi and Blood deficiency — the body budget running into deficit.

## Lamina I Pathway

- C-fiber afferents in peripheral tissues sample temperature, pain, metabolic state, and visceral condition.
- Lamina I spinothalamic neurons carry this information to the posterior insula bypassing the thalamic relay.
- The posterior insula provides a moment-to-moment map of the body's physiological state.
- The anterior insula integrates this map with prediction and context to produce the felt sense.

## Predictive Processing and Emotion

- The brain predicts the causes of interoceptive signals based on past experience and current context.
- When predictions match signals well the result is a neutral undifferentiated background feeling.
- When predictions fail to match signals the brain updates its model — this update is experienced as emotion.
- Emotions are therefore interoceptive prediction errors not direct readings of the body's state.

## Allostasis and Body Budget

- Allostasis is the proactive management of the body budget — anticipating demands before they occur.
- Poor allostatic regulation produces chronic body budget deficit felt as fatigue, anxiety, and depression.
- Sleep, nutrition, social connection, and movement are the primary body budget inputs.
- TCM Qi deficiency patterns correspond directly to chronic body budget deficit from poor allostatic management.
