# Default Mode Network

## Abstract

The default mode network DMN is a set of brain regions that show greater activity during rest and mind-wandering than during externally directed task performance — the opposite pattern from most cortical regions. Marcus Raichle and colleagues identified the DMN through PET studies showing consistent deactivation of medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate cortex PCC, and lateral parietal cortex during cognitive tasks. Randy Buckner established the DMN as a functionally coherent network involved in self-referential processing, mental time travel, and social cognition. The core DMN nodes are: the medial prefrontal cortex mPFC involved in self-referential thought and social reasoning, the posterior cingulate cortex PCC involved in internally directed attention and autobiographical memory retrieval, the angular gyrus involved in semantic processing and theory of mind, and the hippocampal formation involved in episodic memory and future simulation. The DMN is active during mind-wandering — the spontaneous generation of self-relevant thoughts, memories, and future simulations that occupies much of waking mental life. DMN hyperactivity is associated with depression and rumination — excessive self-referential processing in the absence of present-moment engagement. Mindfulness meditation reliably reduces DMN activity corresponding to reduced mind-wandering and self-referential rumination. The DMN supports mental time travel — the capacity to mentally project oneself into past memories or future simulations — a uniquely human cognitive capacity. Hun projection field in TCM — the Liver Hun's capacity to project consciousness beyond the present moment into memory, imagination, planning, and dream — corresponds to the DMN as its primary neural substrate.

## Core DMN Nodes

- Medial prefrontal cortex processes self-relevant information and social reasoning about others minds.
- Posterior cingulate cortex supports internally directed attention and autobiographical memory access.
- Angular gyrus integrates semantic, episodic, and social information for self-narrative construction.
- Hippocampal formation provides episodic memory content and future simulation material to the DMN.

## Mind-Wandering and Self-Reference

- Mind-wandering is the spontaneous generation of self-relevant thoughts occupying 30-50% of waking life.
- DMN activity during mind-wandering generates the narrative self — the autobiographical story of who one is.
- Self-referential processing involves evaluating information in relation to one self using mPFC.
- The narrative self constructed by DMN activity is the primary substrate of personal identity.

## DMN and Depression

- Depression involves excessive DMN activity producing hyperactive rumination and self-critical thought.
- The DMN-salience network anticorrelation is reduced in depression producing failure to shift out of self-focus.
- Antidepressants, mindfulness, and ECT all reduce DMN hyperactivity corresponding to rumination reduction.
- Psychedelics produce dramatic DMN disorganization corresponding to the ego dissolution experience.
