# Neuroplasticity and Practice

## Abstract

Neuroplasticity is the capacity of the nervous system to change its structure and function in response to experience. Hebbian plasticity — neurons that fire together wire together — provides the synaptic mechanism for experience-dependent neural remodeling. Long-term potentiation LTP is the cellular mechanism of Hebbian plasticity producing lasting increases in synaptic strength through repeated co-activation. Cortical map reorganization following skill training demonstrates use-dependent expansion of cortical representations corresponding to trained body regions and movements. Brain-derived neurotrophic factor BDNF is the primary molecular mediator of neuroplasticity, promoting synapse formation, dendritic growth, and neurogenesis. Deliberate practice — focused repetition at the edge of current ability with immediate feedback — is the optimal stimulus for skill-related neuroplasticity. Sinew channel remodeling in TCM through repeated movement practice corresponds to the neuroplastic reorganization of motor circuits underlying skill acquisition.

## Hebbian Plasticity

- Neurons that fire together wire together — co-activation strengthens synaptic connections between neurons.
- This Hebbian principle provides the cellular basis for associative learning and skill acquisition.
- Long-term potentiation produces lasting synaptic strengthening through AMPA receptor insertion and dendritic growth.
- Spike-timing dependent plasticity refines Hebbian learning based on the precise millisecond timing of co-activation.

## Cortical Map Reorganization

- Motor cortex contains topographic maps of body regions that expand with use and contract with disuse.
- String musicians show expanded cortical representation of the fingering hand fingers corresponding to practice demand.
- Phantom limb pain reflects maladaptive cortical reorganization following amputation with expansion into deafferented zones.
- Targeted practice with sensory feedback produces adaptive cortical map expansion improving motor precision.

## BDNF and Structural Plasticity

- BDNF promotes synapse formation, dendritic arborization, and neurogenesis in the hippocampus.
- Exercise is the most potent lifestyle stimulus for BDNF production through FNDC5-irisin signaling.
- Meditation and Tai Chi produce BDNF elevation supporting neuroplasticity and cognitive preservation.
- BDNF Val66Met polymorphism reduces activity-dependent BDNF secretion affecting plasticity capacity.
