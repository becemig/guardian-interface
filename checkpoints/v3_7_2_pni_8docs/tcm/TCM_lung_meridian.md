# Lung Meridian: Metal Element, Defensive Qi, and Breath Governance

## Abstract

The Lung organ system in Traditional Chinese Medicine governs respiration, controls the descending and dispersing of Qi, regulates water passages, and dominates the skin and body hair. The Lung houses the Po corporeal soul which is the somatic aspect of the spirit tied to the physical body and breath. The Lung meridian descends from the chest to the thumb tip along the anterior arm. Lung function is the most immediately accessible organ system for deliberate regulation through breathing practice and directly mediates the connection between voluntary breath control and autonomic state.

## Lung Functions in TCM

### Governance of Qi and Respiration

- The Lung controls the intake of clean air and the exhalation of turbid Qi.
- Lung Qi descends and disperses throughout the body carrying clean Qi to all organ systems.
- The Lung is the only internal organ in direct contact with the external environment through breath.
- Disruption of Lung descending function produces cough, wheezing, and chest fullness.

### Defensive Wei Qi

- The Lung disperses Wei Qi to the skin surface forming the body's first defensive barrier against external pathogens.
- Wei Qi circulates in the superficial layers of the body warming the skin and regulating pores.
- Strong Lung Qi produces robust Wei Qi and resistance to external invasion.
- Deficient Lung Qi produces frequent colds, spontaneous sweating, and sensitivity to wind and cold.

### Water Passage Regulation

- The Lung regulates water metabolism by descending fluids to the Kidney and Bladder.
- Lung pathology can produce edema and urinary difficulty through disruption of water passage.
- The Lung-Kidney axis of Qi reception and fluid regulation is fundamental to breath-based practice.

### Po Corporeal Soul

- The Po is the somatic aspect of spirit that enters the body at birth and leaves at death with the physical body.
- The Po governs physical sensation, somatic instinct, and the felt sense of embodied existence.
- Strong Po function produces clear somatic awareness, good proprioception, and physical vitality.
- Grief and sadness directly injure the Lung and Po, producing somatic numbness and disconnection.
- Internal arts practice that cultivates somatic awareness directly nourishes the Po.

## Meridian Pathway

- Originates in the middle burner and descends to the Large Intestine before ascending through the diaphragm to the Lung.
- Emerges at the chest at LU-1 Zhongfu below the clavicle.
- Descends along the anterior lateral arm through the forearm to the wrist.
- Terminates at LU-11 Shaoshang on the thumb tip.

## Key Acupoints

- LU-1 Zhongfu: Front mu of Lung, below clavicle, cough, asthma, chest pain, Lung Qi disorders.
- LU-5 Chize: He-sea point, elbow crease, cough with phlegm, elbow pain, Lung heat.
- LU-7 Lieque: Luo and command point, above wrist, head and neck disorders, opens Conception Vessel.
- LU-9 Taiyuan: Source and influential point of vessels, wrist, tonifies Lung Qi and Yin, pulse disorders.
- LU-11 Shaoshang: Jing-well point, thumb, sore throat, fever, emergency resuscitation.

## Breath and Autonomic Convergence

- Slow diaphragmatic breathing activates pulmonary Piezo2 stretch receptors producing vagal afferent discharge.
- Extended exhalation directly engages Lung descending function and ventral vagal brake simultaneously.
- Resonance frequency breathing at 0.1 Hz maximizes both Lung-Kidney Qi coordination and HRV coherence.
- The deliberate breath control practiced in Qigong constitutes direct voluntary regulation of autonomic state via Lung function.
