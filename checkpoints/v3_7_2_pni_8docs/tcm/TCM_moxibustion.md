# Moxibustion

## Abstract

Moxibustion Jiu Fa is the therapeutic application of heat to acupuncture points and meridians through the burning of moxa — dried Artemisia vulgaris mugwort — either directly on the skin or indirectly through intermediary materials. Moxibustion complements needling as the other primary physical modality of classical acupuncture — the classical text states needle what cold will not reach, moxa what needle will not reach. Artemisia vulgaris is specifically used because its fine dried leaves produce a slow penetrating heat that can warm deeply without the flash heat of other combustibles. Direct moxa involves burning small moxa cones directly on the skin — either until the patient feels heat and the cone is removed scarring technique, or allowed to burn completely for maximum stimulation. Indirect moxa interposes an intermediary between the burning moxa and the skin — moxa sticks held above the point, moxa on the needle handle, moxa on ginger or garlic slices. Modern research documents that moxa combustion produces near-infrared radiation in the 0.8-5.6 micrometer range that penetrates skin and subcutaneous tissue. Moxibustion at ST-36 has been extensively studied for immune modulation and digestive function. The primary clinical application of moxibustion is Yang tonification — warming Cold patterns, rescuing Yang in collapse, and cultivating the constitutional Yang fire.

## Direct Moxibustion

- Scarring direct moxa burns the cone completely producing a blister that heals with a small scar.
- Non-scarring direct moxa removes the cone when the patient feels intense heat preventing tissue damage.
- Direct moxa produces the strongest local and systemic effects and is used for stubborn cold painful conditions.
- GV-4 Mingmen direct moxa is the classical treatment for Kidney Yang deficiency and constitutional Yang rescue.

## Indirect Moxibustion

- Moxa sticks are rolled cylinders of compressed moxa held 2-3 cm above the point for warming stimulation.
- Moxa on the needle handle warms the needle conducting heat deeply into the tissue along the needle shaft.
- Moxa on ginger slices adds the warming dispersing quality of ginger to the moxa heat for Cold-Dampness patterns.
- Moxa on salt fills the navel CV-8 Shenque for emergency Yang rescue and digestive warming.

## Modern Research

- Moxa combustion produces near-infrared radiation at 0.8-5.6 micrometers penetrating deeply into tissue.
- Moxibustion at ST-36 increases NK cell activity, T lymphocyte proliferation, and IgG production.
- Moxibustion at CV-4 increases serum testosterone and DHEA corresponding to constitutional Yang tonification.
- Heat shock proteins induced by moxibustion may mediate some of its protective and anti-inflammatory effects.

## TCM Correspondence

- Yang tonification as the primary moxibustion indication corresponds to its warming penetrating heat stimulating Yang Qi.
- GV-4 Mingmen as the constitutional Yang fire point is the most important moxibustion point for Yang deficiency.
- ST-36 Zusanli moxibustion for immune enhancement corresponds to the classical longevity moxibustion practice.
- The classical statement needle what cold will not reach moxa what needle will not reach defines moxibustion's domain.
