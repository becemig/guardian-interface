# Wu Xing Five Element Theory in Traditional Chinese Medicine

## Abstract

Wu Xing describes five fundamental phases of transformation governing natural phenomena, physiological function, and pathological change in Traditional Chinese Medicine. The five phases are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. Each phase corresponds to organ systems, emotions, seasons, tissues, sensory organs, and climatic factors. Two primary cycles govern inter-elemental relationships: the Sheng generating cycle and the Ke controlling cycle.

## Five Phase Correspondences

### Wood

- Organ systems: Liver and Gallbladder
- Emotion: Anger and frustration
- Season: Spring, direction East
- Tissue: Sinews and tendons
- Sensory organ: Eyes
- Climate: Wind
- Function: Smooth flow of Qi and Blood, planning, decision-making

### Fire

- Organ systems: Heart, Small Intestine, Pericardium, Triple Burner
- Emotion: Joy and agitation
- Season: Summer, direction South
- Tissue: Blood vessels
- Sensory organ: Tongue
- Climate: Heat
- Function: Circulation, consciousness, spirit (Shen), communication

### Earth

- Organ systems: Spleen and Stomach
- Emotion: Worry and overthinking
- Season: Late summer, center
- Tissue: Muscles and flesh
- Sensory organ: Mouth
- Climate: Dampness
- Function: Transformation and transportation of food and fluids, thought

### Metal

- Organ systems: Lung and Large Intestine
- Emotion: Grief and sadness
- Season: Autumn, direction West
- Tissue: Skin and body hair
- Sensory organ: Nose
- Climate: Dryness
- Function: Respiration, defensive Qi, letting go, boundary

### Water

- Organ systems: Kidney and Bladder
- Emotion: Fear and anxiety
- Season: Winter, direction North
- Tissue: Bones and marrow
- Sensory organ: Ears
- Climate: Cold
- Function: Essence storage, reproduction, willpower, root of Yin and Yang

## Generative and Control Cycles

### Sheng Generating Cycle

- Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth ash, Earth yields Metal, Metal enriches Water, Water nourishes Wood.
- The generating cycle describes mutual support between adjacent phases.
- Deficiency in one phase may deplete the phase it generates over time.

### Ke Controlling Cycle

- Wood restrains Earth, Earth controls Water, Water quenches Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood.
- The controlling cycle maintains equilibrium by preventing any single phase from becoming excessive.
- Disruption of the controlling cycle produces pathological excess or deficiency patterns.

## Relevance to Internal Movement Arts

- Tai Chi and Qigong forms are traditionally organized around Five Element correspondences.
- Seasonal practice variation aligns with the dominant phase to support the associated organ system.
- Wu Qin Xi Five Animal Frolics directly maps each animal to a Five Element phase and organ pair.
- HRV coherence may reflect balanced inter-organ regulation consistent with harmonized Five Element dynamics.
