# Large Intestine Release and Letting Go

## Abstract

The Large Intestine as the yang organ paired with the Lung in the Metal element governs the final stage of digestion — receiving the turbid remainder from the Small Intestine, extracting any remaining fluid, and excreting the waste as stool. At the physiological level this function of elimination requires the capacity to let go — to release what has been fully processed and no longer serves. At the psychological and spiritual level the Large Intestine governs the capacity to release attachment, grief, and what has been held beyond its appropriate time. Constipation at the physical level often mirrors psychological holding and inability to release. LI-4 Hegu the great eliminator is one of the four command points governing the face and head and the most powerful point for moving Qi and Blood in the body. The Po soul housed in the Lung and expressed through the Large Intestine governs the instinctual capacity for rhythmic release corresponding to the natural cycle of taking in and letting go.

## Physiological Function

- The Large Intestine receives approximately 1.5 liters of liquid chyme daily from the Small Intestine.
- It extracts the remaining water and electrolytes concentrating the waste into formed stool.
- Peristaltic movement propels contents toward the rectum for elimination.
- Large Intestine Qi deficiency or dryness produces constipation while excess heat produces diarrhea.

## Letting Go

- The Metal element governs the cycle of taking in — Lung inspiration — and letting go — Large Intestine elimination.
- Psychological inability to release produces physical constipation as the body mirrors the mental holding.
- Grief unresolved beyond its appropriate season accumulates as a holding pattern in the Lung-Large Intestine system.
- Treatment addresses both the physical elimination and the psychological capacity for release simultaneously.

## Lung-Large Intestine Axis

- The Lung descends Qi while the Large Intestine descends waste — complementary descending functions.
- Lung Qi deficiency failing to descend contributes to Large Intestine constipation.
- Large Intestine heat can reverse to the Lung producing respiratory and skin manifestations.
- Treatment of the Lung often improves Large Intestine function and vice versa through the Metal element axis.

## TCM Correspondence

- LI-4 Hegu as the yuan source point is the primary point for moving Qi and Blood and regulating elimination.
- LI-11 Quchi clears Large Intestine heat corresponding to inflammatory bowel and skin conditions.
- Autumn as the Metal season corresponds to the natural time of release, harvest completion, and letting go.
- White color, pungent flavor, and dry climate all correspond to the Metal element Large Intestine nature.
