# Environmental Medicine Detox Protocols

## Abstract

Environmental medicine detox protocols — the clinical application of nutritional, lifestyle, and sauna-based interventions to support the liver's phase I and phase II biotransformation pathways that convert lipophilic environmental toxins including pesticides, solvents, mycotoxins, and heavy metals into water-soluble conjugates for renal and biliary excretion, with the glutathione system serving as the master intracellular antioxidant and phase II conjugation substrate whose depletion by toxic burden increases vulnerability to further toxin damage, and with infrared sauna providing an evidence-based adjunctive route of toxin excretion through sweat that bypasses the liver-kidney axis. Phase I cytochrome P450 detoxification — the superfamily of approximately 57 CYP450 enzymes in the liver endoplasmic reticulum that oxidize, reduce, or hydrolyze lipophilic toxins and drugs to introduce reactive functional groups, with individual genetic polymorphisms producing slow, intermediate, and rapid metabolizer phenotypes that determine individual toxin sensitivity and drug metabolism, with phase I oxidation sometimes producing more reactive and more toxic intermediates than the parent compound that must be rapidly conjugated by phase II enzymes to avoid cellular damage. Phase II conjugation reactions — the hepatic conjugation reactions including glucuronidation, sulfation, glutathione conjugation, amino acid conjugation, and methylation that attach water-soluble groups to phase I metabolites, dramatically increasing their water solubility and enabling renal or biliary excretion, with the overall efficiency of detoxification depending on the balance between phase I activation rate and phase II conjugation capacity and the availability of conjugation substrates including glutathione, glycine, and sulfate. Sauna detoxification evidence — the accumulating evidence that sweat contains measurable quantities of environmental toxins including organochlorine pesticides, phthalates, bisphenol A, heavy metals, and mycotoxins, with repeated infrared sauna sessions in occupationally exposed individuals and firefighters demonstrating significant reductions in body burden of lipophilic toxins and symptomatic improvements, supporting sweat as a clinically relevant toxin excretion route complementing renal and biliary elimination. The Liver dredging free flow detoxification correspondence in TCM describes the classical treatment principle of Shu Gan — dredging the Liver and promoting free flow — as the therapeutic approach for Liver Qi stagnation, with the Liver's TCM function of governing smooth flow corresponding to its biomedical function of governing toxin biotransformation and the free flow of bile acids and metabolic waste products through the hepatobiliary system.

## Phase I CYP450

- CYP3A4: metabolizes 50 percent of drugs — most abundant hepatic CYP.
- CYP1A2: metabolizes caffeine PAHs — induced by cruciferous vegetables and smoking.
- Polymorphisms: slow metabolizers accumulate toxins fast metabolizers produce reactive intermediates.
- Inducers: cruciferous vegetables alcohol St Johns Wort — increase phase I activity.

## Phase II Conjugation

- Glucuronidation: UGT enzymes — most abundant phase II pathway.
- Glutathione conjugation: GST enzymes — critical for reactive electrophile detoxification.
- Sulfation: SULT enzymes — sulfate substrate depleted by high toxic load.
- Methylation: COMT PNMT — catechol estrogen and heavy metal methylation.

## Glutathione System

- GSH: tripeptide glutamate-cysteine-glycine — master cellular antioxidant.
- Synthesis: rate-limited by cysteine availability — NAC precursor.
- Depletion: heavy metals mycotoxins solvents deplete GSH — vulnerability cycle.
- NRF2: sulforaphane activates NRF2 — induces glutathione synthesis and phase II enzymes.

## Sauna Evidence

- Sweat toxins: organochlorines phthalates BPA arsenic — measurable in sweat.
- Firefighters: repeated sauna sessions reduce PCB and pesticide body burden.
- Protocol: 15-20 min sessions progressive — infrared lower temperature better tolerated.
- Adjunctive: sauna complements not replaces hepatorenal detoxification.
