# Neuroinflammation

## Abstract

Neuroinflammation is inflammation within the central nervous system, mediated primarily by microglia and astrocytes responding to infection, injury, metabolic stress, or peripheral immune signals. Unlike peripheral inflammation, neuroinflammation is tightly regulated due to the catastrophic consequences of uncontrolled inflammation in non-regenerating neural tissue. Microglia are the brain's resident immune cells, existing in a ramified surveillance state under homeostatic conditions and transitioning to activated states in response to DAMPs, PAMPs, and cytokine signals. Chronic low-grade neuroinflammation — driven by dysbiosis, chronic stress, obesity, sleep deprivation, and aging — is now implicated as a core pathological mechanism in depression, Alzheimer disease, chronic pain via central sensitization, and multiple neurodegenerative conditions. The NLRP3 inflammasome is a key driver of IL-1 beta production in activated microglia. Resolution of inflammation via specialized pro-resolving mediators (SPMs) is an active process that fails in chronic inflammatory states. In TCM, neuroinflammation maps to Phlegm-Heat or Damp-Heat obstructing the clear orifices of the Heart and Brain.

## Microglia Biology

- Origin: yolk sac macrophages - populate brain during embryogenesis - maintained locally
- Surveillance: ramified microglia extend processes sampling synapses every few hours
- Homeostatic functions: synaptic pruning, debris clearance, neurotrophic factor release
- DAM state: disease-associated microglia - seen in Alzheimer - altered phagocytic profile
- M1-like: pro-inflammatory - releases IL-1, TNF, IL-6, ROS, glutamate
- M2-like: anti-inflammatory and reparative - releases IL-10, TGF-beta, BDNF
- Reality: microglia exist on a spectrum - M1-M2 is a simplification
- Imaging: TSPO PET ligands detect activated microglia in living human brain

## Blood-Brain Barrier

- Structure: tight junction endothelial cells, pericytes, astrocyte end-feet
- Function: selectively permeable - protects brain from peripheral immune and toxic challenges
- Disruption: TNF-alpha, IL-1, ROS, LPS all disrupt tight junctions
- Consequences: peripheral immune cells and cytokines flood CNS - neuroinflammation cascade
- Aging: BBB permeability increases with age - contributes to neurodegeneration
- Stress: chronic stress increases BBB permeability via glucocorticoid receptor signaling
- Circumventricular organs: regions without BBB - entry points for peripheral signals

## NLRP3 Inflammasome

- Platform: cytosolic multiprotein complex - assembles in response to danger signals
- Triggers: ATP, uric acid crystals, cholesterol crystals, LPS, amyloid-beta
- Output: activates caspase-1 - cleaves pro-IL-1 beta and pro-IL-18 to active forms
- Pyroptosis: inflammatory cell death - releases IL-1 beta and cellular contents
- NLRP3 in depression: activated by stress and chronic inflammation - IL-1 beta drives sickness behavior
- NLRP3 in Alzheimer: activated by amyloid-beta - drives neuroinflammatory cascade
- Inhibitors: MCC950, ketogenic diet, omega-3, resveratrol - therapeutic targets

## Central Sensitization

- Definition: amplified pain signaling from CNS - pain without proportionate peripheral input
- Mechanism: microglial activation in dorsal horn - releases IL-1, TNF, prostaglandins
- Synaptic: LTP-like changes in pain synapses - wind-up phenomenon
- Conditions: fibromyalgia, IBS, chronic low back pain, migraine - all have central sensitization
- Neuroinflammation role: spinal microglia activation amplifies and sustains pain
- Treatment: anti-inflammatory strategies, vagal activation, pain neuroscience education
- TCM: Qi and Blood stagnation obstructing channels - pain is blocked flow

## Alzheimer Neuroinflammation

- Amyloid-beta: activates microglia via TLR4 and NLRP3 - triggers IL-1 and TNF release
- Tau: intracellular tau aggregates activate NLRP3 - spreads neuroinflammation
- TREM2: microglial receptor - loss-of-function mutations increase Alzheimer risk
- Complement: C1q tags synapses for microglial pruning - excess pruning in early AD
- Reactive astrogliosis: astrocytes become A1 reactive - lose neurotrophic functions
- Inflammation first?: neuroinflammation may precede amyloid by decades

## Resolution of Inflammation

- Active process: resolution requires dedicated pro-resolving mediators - not passive
- SPMs: specialized pro-resolving mediators - resolvins, protectins, maresins - from omega-3
- Resolvins: D-series from DHA, E-series from EPA - halt neutrophil recruitment, promote clearance
- Failure: SPM deficiency in chronic inflammatory states - inflammation persists
- Omega-3: EPA and DHA are SPM precursors - dietary omega-3 supports resolution
- TCM: clearing Heat and resolving Phlegm - Huang Lian, Zhi Mu, Ban Xia - anti-inflammatory herbs
