# Somatic Psychology

## Abstract

Somatic psychology is the broad field of psychotherapy and psychology that considers the body as a primary site of psychological experience, memory, and healing rather than as a peripheral container for the mind. Eugene Gendlin's focusing approach identified the felt sense — the pre-conceptual bodily knowing that arises when attention is brought to the interior body — as the primary medium through which psychological change occurs. Peter Levine's somatic experiencing SE addresses trauma through tracking bodily sensations and completing thwarted defensive responses frozen in the nervous system. Pat Ogden's sensorimotor psychotherapy works with posture, movement, and gesture as direct expressions of psychological organization. Ron Kurtz's Hakomi method uses mindful body awareness to access core organizing beliefs encoded in characteristic postures and movements. Research documents that trauma is stored somatically — in altered HRV, chronic muscle tension, disrupted breathing patterns, and dysregulated interoception — and that somatic approaches are essential for full trauma resolution. Po as the Lung spirit in TCM — the somatic soul animating the body and mediating the boundary between self and world through breath and skin — corresponds to the somatic psychology understanding of body as the primary site of psychological experience.

## Felt Sense

- The felt sense is a pre-conceptual bodily knowing that arises when attention is brought to the body interior.
- It is fuzzy, holistic, and pre-verbal — the body's way of holding the whole of a situation.
- Gendlin's focusing process guides attention to the felt sense and facilitates its unfolding through gentle inquiry.
- A felt shift — a bodily release accompanying psychological insight — is the marker of genuine therapeutic movement.

## Somatic Experiencing

- SE tracks the body's sensations, movements, and impulses during trauma processing.
- Titration — working with small doses of traumatic material — prevents retraumatization through overwhelming activation.
- Pendulation — cycling between activation and resource states — builds the nervous system's regulatory capacity.
- Completing thwarted defensive responses — the trembling, shaking, and spontaneous movement of nervous system discharge.

## Hakomi Method

- Hakomi uses mindfulness as the therapeutic context for somatic exploration.
- The therapist tracks the client's posture, gesture, breathing, and micro-movements for somatic indicators.
- Experiments probe characteristic somatic organizations — what happens if I offer this touch or this word?
- Core beliefs are accessed through their somatic expressions rather than through cognitive exploration alone.
