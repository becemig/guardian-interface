# Myelin and Nerve Conduction

## Abstract

Myelin is the lipid-rich insulating sheath surrounding axons that dramatically increases nerve conduction velocity through saltatory conduction. Oligodendrocytes produce myelin in the CNS while Schwann cells myelinate peripheral axons. White matter plasticity driven by skilled practice produces measurable changes in fiber tract integrity. The Kidney governs marrow and brain as sea of marrow providing the constitutional substrate for myelination and white matter health.

## Saltatory Conduction

- Action potentials jump between nodes of Ranvier achieving 70 to 120 meters per second in large myelinated fibers.
- Unmyelinated fibers conduct at 0.5 to 2 meters per second.
- Saltatory conduction reduces metabolic cost by limiting ion exchange to nodal regions.
- Axon diameter and myelin thickness together determine conduction velocity.

## White Matter Plasticity

- Piano practice produces measurable white matter changes in corticospinal tracts via diffusion tensor imaging.
- Activity-dependent myelination optimizes conduction in frequently activated circuits.
- Tai Chi and skilled movement practice activate motor circuits repeatedly promoting myelination.
- Improved timing precision in myelinated circuits corresponds to refined practitioner coordination.
