# Nitric Oxide Signaling

## Abstract

Nitric oxide NO is a gaseous signaling molecule produced by nitric oxide synthase NOS enzymes from the amino acid arginine. Louis Ignarro's discovery of endothelium-derived relaxing factor as nitric oxide earned the 1998 Nobel Prize in Physiology. Endothelial NOS eNOS produces NO in blood vessel walls causing vascular smooth muscle relaxation and vasodilation — the primary mechanism of blood pressure regulation and tissue perfusion. Neuronal NOS nNOS produces NO as a retrograde neurotransmitter in the brain modulating synaptic plasticity and neural signaling. Inducible NOS iNOS produces large amounts of NO during immune activation serving antimicrobial functions. Acupuncture stimulation increases local and systemic NO production corresponding to the improved Qi and Blood flow described in TCM. NO as the molecular mediator of vascular tone and tissue perfusion corresponds to the functional expression of Qi as the vital force animating tissue function.

## Vascular NO

- eNOS in endothelial cells is activated by shear stress, acetylcholine, and bradykinin producing vasodilation.
- NO diffuses to vascular smooth muscle activating guanylate cyclase producing cGMP and muscle relaxation.
- This vasodilation mechanism governs blood pressure, tissue perfusion, and erectile function.
- Physical exercise increases shear stress-induced eNOS activation producing NO-dependent cardiovascular adaptation.

## Neural NO

- nNOS produces NO as a retrograde messenger diffusing from postsynaptic neurons to presynaptic terminals.
- NO modulates synaptic plasticity contributing to long-term potentiation and memory consolidation.
- nNOS in the gut contributes to enteric nervous system function and intestinal smooth muscle relaxation.
- NO contributes to the non-adrenergic non-cholinergic NANC neurotransmission in autonomic regulation.

## Acupuncture and NO

- Acupuncture needle stimulation increases local NO production at acupoints through mechanical activation of eNOS.
- Increased NO produces local vasodilation improving tissue perfusion at the point and along the meridian.
- Systemic NO increases following acupuncture correspond to the whole-body Qi regulation described in TCM.
- This NO mechanism provides a molecular correlate for the improved Blood and Qi flow of acupuncture treatment.
