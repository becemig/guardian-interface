# Oncology and Stress Neuroscience

## Abstract

The connection between chronic psychological stress and cancer progression is one of the most clinically significant findings in psychoneuroimmunology — David Spiegel's landmark 1989 study demonstrating that group psychotherapy doubled survival time in metastatic breast cancer patients established that psychological interventions can directly affect cancer biology. The mechanisms connecting stress to cancer involve the HPA axis and sympathetic nervous system: chronic cortisol and catecholamine elevation suppress natural killer NK cell activity, reduce cytotoxic T lymphocyte function, increase tumor-promoting angiogenesis, and activate the NF-kB transcription factor that drives inflammatory tumor microenvironment formation. NF-kB is the master regulator of the tumor-promoting inflammatory cascade — its activation in both tumor cells and surrounding stromal cells drives the production of IL-6, IL-8, VEGF, and other factors that promote tumor survival, growth, angiogenesis, and metastasis. Reishi Ling Zhi triterpenes — particularly ganoderic acids — directly inhibit NF-kB activation in tumor cells while simultaneously stimulating NK cell and macrophage activity through beta-glucan polysaccharide mechanisms. Astragalus Huang Qi polysaccharides restore NK cell and T cell function suppressed by chemotherapy and chronic stress — the basis for its use as an adjunctive cancer treatment in integrative oncology. Adaptogenic defense through chronic stress buffering — the primary mechanism of TCM tonic herbs in cancer prevention and recovery — involves normalizing the HPA axis response reducing the chronic cortisol elevation that suppresses anti-tumor immunity. The tumor microenvironment as a TCM pattern corresponds to Toxic Heat and Phlegm-Blood stasis — the typical TCM pattern diagnosis of cancer corresponding to the chronic inflammatory state of the tumor microenvironment.

## HPA Axis and Cancer

- Chronic cortisol suppresses NK cell cytotoxicity reducing tumor surveillance capacity.
- Catecholamine signaling through beta-adrenergic receptors on tumor cells promotes proliferation and metastasis.
- Stress-induced angiogenesis through VEGF upregulation provides the blood supply for tumor growth.
- Psychosocial interventions reducing stress burden directly improve immune anti-tumor function.

## NF-kB and Tumor Microenvironment

- NF-kB activation drives the inflammatory tumor microenvironment through IL-6, IL-8, and VEGF production.
- Tumor cells hijack NF-kB signaling to produce survival signals resistant to apoptosis.
- Chronic inflammation activating NF-kB creates the microenvironment that initiates and sustains carcinogenesis.
- NF-kB inhibition is a primary molecular target of TCM anti-tumor herbs including Reishi and Huang Lian berberine.

## TCM Medicinal Mushrooms

- Reishi ganoderic acid triterpenes inhibit NF-kB and directly induce apoptosis in cancer cells.
- Reishi beta-glucan polysaccharides stimulate NK cell and macrophage anti-tumor activity.
- Shiitake lentinan enhances T cell and NK cell activity and has clinical evidence in gastric cancer.
- Astragalus polysaccharides restore chemotherapy-suppressed immune function — widely used in Chinese integrative oncology.
