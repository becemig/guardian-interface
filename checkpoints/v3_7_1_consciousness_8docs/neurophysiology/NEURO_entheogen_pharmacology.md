# Entheogen Pharmacology

## Abstract

Classical psychedelics — psilocybin, mescaline, LSD, and DMT — produce their profound alterations of consciousness primarily through agonist activity at the 5-HT2A serotonin receptor, particularly in cortical layer V pyramidal neurons where 5-HT2A activation produces the glutamatergic cascade underlying the psychedelic experience. David Nichols' comprehensive pharmacological framework established that classical psychedelics share the 5-HT2A agonism mechanism despite their structural diversity — tryptamines like psilocybin and DMT structurally resemble serotonin, phenethylamines like mescaline are structurally distinct but converge on the same receptor. Robin Carhart-Harris demonstrated through fMRI studies that psilocybin produces its subjective effects primarily through suppression of the default mode network DMN — the self-referential resting state network whose activity correlates with the ordinary sense of self, rumination, and ego-maintenance. DMN suppression produces ego dissolution — the temporary dissolution of the ordinary sense of separate self — which correlates with the mystical experience and with therapeutic outcomes in depression, addiction, and existential distress. Ibogaine is pharmacologically unique among entheogens — it is not a 5-HT2A agonist but acts as a sigma-1 receptor agonist, kappa opioid receptor agonist, NMDA antagonist, and serotonin-dopamine reuptake inhibitor, producing its distinctive waking dream state and remarkable anti-addictive properties through this complex multi-receptor profile. Mescaline from peyote and San Pedro cactus is the phenethylamine entheogen with the longest documented ceremonial use — its 5-HT2A agonism with additional dopaminergic activity produces the characteristic mescaline quality of visual beauty and emotional warmth. All classical psychedelics produce lasting neuroplasticity through upregulation of BDNF and TrkB signaling, increased dendritic spine density, and enhanced synaptic plasticity — changes that outlast the acute drug effect and correspond to the lasting therapeutic benefits.

## 5-HT2A Mechanism

- 5-HT2A receptors on cortical layer V pyramidal neurons are the primary site of psychedelic action.
- Agonism activates a glutamatergic cascade producing thalamocortical desynchronization and perceptual alteration.
- 5-HT2A activation also activates the AMPA receptor pathway driving BDNF release and synaptogenesis.
- The density of cortical 5-HT2A receptors correlates with the intensity of psychedelic effects.

## Default Mode Network Suppression

- Psilocybin suppresses DMN activity producing the dissolution of ordinary self-referential processing.
- DMN suppression correlates with ego dissolution and mystical experience scores.
- Post-psilocybin increases in between-network connectivity persist for weeks corresponding to lasting therapeutic effects.
- The therapeutic mechanism in depression involves breaking the rigid ruminative self-narrative through DMN suppression.

## Ibogaine

- Ibogaine acts as sigma-1 agonist, kappa opioid agonist, NMDA antagonist, and monoamine reuptake inhibitor.
- It produces a waking dream state lasting 12-24 hours with autobiographical memory review.
- Its anti-addictive properties involve resetting dopamine receptor sensitivity and interrupting withdrawal neurochemistry.
- Ibogaine is the only substance demonstrating single-dose interruption of opioid and stimulant addiction.
