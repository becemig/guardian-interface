# Bazi Four Pillars

## Abstract

Bazi — the four pillars of destiny — is the most sophisticated system of constitutional analysis in the Chinese cosmological tradition, using the precise year, month, day, and hour of birth to construct an eight-character chart Ba Zi that reveals the constitutional balance of the five elements in the individual, the natural strengths and vulnerabilities of the constitution, the major life cycles and their elemental character, and the types of environmental, relational, and lifestyle conditions that best support the individual's health and flourishing. The day master — the Heavenly Stem of the day pillar — represents the self and is the central reference point for all chart analysis: it is the element of the self, and all other chart elements are analyzed in terms of their relationship to the day master through the generating and controlling cycles of the five elements. The ten gods — the ten possible relationships between any stem or branch element and the day master element — provide the vocabulary for chart analysis: Rob Wealth, Friend, Eating God, Hurting Officer, Direct Wealth, Indirect Wealth, Direct Officer, Indirect Officer, Direct Resource, and Indirect Resource — each describing a specific functional relationship between the day master and the environmental element. Ten-year luck pillars represent the major constitutional cycles through the lifespan — each person moves through a sequence of ten-year periods governed by specific elemental energies that either support or challenge the day master, producing the characteristic expansions and contractions of fortune, health, and vitality across the lifespan. Annual and monthly luck layers add additional resolution to the ten-year cycles, allowing specific years and months of constitutional challenge or support to be identified. Destiny as constitutional medicine uses the Bazi chart as a diagnostic tool for TCM constitutional assessment — identifying which organ networks are constitutionally strong and which are vulnerable, which pathogenic factors the constitution is most susceptible to, and which lifestyle and dietary adjustments best support constitutional balance.

## Day Master Analysis

- The day master Heavenly Stem represents the self and is the central reference point.
- A strong day master has abundant supporting elements — resource and friend elements in the chart.
- A weak day master has depleting elements dominant — wealth and officer elements challenging it.
- Treatment strategy differs: strengthen weak day master, channel excess strong day master.

## Ten Gods

- Direct Resource and Indirect Resource: elements that generate the day master — nourishing, supportive.
- Friend and Rob Wealth: same element as day master — competitive, parallel, peer.
- Eating God and Hurting Officer: elements generated by day master — expressive, output.
- Direct Wealth and Indirect Wealth: elements controlled by day master — resources, achievements.
- Direct Officer and Indirect Officer: elements controlling day master — authority, discipline, challenge.

## Luck Cycle Analysis

- Ten-year luck pillars rotate through the elemental cycle from birth onward.
- Each luck period brings its governing element into prominence changing the chart dynamics.
- Favorable luck periods activate the chart's beneficial configurations — health, vitality, prosperity.
- Challenging luck periods activate the chart's stress configurations — constitutional vulnerability, disease tendency.
