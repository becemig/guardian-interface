# Reward and Dopamine

## Abstract

The mesolimbic dopamine system comprising the ventral tegmental area VTA and its projections to the nucleus accumbens prefrontal cortex and amygdala is the primary neural substrate of reward, motivation, and reinforcement learning. Wolfram Schultz discovered that dopamine neurons fire not to reward itself but to reward prediction errors — the difference between expected and received reward. This prediction error signal updates value predictions enabling adaptive learning about reward-predictive stimuli. Kent Berridge distinguished wanting — dopamine-mediated incentive salience driving approach behavior — from liking — opioid-mediated hedonic pleasure from reward consumption. Addiction involves progressive dysregulation of dopamine signaling with escalating wanting and reduced liking. Heart Fire motivation in TCM — the Shen's orientation toward meaningful engagement and the joy of purposeful activity — corresponds to the healthy mesolimbic dopamine system supporting motivated action toward valued goals.

## Mesolimbic Pathway

- VTA dopamine neurons project to nucleus accumbens, prefrontal cortex, amygdala, and hippocampus.
- The nucleus accumbens is the primary site of reward valuation and motivated approach behavior.
- Prefrontal cortex projections modulate executive control over reward-driven behavior.
- Amygdala projections link reward signals to emotional memory and conditioned motivational responses.

## Prediction Error Signal

- Dopamine neurons fire to unpredicted rewards and pause to predicted reward omission.
- When reward is fully predicted dopamine neurons do not fire — the cue alone drives anticipatory dopamine.
- Positive prediction errors — better than expected — increase dopamine and update value predictions upward.
- Negative prediction errors — worse than expected — decrease dopamine and update value predictions downward.

## Wanting vs Liking

- Wanting is dopamine-mediated incentive salience — the motivational pull toward reward-predictive stimuli.
- Liking is opioid-mediated hedonic pleasure from actual reward consumption.
- Addiction involves escalating wanting with reduced liking — compulsive seeking without proportional pleasure.
- Flow states involve optimal dopamine tone supporting sustained motivated engagement without addictive escalation.
