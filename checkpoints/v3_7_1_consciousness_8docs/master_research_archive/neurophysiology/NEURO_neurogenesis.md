# Neurogenesis

## Abstract

Adult neurogenesis — the generation of new neurons in the adult brain — was considered impossible for most of the twentieth century, with the dogma that the adult brain is fixed and cannot generate new neurons persisting until Joseph Altman's autoradiographic evidence in the 1960s and the definitive confirmation by Fernando Nottebohm in songbirds and Elizabeth Gould in primates in the 1980s and 1990s. Adult hippocampal neurogenesis — the ongoing generation of new granule neurons in the dentate gyrus of the hippocampus — is the best established form of adult neurogenesis and has been demonstrated in all mammalian species examined including humans. The dentate gyrus is one of only two brain regions where adult neurogenesis occurs — the other being the olfactory bulb — and the new neurons generated there are functionally integrated into the hippocampal circuit where they contribute to pattern separation, the ability to distinguish similar but distinct memories. BDNF brain-derived neurotrophic factor is the primary molecular mediator of neurogenesis and neuroplasticity — it promotes the survival, differentiation, and integration of new neurons, enhances synaptic plasticity, and is elevated by exercise, environmental enrichment, learning, and antidepressant treatment. Aerobic exercise is the most potent known stimulator of hippocampal neurogenesis — running produces a dose-dependent increase in BDNF and new neuron production that corresponds to improved memory, reduced anxiety, and antidepressant effects. Chronic stress suppresses neurogenesis through glucocorticoid-mediated inhibition of BDNF and neural stem cell proliferation — providing a mechanistic link between chronic stress, hippocampal atrophy, and the memory and mood impairments of depression. Kidney Jing as neural stem reserve corresponds to the TCM understanding that the constitutional Kidney Essence is the root from which all tissues including neural tissue are generated and regenerated — the depletion of Kidney Jing in aging corresponds to the age-related decline in neurogenesis.

## Adult Hippocampal Neurogenesis

- New granule neurons generated in the dentate gyrus integrate into hippocampal circuits within weeks.
- Young new neurons have enhanced excitability and synaptic plasticity contributing to pattern separation.
- Pattern separation — distinguishing similar memories — is the primary cognitive function of new neurons.
- Impaired neurogenesis reduces pattern separation producing the memory confusion of chronic stress and aging.

## BDNF

- BDNF is elevated by exercise, learning, environmental enrichment, social engagement, and antidepressants.
- BDNF is suppressed by chronic stress, sleep deprivation, social isolation, and glucocorticoids.
- BDNF signaling through TrkB receptors promotes new neuron survival and synaptic strengthening.

## Exercise and Neuroplasticity

- The neurogenic effect of exercise corresponds to dose — more exercise produces more neurogenesis up to a threshold.
- Exercise neurogenesis mediates the antidepressant and anxiolytic effects of physical activity.
