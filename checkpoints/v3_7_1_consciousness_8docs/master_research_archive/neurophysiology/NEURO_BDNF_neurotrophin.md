# BDNF and Neurotrophins

## Abstract

Brain-derived neurotrophic factor BDNF is the most abundant and widely studied neurotrophin in the adult brain — a secreted protein belonging to the nerve growth factor family that promotes neuronal survival, synaptic plasticity, and neurogenesis through TrkB receptor activation. Yves-Alain Barde purified BDNF from pig brain in 1982 establishing it as a distinct member of the neurotrophin family alongside NGF, NT-3, and NT-4/5. BDNF is activity-dependent — neural activity drives BDNF expression through CREB activation creating a positive feedback loop between neural use and trophic support. BDNF at synapses promotes LTP through TrkB activation of PI3K and MAPK pathways enhancing protein synthesis and synaptic strengthening. The neurotrophic hypothesis of depression proposes that stress-induced BDNF reduction in the hippocampus produces dendritic atrophy and reduced neurogenesis contributing to depression pathophysiology — and that antidepressants work in part by restoring hippocampal BDNF. Exercise is the most potent lifestyle intervention for increasing BDNF — aerobic exercise reliably increases serum BDNF and hippocampal BDNF expression corresponding to improved memory and mood. Kidney Essence nourishment in TCM — the cultivation of the constitutional Jing that sustains brain vitality and neural regeneration — corresponds to BDNF as the molecular trophic factor mediating the Kidney's nourishment of brain and marrow.

## TrkB Signaling

- BDNF binds TrkB receptors activating PI3K-Akt, MAPK-ERK, and PLCgamma-PKC signaling cascades.
- PI3K-Akt pathway promotes neuronal survival by phosphorylating and inactivating pro-apoptotic proteins.
- MAPK-ERK pathway activates CREB driving expression of plasticity genes including BDNF itself.
- TrkB signaling at synapses enhances AMPA receptor insertion and dendritic spine growth supporting LTP.

## Exercise and BDNF

- Aerobic exercise increases hippocampal BDNF expression through lactate, FNDC5/irisin, and PGC-1alpha pathways.
- Serum BDNF increases acutely after exercise and shows chronic elevation with regular training.
- Exercise-induced BDNF corresponds to improved memory consolidation and reduced depression risk.
- The minimum effective exercise dose for BDNF elevation is approximately 20-30 minutes of moderate aerobic activity.

## Depression and BDNF

- Chronic stress reduces hippocampal BDNF expression through glucocorticoid suppression of CREB.
- Reduced BDNF produces dendritic retraction in the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex corresponding to volume loss.
- All major antidepressant classes increase hippocampal BDNF through distinct but converging mechanisms.
- BDNF Val66Met polymorphism reduces activity-dependent BDNF secretion increasing depression and anxiety risk.
