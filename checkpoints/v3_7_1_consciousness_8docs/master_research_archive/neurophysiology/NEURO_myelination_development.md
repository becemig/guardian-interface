# Myelination and Neural Development

## Abstract

Myelination is the process by which oligodendrocytes wrap axons in myelin sheaths dramatically increasing neural conduction velocity and reducing metabolic cost. The myelination trajectory follows a characteristic developmental pattern — sensory and motor pathways myelinate early in infancy while association cortices and prefrontal white matter myelinate progressively through adolescence and into the mid-twenties. This protracted prefrontal myelination provides the biological basis for the late development of executive function and impulse control. Yakovlev and Lecours documented the regional myelogenetic cycles showing that the last regions to myelinate are the frontal lobes — the substrate of executive function and personality integration. Diffusion tensor imaging DTI enables in vivo measurement of white matter tract integrity and myelination through fractional anisotropy. TCM Kidney Jing maturation through the seven-year cycles in women and eight-year cycles in men corresponds to the developmental myelination trajectory as the constitutional substrate of cognitive and physical maturation.

## Myelination Trajectory

- Sensory and motor pathways myelinate rapidly in the first two years of life enabling basic sensorimotor function.
- Language areas myelinate through middle childhood corresponding to the critical period for language acquisition.
- Association cortices myelinate through adolescence enabling increasingly sophisticated cognitive integration.
- Prefrontal white matter continues myelinating into the mid-twenties corresponding to continued executive function development.

## Critical Periods

- Critical periods are windows of heightened neural plasticity when specific experiences are required for normal development.
- Visual critical period requires normal binocular experience in the first years for stereoscopic vision development.
- Language critical period enables native-like language acquisition only during childhood.
- Critical period closure corresponds to myelination of the relevant pathways reducing their plasticity.

## White Matter and Cognition

- Higher fractional anisotropy in prefrontal white matter tracts predicts better executive function performance.
- Corpus callosum integrity supports interhemispheric communication and cognitive integration.
- White matter hyperintensities in aging reflect myelin breakdown and predict cognitive decline.
- Exercise, sleep, and omega-3 fatty acids support oligodendrocyte health and white matter integrity.

## TCM Correspondence
