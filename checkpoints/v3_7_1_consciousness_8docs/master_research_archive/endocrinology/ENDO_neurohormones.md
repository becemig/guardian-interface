# Neurohormones

## Abstract

Neurohormones are signaling molecules produced by neurons and released into the bloodstream to exert effects on distant target tissues, bridging the nervous and endocrine systems. The hypothalamus is the master neuroendocrine integration center, producing oxytocin and vasopressin in the paraventricular and supraoptic nuclei, and coordinating appetite regulation through arcuate nucleus circuits involving leptin, ghrelin, NPY, AgRP, and POMC. Oxytocin mediates social bonding, trust, and prosocial behavior and is profoundly influenced by early attachment experience. Vasopressin regulates fluid balance and contributes to social memory and aggression. Melatonin from the pineal gland signals darkness and governs circadian entrainment. Leptin and ghrelin form a bidirectional hunger-satiety axis disrupted by sleep deprivation, stress, and ultra-processed food environments.

## Oxytocin

- Source: paraventricular and supraoptic nuclei of hypothalamus
- Peripheral: uterine contractions during labor, milk ejection during nursing
- Central: released within brain - social bonding, trust, prosocial behavior, fear reduction
- Attachment: oxytocin mediates mother-infant bonding, pair bonding in monogamous species
- Amygdala: oxytocin reduces amygdala reactivity to social threats
- Touch: physical contact, eye contact, orgasm all release oxytocin
- Stress buffer: oxytocin attenuates HPA stress response
- TCM: Heart-Shen connection with others - oxytocin is neurobiological substrate of Heart resonance

## Vasopressin (ADH)

- Source: supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei
- Peripheral: antidiuretic hormone - increases water reabsorption in renal collecting ducts
- Central: social memory, territorial behavior, aggression, pair bonding in males
- Osmosensor: released in response to rising plasma osmolarity or falling blood volume
- V1a receptor: mediates social and behavioral effects - distributed in limbic system
- SIADH: syndrome of inappropriate ADH - hyponatremia from excess vasopressin

## Melatonin

- Source: pineal gland - driven by SCN via superior cervical ganglion
- Synthesis: tryptophan to serotonin to N-acetylserotonin to melatonin
- Signal: darkness signal - tells body it is night - not directly sleep-inducing
- Onset: DLMO occurs 2 hours before habitual sleep onset
- Antioxidant: melatonin is a potent mitochondrial antioxidant - reduces oxidative stress
- Immune: melatonin has immune-enhancing effects - peaks during sleep
- Therapeutic: low dose 0.5 mg shifts circadian phase - higher doses are sedative only

## Prolactin

- Source: anterior pituitary lactotrophs
- Primary function: initiates and maintains lactation
- Sleep: prolactin peaks during sleep - immune modulation and tissue repair
- Stress response: prolactin rises with acute stress
- Dopamine inhibition: dopamine tonically suppresses prolactin - loss of dopamine causes hyperprolactinemia
- Hyperprolactinemia: causes amenorrhea, galactorrhea, reduced libido, infertility

## Leptin and Ghrelin - Appetite Axis

- Leptin: from adipose tissue - signals energy sufficiency to hypothalamus - reduces hunger
- Leptin receptor: in arcuate nucleus - activates POMC neurons, inhibits NPY-AgRP neurons
- Leptin resistance: obesity causes leptin resistance - hypothalamus cannot sense satiety
- Ghrelin: from stomach - hunger hormone - rises before meals, falls after eating
- Ghrelin and GH: ghrelin also stimulates GH release - links hunger and anabolism
- Sleep deprivation: reduces leptin 18 percent, raises ghrelin 28 percent - drives overeating
- TCM: Spleen-Stomach harmony governs appetite - leptin-ghrelin axis is its neurohormonal substrate

## Hypothalamic Integration

- Arcuate nucleus: integrates leptin, insulin, ghrelin signals - master appetite regulator
- POMC neurons: release alpha-MSH - reduces food intake, increases energy expenditure
- NPY-AgRP neurons: release NPY and AgRP - increases food intake, reduces energy expenditure
- PVN: paraventricular nucleus - integrates stress, reproduction, thyroid, adrenal axes
- Lateral hypothalamus: orexin neurons - hunger, arousal, reward motivation
- Hypothalamus as TCM parallel: Huang Ting (Yellow Court) - central palace of inner alchemy
