# Leg, Ankle, and Foot Acupressure Point Cluster

## Abstract

The leg, ankle, and foot acupressure point cluster is the most point-dense region in basic acupressure practice, containing four of the eight extraordinary vessel master points - K 6 opening Yinqiao, B 62 opening Yangqiao, Sp 4 opening Chong Mai, and GB 41 opening Dai Mai - as well as St 36 Three Miles (the primary whole-body revitalizing tonic point), GB 34 (master point for all tendons and ligaments), and Lv 3 (the great yin opener). This cluster governs systemic vitality, blood quality, menstrual and reproductive health, musculoskeletal integrity of the entire lower body, sleep, and the full extraordinary vessel reservoir system accessed from the feet.

## Point Inventory

### Sp 10 - Sea of Blood (Xue Hai)
- Location: 3-4 fingers above the patella on the medial quadriceps
- Benefits: anemia, menstrual cramps, poor circulation, blood quality

### Sp 9 - Shady Side of the Mountain (Yin Ling Quan)
- Location: inside of the leg in the depression below the medial tibial condyle
- Benefits: knee pain, edema, water retention, worry, yin deficiency conditions

### St 36 - Three Miles (Zu San Li)
- Location: outside of the leg 4 fingers below the kneecap lateral to the tibia
- Benefits: revitalizes the whole body, physical fatigue, appetite, digestion
- TONIC POINT: one of the most powerful general vitality points in the system

### GB 34 - Sunny Side of the Mountain (Yang Ling Quan)
- Location: outside lower leg below and anterior to the fibula head
- Benefits: muscular tension, back pain, shin splints, yang conditions
- MASTER POINT: governing point for all tendons and ligaments in the body

### K 6 - Joyful Sleep (Zhao Hai)
- Location: directly below the medial malleolus in the groove
- Benefits: urinary and reproductive disorders, insomnia, fear, ankle
- MASTER POINT: opens Yinqiao Mai Yin Motility Vessel and Great Bridge Channel
- Pair: Lu7 + K6 opens Ren Mai and Yinqiao simultaneously

### B 62 - Calm Sleep (Shen Mai)
- Location: directly below the lateral malleolus in the groove
- Benefits: back pain, insomnia, epilepsy, sciatica, ankle
- MASTER POINT: opens Yangqiao Mai Yang Motility Vessel / Bridge Channel
- Pair: SI3 + B62 opens Du Mai and Yangqiao simultaneously

### Sp 4 - Grandparent-Grandchild (Gong Sun)
- Location: medial arch of foot along the border of the 1st metatarsal
- Benefits: indigestion, worry, cold feet, abdominal cramping
- MASTER POINT: opens Chong Mai Penetrating Channel / Sea of Blood
- Pair: Sp4 + P6 opens Chong Mai and Yinwei simultaneously

### GB 41 - Above Tears (Zu Lin Qi)
- Location: dorsal foot between 4th and 5th metatarsals
- Benefits: hip pain, sciatica, migraines, lateral foot
- MASTER POINT: opens Dai Mai Belt Channel
- Pair: GB41 + TW5 opens Dai Mai and Yangwei simultaneously

### Lv 3 - Supreme Rushing (Tai Chong)
- Location: dorsal foot between 1st and 2nd metatarsals in the depression
- Benefits: weakness, arthritis, cramps, headaches, liver congestion
- ENERGY GATE: opens all yin meridians - complement to LI 4 yang gate

### B 57 - Mountain Support (Cheng Shan)
- Location: base of calf muscle belly in the V-shaped depression
- Benefits: hemorrhoids, constipation, lumbago, calf cramping

### B 60 - High Mountain (Kun Lun)
- Location: between the lateral malleolus and the Achilles tendon
- Benefits: lower back pain, convulsions in children, ankle pain

### B 67 - Extremity of Yin (Zhi Yin)
- Location: outside base of the little toenail
- Benefits: difficult labor, fetal malposition, urinary dysfunction

## Clinical Protocols

- Lower Back Release: B 54, B 57, B 60, B 67 (full Bladder leg sequence)
- Woman's Balance Flow: Sp 10, Sp 9, Sp 6, Sp 4, GB 41, GB 34, GB 31, K 6
- Increasing Vitality: Sp 9, Lv 3, St 36 (closing body clock balance)
