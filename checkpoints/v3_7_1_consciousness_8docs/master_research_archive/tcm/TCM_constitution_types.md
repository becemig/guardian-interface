# TCM Constitutional Types

## Abstract

The nine constitutional types system developed by Wang Qi represents the most systematically researched modern framework for TCM constitutional medicine providing an evidence-based classification of constitutional variation with clinical, epidemiological, and preventive medicine applications. The nine types are: balanced He Ping as the healthy baseline, Qi deficiency Qi Xu with fatigue and immune susceptibility, Yang deficiency Yang Xu with cold sensitivity and low metabolism, Yin deficiency Yin Xu with heat signs and dryness, Phlegm-Dampness Tan Shi with obesity and metabolic tendency, Damp-Heat Shi Re with inflammatory and infectious susceptibility, Blood stasis Xue Yu with circulatory and pain tendencies, Qi stagnation Qi Yu with emotional and digestive sensitivity, and Special Intrinsic Te Bing with allergic and hypersensitive tendencies. Wang Qi's research team validated the constitution questionnaire through large-scale epidemiological studies demonstrating correlations between constitutional type and disease susceptibility — Yang deficiency constitution correlates with hypothyroidism and cold intolerance, Phlegm-Dampness with metabolic syndrome, Damp-Heat with inflammatory conditions, and Yin deficiency with hypertension and insomnia. Constitutional type can be modified through appropriate lifestyle, diet, herbal medicine, and acupuncture — making it a primary preventive medicine framework. Jing-Shen typology — the constitutional character of the Essence-Spirit axis determining individual psychological and physical tendencies — corresponds to the nine constitutional types as the TCM framework of constitutional individuation.

## Balanced Constitution

- The balanced He Ping constitution represents the optimal constitutional baseline with adequate Qi, Blood, Yin, and Yang.
- Physical signs include appropriate weight, good energy, regular sleep, and emotional stability.
- Maintenance through balanced lifestyle preserves the balanced constitution through life transitions.
- Even balanced constitutions shift under excessive stress, poor diet, and inadequate rest.

## Deficiency Constitutions

- Qi deficiency constitution manifests as fatigue, frequent illness, weak voice, and poor digestive function.
- Yang deficiency constitution manifests as cold sensitivity, low libido, frequent urination, and slow metabolism.
- Yin deficiency constitution manifests as heat sensations, night sweats, dry mouth, insomnia, and thin build.
- Each deficiency type has characteristic disease susceptibilities and specific dietary, lifestyle, and herbal recommendations.

## Excess Constitutions

- Phlegm-Dampness constitution manifests as obesity, heaviness, oily skin, and metabolic syndrome tendency.
- Damp-Heat constitution manifests as acne, inflammatory conditions, digestive heat, and infectious susceptibility.
- Blood stasis constitution manifests as dark complexion, pain conditions, varicosities, and cardiovascular risk.
- Qi stagnation constitution manifests as emotional sensitivity, sighing, chest tightness, and digestive irregularity.

## TCM Correspondence

- Kidney Jing as the constitutional inheritance determines the baseline constitutional type from birth.
- The nine constitutions correspond to characteristic Jing-Shen typologies with specific organ network emphases.
- Constitutional modification through targeted treatment corresponds to epigenetic modulation of constitutional expression.
- Preventive medicine based on constitutional type targets interventions to specific susceptibility patterns.
