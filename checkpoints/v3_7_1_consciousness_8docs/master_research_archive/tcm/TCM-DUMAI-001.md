# Du Mai - Governing Vessel

## Abstract

Du Mai the Governing Vessel ascends along the posterior spinal column from the perineum through every vertebra to the crown of the head, constituting the primary yang regulatory channel and the Sea of Yang Channels because every yang meridian intersects through this posterior midline pathway. Governs the spinal column structurally, regulates nervous system function, balances the mind and spirit at every level from brainstem through limbic to cortical awareness, and provides the primary constitutional yang resource all yang organ systems draw from when depleted. Runs as the Great Father Flow in complementary opposition to Ren Mai Great Mother Flow, the two forming the Central Channel pair that underlies all meridian development and constitutes the primary energetic axis of the body.

## Channel Pathway

- GV 1 (Chang Qiang) perineum-coccyx junction: origin point
- GV 4 (Ming Men / Gate of Life) L2-L3: constitutional fire, kidney yang root
- GV 9 (Zhi Yang / Utmost Yang) T7: solar plexus level crossing
- GV 12 (Shen Zhu / Body Pillar) T3: heart-back level
- GV 14 (Da Zhui / Reunion of Yang) C7: meeting point of all six yang meridians
- GV 16 (Feng Fu / Wind Palace) occiput: mind-clearing gateway to brain
- GV 20 (Bai Hui / Hundred Meeting Point) crown: all yin-yang meridians meet
- GV 24.5 (Yin Tang / Third Eye) between eyebrows: inner vision, intuition
- GV 28 upper gum: terminal point

## Master Point and Release Pairs

- SI 3 (Hou Xi / Back Stream): end of deepest palm crease on ulnar hand
- Primary quick release pair: SI3 + B62 (Calm Sleep below lateral malleolus)
