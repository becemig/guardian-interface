# Cupping and Gua Sha

## Abstract

Cupping creates negative pressure lifting skin and fascial layers while Gua Sha creates compressive friction strokes producing petechiae from extravasated red blood cells. Both modalities produce anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects through heme oxygenase-1 upregulation, local circulation enhancement, and fascial mechanoreception. Modern fascia research supports clinical observation that these techniques release myofascial restriction and reduce pain.

## Cupping Mechanisms

- Fire cupping creates suction by introducing flame into a glass cup then applying it to skin.
- Negative pressure lifts skin and superficial fascia creating tensile forces in connective tissue.
- Fascial adhesions separate and local circulation including lymphatic flow increases.
- Sliding cupping with oil combines suction with gliding for broader myofascial release.

## Gua Sha Mechanisms

- Friction strokes produce controlled micro-trauma releasing extravasated red blood cells as Sha petechiae.
- Heme oxygenase-1 upregulation from Sha breakdown produces systemic anti-inflammatory effects.
- Sha color and resolution speed indicates severity and nature of Blood stasis.
- Dark slow-resolving Sha indicates severe cold Blood stasis while bright fast-resolving Sha indicates heat.

## Back Shu Cupping

- BL-13 Feishu cupping treats Lung and respiratory conditions through segmental fascial effects.
- BL-23 Shenshu cupping tonifies Kidney Qi and treats lumbar pain and fatigue.
- Walking cupping along the Bladder meridian back line treats whole-body Qi and Blood stagnation.
