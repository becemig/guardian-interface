# Zhen Jiu Da Cheng

## Abstract

The Zhen Jiu Da Cheng — Great Compendium of Acupuncture and Moxibustion — compiled by Yang Ji Zhou and published in 1601 during the Ming dynasty, is the most comprehensive synthesis of acupuncture and moxibustion knowledge in the classical literature, drawing on all prior texts from the Nei Jing through the Ming dynasty to create a ten-volume encyclopedic reference that became the standard clinical text for acupuncture practice in China, Japan, Korea, and Vietnam. Yang Ji Zhou organized the accumulated clinical experience of two thousand years of acupuncture practice into systematic point combinations, needling sequences, and clinical protocols for the full range of conditions encountered in practice. The Zhen Jiu Da Cheng preserves numerous clinical techniques and point combinations not found elsewhere in the classical literature, including the famous ode to the jade dragon Bi Yu Ge point combinations and the Golden needle ode Jin Zhen Fu sequences that encode clinical experience in mnemonic verse form. The eight influential points Hui Xue are systematically applied in the Zhen Jiu Da Cheng — Zhongwan CV-12 for the Zangfu organs, Zhangmen LV-13 for the solid Zang organs, Geshu BL-17 for Blood, Yanglingquan GB-34 for sinews, Xuanzhong GB-39 for marrow, Taiyuan LU-9 for vessels, Danzhong CV-17 for Qi, and Dazhui DU-14 for Yang — providing a systematic framework for constitutional treatment through influential point selection. Sun Si Miao's thirteen ghost points for psychiatric and spirit disorders are documented and clinically contextualized in the Zhen Jiu Da Cheng, establishing the acupuncture protocol for severe mental-emotional conditions that remains influential in modern psychiatric acupuncture.

## Eight Influential Points

- CV-12 Zhongwan: influential point for all Zangfu organs — the center of all organ treatment.
- BL-17 Geshu: influential point for Blood — the foundation of all Blood disorder treatment.
- GB-34 Yanglingquan: influential point for sinews — the foundation of all musculoskeletal treatment.
- CV-17 Danzhong: influential point for Qi — the Sea of Qi governing all Qi disorder treatment.

## Ghost Points

- Sun Si Miao thirteen ghost points address severe psychiatric conditions through direct Shen regulation.
- GV-26 Renzhong ghost palace: the primary emergency and psychiatric point.
- LU-11 Shaoshang ghost letter: Lung-Shen connection for grief-driven psychiatric patterns.
- The ghost point protocol is needled in sequence for conditions where Shen is severely disturbed.

## Clinical Odes

- Bi Yu Ge Jade Dragon Ode: systematic point combinations for common clinical presentations in verse.
- Jin Zhen Fu Golden Needle Ode: needling technique sequences encoded in mnemonic verse.
- These clinical odes encode decades of practitioner experience into memorizable clinical protocols.
- The ode format reflects the oral transmission tradition of clinical knowledge before widespread literacy.
