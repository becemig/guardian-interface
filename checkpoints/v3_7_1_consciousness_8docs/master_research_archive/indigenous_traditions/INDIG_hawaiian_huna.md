# Hawaiian Huna

## Abstract

Hawaiian Huna — the esoteric knowledge system of the traditional Hawaiian Kahuna healers — presents a sophisticated model of the human being as three selves operating through a shared life force Mana, connected to all other beings through invisible aka cords, and capable of healing through the restoration of right relationship Pono through the reconciliation process Ho'oponopono. The three selves of Huna — the Unihipili lower self, the Uhane middle self, and the Aumakua higher self — correspond to different levels of the psyche and body: the Unihipili is the subconscious body-based self that stores memories, emotions, and somatic patterns; the Uhane is the conscious rational self that reasons and makes decisions; and the Aumakua is the higher self or superconscious — the connection to the divine and the source of spiritual guidance, healing power, and inspiration. Mana — the Hawaiian life force equivalent to Qi, Prana, and other universal life force concepts — is generated primarily by the Unihipili through the process of breathing and physical vitality, stored in the aka or etheric body, and can be accumulated, directed, and transmitted for healing purposes. Aka cords are the invisible energetic connections between people formed through sustained attention and relationship — every significant relationship leaves aka cord connections that transmit both positive and negative energetic influences between the connected people. Ho'oponopono — the Hawaiian reconciliation practice — is a family-based healing ceremony in which conflicts, grievances, and accumulated negative energies are resolved through prayer, confession, forgiveness, and the release of aka cord entanglements. The modern adaptation of Ho'oponopono distills the practice into four phrases: I love you, I am sorry, Please forgive me, Thank you — directed to the Aumakua and to all the persons involved in the problem, producing the energetic clearing that allows healing to occur.

## Three Selves

- Unihipili lower self: subconscious body-based self storing memory, emotion, and somatic patterns.
- Uhane middle self: conscious rational self reasoning and making decisions.
- Aumakua higher self: superconscious connection to the divine, source of inspiration and healing power.
- Healing requires all three selves working in harmony — the Uhane directing, the Unihipili supplying Mana.

## Ho'oponopono

- Family reconciliation ceremony resolving conflicts through prayer, confession, forgiveness, and release.
- Modern adaptation: I love you, I am sorry, Please forgive me, Thank you — directed to source.
- Releases aka cord entanglements that transmit negative influences between conflicted parties.
- This corresponds to the TCM understanding that interpersonal conflict produces Liver Qi stagnation and Heart Shen disturbance.

## Mana and Aka

- Mana is generated through breath and physical vitality — accumulated through healthy living and practice.
- Aka etheric body stores and transmits Mana — the medium of healing transmission.
- Aka cords form through sustained attention and relationship — both nurturing and draining connections.
- This corresponds to the TCM understanding that Qi is transmitted through intention and physical contact in healing.

## TCM Correspondence

- Mana life force corresponds to TCM Qi as the universal life force animating all living beings.
- Three selves Unihipili-Uhane-Aumakua corresponds to TCM Jing-Qi-Shen as the three levels of the human being.
- Ho'oponopono reconciliation corresponds to the TCM understanding that emotional and relational resolution is essential for physical healing.
- Aka cords corresponds to the TCM understanding that Qi connections exist between people in significant relationships.
