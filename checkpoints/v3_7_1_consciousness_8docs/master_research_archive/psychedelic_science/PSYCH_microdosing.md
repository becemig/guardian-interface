# Microdosing Evidence and Practice

## Abstract

Microdosing evidence and practice — the scientific investigation of the practice of taking sub-perceptual doses of psychedelic compounds typically one-tenth of a full dose every two to four days to produce purported benefits in mood, creativity, focus, and productivity without the full psychedelic experience — has emerged as a major area of psychedelic research driven by widespread popular adoption, with the initial optimistic anecdotal reports requiring calibration against placebo-controlled trials that reveal significant expectancy effects and more modest measurable benefits than self-reported outcomes suggest. Microdosing protocols — the standard microdosing schedules including Fadiman's one day on two days off protocol, the Stamets stack combining psilocybin with lion's mane mushroom and niacin on four days on three days off, and the every other day protocol, with psilocybin doses of 0.1-0.3 grams of dried mushrooms approximately 1-3 mg psilocybin and LSD doses of 5-20 micrograms as the most common psychedelic choices. Placebo-controlled microdosing trials — the Imperial College self-blinding citizen science study and the subsequent randomized placebo-controlled trials demonstrating that while participants correctly guess their condition above chance suggesting some pharmacological signal, the improvements in mood and wellbeing are significantly attenuated compared to open-label reports, with expectancy accounting for a substantial proportion of reported benefits and the pharmacological contribution being modest in most outcome measures. Microdosing mechanisms — the proposed biological mechanisms for microdosing effects including sub-threshold 5-HT2A agonism producing neuroplasticity through TrkB and BDNF pathways without perceptual effects, sigma-1 receptor activation producing neuroprotection and neuroplasticity, and the direct TrkB binding of psilocin at sub-hallucinogenic concentrations activating neuroplasticity programs. The subtle cultivation Wei Gong correspondence in TCM describes the classical Daoist cultivation practice of working with subtle energies through gentle consistent practice rather than dramatic intervention — a correspondence with microdosing as a subtle pharmacological cultivation practice rather than the dramatic full-dose psychedelic journey.

## Standard Protocols

- Fadiman protocol: day on two days off — prevents tolerance accumulation.
- Stamets stack: psilocybin lion's mane niacin — four days on three off.
- Psilocybin dose: 0.1-0.3 grams dried mushroom — sub-perceptual.
- LSD dose: 5-20 micrograms — sub-perceptual.

## Placebo-Controlled Evidence

- Self-blinding study: participants guess condition above chance — pharmacological signal present.
- Effect size: significantly smaller than open-label reports — expectancy contribution large.
- Mood: modest improvements in some controlled studies.
- Creativity focus: mixed results across controlled studies.

## Proposed Mechanisms

- Sub-threshold 5-HT2A: neuroplasticity without perceptual effects.
- Direct TrkB binding: psilocin activates TrkB at sub-hallucinogenic concentrations.
- Sigma-1 activation: neuroprotection neuroplasticity at trace concentrations.
- Anti-inflammatory: 5-HT2A immunomodulatory effects at low doses.
