# Psilocybin Clinical Trials

## Abstract

Psilocybin clinical trials — the rigorous scientific investigation of therapeutic psilocybin in randomized controlled settings that has produced compelling evidence for efficacy in treatment-resistant depression, major depressive disorder, cancer-related existential distress, alcohol use disorder, and tobacco addiction — represents the most significant development in psychiatry since the introduction of SSRIs, with effect sizes that dwarf conventional antidepressants and therapeutic mechanisms that address the underlying psychological rigidity of psychiatric disorders rather than merely suppressing symptoms, and with the mystical-type experiences reliably produced by therapeutic psilocybin doses corresponding to the TCM understanding of Shen opening as the profound expansion of consciousness that constitutes spiritual healing. Depression treatment evidence — the landmark 2021 Imperial College London randomized trial comparing psilocybin to escitalopram in major depressive disorder demonstrating comparable primary outcome efficacy with superiority of psilocybin on multiple secondary measures of emotional wellbeing, meaning, and connectedness, the Johns Hopkins open-label trials in treatment-resistant depression showing rapid and sustained antidepressant effects lasting months after two sessions, and the COMPASS Pathways Phase 2b trial of COMP360 synthetic psilocybin demonstrating significant dose-dependent antidepressant effects at three weeks. Cancer anxiety and existential distress — Roland Griffiths' and Charles Grob's pioneering trials demonstrating that a single high-dose psilocybin session produced substantial and sustained reductions in depression, anxiety, and existential distress in patients with life-threatening cancer diagnoses, with effects persisting at six-month follow-up rated as among the most personally meaningful experiences of participants' lives, mediated by the completeness of the mystical-type experience during the session. The Shen opening sacred medicine correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding that certain sacred medicines can open and expand the Shen spirit-mind, allowing direct encounter with the deeper nature of consciousness and reality — a traditional framework for understanding the profound transformative experiences produced by psilocybin that may constitute the primary mechanism of its therapeutic effects.

## Depression Trial Evidence

- Imperial College 2021: psilocybin vs escitalopram — comparable QIDS-SR primary outcome.
- Secondary measures: psilocybin superior on emotional blunting meaning connectedness.
- Johns Hopkins TRD: 71 percent response rate 54 percent remission at four weeks.
- COMPASS Phase 2b: dose-dependent antidepressant effect at three weeks post-session.

## Cancer Existential Distress

- Griffiths 2016: single session produces sustained depression anxiety reduction at 6 months.
- Grob 2011: pilot RCT in advanced cancer — significant anxiety reduction.

## Addiction Treatment

- Tobacco: 80 percent abstinence at 6 months in open-label trial — exceeds all pharmacotherapy.
- Alcohol: significant reduction in drinking days and heavy drinking days vs placebo.
- Mechanism: insight into addiction patterns increased self-efficacy openness to change.
- Predictor: mystical experience completeness predicts addiction treatment outcome.
