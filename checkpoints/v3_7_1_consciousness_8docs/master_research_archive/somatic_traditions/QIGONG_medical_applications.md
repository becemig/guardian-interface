# Medical Qigong: Clinical Applications, Emission Qi, and Therapeutic Protocols

## Abstract

Medical Qigong is the clinical branch of Qigong practice in which trained practitioners use their own cultivated Qi to assess and treat patients through emission of external Qi Wai Qi. The practitioner develops the capacity to sense and project Qi through the Laogong point PC-8 in the palm and through intention and breath. Medical Qigong protocols address specific clinical presentations including cancer support, neurological rehabilitation, pain management, and immune enhancement. Self-practice Qigong prescribed as therapeutic exercise is increasingly integrated into integrative oncology and rehabilitation medicine. The evidence base for Qigong in chronic disease management continues to grow across multiple systematic reviews.

## Emission Qi — Wai Qi

- Wai Qi external Qi emission is the practice of projecting cultivated Qi from the practitioner to the patient.
- The Laogong point PC-8 in the palm is the primary emission site for external Qi projection.
- Practitioners train emission Qi through years of internal cultivation practice before clinical application.
- Some emission Qi practitioners demonstrate measurable far-infrared emission from the palm during Qi projection.

## Clinical Applications

### Cancer Support

- Medical Qigong reduces fatigue, anxiety, and depression in cancer patients undergoing treatment.
- Immune enhancement through NK cell and lymphocyte activation supports anti-tumor immunity.
- Quality of life improvements are consistently documented across cancer populations in randomized trials.
- Qigong protocols are increasingly integrated into integrative oncology programs at major cancer centers.

### Neurological Rehabilitation

- Qigong practice improves motor function, balance, and quality of life in Parkinson disease.
- Stroke rehabilitation benefits include improved motor recovery and reduced spasticity.
- Cognitive function preservation in mild cognitive impairment and dementia risk reduction are documented.

### Pain Management

- Medical Qigong reduces chronic pain scores in musculoskeletal, cancer, and fibromyalgia populations.
- Endorphin release and descending pain modulation provide plausible neurophysiological mechanisms.
- The mindfulness component of Qigong activates pain acceptance and catastrophizing reduction pathways.

## Self-Practice Protocols

- Eight Brocades Ba Duan Jin is the most widely researched self-practice Qigong form with strong evidence base.
- Six Healing Sounds Liu Zi Jue combines breath, sound, and movement to regulate specific organ systems.
- Five Animal Frolics Wu Qin Xi uses animal movement archetypes to stimulate five element organ systems.
- Yi Jin Jing Muscle Tendon Changing Classic develops myofascial strength and Qi flow simultaneously.
