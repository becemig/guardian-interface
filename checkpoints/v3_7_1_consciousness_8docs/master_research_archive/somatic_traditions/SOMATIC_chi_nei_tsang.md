# Chi Nei Tsang

## Abstract

Chi Nei Tsang CNT is a Taoist abdominal organ massage developed by Mantak Chia that works directly with the internal organs to release emotional holding, improve organ function, and restore Qi flow through the middle Jiao. CNT is based on the Taoist understanding that the abdominal organs store unprocessed emotional experiences as knots of stagnant Qi — wind knots — that obstruct normal organ function and energy flow. The navel is treated as the center of the body's Qi field — the original point of nourishment through the umbilical cord and the convergence point of all meridians in the prenatal body. Work begins at the navel and moves outward to address each organ in sequence. Abdominal breathing is cultivated throughout to enhance the self-massage effect of the diaphragm on the abdominal organs. CNT corresponds directly to visceral manipulation in osteopathy and addresses the same middle Jiao organ functions that the Spleen, Stomach, Liver, and Kidney govern in TCM.

## Wind Knots

- Wind knots are tangible areas of tissue tension in the abdominal organs corresponding to stagnant Qi.
- Each organ stores characteristic emotional patterns — Liver stores anger, Kidney stores fear, Lung stores grief.
- Skilled palpation identifies wind knots as areas of resistance, temperature change, or pulsation asymmetry.
- Gentle sustained pressure with specific spiral techniques releases wind knots over the treatment session.

## Navel Center Work

- The navel is the anatomical center of the prenatal Qi field and the convergence of all meridians.
- CNT begins with navel center assessment — palpating the aortic pulse and sensing Qi distribution.
- Releasing navel center tension improves Qi distribution throughout the entire meridian system.
- The navel center corresponds to the lower Dan Tian as the primary Qi storage and distribution center.

## Organ Sequence

- Work progresses from the small intestine center outward to the large intestine, stomach, liver, and spleen.
- Each organ is addressed with specific techniques matching its Qi nature — dispersing for excess, tonifying for deficiency.
- Organ detoxification through CNT corresponds to improving lymphatic drainage and visceral mobility.
- The emotional dimension of organ work releases stored emotional patterns held as chronic tissue tension.

## TCM Correspondence

- Middle Jiao organs — Spleen, Stomach, Liver — are the primary focus of Chi Nei Tsang work.
- Wind knots as stagnant Qi in the organs correspond to Liver Qi stagnation and Blood stasis patterns.
- Navel center as the convergence of meridians corresponds to CV-8 Shenque and the lower Dan Tian.
- Abdominal breathing cultivated in CNT corresponds to the Qigong practice of abdominal Dan Tian breathing.
