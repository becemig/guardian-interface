# Baguazhang Cosmology

## Abstract

Baguazhang is rooted in one of the most comprehensive cosmological frameworks in Chinese thought — the I Ching system of eight trigrams and sixty-four hexagrams that maps the fundamental patterns of change and transformation in all phenomena. The art was systematized by Dong Haichuan in Beijing in the late nineteenth century, though he drew on pre-existing Taoist circle walking practices of the Quan Zhen and other Taoist orders that used sustained circular walking as a meditation and cultivation practice. The cosmological foundation of Baguazhang rests on the Taoist creation sequence: Wu Ji the undifferentiated void gives rise to Tai Ji the supreme ultimate which differentiates into Yin and Yang the two poles which generate the four images Si Xiang which unfold into the eight trigrams Ba Gua which combine into sixty-four hexagrams representing all possible states of change. The Pre-Heaven arrangement Xiantian of the trigrams maps the primordial energetic relationships before manifestation — Heaven and Earth as the primary poles with the other six trigrams arranged in their original complementary relationships. The Post-Heaven arrangement Houtian maps the seasonal and directional correspondences of the trigrams in the manifest world — this is the arrangement used in TCM, Feng Shui, and daily practice. The Baguazhang body is the cosmological body — the practitioner embodies the trigram system through sustained circle walking and palm practice, progressively aligning their psychophysiology with the cosmological principles of the I Ching. Bagua Medical Qigong extracts the health-cultivating aspects of Baguazhang practice for therapeutic application.

## Dong Haichuan

- Dong Haichuan systematized Baguazhang in Beijing in the 1860s-1880s drawing on Taoist circle walking traditions.
- He taught students of diverse martial backgrounds producing the lineage diversity of modern Baguazhang.
- The art integrates Taoist cosmological principles with sophisticated martial application.
- Dong's teaching emphasized the cosmological dimension as primary — the martial application as secondary expression.

## Pre and Post Heaven Arrangements

- Pre-Heaven arrangement maps the primordial trigram relationships before manifestation — the ideal archetypal order.
- Post-Heaven arrangement maps the seasonal directional correspondences — the manifest operational order.
- Baguazhang circle walking embodies both arrangements — Pre-Heaven as the internal cultivation, Post-Heaven as the directional training.
- The transition between arrangements corresponds to the Taoist cultivation of returning from Post to Pre Heaven.

## Taoist Cosmological Body

- The Baguazhang practitioner progressively embodies the trigram system through sustained practice.
- Each trigram quality is cultivated through its corresponding palm and direction training.
- The goal is to embody the complete I Ching — to become a living expression of the eight fundamental forces.
- This corresponds to the Taoist cultivation goal of aligning the individual with the Tao through body practice.

## TCM Correspondence

- Eight trigrams corresponding to eight extraordinary vessels corresponds to the pakua organization of the constitutional body.
- Pre-Heaven arrangement corresponds to the prenatal Jing constitution — the original endowment.
- Post-Heaven arrangement corresponds to the postnatal Qi — the operational daily cultivation.
- Bagua Medical Qigong corresponds to the therapeutic application of TCM principles through movement medicine.
