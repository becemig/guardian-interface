# Interoception and Emotion

## Abstract

Interoception — the sensing of the body's internal state — provides the physiological basis for emotional experience through the somatic marker hypothesis and interoceptive prediction frameworks. Antonio Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis proposes that emotional decision-making relies on somatic signals from the body that mark options as good or bad through visceral feeling states. Emotional granularity — the capacity to differentiate among similar emotional states — depends on interoceptive precision and conceptual emotional knowledge. Alexithymia — the difficulty identifying and describing one's own feelings — reflects impaired interoceptive awareness and is associated with psychosomatic conditions and impaired empathy. The felt sense described by Eugene Gendlin is the holistic bodily knowing that precedes verbal articulation of emotional experience. Guardian Interface telemetry cultivates interoceptive awareness by providing real-time feedback on physiological states during practice.

## Somatic Marker Hypothesis

- Somatic markers are body signals that tag potential outcomes of decisions with emotional valence.
- The ventromedial prefrontal cortex integrates somatic markers from the body into value-based decision making.
- Damage to vmPFC produces decision-making impairment despite intact analytical reasoning.
- Somatic markers operate through both actual body state changes and somatic simulations in the insular cortex.

## Emotional Granularity

- Emotional granularity is the capacity to differentiate among fine-grained emotional states within broad categories.
- High granularity individuals can distinguish anxiety from excitement, sadness from disappointment, and anger from contempt.
- Granularity depends on interoceptive precision and richness of emotional conceptual knowledge.
- Practice that cultivates interoceptive attention improves emotional granularity and self-regulatory capacity.

## Alexithymia

- Alexithymia describes difficulty identifying feelings, distinguishing emotions from body sensations, and describing feelings.
- It reflects impaired interoceptive awareness and reduced connectivity between insular and prefrontal cortex.
- Alexithymia is associated with psychosomatic conditions, impaired empathy, and substance use disorders.
- Somatic practices including Tai Chi, Qigong, and body-centered psychotherapy reduce alexithymia.
