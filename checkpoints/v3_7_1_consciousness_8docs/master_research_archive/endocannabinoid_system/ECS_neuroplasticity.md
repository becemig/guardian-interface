# ECS Neuroplasticity: Synaptic Remodeling and Structural Plasticity

## Abstract

The endocannabinoid system is a master regulator of synaptic plasticity, governing both short-term and long-term changes in synaptic strength through retrograde CB1 signaling. Endocannabinoid-mediated long-term depression (eCB-LTD) is the most widespread form of synaptic plasticity in the brain, occurring at glutamatergic and GABAergic synapses throughout the cortex, hippocampus, striatum, cerebellum, and amygdala. The ECS also promotes adult hippocampal neurogenesis, regulates BDNF expression and TrkB signaling, governs fear memory extinction, and mediates the synaptic effects of antidepressants. Disruption of ECS-mediated plasticity underlies vulnerability to PTSD, depression, and addiction.

## Short-Term Plasticity

- DSI (depolarization-induced suppression of inhibition): postsynaptic depolarization triggers AEA/2-AG release, retrogradely suppresses GABA release for seconds to minutes
- DSE (depolarization-induced suppression of excitation): same mechanism at glutamatergic synapses
- Function: local homeostatic dampening of overactive circuits - prevents excitotoxicity
- Time course: onset within 1-2 seconds, duration 30-60 seconds

## Long-Term Depression (eCB-LTD)

- Induction: sustained postsynaptic activity activates mGluR5 and DAGL producing 2-AG burst
- Expression: prolonged reduction in presynaptic neurotransmitter release via CB1
- Sites: striatum (habit formation), hippocampus (memory), amygdala (fear), PFC (executive control)
- Heterosynaptic LTD: 2-AG can diffuse to neighboring synapses - volume transmission
- Requirement: CB1 activation plus postsynaptic calcium elevation for full induction

## BDNF and TrkB Signaling

- CB1 activation upregulates BDNF mRNA and protein in hippocampus and cortex
- BDNF-TrkB cascade promotes dendritic arborization, spine density, synaptic strengthening
- Antidepressant mechanism: ECS activation required for BDNF-dependent antidepressant effects
- FAAH inhibition mimics antidepressant BDNF upregulation without direct CB1 agonism
- Stress suppresses BDNF via CRF-FAAH axis - ECS restoration reverses this

## Adult Neurogenesis

- CB1 and CB2 expressed on neural progenitor cells in hippocampal dentate gyrus
- ECS activation promotes proliferation, survival, and differentiation of new neurons
- THC and CBD both independently promote neurogenesis at low doses
- Hippocampal neurogenesis required for ECS-mediated anxiolytic and antidepressant effects
- Chronic stress suppresses neurogenesis - ECS restoration is a therapeutic target

## Fear Extinction

- CB1 in basolateral amygdala required for extinction of conditioned fear responses
- CB1 knockout mice show impaired fear extinction - fear memories become permanent
- AEA at amygdala CB1 drives extinction learning by weakening fear memory synapses
- PTSD model: deficient ECS signaling prevents normal fear extinction
- Therapeutic: FAAH inhibition or low-dose CBD enhances extinction in PTSD models
