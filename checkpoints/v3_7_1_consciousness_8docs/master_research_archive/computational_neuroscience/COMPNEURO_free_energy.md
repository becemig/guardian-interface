# Free Energy Principle

## Abstract

Free energy principle — Karl Friston's ambitious theoretical framework proposing that all self-organizing biological systems from single cells to brains maintain their existence by minimizing variational free energy — a quantity from statistical physics that bounds the surprise or improbability of sensory states — through perception that updates internal models to better explain sensory data and action that samples sensory data confirming internal model predictions, with the framework unifying the Bayesian brain hypothesis, predictive coding, active inference, and the theory of autopoietic self-organization within a single mathematical formalism grounded in the imperative of biological self-preservation. Variational free energy — the tractable upper bound on the true surprise of sensory data used as the objective function minimized by inference and learning in the free energy principle, decomposable into the accuracy of the generative model in explaining sensory data minus the complexity of the model updates required to explain the data, with minimizing free energy equivalent to maximizing model evidence and therefore to Bayesian inference when the variational distribution over hidden states is optimal. Markov blankets and self-organization — the statistical boundary defined by the Markov blanket separating internal states of a system from external states through sensory and active states at the boundary, providing the formal definition of self-organization as the maintenance of a statistical boundary that preserves the mutual information between internal and external states, and allowing the free energy principle to be applied to any system with a Markov blanket from individual cells through organs to organisms and social systems. Autopoiesis and living systems — the connection of the free energy principle to Humberto Maturana and Francisco Varela's theory of autopoiesis — the self-production and self-maintenance that characterizes living systems — with the free energy principle providing the mathematical formalization of how autopoietic systems maintain their organization against entropic dissolution by minimizing the surprise of their sensory states through continuous active self-modeling and environmental sampling. The Dao Te Ching self-organizing principle correspondence describes the classical Daoist understanding of the Dao as the self-organizing principle that spontaneously generates and maintains ordered patterns from apparent chaos without external direction — a philosophical correspondence with the free energy principle's mathematical account of how biological systems spontaneously self-organize and maintain their ordered existence through variational free energy minimization.

## Variational Free Energy

- Surprise: negative log model evidence — FE is upper bound on surprise.
- Decomposition: FE equals complexity minus accuracy — or energy minus entropy.
- Minimization: perception minimizes FE by updating beliefs active inference by acting.
- Equivalence: minimizing FE equivalent to maximizing Bayesian model evidence.

## Markov Blankets

- Definition: set of states separating internal from external — parents children and co-parents.
- Sensory states: external influences on internal states — input boundary.
- Active states: internal influences on external states — output boundary.
- Self-organization: maintaining Markov blanket integrity — statistical self-preservation.

## Expected Free Energy

- Future-oriented: expected FE evaluates future policies before acting.
- Epistemic value: information gain from policy — drives exploration curiosity.
- Pragmatic value: expected reward from policy — drives exploitation.
- Balance: expected FE balances exploration and exploitation — optimal behavior.
