# UV Medicine and Vitamin D

## Abstract

UV medicine and vitamin D — the field studying the therapeutic and physiological effects of solar ultraviolet radiation, encompassing vitamin D photosynthesis, the direct immunomodulatory effects of UV radiation, the nitric oxide release from UV-irradiated skin, and the epidemiological evidence linking sun avoidance to increased mortality from multiple causes — reveals sunlight as an essential medicine whose absence contributes to a global pandemic of vitamin D deficiency affecting over one billion people worldwide and corresponding to the TCM understanding of Heaven light as an essential Yang Qi nourishing the Kidney Jing and constitutional vitality. Solar UVB photosynthesis of vitamin D — the photochemical conversion of 7-dehydrocholesterol in the skin to pre-vitamin D3 by UVB radiation at 290-315 nm, followed by thermal isomerization to vitamin D3 and hydroxylation in the liver and kidney to the active hormone 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3 — is the primary source of vitamin D for most humans throughout evolutionary history, with dietary sources insufficient to maintain adequate levels in the absence of sun exposure. Vitamin D receptor genomic actions — the nuclear receptor VDR acting as a transcription factor when bound to 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3 to regulate the expression of over 2000 genes in virtually every tissue — governs calcium and phosphate absorption, bone mineralization, immune function, cell proliferation and differentiation, cardiovascular function, and neurodevelopment, explaining the widespread health consequences of vitamin D deficiency beyond the classical rickets and osteomalacia. Vitamin D immune regulation — the VDR expression in virtually all immune cells and the production of cathelicidin and defensins stimulated by vitamin D in innate immune cells — provides the molecular mechanism for the observed associations between vitamin D deficiency and increased susceptibility to respiratory infections, autoimmune diseases, and cancer, with vitamin D acting as a master regulator of the transition between pro-inflammatory and tolerogenic immune responses. The vitamin D deficiency pandemic — with an estimated 41 percent of US adults and over one billion people worldwide having insufficient vitamin D levels due to indoor living, sun avoidance, sunscreen use, and inadequate dietary intake — represents one of the most widespread and correctable nutritional deficiencies, with emerging evidence linking vitamin D insufficiency to increased risk of respiratory infections, autoimmune diseases, cardiovascular disease, depression, cognitive decline, and all-cause mortality. The Heaven light nourishing Jing correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding of natural sunlight as an expression of Heaven Yang Qi that nourishes the constitutional Kidney Jing — a correspondence supported by the modern evidence that solar radiation through vitamin D synthesis is essential for constitutional immune function, bone health, and vitality.

## Vitamin D Synthesis Pathway

- 7-dehydrocholesterol in skin: UVB 290-315 nm cleaves B ring to form pre-vitamin D3.
- Thermal isomerization: pre-vitamin D3 converts to vitamin D3 over 24-48 hours.
- Liver 25-hydroxylation: produces 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 — main circulating form.
- Kidney 1-alpha-hydroxylation: produces 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3 — active hormone.

## VDR Genomic Actions

- VDR expressed in virtually every tissue — over 2000 target genes.
- Classical actions: calcium phosphate absorption bone mineralization.
- Non-classical actions: immune regulation cell proliferation cardiovascular neurodevelopment.
- VDR polymorphisms: influence vitamin D responsiveness and disease susceptibility.

## Vitamin D Immune Regulation

- Cathelicidin production: innate antimicrobial peptide — reduced in vitamin D deficiency.
- Th1-Th2 balance: vitamin D promotes Th2 and Treg responses — reduces autoimmunity.
- COVID-19: vitamin D deficiency associated with severe disease — immune regulation mechanism.
