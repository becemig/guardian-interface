# Tensegrity and Biotensegrity

## Abstract

Tensegrity and biotensegrity — the application of Buckminster Fuller's tensegrity structural principle to biological systems, from the cytoskeleton of individual cells through the musculoskeletal system of the whole body — provides a fundamentally different mechanical model of biological structure from the classical lever-beam-fulcrum model, describing instead a system of continuous tensional elements in which compressive elements float without touching, generating pre-stress throughout the system that enables non-linear mechanical responses, global force distribution, and the simultaneous stability and flexibility characteristic of living movement. Buckminster Fuller's tensegrity principle — the structural principle in which isolated compression members are suspended within and stabilized by a continuous network of tension elements, with the name coined from tensional integrity, producing structures that are lighter and stronger than conventional compression-based architectures — was identified by Donald Ingber as the fundamental principle governing both cytoskeletal mechanics in individual cells and the organization of the musculoskeletal system, with the insight that bones are the compression struts floating within the continuous tensional network of fascia, ligament, and muscle. Cellular tensegrity — Ingber's model demonstrating that the cytoskeleton of individual cells is organized as a tensegrity structure with microtubules as compression struts, actin filaments as tension cables, and intermediate filaments as the geodesic network distributing forces, with pre-stress maintained by actomyosin contractility — explains how cells can transmit mechanical forces from the extracellular matrix through integrin receptors directly to the nucleus, providing the mechanism for mechanotransduction and the sensitivity of cells to substrate stiffness that governs differentiation. Whole-body biotensegrity — Stephen Levin's model extending tensegrity principles to the musculoskeletal system, with bones as discontinuous compression members suspended within the continuous fascial-ligamentous-muscular tension network, and with the spine modeled as a tensegrity mast rather than a compression column — predicts the non-linear, whole-body load distribution and injury response patterns observed clinically and explains why local fascial restriction produces distant pain and dysfunction through the global tension network. The Yuan Qi structural field correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding of Yuan Qi as the constitutional organizing force that maintains the structural integrity and coherence of the organism — a correspondence with the biotensegrity model's prestress field as the fundamental organizational principle maintaining structural coherence across all scales from cell to organism.

## Cellular Tensegrity

- Integrin mechanotransduction: ECM forces transmitted through cytoskeleton to nucleus.
