# Stomach Reception and Descending

## Abstract

The Stomach as the yang organ paired with the Spleen in the Earth element governs the reception and preliminary transformation of food and fluid — the process described as rotting and ripening. The Stomach receives food, begins its breakdown, and directs the resulting turbid matter downward to the Small Intestine for further separation. The Stomach Qi must descend — any reversal of this downward direction produces rebellious Stomach Qi with nausea, vomiting, hiccups, and belching. The Spleen-Stomach pair constitutes the middle Jiao as the central axis of post-heaven Qi production and the pivot of the entire digestive system. ST-36 Zusanli the leg three miles point is the most important tonification point in the entire system strengthening Stomach and Spleen Qi and post-heaven production. Appetite as the Stomach's expression of receptivity to nourishment corresponds to the will to engage with and receive what life offers.

## Reception and Rotting-Ripening

- The Stomach receives food and fluid beginning the transformation through its warming rotting and ripening action.
- This preliminary transformation relies on Stomach Yin to provide the necessary moisture and Stomach Yang for warmth.
- The refined essence extracted is sent upward to the Spleen for further transformation and distribution.
- The turbid remainder is sent downward to the Small Intestine for separation of clear from turbid.

## Descending Function

- Stomach Qi must descend — this is its fundamental directional dynamic complementing the Spleen's ascending.
- Rebellious Stomach Qi reversing upward produces nausea, vomiting, hiccups, acid reflux, and belching.
- Liver Qi invading the Stomach is the most common cause of rebellious Stomach Qi in clinical practice.
- ST-21 Liangmen and CV-12 Zhongwan are the primary points to direct Stomach Qi downward.

## Stomach Yin

- Stomach Yin provides the moisture necessary for the rotting and ripening transformation.
- Stomach Yin deficiency produces dry mouth, hunger without appetite, dry stools, and a peeled tongue coat.
- Heat and dryness from febrile disease, excess spicy food, or Yin-deficient constitution easily injure Stomach Yin.
- Mai Men Dong and Sha Shen are the primary herbs nourishing Stomach Yin in clinical practice.

## TCM Correspondence

- ST-36 Zusanli as the He sea point of the Stomach is the premier tonification point strengthening post-heaven Qi.
- The Spleen-Stomach pair as the central Earth axis governs the production of all post-heaven Qi and Blood.
- Appetite corresponding to receptivity to nourishment reflects the Stomach's openness to receiving what life offers.
- Worry and overthinking injuring the Spleen-Stomach pair corresponds to the gut-brain axis stress response.
