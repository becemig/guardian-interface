# Wei Qi Defensive Energy

## Abstract

Wei Qi defensive energy is the most exterior and active form of Qi in the body — a Yang-natured, warm, fierce, and fast-moving energy that circulates in the exterior of the body between the skin and muscles, warming and nourishing the skin and muscles, regulating the opening and closing of the pores, and protecting the body from the invasion of external pathogenic factors Wind, Cold, Heat, Dampness, Dryness, and Fire. The Lung is primarily responsible for diffusing Wei Qi to the body surface through its dispersing function — Wei Qi is derived from the Gu Qi of food digestion processed by the Spleen and Stomach and then diffused by the Lung to the surface. During the day Wei Qi circulates in the Yang portions of the body — the exterior, the head, and the back — traversing the six Yang meridians twenty-five times. At night Wei Qi enters the interior circulating through the Yin organs twenty-five times. This daily cycling of Wei Qi from exterior to interior and back corresponds to the sleep-wake cycle — when Wei Qi enters the interior at night the eyes close and sleep begins, when Wei Qi returns to the exterior at dawn the eyes open and waking begins. The Triple Burner is responsible for the distribution of Wei Qi throughout the entire body including its movement between exterior and interior. Wind invasion — the most common external pathogenic factor — penetrates the exterior when Wei Qi is weakened, typically entering at GB-20 Fengchi and BL-12 Fengmen. Modern research documents acupuncture and Qigong effects on immune parameters corresponding to Wei Qi regulation.

## Surface Protection

- Wei Qi warms the skin and muscles maintaining the defensive barrier against external pathogenic invasion.
- It regulates the opening and closing of the pores controlling sweating and the movement of fluids.
- Strong Wei Qi prevents external pathogens from penetrating to the interior organ level.
- Wei Qi weakness allows wind and cold to penetrate producing the exterior patterns of common cold and flu.

## Lung Diffusion

- Lung dispersing function spreads Wei Qi from the chest outward to the entire body surface.
- Lung Qi deficiency impairs Wei Qi diffusion producing susceptibility to recurrent external pathogen invasion.
- LU-7 Lieque is the primary point for releasing exterior wind invasion by opening the Lung diffusion.
- The Lung governing the skin and body hair corresponds to its role in Wei Qi surface distribution.

## Sleep Cycling

- Wei Qi enters the interior at night as the Yang Qi of the day retreats inward enabling sleep.
- Wei Qi returns to the exterior at dawn with the rising Yang enabling waking and alertness.
- Insomnia from Wei Qi failing to enter the interior at night corresponds to Yang Qi not anchoring in Yin.
- The Wei Qi sleep cycle corresponds to the circadian alternation of sympathetic and parasympathetic dominance.

## TCM Correspondence

- Wei Qi immune function corresponds to the innate immune system's surface barrier and rapid response.
- Triple Burner Wei Qi distribution corresponds to the lymphatic and autonomic distribution of immune function.
- Wei Qi circadian cycling corresponds to the circadian rhythm of immune activity and inflammatory markers.
- Wind invasion of weakened Wei Qi corresponds to viral upper respiratory infection penetrating mucosal barriers.
