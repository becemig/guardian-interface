# Extraordinary Vessel Master Points, Energy Gates, Tonic Points, and Calming Points

## Abstract

This node documents the complete special point category taxonomy from Basic Acupressure (Gach 2008), organizing the 38 primary acupressure points into four functional categories: Extraordinary Vessel Master Points (8 points that open and balance the eight extraordinary vessels), Energy Gates (5 points that open the yang and yin meridian systems for whole-body chi circulation), Tonic Points (6 points that build and replenish constitutional energy), and Calming Points (3 points that settle the nervous system and quiet the mind). This taxonomy provides the functional architecture for clinical protocol design - any acupressure session can be organized around selecting from these categories based on the presenting pattern.

## Category 1: Extraordinary Vessel Master Points

Eight points - one per extraordinary vessel - that open and regulate each vessel:

- TW 5 (Outer Gate): master point Yangwei Mai Yang Linking Vessel
- P 6 (Inner Gate): master point Yinwei Mai Yin Linking Vessel
- K 6 (Joyful Sleep): master point Yinqiao Mai Yin Motility Vessel / Great Bridge Channel
- B 62 (Calm Sleep): master point Yangqiao Mai Yang Motility Vessel / Bridge Channel
- GB 41 (Above Tears): master point Dai Mai Belt Channel
- Sp 4 (Grandparent-Grandchild): master point Chong Mai Penetrating Channel
- SI 3 (Back Stream): master point Du Mai Governing Vessel
- Lu 7 (Narrow Defile): master point Ren Mai Conception Vessel

## Release Pair Combinations

Paired activation opens two vessels simultaneously:

- P6 + Sp4: Yinwei + Chong Mai (interior yin nourishment)
- TW5 + GB41: Yangwei + Dai Mai (exterior yang defense + belt regulation)
- Lu7 + K6: Ren Mai + Yinqiao (anterior yin axis + yin motility)
- SI3 + B62: Du Mai + Yangqiao (posterior yang axis + yang motility)

## Category 2: Energy Gates

Five points that open the major energetic gateways of the body:

- GV 4 (Gate of Life / Ming Men): root of all yang energy - constitutional fire gateway
- LI 4 (Joining the Valleys / Hoku): opens all yang meridians - FORBIDDEN in pregnancy
- Lv 3 (Supreme Rushing / Great Rush): opens all yin meridians
- TW 5 (Outer Gate): protection and resistance gateway - exterior defense
- P 6 (Inner Gate): internal nourishment gateway - yin organ support

## Category 3: Tonic Points

Six points that build and replenish constitutional energy:

- CV 6 (Sea of Chi / Hara): primary whole-body energy tonic - lower dan tian
- St 36 (Three Miles): physical vitality tonic - revitalizes entire body
- B 23 (Kidney Correspondences): kidney back-shu tonic - essence replenishment
- LI 11 (Crooked Pond): immune tonic and heat clearing
- K 27 (Elegant Mansion): opens all meridians to run correctly - systemic reset
- B 47 (Sea of Vitality): kidney vitality tonic - fatigue and low back

## Category 4: Calming Points

Three points that settle the nervous system and quiet the mind:

- CV 17 (Sea of Tranquility): primary emotional calming point - heart center
- B 10 (Heavenly Pillar): nervous tension, cervical calming, thyroid
- GV 20 (Hundred Meeting Point): all yin-yang meridians meet - supreme calming

## Clinical Application Logic

- Deficiency pattern: lead with Tonic Points (CV 6, St 36, B 47)
- Excess or stagnation: lead with Energy Gates (LI 4, Lv 3, GV 4)
- Emotional disturbance: lead with Calming Points (CV 17, B 10, GV 20)
- Extraordinary vessel imbalance: use Master Point release pairs
- Full system reset: K 27 (Elegant Mansion) - opens all meridians simultaneously
