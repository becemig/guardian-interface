# Authentic Movement Advanced Practice

## Abstract

Authentic Movement in its advanced form as developed by Janet Adler moves beyond the individual therapeutic application to address the collective body, ritual space, and the development of witness consciousness as a spiritual practice. Mary Starks Whitehouse founded Authentic Movement from her integration of Jungian active imagination with movement, establishing the basic dyadic practice of mover and witness. Janet Adler's life work expanded this into a collective practice addressing what she calls the mystical body — the collective field of consciousness that arises when multiple movers and witnesses practice together in ritual space. The development of the inner witness — the capacity to simultaneously move and observe one's movement from within — is the central developmental arc of the practice. The outer witness evolves from projection-laden observation through increasingly transparent seeing to what Adler calls the witness who knows — a state of clear non-interfering presence. Collective Authentic Movement produces field phenomena — spontaneous synchronization of movement themes, shared imagery, and collective emotional processes — that suggest a transpersonal dimension of the group body. Shen witness in TCM — the Heart-Mind's luminous non-interfering awareness attending to all phenomena without grasping or aversion — corresponds to the witness consciousness cultivated through advanced Authentic Movement practice.

## Inner Witness Development

- The inner witness is the capacity to simultaneously move and observe one's movement from within.
- Early practice separates mover and witness roles — the outer witness holds the observing function.
- As the inner witness develops movers can hold their own observing function during movement.
- The mature inner witness enables the mover to be simultaneously fully present to impulse and to self-observation.

## Collective Field

- Group Authentic Movement generates a collective field that transcends individual experience.
- Movers frequently report shared imagery, synchronized movement themes, and collective emotional processing.
- The ritual container of the group creates a held space within which collective phenomena can emerge.
- The collective field corresponds to the Jungian collective unconscious made somatically present in shared movement.

## Witness Consciousness

- The outer witness evolves through stages from projection-laden to increasingly transparent observation.
- The witness who knows is a state of clear non-interfering presence that sees without interpretation.
- This witness state corresponds to the contemplative witness of various meditative traditions.
- Witness consciousness serves both therapeutic and spiritual developmental functions in advanced practice.

## TCM Correspondence

- Shen witness as luminous non-interfering awareness corresponds to the witness consciousness of advanced Authentic Movement.
- Heart governing the integrating witness function of consciousness corresponds to the witness who knows state.
- Liver Hun as the projective dimension that must become transparent corresponds to the purification of the outer witness.
- The collective Qi field of group practice corresponds to the TCM understanding of group cultivation amplifying individual practice.
