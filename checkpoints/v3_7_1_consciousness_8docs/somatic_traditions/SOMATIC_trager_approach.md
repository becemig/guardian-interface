# Trager Approach

## Abstract

The Trager Approach developed by Milton Trager MD is a somatic bodywork and movement reeducation system based on the principle that pleasurable sensory experience of movement — particularly gentle rocking, jiggling, and supported weight — can reorganize the nervous system's holding patterns more effectively than forceful or corrective techniques. Trager developed the approach through his own experience that pleasant surprising movement sensations produced spontaneous tissue release that persisted because the nervous system remembered the pleasure rather than bracing against the discomfort. Hook-up is Trager's term for the meditative state the practitioner cultivates before and during a session — a state of light meditative awareness and physical ease that transmits through touch as a quality of presence that enables rather than directs release. Pauses — moments of complete stillness within the rocking and jiggling sequences — create a contrast that heightens the recipient's awareness of their own holding patterns. Mentastics is the self-directed movement practice component of Trager work consisting of simple pleasurable movements that recreate the quality of the table work in everyday activity. The Trager question — What could be lighter? What could be freer? — guides both practitioner and recipient toward discovering the nervous system's innate capacity for ease. Liver governing the sinews and tendons in TCM and the Liver's role in the free flow of Qi through the musculoskeletal system corresponds to the Trager principle of releasing sinew tension through pleasurable quality of movement.

## Hook-Up

- Hook-up is a meditative state of light awareness and physical ease cultivated before and during sessions.
- In hook-up the practitioner maintains contact with a quality of presence larger than the treatment interaction.
- This meditative quality transmits through touch producing a quality of ease and invitation rather than direction.
- Hook-up prevents the practitioner from imposing their concept of correct tissue organization on the recipient.

## Pleasurable Weight

- Trager uses the weight of the body part being worked with rather than adding practitioner force.
- Lifting and releasing a limb with its natural weight creates a pleasurable rocking motion through the joint chain.
- The nervous system responds to pleasurable movement by reducing protective holding around joints.
- This pleasurable quality is remembered by the nervous system enabling lasting change beyond the session.

## Mentastics

- Mentastics are simple self-directed movements recreating the quality of Trager table work in daily life.
- They are performed with a light wondering quality — What could be lighter? — rather than effortful stretching.
- Regular Mentastics practice develops the capacity to access ease and fluidity in ordinary movement.
- Mentastics corresponds to Qigong self-cultivation as the home practice component supporting bodywork sessions.

- Gallbladder governing decisive action corresponds to the Trager pause that reveals the contrast between holding and ease.
- The pleasurable quality of Trager work corresponds to the TCM principle that joy and ease support Liver Qi flow.
