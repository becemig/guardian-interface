# Nei Jing Tu Visualization Practice

## Abstract

Nei Jing Tu visualization practice is the meditative technology that animates the inner landscape map — using sustained directed visualization to journey through the body's inner cosmos, encounter and cultivate the divine inhabitants of the organ palaces, and progressively refine the practitioner's inner landscape through the power of conscious attention. Each organ palace has its guardian deity — a divine figure that embodies and governs the spiritual dimension of the organ's function. The five organ gods are: the Green Dragon of the Liver Hun spirit, the Red Phoenix of the Heart Shen spirit, the Yellow Emperor of the Spleen Yi spirit, the White Tiger of the Lung Po spirit, and the Black Tortoise of the Kidney Zhi spirit. Visualizing, invoking, and cultivating relationship with these inner deities is a primary practice of the inner landscape tradition — the organ god becomes an inner ally and guide for the spiritual development of that organ's domain. Inner landscape journey meditation begins with the practitioner entering the lower Dan Tian and ascending through the inner landscape following the Governing Vessel up the spine, encountering each organ palace and its inhabitant along the way, culminating in the upper Dan Tian brain palaces. Five colors meditation assigns the five elemental colors — green, red, yellow, white, and black — to the five organ networks and uses sustained color visualization to nourish and strengthen each organ's Qi. Shen travel is the advanced practice of directing the practitioner's own spirit-consciousness to specific inner locations — the experienced practitioner can direct their Shen to any organ palace to assess its condition and provide healing attention.

## Five Organ Gods

- Green Dragon of Liver Hun — the creative visionary spirit governing planning, dreaming, and flexible strategy.
- Red Phoenix of Heart Shen — the luminous conscious spirit governing awareness, love, and spiritual clarity.
- Yellow Emperor of Spleen Yi — the centered thinking spirit governing intention, study, and earth-grounded wisdom.
- White Tiger of Lung Po — the corporeal earthbound spirit governing instinct, boundaries, and physical vitality.
- Black Tortoise of Kidney Zhi — the deep constitutional will governing survival, longevity, and ancestral wisdom.

## Inner Landscape Journey

- Begin at the lower Dan Tian — feel the warmth of the cauldron, the Mingmen fire below.
- Ascend the Governing Vessel up the spine — feel the mountain path rising through the three gates.
- Visit each organ palace — encounter its guardian, assess its condition, offer healing attention.
- Arrive at the Niwan Palace — rest in the luminous awareness of the original spirit seat.

## Five Colors Meditation

- Green light fills and nourishes the Liver — cultivating the Hun spirit's creative vision and flexibility.
- Red light fills and nourishes the Heart — cultivating the Shen spirit's clarity and loving awareness.
- Yellow light fills and nourishes the Spleen — cultivating the Yi spirit's centered grounded intentionality.
- White light fills and nourishes the Lung — cultivating the Po spirit's vitality and appropriate boundaries.
- Dark blue-black light fills and nourishes the Kidney — cultivating the Zhi spirit's deep will and wisdom.

## TCM Correspondence

- Five organ gods correspond to the five spirits of TCM — Hun, Shen, Yi, Po, Zhi — housed in the five organ networks.
- Inner landscape journey corresponds to the TCM cultivation of the extraordinary vessels as the body's spiritual channels.
- Five colors meditation corresponds to five element color therapy in TCM acupuncture and qigong.
- Shen travel to organ palaces corresponds to the TCM diagnostic skill of reading the spirit of each organ through palpation.
