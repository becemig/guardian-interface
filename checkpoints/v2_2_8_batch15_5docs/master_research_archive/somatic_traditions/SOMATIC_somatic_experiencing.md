# Somatic Experiencing

## Abstract

Somatic Experiencing SE is a body-oriented trauma therapy developed by Peter Levine based on the observation that animals in the wild rarely develop chronic trauma symptoms despite frequent life-threatening experiences because they complete the defensive response cycle through post-threat trembling and discharge. Humans, with their capacity for cognitive override, interrupt this completion process producing frozen incomplete defensive responses that remain as chronic activation in the nervous system. SE works with the felt sense, tracking body sensation with precision while using pendulation between resource and activation states, and titration — working with small manageable amounts of activation — to gradually process and complete thwarted defensive responses. The Kidney governing fear and the constitutional freeze response corresponds to the primary organ system addressed in SE trauma work.

## Trauma Physiology

- Threat activates sympathetic fight-flight or dorsal vagal freeze depending on threat assessment.
- Incomplete fight-flight activation produces chronic sympathetic hyperarousal stored as body tension and reactivity.
- Freeze response produces dorsal vagal shutdown with dissociation, numbness, and collapse.
- SE addresses both poles — hyperarousal and hypoarousal — through regulated titrated processing.

## Pendulation

- Pendulation moves attention between activation vortex and resourcing vortex building nervous system flexibility.
- The resourcing vortex includes felt sense of safety, support, competence, and positive embodied experience.
- Moving between these poles expands the window of tolerance allowing greater activation without overwhelm.
- Pendulation corresponds to the oscillation between Yin rest and Yang activation in TCM regulation.

## Titration and Discharge

- Titration works with small manageable doses of traumatic activation rather than full immersion.
- Small doses allow the nervous system to process and discharge without re-traumatization.
- Discharge appears as trembling, spontaneous movement, heat waves, deep breathing, and spontaneous tears.
- Completion of the thwarted defensive response resolves the chronic activation restoring nervous system regulation.
