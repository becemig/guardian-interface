# Emotion and Somatic Markers: Embodied Feeling and Decision-Making

## Abstract

The somatic marker hypothesis proposed by Antonio Damasio holds that emotions are embodied states arising from interoceptive representations of bodily change. Somatic markers are feeling states associated with past outcomes that bias decision-making toward advantageous choices through visceral signaling. Contemporary constructed emotion theory extends this framework proposing that all emotional experience is actively constructed by the brain from interoceptive predictions and contextual inference. These frameworks position the body not as a passive substrate of emotion but as the generative source of feeling, with profound implications for somatic practice and emotional regulation.

## Somatic Marker Hypothesis

### Core Mechanism

- Emotional experiences produce characteristic patterns of bodily change including heart rate, skin conductance, respiration, and muscle tone.
- The brain represents these bodily changes via interoceptive pathways producing feeling states.
- Past outcomes become associated with somatic marker patterns that can be reactivated in decision contexts.
- Somatic markers bias decision-making by generating visceral gut feelings that rapidly evaluate options before conscious deliberation.

### Neural Substrates

- The ventromedial prefrontal cortex stores learned somatic marker associations between situations and bodily outcomes.
- The amygdala rapidly activates somatic marker patterns in response to emotionally significant stimuli.
- The insula integrates ascending interoceptive signals generating the conscious feeling dimension of somatic markers.
- Patients with ventromedial prefrontal damage lose somatic markers producing impaired decision-making despite intact reasoning.

## Constructed Emotion Theory

### Active Inference Framework

- The brain continuously generates predictions about incoming interoceptive signals based on prior experience.
- Emotional categories are not pre-wired modules but learned concepts applied to interoceptive prediction error patterns.
- The same interoceptive state can be categorized as different emotions depending on context and prior learning.
- Emotional granularity the ability to distinguish subtle feeling states depends on the richness of learned emotional concepts.

### Interoceptive Basis of Emotion

- All emotional experience has an interoceptive substrate reflecting the body state underlying the feeling.
- High interoceptive accuracy predicts richer emotional experience and better emotional regulation capacity.
- Alexithymia the difficulty identifying feelings corresponds to impaired interoceptive representation in the insula.
- Somatic practices that cultivate interoceptive attention directly expand emotional awareness and regulation capacity.

## HRV as Somatic Marker Proxy

- HRV reflects the autonomic component of somatic marker activation in real time.
- Positive emotional states associated with safety and social connection produce high HRV parasympathetic profiles.
- Threat and negative affect produce HRV suppression through sympathetic dominance and vagal withdrawal.
- HRV biofeedback may train somatic marker recalibration by providing real-time visceral feedback during emotional processing.

## TCM Correspondence

- The five emotions of TCM each correspond to an organ system and produce characteristic somatic patterns.
- Anger rising in the Liver produces upward flushing Qi movement and temporal pressure.
- Fear descending in the Kidney produces lower abdominal contraction and urinary urgency.
- Joy scattering in the Heart produces chest expansion and facial brightening.
- These organ-emotion-soma correspondences in TCM anticipate the somatic marker framework by millennia.

## Relevance to Internal Movement Arts

- Internal arts practice cultivates somatic marker awareness through sustained interoceptive attention during movement.
- The refinement of Yi intention in Tai Chi develops the capacity to distinguish subtle somatic marker signals.
- Reduced emotional reactivity in long-term practitioners reflects recalibrated somatic marker thresholds.
- HRV coherence during practice may reflect harmonized somatic marker baseline supporting stable emotional regulation.
