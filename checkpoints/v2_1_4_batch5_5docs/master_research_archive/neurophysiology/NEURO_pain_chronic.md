# Chronic Pain Neuroscience

## Abstract

Chronic pain is a complex biopsychosocial condition involving peripheral sensitization, central sensitization, and psychosocial amplification that persists beyond tissue healing. Central sensitization involves synaptic plasticity in the dorsal horn producing allodynia and hyperalgesia through NMDA receptor activation and descending facilitation. Nociplastic pain describes pain arising from altered nociception without clear evidence of tissue damage or nerve injury. Pain catastrophizing involving rumination, magnification, and helplessness is the strongest psychological predictor of chronic pain disability. Bi syndrome painful obstruction syndrome in TCM corresponds to inflammatory and degenerative joint and soft tissue pain conditions with Wind, Cold, and Damp as the primary pathogenic factors.

## Central Sensitization

- Central sensitization is synaptic amplification in the spinal dorsal horn producing pain hypersensitivity.
- NMDA receptor activation drives wind-up and long-term potentiation in pain processing circuits.
- Allodynia — pain from normally non-painful stimuli — and hyperalgesia — exaggerated pain from painful stimuli — are the hallmarks.
- Descending facilitation from the brainstem amplifies ascending pain signals in central sensitization.

## Nociplastic Pain

- Nociplastic pain arises from altered nociception without clear peripheral tissue damage or nerve injury.
- Fibromyalgia, irritable bowel syndrome, and chronic widespread pain are primary nociplastic conditions.
- Central sensitization and descending facilitation provide the neural mechanism for nociplastic pain.
- Treatment targets central nervous system sensitization rather than peripheral tissue pathology.

## Pain Catastrophizing

- Catastrophizing involves rumination, magnification, and helplessness in response to pain.
- It is the strongest psychological predictor of pain intensity, disability, and opioid misuse.
- Pain neuroscience education reduces catastrophizing by correcting the threat appraisal driving sensitization.
- Tai Chi and Qigong reduce catastrophizing through mindful movement and interoceptive reappraisal.

## TCM Correspondence

- Bi syndrome results from Wind, Cold, and Damp invasion obstructing Qi and Blood in the channels.
- Wind Bi produces moving pain corresponding to nociplastic widespread pain with variable location.
- Cold Bi produces severe fixed pain with cold sensation corresponding to peripheral sensitization with vasoconstriction.
- Damp Bi produces heavy fixed pain with swelling corresponding to inflammatory peripheral sensitization.
- Hot Bi produces red hot swollen painful joints corresponding to acute inflammatory arthritis.
