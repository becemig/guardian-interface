# Trauma-Informed Somatic Practice

## Abstract

Trauma-informed somatic practice integrates the understanding of trauma physiology, nervous system dysregulation, and the primacy of felt safety into all somatic work. The four core principles of trauma-informed care — safety, trustworthiness, choice, and collaboration — are applied at every level of the somatic practice context. The window of tolerance describes the zone of nervous system arousal within which a person can integrate experience — trauma pushes arousal above or below this window into hyperarousal or hypoarousal. Neuroception — the autonomic nervous system's continuous below-conscious assessment of safety and threat — determines whether the social engagement system, sympathetic fight-flight, or dorsal vagal shutdown is active. Co-regulation through the practitioner's own regulated nervous system is the primary mechanism by which somatic practice supports the client's nervous system regulation. Kidney Yin providing the deep constitutional ground of safety and Kidney Yang providing the constitutional capacity for activation correspond to the two poles of nervous system regulation addressed in trauma-informed practice.

## Felt Safety

- Neuroception continuously assesses safety through facial cues, voice tone, posture, and environmental signals.
- Safety is felt in the body before it is consciously recognized — safety enables social engagement system activation.
- Trauma-informed environments deliberately cultivate neuroceptive safety through specific relational and environmental cues.
- The practitioner's own regulated nervous system is the most powerful safety signal in the relational field.

## Window of Tolerance

- The window of tolerance is the arousal zone within which integration, learning, and healing occur.
- Hyperarousal above the window produces anxiety, overwhelm, and fight-flight activation.
- Hypoarousal below the window produces dissociation, numbness, collapse, and freeze.
- Trauma-informed practice expands the window of tolerance through titrated challenge and resourcing.

## Choice and Agency

- Trauma frequently involves loss of choice and agency — restoration of choice is therapeutic in itself.
- Trauma-informed practice offers multiple options and respects refusal without pressure or interpretation.
- Invitational language replaces directive instructions acknowledging the client's authority over their own body.
- Agency restoration corresponds to the Kidney Zhi will being recognized and supported rather than overridden.

## TCM Correspondence

- Kidney governing fear corresponds to the primary organ system affected by trauma and chronic threat activation.
- Kidney Yin providing the deep constitutional ground of stillness corresponds to hypoarousal regulation capacity.
- Kidney Yang providing the constitutional activation capacity corresponds to healthy sympathetic engagement capacity.
- Heart-Kidney axis regulation corresponds to the balance between arousal and settling addressed in trauma-informed work.
