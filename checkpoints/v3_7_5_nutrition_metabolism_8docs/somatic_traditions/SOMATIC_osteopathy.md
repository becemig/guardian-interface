# Osteopathic Medicine

## Abstract

Osteopathic medicine founded by Andrew Taylor Still in the 1870s is based on the principle that structure and function are reciprocally interrelated and that the body possesses self-healing and self-regulatory mechanisms that can be supported through manipulation. Somatic dysfunction describes the impaired or altered function of related components of the somatic system including skeletal, arthrodial, and myofascial structures and related vascular, lymphatic, and neural elements. Muscle energy technique MET uses the patient's own muscle contractions to release somatic dysfunction through post-isometric relaxation and reciprocal inhibition. Fascial unwinding follows the inherent tissue motion until spontaneous release occurs. High-velocity low-amplitude HVLA thrust techniques restore joint motion through rapid mobilization beyond the restrictive barrier.

## Still's Principles

- The body is a unit — structure, function, mind, and spirit are inseparable.
- The body possesses self-regulatory and self-healing mechanisms that can be supported.
- Structure and function are reciprocally interrelated — structural normalization supports functional recovery.
- Rational treatment is based on these principles applied to the specific somatic dysfunction present.

## Somatic Dysfunction

- Somatic dysfunction is diagnosed through TART criteria — tissue texture change, asymmetry, restriction, and tenderness.
- Acute somatic dysfunction shows boggy edematous tissue texture with warmth and hypersensitivity.
- Chronic somatic dysfunction shows fibrotic ropy tissue texture with coolness and reduced sensitivity.
- Facilitated segments show reduced threshold for neural activation corresponding to TCM Back Shu point reactivity.

## Muscle Energy Technique

- MET uses gentle patient muscle contractions against practitioner resistance to release somatic dysfunction.
- Post-isometric relaxation follows isometric contraction with reduced muscle spindle sensitivity allowing lengthening.
- Reciprocal inhibition uses contraction of the antagonist muscle to produce reflexive relaxation of the agonist.
- MET is effective for spinal, rib, and extremity somatic dysfunction with minimal force and low risk.

## TCM Correspondence

- Somatic dysfunction corresponds to Qi and Blood stagnation in the sinew channels at specific spinal segments.
- Back Shu point reactivity corresponds to facilitated spinal segments in osteopathic terminology.
- HVLA thrust releasing joint restriction corresponds to sudden dispersal of Qi stagnation in the channels.
- Fascial unwinding following inherent tissue motion corresponds to the Taoist principle of Wu Wei allowing self-correction.
