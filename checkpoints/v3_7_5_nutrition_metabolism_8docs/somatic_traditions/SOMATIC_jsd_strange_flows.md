# Jin Shin Do Strange Flows

## Abstract

The eight extraordinary vessels are called strange flows in Jin Shin Do — emphasizing their mysterious, constitutional, and pre-natal quality that sets them apart from the twelve primary organ-network channels. In JSD theory the strange flows are the rivers that carry the deepest constitutional Jing essence — the fundamental body organization established before birth and maintained throughout life as the template for the primary channels. Strange flow patterns in JSD correspond to the most fundamental constitutional holding patterns — the body-mind organization that was established in the earliest developmental period and that underlies all subsequent adaptive patterns. Working the strange flows through master-coupled point pairs produces the deepest and most transformative releases in JSD practice — releases that affect the fundamental constitutional organization rather than just the surface adaptive patterns. The Chong Mai penetrating vessel governs the constitutional Qi of the Blood and the Heart-uterus-Kidney axis — its master point SP-4 and coupled point PC-6 access the deepest level of constitutional Blood and Shen. The Ren Mai conception vessel governs the constitutional Yin and the Yin organ networks — its master point LU-7 and coupled point KI-6 address constitutional Yin deficiency and the Lung-Kidney axis. The Du Mai governing vessel governs the constitutional Yang and the spinal axis — its master point SI-3 and coupled point BL-62 address constitutional Yang deficiency and Shen-spine integration. The Dai Mai girdling vessel governs the lateral body and the Gallbladder-Liver axis — its master point GB-41 and coupled point TW-5 address constitutional holding patterns in the lateral body and waist. Deep release through strange flow work often produces constitutional shifts — fundamental changes in habitual posture, emotional baseline, and relationship to life — that distinguish it from ordinary acupressure session effects.

## Constitutional Patterns

- Strange flows hold the constitutional template established in the earliest developmental period.
- Constitutional patterns express as fundamental body organization: the characteristic posture, movement quality, and emotional baseline.
- JSD strange flow work addresses the level beneath adaptive patterns to the constitutional organization itself.
- This level of work produces changes that are more durable and fundamental than surface pattern treatment.

## Master-Coupled Pairs

- Chong Mai SP-4 and PC-6: Blood constitution, Heart-uterus axis, deepest Shen and Jing connection.
- Ren Mai LU-7 and KI-6: Yin constitution, Lung-Kidney axis, constitutional moisture and receptivity.
- Du Mai SI-3 and BL-62: Yang constitution, spinal axis, constitutional drive and Shen-body integration.
- Dai Mai GB-41 and TW-5: lateral constitution, Gallbladder-Triple Burner axis, constitutional boundaries.

## Deep Release

- Strange flow release produces constitutional shifts affecting fundamental body-mind organization.
- Clients often report profound relaxation, imagery of early life, or sense of fundamental reorganization.
- The release is often quieter and deeper than primary channel releases — less dramatic but more fundamental.
- Integration time after strange flow work is important — the constitutional system needs time to reorganize.

## TCM Correspondence

- Strange flows as pre-natal Jing vessels corresponds to the TCM eight extraordinary vessel constitutional function.
- Master-coupled pairs accessing constitutional level corresponds to the TCM extraordinary vessel treatment strategy.
- Constitutional release producing fundamental reorganization corresponds to the TCM concept of treating the root Ben rather than the branch Biao.
- Deep quiet release corresponds to the Taoist principle that the deepest transformations occur in stillness.
