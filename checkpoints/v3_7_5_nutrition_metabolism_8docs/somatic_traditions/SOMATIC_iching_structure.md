# I Ching Structure

## Abstract

The I Ching Book of Changes is the foundational cosmological text of Chinese civilization — a system for mapping and navigating change through a symbolic language of sixty-four hexagrams composed of combinations of eight trigrams representing the fundamental forces of nature and cosmos. The structural foundation is the Yin-Yang polarity: a broken line represents Yin receptive yielding darkness and a solid line represents Yang active initiating light. Three lines stacked produce the eight trigrams Ba Gua — each a unique combination of Yin and Yang lines representing a fundamental natural force: Heaven Qian three Yang lines, Earth Kun three Yin lines, Water Kan Yang between two Yin, Fire Li Yin between two Yang, Thunder Zhen Yang below two Yin, Wind Xun Yin below two Yang, Mountain Gen Yang above two Yin, Lake Dui Yin above two Yang. Pairing any two trigrams produces the sixty-four hexagrams — each a six-line figure representing a specific situation of change with its own judgment and line commentaries. The Ten Wings are the philosophical commentaries attributed to Confucius that transform the I Ching from an oracle text into a comprehensive cosmological philosophy. The two primary trigram arrangements — Fuxi Pre-Heaven showing the primordial relationships and Wen Wang Post-Heaven showing the seasonal operational relationships — provide complementary maps of cosmological organization. The cosmological body map assigns the eight trigrams to eight body regions and organ networks providing a somatic reading of the hexagram system — Heaven to head, Earth to abdomen, Water to ear-kidney, Fire to eye-heart, Thunder to foot-liver, Wind to thigh-gallbladder, Mountain to hand-stomach, Lake to mouth-lung.

## Eight Trigrams

- Heaven Qian — three Yang lines — creative force, father, head, northwest.
- Earth Kun — three Yin lines — receptive force, mother, abdomen, southwest.
- Water Kan — Yang flanked by Yin — danger, second son, ear-kidney, north.
- Fire Li — Yin flanked by Yang — clarity, second daughter, eye-heart, south.
- Thunder Zhen — Yang below Yin — arousing, first son, foot-liver, east.
- Wind Xun — Yin below Yang — gentle penetrating, first daughter, thigh-gallbladder, southeast.
- Mountain Gen — Yang above Yin — keeping still, third son, hand-stomach, northeast.
- Lake Dui — Yin above Yang — joyful, third daughter, mouth-lung, west.

## Yao Lines

- Each hexagram line Yao has its own specific meaning within the overall hexagram situation.
- Line 1 bottom is the beginning of the situation — early stage, unclear, potential.
- Line 6 top is the culmination — beyond the situation, transition to the next change.
- Moving lines are the lines that are in transition — changing from Yin to Yang or Yang to Yin.

## Cosmological Body Map

- Trigram body assignments provide a somatic reading system mapping body regions to cosmological forces.
- Heaven governing the head corresponds to Shen residing in the upper Dan Tian crown.
- Earth governing the abdomen corresponds to the lower Dan Tian as the root of bodily constitution.
- Water-Kidney governing the ear corresponds to the TCM Kidney opening to the ears.

## TCM Correspondence

- Eight trigram body map corresponds directly to the eight extraordinary vessel constitutional body organization.
- Pre-Heaven arrangement corresponds to prenatal Jing constitutional endowment.
- Post-Heaven arrangement corresponds to postnatal Qi operational cultivation.
- The sixty-four hexagram system corresponds to the sixty-four acupoint categories of classical TCM organization.
