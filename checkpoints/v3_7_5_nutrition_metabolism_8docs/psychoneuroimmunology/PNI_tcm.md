# TCM Immunology

## Abstract

Traditional Chinese Medicine developed a sophisticated immunological framework organized around Zheng Qi (righteous Qi, the body's overall defensive and restorative capacity) and Wei Qi (defensive Qi, the surface immune barrier). Wei Qi circulates at the body surface and in the skin and muscles, warming and defending against external pathogenic factors (Xie Qi). Zheng Qi encompasses the deeper adaptive and restorative capacities of the organ systems, with Kidney Qi as the constitutional root, Spleen Qi as the source of post-natal immune resources, and Lung Qi as the distributor of Wei Qi to the surface. The six external pathogenic factors (Wind, Cold, Heat, Dampness, Dryness, Summer Heat) map with striking accuracy onto classes of immune challenges: Wind as viral invasion, Cold as metabolic suppression of immunity, Dampness as inflammatory and microbiome disruption, Heat as febrile immune activation. Autoimmune conditions are understood as Zheng-Xie imbalance — the body's defensive forces attacking its own tissues. Adaptogenic herbs including Astragalus, Reishi, Cordyceps, and Codonopsis have robust evidence for immune modulation.

## Wei Qi

- Nature: yang Qi - warm, mobile, protective - circulates at body surface
- Circulation: outside the vessels - in skin, muscles, and between organs
- Daytime: circulates in yang (exterior) - 50 circuits per day
- Nighttime: moves inward to yin organs - explains night vulnerability to cold
- Source: root in Kidney Yang, nourished by Spleen Qi, distributed by Lung
- Functions: warms skin, controls pores, defends against external pathogens
- Modern parallel: innate immunity - NK cells, neutrophils, macrophages, skin barrier
- Deficiency: frequent colds, easy sweating, cold sensitivity, low energy

## Zheng Qi

- Nature: overall righteous Qi - body's capacity to resist, adapt, and recover
- Components: Yuan Qi (constitutional), Ying Qi (nutritive), Wei Qi (defensive), Zong Qi (chest)
- Modern parallel: total immunocompetence - innate plus adaptive plus neuroimmune regulation
- Strength determines outcome: Zheng Qi strong - pathogen repelled - Zheng Qi weak - disease
- Spleen: primary postnatal source of Zheng Qi through food transformation
- Kidney: constitutional root - determines baseline Zheng Qi capacity
- Nei Jing: when Zheng Qi is present within, pathogenic Qi cannot invade

## Six Pathogenic Factors and Immune Parallels

- Wind: changes rapidly, moves upward, opens pores - parallel to viral pathogens
- Cold: contracts, slows, painful - parallel to immune suppression, reduced metabolic rate
- Heat: inflames, dries, agitates - parallel to febrile infection and inflammatory cytokines
- Dampness: heavy, turbid, persistent - parallel to dysbiosis, metabolic endotoxemia, chronic inflammation
- Dryness: depletes fluids, affects Lung - parallel to mucosal barrier dysfunction
- Summer Heat: exhausting, combines with Damp - parallel to heat stress immune suppression
- Internal vs external: external pathogens invade from outside - internal arise from organ dysfunction

## Autoimmunity as Zheng-Xie Imbalance

- Zheng-Xie: righteous Qi vs pathogenic Qi - health is their dynamic balance
- Autoimmunity: Wei Qi loses ability to distinguish self from non-self
- Rheumatoid arthritis: Wind-Cold-Damp Bi syndrome - joint obstruction by pathogenic factors
- Lupus: Yin deficiency with Heat - Fire consuming Yin - autoantibody production
- Hashimoto: Liver Qi stagnation with Phlegm - immune attack on thyroid
- Treatment principle: expel pathogen AND tonify Zheng Qi simultaneously
- Bi syndrome: painful obstruction - Wind, Cold, or Damp predominant - maps to autoimmune arthritis

## Immune-Tonifying Herbs

- Astragalus (Huang Qi): tonifies Wei Qi and Spleen Qi - increases NK cells, IFN, antibody response
- Reishi (Ling Zhi): calms Shen, tonifies all three treasures - beta-glucans modulate immune response
- Cordyceps (Dong Chong Xia Cao): tonifies Lung and Kidney Yang - NK enhancement, anti-tumor
- Codonopsis (Dang Shen): gentle Qi tonic - Spleen and Lung - improves T cell function
- Eleuthero (Ci Wu Jia): adaptogen - modulates cortisol and NK activity - reduces stress immunosuppression
- Schisandra (Wu Wei Zi): five-flavor berry - adaptogenic - liver protection and immune modulation
- Licorice (Gan Cao): harmonizes formulas - anti-inflammatory glycyrrhizin - modulates cortisol metabolism

- Cold exposure: brief cold stimulates Wei Qi activation - Wim Hof parallel
