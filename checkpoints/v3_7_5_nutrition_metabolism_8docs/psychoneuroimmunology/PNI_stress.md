# Stress and Immunity

## Abstract

The relationship between psychological stress and immune function is biphasic and duration-dependent. Acute stress produces adaptive immune mobilization — redistributing NK cells and neutrophils to sites of potential injury, enhancing innate responses for immediate defense. Chronic stress produces the opposite pattern: glucocorticoid resistance develops in immune cells, cytokine suppression fails, and systemic inflammation rises despite continued cortisol elevation. Social stressors — loneliness, low social status, bereavement, caregiver burden — are among the most potent drivers of immune dysregulation, operating through conserved transcriptional response to adversity (CTRA) gene expression changes that upregulate pro-inflammatory genes and downregulate antiviral genes. Cole's social genomics work demonstrated that loneliness reshapes leukocyte gene expression within weeks. Glucocorticoid resistance — impaired immune cell response to cortisol's anti-inflammatory signal — is the central mechanism linking chronic stress to inflammatory disease, explaining how chronic stress simultaneously maintains high cortisol yet fails to suppress inflammation.

## Acute Stress Immune Effects

- Fight-or-flight preparation: immune system anticipates potential wounding and infection
- NK cell mobilization: acute stress increases NK cell number in blood by 50-100 percent
- Neutrophil mobilization: bone marrow releases neutrophils to bloodstream
- Lymphocyte redistribution: lymphocytes move from blood to skin, lymph nodes, gut
- Mechanism: SNS catecholamines drive rapid immune redistribution via adrenergic receptors
- Duration: peaks within 30 minutes - resolves within 1-2 hours post-stressor
- Adaptive value: prepares immune system to respond to injury at sites of physical threat

## Chronic Stress Immune Effects

- Meta-analysis Segerstrom 2004: chronic stress suppresses cellular and humoral immunity
- NK cell activity: chronic stress reduces NK cytotoxicity - impairs tumor surveillance
- T cell proliferation: chronic stress reduces mitogen-stimulated T cell proliferation
- Antibody response: stress during vaccination reduces antibody titers
- Wound healing: caregiver stress slows wound healing by 24 percent
- Vaccine response: medical students under exam stress mount weaker flu vaccine response
- Latent viruses: chronic stress reactivates EBV, HSV, CMV - measurable antibody rises

## Glucocorticoid Resistance

- Normal: cortisol binds GR on immune cells - suppresses NF-kB - reduces cytokine production
- Resistance: chronic stress reduces GR expression and sensitivity on immune cells
- Paradox: high cortisol yet high inflammation - cortisol signal fails to suppress
- Mechanism: chronic NF-kB activation reduces GR nuclear translocation
- Consequence: pro-inflammatory cytokines rise despite elevated cortisol
- Clinical: GC resistance predicts depression severity and antidepressant non-response
- Reversal: exercise, sleep restoration, social support improve GC sensitivity

## Social Stress and Immune Dysregulation

- Social defeat: subordinate animals show sustained inflammation and immune suppression
- Low social status: in humans and primates - low rank predicts higher inflammation markers
- Loneliness: subjective social isolation - elevates IL-6, CRP, and fibrinogen
- Bereavement: grief raises cortisol, suppresses NK activity, increases infection risk
- Caregiver stress: Alzheimer caregivers show 15 percent slower wound healing, reduced vaccine response
- Childhood adversity: early social stress permanently programs immune reactivity

## CTRA Gene Expression

- Cole: conserved transcriptional response to adversity - evolutionary stress immune program
- CTRA profile: upregulated pro-inflammatory genes (IL-1, IL-6, IL-8, TNF) - downregulated antiviral genes
- Logic: prepare for bacterial infection from wounds - reduce antiviral response
- Loneliness CTRA: lonely individuals show CTRA profile in leukocytes
- Reversal: social connection, mindfulness, and meaning-making reverse CTRA within weeks
- TCM: Heart-Shen isolation disrupts Qi flow - social connection nourishes Heart and Wei Qi
