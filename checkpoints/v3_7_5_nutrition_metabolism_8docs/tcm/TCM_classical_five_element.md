# Classical Five Element Acupuncture

## Abstract

Classical Five Element Acupuncture as systematized by J.R. Worsley is a constitutional approach that identifies each patient primary elemental imbalance — the Causative Factor — and treats it directly to restore the whole person. The Causative Factor is identified through the four sensory indicators of color, sound, odor, and emotion that reflect the state of each element. Treatment addresses the Officials — the twelve organ-meridian systems understood as governing officials of the bodily kingdom. Entry-exit blocks, aggressive energy drains, and constitutional command point protocols are key clinical tools. This tradition provides a complementary constitutional framework alongside the pattern-based approach of TCM.

## Causative Factor

- The Causative Factor CF is the primary elemental imbalance present since early life governing the patient constitutional pattern.
- The CF is identified through the four sensory indicators expressed by each element when imbalanced.
- Wood CF shows a green facial hue, shouting voice quality, rancid odor, and anger emotion.
- Fire CF shows a red facial hue, laughing voice quality, scorched odor, and joy or lack of joy emotion.
- Earth CF shows a yellow facial hue, singing voice quality, fragrant odor, and sympathy emotion.
- Metal CF shows a white facial hue, weeping voice quality, rotten odor, and grief emotion.
- Water CF shows a blue-black facial hue, groaning voice quality, putrid odor, and fear emotion.

## The Officials

- Each of the twelve meridians is understood as an Official governing a specific function of the bodily kingdom.
- The Heart Official is the Supreme Controller governing all mental and spiritual activity.
- The Small Intestine Official is the Separator of Pure from Impure providing discernment.
- The Bladder Official is the Controller of Storage of Reserves governing constitutional reserve.
- The Kidney Official is the Controller of Water and the Architect governing constitutional foundation.
- The Pericardium Official is the Heart Protector governing relationship and emotional boundaries.
- The Triple Burner Official is the Official of Balance and Harmony governing systemic integration.

## Entry-Exit Blocks

- Entry-exit blocks occur when Qi cannot flow from one meridian to the next in the circulation sequence.
- They prevent effective treatment of points on affected meridians producing treatment resistance.
- The block is cleared by needling the exit point of the blocked meridian and the entry point of the receiving meridian.
- Entry-exit blocks are a key obstacle to treatment response in classical five element practice.

## Aggressive Energy

- Aggressive energy AE is pathological Qi that has accumulated in the meridian system producing chronic constitutional toxicity.
- AE drain is performed by needling the back shu points of all five elements and observing redness around the needles.
- Redness indicates AE draining from the system a positive prognostic sign.
- AE treatment is performed before constitutional treatment to clear the field for effective work.
