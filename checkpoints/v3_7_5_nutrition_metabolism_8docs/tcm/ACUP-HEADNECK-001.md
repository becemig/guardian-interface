# Head and Neck Acupressure Point Cluster

## Abstract

The head and neck acupressure point cluster encompasses the primary cranial, facial, and cervical points used in basic acupressure practice, covering the forehead, cheekbone, skull base, posterior neck, and crown of head. These points collectively govern headache, hypertension, nervous system tension, sinus conditions, memory, insomnia, emotional clarity, and the opening of the sensory gates of the face. The cluster includes both local points addressing specific anatomical regions and distal convergence points where multiple yang meridians meet at the crown and cervical junction, making this cluster foundational for calming the nervous system, clearing the mind, and treating conditions that rise upward to the head.

## Point Inventory

### GB 14 - Yang White (Yang Bai)
- Location: forehead 1 finger above center of eyebrow directly above pupil
- Benefits: clear thinking, emotional balance, hypertension, frontal headaches
- Technique: light fingertip pressure

### St 3 - Facial Beauty (Ju Liao)
- Location: bottom of cheekbone directly below the pupil
- Benefits: sinus congestion, facial paralysis, nausea, toothache, facial pain
- Technique: medium finger pressure upward into cheekbone

### B 10 - Heavenly Pillar (Tian Zhu)
- Location: C1-C2 level 1 finger lateral from midline in posterior neck musculature
- Benefits: nervous tension, headaches, stiff neck, thyroid support, depression
- Technique: firm bilateral pressure with thumbs

### GB 20 - Gates of Consciousness (Feng Chi)
- Location: below occipital ridge in the groove between trapezius and SCM
- Benefits: headaches, hypertension, memory, insomnia, dizziness, neck tension
- Technique: thumbs pressing upward into occiput groove

### SI 10 - Shoulder Correspondence (Nao Shu)
- Location: directly below the acromion in the shoulder socket depression
- Benefits: stiff neck, shoulder tension, cold hands, hypertension
- Technique: finger pressure into shoulder socket

### GV 20 - Hundred Meeting Point (Bai Hui)
- Location: crown of head at vertex - meeting point of all yin and yang meridians
- Benefits: headaches, strengthens mind and morale, raises yang qi, clarity
- Technique: gentle fingertip pressure at crown

### GV 24.5 - Third Eye (Yin Tang)
- Location: between the eyebrows at the glabella
- Benefits: calms spirit, intuition, meditation support, overall energy balance
- Technique: very light fingertip pressure or hovering

## Clinical Protocols

- Calming Flow sequence includes: GB 20, GB 14, St 3, GV 20, CV 17, CV 6
- Shoulder and Neck Release uses: GB 20, GV 16, GV 24.5, GB 14, B 10, GB 21
- Increasing Vitality Flow begins with: GB 20, followed by LI 11 and LI 4
- Immune Strengthening Flow uses: B 10, GB 14 in upper sequence

## Indications by Condition

- Headache: GB 20, GB 14, GV 20, B 10
- Hypertension: GB 20, GB 14, SI 10, B 10
- Sinus and facial: St 3, GB 14
- Nervous tension and depression: B 10, GB 20, GV 20
- Meditation and clarity: GV 20, GV 24.5, GB 20
