# Arm and Hand Acupressure Point Cluster

## Abstract

The arm and hand acupressure point cluster covers the primary upper extremity points from the elbow through the forearm, wrist, and hand, with special significance because four of the six extraordinary vessel master points are located here - TW 5 opening Yangwei, P 6 opening Yinwei, SI 3 opening Du Mai, and Lu 7 opening Ren Mai. This makes the arm and hand the primary access zone for balancing the extraordinary vessel system through distal point therapy. Additionally LI 4 Joining the Valleys is one of the most powerful general-purpose points in the entire system - governing allergies, headaches, constipation, and immune function - and LI 11 Crooked Pond is a major tonic and heat-clearing point used across multiple system conditions.

## Point Inventory

### LI 11 - Crooked Pond (Qu Chi)
- Location: lateral end of the elbow crease when arm is flexed
- Benefits: constipation, skin disorders, fever, colds, immune tonic
- Note: major tonic point - used in Increasing Vitality and Immune Strengthening flows

### TW 5 - Outer Gate (Wai Guan)
- Location: posterior forearm 2 fingers above the wrist crease between the bones
- Benefits: rheumatism, wrist pain, tendonitis, carpal tunnel, lateral arm
- MASTER POINT: opens Yangwei Mai Yang Linking Vessel
- Pair: TW5 + GB41 activates Yangwei and Dai Mai together

### P 6 - Inner Gate (Nei Guan)
- Location: anterior forearm 2 fingers above the wrist crease between the tendons
- Benefits: nausea, insomnia, emotional imbalance, carpal tunnel, palpitations
- MASTER POINT: opens Yinwei Mai Yin Linking Vessel
- Pair: P6 + Sp4 activates Yinwei and Chong Mai together

### SI 3 - Back Stream (Hou Xi)
- Location: end of the deepest crease on the ulnar palm when fist is made
- Benefits: stiff neck, ear problems, occipital headache, upper back
- MASTER POINT: opens Du Mai Governing Vessel
- Pair: SI3 + B62 activates Du Mai and Yangqiao together

### Lu 7 - Narrow Defile (Lie Que)
- Location: radial groove on the anterior wrist 3 fingers above wrist crease
- Benefits: asthma, coughs, respiratory conditions, neck stiffness
- MASTER POINT: opens Ren Mai Conception Vessel
- Pair: Lu7 + K6 activates Ren Mai and Yinqiao together

### LI 4 - Joining the Valleys (He Gu)
- Location: webbing between thumb and index finger at the highest point of the muscle
- Benefits: allergies, frontal headaches, constipation, depression, facial pain
- ENERGY GATE: opens all yang meridians - one of the most powerful distal points
- CAUTION: forbidden during pregnancy - strongly moves qi downward

## Master Point Summary for This Cluster

- TW 5: Yangwei Mai master point
- P 6: Yinwei Mai master point
- SI 3: Du Mai (Governing Vessel) master point
- Lu 7: Ren Mai (Conception Vessel) master point
- LI 4: Energy Gate opening all yang meridians

## Clinical Protocols

- Calming Flow: TW 5, P 6 (final wrist pair)
- Immune Strengthening: Lu 1, Lu 7, Lu 11 (lung meridian sequence)
- Increasing Vitality: LI 11, LI 4 (large intestine opener sequence)
