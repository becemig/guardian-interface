# Wu Xing Five Phase Theory

## Abstract

Wu Xing — the five phases or five movements — is a dynamic process model describing the cyclical transformation of energy through five archetypal qualities: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. Far from a static classification system, Wu Xing describes the characteristic movements and transformations of phenomena as they cycle through growth, culmination, harvest, storage, and renewal. Each phase corresponds to a season, organ pair, emotion, color, sound, taste, direction, and developmental stage in a vast web of cosmological correspondences that links the microcosm of the human body to the macrocosm of heaven and earth. The Sheng (generation) cycle describes how each phase nourishes the next in sequence. The Ke (control) cycle describes how each phase regulates an alternate phase. Disease arises when these cycles are disrupted — phases become excessive, deficient, or inverted. Five Element acupuncture (Worsley tradition) identifies a Causative Factor — the constitutional phase imbalance underlying a patient's pattern throughout life.

## Five Phases as Process

- Wood: uprising, expansion, initiation - Spring - new growth pushing upward
- Fire: culmination, radiance, connection - Summer - full bloom and warmth
- Earth: centering, harvest, nourishment - Late Summer - gathering and transformation
- Metal: contraction, refinement, letting go - Autumn - distilling essence, releasing what is spent
- Water: storage, stillness, potential - Winter - deep rest and conservation
- Movement not substance: phases describe qualities of movement - not literal elements
- Universal applicability: any process in time moves through these five qualities

## Sheng Cycle - Generation

- Wood generates Fire: Wood fuels Fire - Liver nourishes Heart - vision animates passion
- Fire generates Earth: Fire creates ash-Earth - Heart warms Spleen - joy enables nourishment
- Earth generates Metal: Earth condenses to Metal ore - Spleen supports Lung - nutrition enables refinement
- Metal generates Water: Metal holds and conducts Water - Lung supports Kidney - breath deepens essence
- Water generates Wood: Water nourishes Wood growth - Kidney nourishes Liver - reserves fuel vision
- Mother-child: each phase is mother to the next and child to the previous
- Clinical: tonify the mother to strengthen the child - sedate the child to relieve the mother

## Ke Cycle - Control

- Wood controls Earth: roots and wood bind soil - Liver regulates Spleen - vision organizes nourishment
- Earth controls Water: dams and banks contain water - Spleen regulates Kidney - center grounds depth
- Water controls Fire: water extinguishes fire - Kidney regulates Heart - reserves cool passion
- Fire controls Metal: fire melts and shapes metal - Heart regulates Lung - warmth tempers refinement
- Metal controls Wood: ax cuts wood - Lung regulates Liver - letting go disciplines expansion
- Regulation: Ke cycle prevents any phase from becoming excessive
- Pathology: over-control (Cheng) and counter-control (Wu) are pathological Ke variations

## Cosmological Correspondences

- Wood: Spring, East, Wind, Green, Sour, Shouting, Anger, Vision, Birth
- Fire: Summer, South, Heat, Red, Bitter, Laughing, Joy, Speech, Growth
- Earth: Late Summer, Center, Damp, Yellow, Sweet, Singing, Worry, Taste, Transformation
- Metal: Autumn, West, Dryness, White, Pungent, Weeping, Grief, Smell, Harvest
- Water: Winter, North, Cold, Black-Blue, Salty, Groaning, Fear, Hearing, Storage
- Resonance principle: like resonates with like across all levels of scale
- Clinical diagnosis: color, sound, odor, emotion reveal constitutional phase imbalance

## Emotional Alchemy

- Anger: Wood - Liver - excessive Liver Yang or Qi stagnation - transforms to assertiveness
- Joy: Fire - Heart - excessive stimulation scatters Shen - transforms to warmth and connection
- Worry: Earth - Spleen - excessive thinking depletes Spleen Qi - transforms to focused intention
- Grief: Metal - Lung - unresolved loss depletes Lung Qi - transforms to appreciation and release
- Fear: Water - Kidney - chronic fear depletes Kidney Qi - transforms to wisdom and discernment
- Alchemy: each difficult emotion contains the seed of its transformation - same energy different direction
- Treatment: address organ system to transform emotional pattern - bidirectional relationship

## Five Element Acupuncture

- Worsley tradition: identifies Causative Factor - constitutional element imbalanced from early life
- CF: shows in color, sound, odor, emotion - the patient's deepest pattern
- Treatment: treat the CF element primarily - address root not just branch
- Spirit-level: Worsley tradition treats the spirit of each element - not just physical symptoms
- Command points: each meridian has five command points - one for each phase
- Mother-child points: tonify or sedate according to Sheng cycle relationships
