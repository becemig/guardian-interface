# Galvanic Skin Response and Electrodermal Activity

## Abstract

Galvanic skin response and electrodermal activity EDA — the measurement of the electrical conductance of the skin surface that reflects the sweat gland activity governed exclusively by sympathetic cholinergic innervation, providing a continuous, objective, non-invasive index of sympathetic arousal and emotional activation — is one of the most widely used psychophysiological measures in clinical, research, and consumer biofeedback applications, providing real-time access to the sympathetic nervous system state that corresponds in TCM to the Wei Qi defensive response expressed through the skin surface. Sweat gland sympathetic innervation — the eccrine sweat glands of the palmar and plantar surfaces that are densely innervated by sympathetic cholinergic fibers and activated in response to emotional and cognitive arousal rather than thermal stimulation — produce the rapid changes in skin electrical conductance that constitute the electrodermal response, with the unique sympathetic cholinergic innervation making EDA a pure index of sympathetic activation unconfounded by parasympathetic influences that complicate heart rate interpretation. Tonic and phasic EDA components — the slowly varying tonic skin conductance level SCL reflecting the background sympathetic tone and the rapidly occurring phasic skin conductance responses SCR superimposed on the tonic level in response to discrete stimuli — provide complementary information about chronic sympathetic arousal and acute sympathetic reactivity, with SCL elevated in anxiety disorders and chronic stress and SCR amplitude reflecting emotional significance of individual stimuli through Damasio's somatic marker system. EDA biofeedback — the use of real-time skin conductance feedback to train voluntary reduction of sympathetic arousal — is one of the most established and effective biofeedback modalities for anxiety, stress, and PTSD, with EDA biofeedback producing measurable reductions in sympathetic tone and clinical anxiety through the development of cognitive and somatic regulation skills. The Wei Qi skin defensive response correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding that the Wei Qi defensive energy circulates in the superficial tissues and governs the opening and closing of the pores — a functional description of the sweat gland sympathetic regulation that EDA measures, with Wei Qi weakness corresponding to dysregulated sympathetic skin response and excessive sweating.

## EDA Physiology

- Eccrine sweat glands: palmar plantar axillary — pure sympathetic cholinergic innervation.
- Skin conductance: proportional to sweat gland secretory activity — emotional arousal index.
- Tonic SCL: background sympathetic tone — elevated in anxiety chronic stress.
- Phasic SCR: discrete response to stimuli — amplitude reflects emotional significance.
