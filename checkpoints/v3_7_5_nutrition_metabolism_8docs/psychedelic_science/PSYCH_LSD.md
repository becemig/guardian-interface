# LSD Neuroscience

## Abstract

LSD neuroscience — the scientific investigation of the neurobiological mechanisms underlying the profound perceptual, cognitive, and consciousness-altering effects of lysergic acid diethylamide, the most potent psychedelic compound known, active at microgram doses through agonism at 5-HT2A receptors producing the thalamocortical disinhibition that underlies psychedelic consciousness — has generated fundamental insights into the neural correlates of consciousness, the role of predictive processing in normal perception, and the mechanisms of psychedelic-assisted therapy through the profound alteration of the brain's default information processing architecture. LSD receptor pharmacology — the complex polypharmacology of LSD at multiple receptor systems including 5-HT2A, 5-HT2B, 5-HT1A, dopamine D1 and D2, adrenergic, and histamine receptors, with the psychedelic effects primarily mediated through 5-HT2A agonism in cortical pyramidal neurons that disrupts the normal top-down predictive filtering of sensory information, and with LSD's unique beta-arrestin biased signaling at 5-HT2A receptors contributing to its extraordinarily prolonged duration of action through receptor internalization resistance. LSD thalamocortical neuroimaging — Preller's fMRI research demonstrating that LSD produces profound changes in thalamocortical connectivity, with increased sensory thalamus to cortex connectivity producing the sensory flooding of psychedelic perception as normally filtered sensory signals gain unrestricted cortical access, and with global increases in functional connectivity between normally segregated brain networks producing the synesthesia, ego dissolution, and cosmic unity experiences of high-dose LSD. LSD microdosing cognition — the investigation of the cognitive and psychological effects of sub-perceptual LSD doses of 5-25 micrograms taken every three days, with controlled studies showing modest improvements in convergent and divergent thinking, mood enhancement, and increased mindfulness in some studies while others show null results, establishing that microdosing cognitive enhancement claims require more rigorous investigation. The expanded perception Wei Shen correspondence in TCM describes the classical understanding of Wei Shen as the extended or subtle dimension of the Shen spirit that perceives beyond ordinary sensory boundaries — a correspondence with the psychedelic expansion of perceptual boundaries and dissolution of the filtering mechanisms that normally constrain awareness.

## 5HT2A Receptor Mechanism

- Primary target: 5-HT2A on cortical layer V pyramidal neurons.
- Effect: disrupts top-down predictive filtering — sensory flooding.
- Beta-arrestin bias: LSD receptor internalization resistance — prolonged binding.
- Thalamic gating: disrupts thalamic sensory filtering — cortical sensory inundation.

## Thalamocortical Changes

- Sensory thalamus: increased connectivity to sensory cortex — visual auditory flooding.
- Network segregation: dissolution of normal network boundaries — synesthesia.
- DMN suppression: consistent with other serotonergic psychedelics.
- Global connectivity: increased between-network connectivity — unity experience.

## Microdosing Evidence

- Protocol: 5-25 micrograms every third day — sub-perceptual dose.
- Placebo-controlled findings: mixed — some convergent thinking improvement.
- Mood: modest improvements in some controlled studies.
- Concerns: expectancy effects large — blinding difficult at any active dose.
