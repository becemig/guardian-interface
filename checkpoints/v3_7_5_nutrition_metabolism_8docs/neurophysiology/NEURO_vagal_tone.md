# Vagal Tone Deep Dive

## Abstract

The vagus nerve — the tenth cranial nerve and the primary parasympathetic nerve of the body — is the most complex and functionally important nerve in the autonomic nervous system, carrying both afferent sensory information from the viscera to the brain and efferent motor commands from the brain to the viscera. Vagal tone refers to the baseline level of vagal activity — the degree to which the parasympathetic brake is applied to the heart and other organs — and is the primary determinant of heart rate variability HRV, immune regulation, emotional regulation, and the body's overall capacity for flexible adaptive response. Kevin Tracey's discovery of the inflammatory reflex — the vagal efferent pathway through which the brain inhibits peripheral inflammation via the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway — established the vagus nerve as a key regulator of immune function and opened the field of bioelectronic medicine. The cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway works through alpha-7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors on macrophages — vagal acetylcholine release inhibits TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, and IL-6 production from macrophages, providing rapid neural control of peripheral inflammation. The nucleus tractus solitarius NTS in the medulla is the primary recipient of vagal afferent information — integrating interoceptive signals from the heart, lungs, liver, gut, and kidneys into the brainstem regulatory circuits that control cardiovascular, respiratory, and digestive function. High cardiac vagal tone correlates with greater prefrontal cortex control over subcortical threat responses, better emotional regulation, more accurate interoception, greater social engagement capacity, and lower inflammatory markers. All effective somatic practices — meditation, qigong, taijichuan, acupuncture, slow breathing, cold exposure — increase vagal tone through multiple pathways.

- Vagal efferents release acetylcholine at the spleen inhibiting macrophage TNF-alpha production.
- Alpha-7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors on macrophages transduce the anti-inflammatory signal.
- Vagal nerve stimulation reduces systemic inflammatory markers in rheumatoid arthritis and Crohn disease.

## Cardiac Vagal Tone and HRV

- Cardiac vagal tone is quantified through HRV — higher vagal tone produces higher HRV.
- RSA respiratory sinus arrhythmia is the HRV component most directly reflecting cardiac vagal tone.
- High vagal tone allows rapid heart rate recovery after exertion or stress.
- Low vagal tone predicts cardiovascular disease, metabolic syndrome, depression, and mortality.
