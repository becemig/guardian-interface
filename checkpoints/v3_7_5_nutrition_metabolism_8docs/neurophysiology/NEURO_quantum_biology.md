# Quantum Biology

## Abstract

Quantum biology is the emerging field documenting that quantum mechanical phenomena — coherence, tunneling, entanglement — play functional roles in biological systems at physiological temperatures, challenging the assumption that quantum effects are only relevant at very cold temperatures or in isolated laboratory systems. The photosynthesis quantum coherence discovery by Graham Fleming's group demonstrated that energy transfer in photosynthetic light-harvesting complexes uses quantum wave-like coherence to achieve near-perfect efficiency — the energy explores all possible pathways simultaneously through quantum superposition, finding the optimal route. This was the first demonstration of warm, wet quantum biology — quantum coherence functioning in a hot noisy biological environment. Enzyme catalysis through quantum tunneling — protons and electrons tunneling through energy barriers rather than climbing over them — provides rate enhancements that cannot be explained classically, with kinetic isotope effects demonstrating quantum tunneling in alcohol dehydrogenase, aromatic amine dehydrogenase, and other enzymes. Cryptochrome magnetoreception in birds uses the radical pair mechanism — a quantum entanglement-based compass where Earth's magnetic field affects the spin states of radical pairs in cryptochrome proteins of the avian eye, enabling navigation by magnetic field. Human cryptochrome proteins in the retina may enable a vestigial magnetic sense. Penrose-Hameroff orchestrated objective reduction Orch OR proposes that quantum computations in microtubules of neurons underlie conscious experience — though this remains highly controversial. Qi as quantum field proposes that the coherent quantum states of biological systems — photosynthetic coherence, enzyme tunneling, cryptochrome entanglement — constitute a quantum information field corresponding to the Taoist understanding of Qi as the fundamental ordering principle of life.

## Photosynthesis Coherence

- Light-harvesting complexes use quantum wave-like superposition to explore all energy transfer pathways simultaneously.
- This quantum optimization achieves near-perfect energy transfer efficiency exceeding classical possibilities.
- Quantum coherence persists for hundreds of femtoseconds in the warm wet photosynthetic environment.
- This discovery established that biology actively uses quantum mechanics for functional optimization.

## Enzyme Tunneling

- Proton and electron tunneling through energy barriers provides rate enhancements in enzyme catalysis.
- Kinetic isotope effects — different rates for hydrogen versus deuterium — demonstrate quantum tunneling.
- Many essential metabolic enzymes use tunneling including those in the methylation cycle and energy metabolism.
- Quantum tunneling in enzymes connects quantum biology directly to the biochemistry of the methylation pharmacology node.

## Cryptochrome Navigation

- Radical pairs in cryptochrome proteins of the avian retina create quantum entangled spin states.
- Earth's magnetic field affects these spin states enabling navigation by geomagnetic orientation.
- Human cryptochrome proteins may retain vestigial magnetic sensitivity — an unexplored human sense.
