# Insula and Interoception

## Abstract

The insula — also called the insular cortex — is a cortical region folded within the lateral sulcus that serves as the primary cortical representation of the body's internal state and the neural substrate of interoception — the sense of the physiological condition of the body. Bud Craig's landmark work established the posterior-to-anterior processing hierarchy of the insula — the posterior insula receives primary interoceptive input from the spinothalamic tract representing the current homeostatic condition of the body, the middle insula integrates this with prior context, and the anterior insula generates the subjective feeling state — the felt quality of the body's condition. This processing generates what Craig calls the global emotional moment — the integrated interoceptive representation that constitutes the substrate of all emotional feeling. Antonio Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis proposes that the insula and related structures mark past decision outcomes with somatic signals — gut feelings — that guide future decision-making by rapidly biasing choice toward or away from options associated with positive or negative somatic markers. The anterior insula shows robust activation in empathy tasks — observing pain, disgust, or distress in others activates one's own anterior insula through embodied simulation. Mindfulness meditation increases insula cortical thickness corresponding to enhanced interoceptive sensitivity and emotional awareness. Addiction involves insular hyperactivation to drug cues producing the craving urge — insula lesions can extinguish smoking addiction. Po somatic soul in TCM — the Lung spirit as the somatic witness that inhabits the body boundary and registers all physical sensation as the ground of somatic knowing — corresponds to the insula as the primary cortical substrate of somatic self-awareness.

## Posterior-to-Anterior Hierarchy

- Posterior insula receives primary interoceptive input representing the body's current homeostatic condition.
- Middle insula integrates current condition with prior interoceptive context and predictive models.
- Anterior insula generates the subjective feeling state — the global emotional moment of felt body experience.
- This hierarchy produces the progression from raw body signal to felt emotional experience in 100-500ms.

## Somatic Marker Hypothesis

- Past decision outcomes are marked with somatic signals stored as body-based memories.
- When facing similar decisions the insula rapidly retrieves the associated somatic marker biasing choice.
- Positive somatic markers produce approach while negative markers produce avoidance.
- Patients with insula and vmPFC damage lose somatic markers and show impaired decision-making despite intact reasoning.

## Empathy and Mirror

- Observing pain in others activates the anterior insula in the observer through embodied simulation.
- This activation corresponds to the felt quality of the observed experience — a form of somatic empathy.
- Insula activation in empathy correlates with the observer's reported empathic concern.
- Higher insula activation predicts greater prosocial behavior and helping responses.
