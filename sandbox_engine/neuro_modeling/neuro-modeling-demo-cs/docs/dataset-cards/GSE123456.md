# Dataset card — GSE123456

## Identifier
- Repository: NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus
- Accession: GSE123456
- Platform: GPL23126
- Publication: PMID 30872783
- Data type: Expression profiling by array
- Retrieved: 2026-08-07

## Study design
- Organism: Homo sapiens
- Biological system: SAS tongue squamous carcinoma cell line
- Intervention: C6orf141 overexpression
- Comparator: Scrambled control
- Samples: 4 total
  - Control: 2
  - C6orf141 overexpression: 2

## Research question
Within this specified SAS cell-line experiment, which measured probes have the
largest observed expression differences between C6orf141-overexpression and
scrambled-control conditions?

## Intended use
Educational, reproducible exploration of public microarray-expression data.

## Not intended for
Clinical prediction, diagnosis, prognosis, treatment recommendations, causal
claims about oral cancer, or generalization to patients.

## Key limitations
- Two samples per condition.
- Cell-line model, not patient tissue.
- Microarray probe-level measurements require platform annotation.
- The manipulation/selection process may affect observed expression.
- Differential expression is not proof of a direct C6orf141 mechanism.
