# Tongue Diagnosis

## Abstract

Tongue diagnosis She Zhen is a cornerstone of TCM diagnosis providing a visible window into the internal organ systems through the tongue body color, coating, shape, moisture, and movement. The tongue reflects the state of Qi, Blood, Yin, Yang, and the presence of pathogenic factors through its visual appearance in ways that are relatively objective and resistant to voluntary control. The tongue body color reflects the state of Blood and Yin — pale indicating Blood or Yang deficiency, red indicating Heat or Yin deficiency, purple indicating Blood stasis, and bluish indicating severe Cold or Blood stasis. The tongue coating reflects the state of the digestive system and the presence of pathogenic factors — thin white coating is normal, thick white coating indicates Cold-Dampness, thick yellow coating indicates Heat-Dampness, and absence of coating indicates Yin deficiency. The tongue shape reflects the state of Qi and Blood — swollen indicating Dampness or Phlegm, thin indicating Blood deficiency, cracked indicating Yin deficiency, and teethmarks indicating Spleen Qi deficiency. Organ zone mapping divides the tongue into regions corresponding to specific organ systems — the tip corresponds to the Heart and Lung, the sides to the Liver and Gallbladder, the center to the Spleen and Stomach, and the root to the Kidney. Modern research documents correlations between tongue characteristics and biomarkers of gastrointestinal health, immune status, and cardiovascular function.

## Tongue Body Color

- Pale tongue body indicates Blood deficiency when pale-white or Yang deficiency when pale-wet.
- Red tongue body indicates Heat — Excess Heat when with thick yellow coat, Yin deficiency Heat when without coat.
- Purple tongue body indicates Blood stasis — dark purple with dark spots indicating severe chronic stasis.
- Bluish or dark tongue body indicates severe Cold obstructing Blood circulation in critical conditions.

## Tongue Coating

- Normal thin white coat reflects healthy Stomach Qi and normal digestive function.
- Thick white coat indicates Cold-Dampness obstructing the middle Jiao digestive function.
- Thick yellow coat indicates Heat-Dampness or Stomach Heat in the digestive system.
- Absent coat indicates Stomach Yin deficiency with insufficient fluids to produce a coating.

## Organ Zone Mapping

- Tongue tip corresponds to Heart and Lung — redness at the tip indicates Heart Fire or Lung Heat.
- Tongue sides correspond to Liver and Gallbladder — red or purple sides indicate Liver Blood stasis or Heat.
- Tongue center corresponds to Spleen and Stomach — thick coating in center indicates Dampness in middle Jiao.
- Tongue root corresponds to Kidney — thick coat at root indicates Kidney Dampness, absent root coat indicates Kidney Yin deficiency.

## TCM Correspondence

- Tongue diagnosis provides objective visual confirmation of pattern diagnosis derived from pulse and symptoms.
- Heart opening to the tongue as its orifice makes the tongue tip the direct reflection of Heart Shen and Fire.
- Changes in tongue appearance during treatment provide objective feedback on treatment effectiveness.
- Guardian Interface tongue data could theoretically be captured through standardized photography for digital diagnosis support.
