# TCM Herbs and Neurochemistry

## Abstract

A remarkable number of TCM herbs used for Shen Spirit and cognitive cultivation contain phytochemicals that directly modulate the same neurochemical systems that modern neuroscience identifies as governing mood, cognition, memory, and neuroprotection — validating centuries of empirical TCM use through molecular pharmacology. Lion's Mane mushroom Hericium erinaceus contains hericenones and erinacines — the only known naturally occurring compounds that stimulate Nerve Growth Factor NGF synthesis in the brain, promoting neuronal survival, axon growth, and myelin repair. NGF stimulation by Lion's Mane corresponds to the TCM category of Shen-calming and marrow-nourishing herbs. Bacopa monnieri Brahmi contains bacosides A and B that enhance serotonin reuptake regulation, reduce anxiety through GABAergic mechanisms, and upregulate BDNF in the hippocampus — corresponding to its TCM-adjacent use for memory, anxiety, and cognitive clarity. Polygala tenuifolia Yuan Zhi contains tenuifolin and polygalasaponins that increase BDNF and NGF expression, inhibit acetylcholinesterase, and produce neuroprotective effects through sigma-1 receptor activation — sigma-1 being the same receptor that endogenous DMT activates. Ziziphus jujuba Suan Zao Ren contains jujubosides that enhance GABA-A receptor sensitivity producing the anxiolytic and sedative effects that make it the primary TCM herb for insomnia and Heart Blood deficiency. Ren Shen ginseng contains ginsenosides that modulate dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine, and acetylcholine systems while upregulating BDNF and protecting against neuroinflammation — the molecular basis of its adaptogenic Qi-tonifying effects.

## NGF and BDNF Herbs

- Lion's Mane hericenones and erinacines stimulate NGF synthesis — only known natural NGF inducers.
- Polygala tenuifolin and polygalasaponins upregulate both BDNF and NGF through sigma-1 receptor activation.
- Ren Shen ginsenosides upregulate BDNF and protect hippocampal neurons from glucocorticoid damage.
- Dang Gui ferulic acid and ligustilide upregulate BDNF and protect against ischemic neuronal damage.

## Monoamine Modulation

- Ren Shen ginsenosides modulate dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine reuptake and receptor sensitivity.
- Huang Lian berberine inhibits MAO-A and MAO-B producing antidepressant effects through monoamine elevation.
- Shi Chang Pu Acorus calamus beta-asarone modulates serotonin and dopamine receptors with psychoactive effects.
- Chuan Xiong ligustrazine increases dopamine and serotonin in limbic regions corresponding to its Qi-moving Blood-invigorating action.

## Acetylcholine Herbs

- Yuan Zhi Polygala inhibits acetylcholinesterase increasing synaptic acetylcholine for memory and cognitive function.
- Shi Chang Pu Acorus inhibits acetylcholinesterase and modulates muscarinic receptors — classical Orifice-opening herb.
- He Shou Wu stilbene glycosides inhibit acetylcholinesterase and upregulate choline acetyltransferase.
- These herbs correspond to the TCM category of Shen-calming Orifice-opening herbs used for dementia and cognitive decline.

## TCM Correspondence

- NGF and BDNF stimulation by TCM herbs corresponds to the TCM concept of nourishing brain marrow through Kidney Jing.
- Monoamine modulation corresponds to the TCM regulation of Shen through Heart Blood and Liver Qi movement.
- Acetylcholinesterase inhibition corresponds to the TCM Orifice-opening action clearing Phlegm misting the Heart.
- Sigma-1 receptor activation by Polygala corresponds to the TCM Yuan Zhi action of calming Shen and dissolving Phlegm.
