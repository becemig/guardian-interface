# Five Element Correspondences

## Abstract

The Five Element Wu Xing system is one of the two primary organizational frameworks of TCM alongside the Yin-Yang model providing a comprehensive map of correspondence between the five fundamental phases of Qi transformation and all phenomena of nature and human physiology. The five elements Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water each correspond to specific organs, emotions, sensory organs, body tissues, seasons, climates, colors, tastes, sounds, odors, and developmental stages. Two primary dynamic relationships govern Five Element interaction — the generating Sheng cycle where each element nourishes the next in sequence, and the controlling Ke cycle where each element regulates and restrains a specific other element. The generating cycle flows Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth, Earth generates Metal, Metal generates Water, Water generates Wood. The controlling cycle flows Wood controls Earth, Earth controls Water, Water controls Fire, Fire controls Metal, Metal controls Wood. These two cycles maintain the dynamic equilibrium of the Five Element system — the Sheng cycle ensures continuous generation and the Ke cycle prevents any element from becoming excessive. Clinical Five Element diagnosis identifies which elements are deficient or excessive and which cycle relationships are disturbed, guiding treatment strategy toward restoring dynamic balance.

## Five Element Organ Networks

- Wood element corresponds to Liver and Gallbladder, sinews, eyes, anger, spring, wind, sour taste, green color.
- Fire element corresponds to Heart, Small Intestine, Pericardium, Triple Burner, vessels, tongue, joy, summer, heat, bitter taste, red color.
- Earth element corresponds to Spleen and Stomach, muscles, mouth, pensiveness, late summer, dampness, sweet taste, yellow color.
- Metal element corresponds to Lung and Large Intestine, skin and pores, nose, grief, autumn, dryness, pungent taste, white color.
- Water element corresponds to Kidney and Bladder, bones and marrow, ears, fear, winter, cold, salty taste, black color.

## Generating Cycle

- Wood generates Fire — Liver Blood nourishes Heart Shen and the Liver Hun supports the Heart Shen.
- Fire generates Earth — Heart warmth supports Spleen transformation and the ministerial fire warms digestion.
- Earth generates Metal — Spleen Qi ascends to nourish Lung Qi and the middle Jiao supports the upper Jiao.
- Metal generates Water — Lung descending governs water metabolism and nourishes Kidney through the upper water source.
- Water generates Wood — Kidney Yin nourishes Liver Yin and Blood providing the constitutional root for Liver function.

## Controlling Cycle

- Wood controls Earth — Liver Qi ensures smooth Spleen-Stomach function preventing excessive Earth dampening.
- Earth controls Water — Spleen transformation prevents excessive Kidney Water accumulation and edema.
- Water controls Fire — Kidney Water cools Heart Fire maintaining the Heart-Kidney axis balance.
- Fire controls Metal — Heart Fire prevents excessive Lung contraction maintaining the chest Qi dynamic.
- Metal controls Wood — Lung descending prevents excessive Liver Yang rising maintaining the Metal-Wood balance.

## TCM Correspondence

- Five Element diagnosis identifies constitutional element type and current elemental imbalance patterns.
- Acupuncture Five Element treatment uses the Five Shu transport points of each element type.
- Herbal treatment follows Five Element taste correspondences matching herb taste to target organ.
- The Five Element framework provides the integrative map linking all physiological systems through elemental correspondence.
