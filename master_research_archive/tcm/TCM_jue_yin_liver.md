# Jue Yin Stage

## Abstract

The Jue Yin stage is the deepest and most complex of the Six Stages representing the final stage of pathogenic penetration to the Liver-Pericardium constitutional axis. Jue Yin means reverting Yin and describes the reversal of Yin Qi into cold below and heat above producing the characteristic cold-heat inversion pattern. The presentation of wasting thirst, rising Qi with heat sensation in the chest, pain below the heart, hunger without desire to eat, and vomiting of roundworms describes the Jue Yin pattern in the Shang Han Lun. Wu Mei Wan sour plum pill is the classical formula treating this complex pattern by simultaneously warming cold below and clearing heat above while treating the Liver. The Jue Yin stage corresponds to the most severe depletion states with mixed hot and cold presentations and complex organ dysfunction. Liver-Pericardium axis failure produces the simultaneous Heart disturbance and Liver Wind of severe neurological and cardiac emergencies.

## Cold-Heat Inversion

- Yang cannot reach the extremities due to Yin cold below producing cold hands and feet.
- Heat accumulates above producing chest heat, irritability, and hunger.
- This simultaneous cold below and heat above represents the inversion of normal Yin-Yang circulation.
- The pattern resembles modern descriptions of peripheral vascular shutdown with central hyperthermia.

## Wu Mei Wan

- Wu Mei sour plum as the chief herb sedates Liver Yang and generates fluids addressing wasting thirst.
- Cinnamon and Aconite warm Kidney Yang addressing the cold below component.
- Coptis and Phellodendron clear heat above addressing the chest heat component.
- This formula simultaneously addresses both poles of the cold-heat inversion unique to Jue Yin.

## Roundworm Pattern

- The Shang Han Lun describes vomiting of roundworms as a Jue Yin presentation.
- Ascaris worms migrate upward in cold intestinal environments seeking warmth.
- The cold-heat inversion of Jue Yin creates the exact intestinal environment that drives worm migration.
- This observation reflects the sophisticated ancient understanding of parasite behavior and host physiology.

## TCM Correspondence

- Liver governing the free flow of Qi and emotion is the primary organ of the Jue Yin stage.
- Pericardium protecting the Heart is the second Jue Yin organ affected by constitutional exhaustion.
- The deepest Yin stage corresponds to the most severe depletion of constitutional Yin-Yang resources.
- Recovery from Jue Yin stage requires simultaneous warming of Yang and nourishing of Yin.
