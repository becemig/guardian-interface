# Mold and Mycotoxin Illness

## Abstract

Mold and mycotoxin illness — the multisystem chronic illness triggered by exposure to water-damaged buildings containing mold, bacteria, and their toxic byproducts including mycotoxins, with Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome representing the most comprehensive clinical framework describing how biotoxin exposure in genetically susceptible individuals produces a self-perpetuating innate immune inflammatory response causing fatigue, cognitive dysfunction, pain, autonomic dysregulation, and hormonal disruption that persists long after the exposure source is removed — represents one of the most underrecognized and underdiagnosed causes of chronic multisystem illness in modern medicine. CIRS mechanism — Ritchie Shoemaker's framework proposing that mycotoxins and other biotoxins from water-damaged buildings bind to innate immune receptors in genetically susceptible individuals, triggering a dysregulated innate immune response characterized by elevated TGF-beta, elevated MMP-9, suppressed VEGF, elevated C4a and C3a complement fragments, and dysregulated MSH and VIP neuropeptides that collectively produce the multisystem symptoms of CIRS through neuroinflammation, vascular dysfunction, and autonomic dysregulation. HLA-DR genetic susceptibility — the discovery that approximately 24 percent of the population carries HLA-DR haplotypes that impair the immune system's ability to mount an effective antibody response against biotoxins, preventing clearance of the antigen and leading to the persistent innate immune activation of CIRS, with specific high-risk haplotypes including HLA-DR 4-3-53 and HLA-DR 11-3-52B associated with the most severe CIRS presentations. Mycotoxin mechanisms — the toxic mechanisms of common indoor mold mycotoxins including ochratoxin A produced by Aspergillus and Penicillium species causing nephrotoxicity, immunosuppression, and neurotoxicity through mitochondrial inhibition and oxidative stress, trichothecenes from Stachybotrys chartarum inhibiting protein synthesis and causing profound immunosuppression, and gliotoxin from Aspergillus fumigatus suppressing adaptive immunity through inhibition of NF-kB. The Damp accumulation Spleen deficiency correspondence in TCM describes the classical etiology of Dampness accumulation arising from Spleen Qi deficiency failing to transform and transport fluids, with chronic Damp accumulation producing the fatigue, brain fog, heavy sensation, and digestive dysfunction that characterize both TCM Damp patterns and the clinical presentation of mold illness.

## CIRS Biomarkers

- TGF-beta1: elevated — fibrotic inflammatory mediator.
- MMP-9: elevated — matrix metalloproteinase producing vascular inflammation.
- VEGF: suppressed — reduced angiogenesis impairs tissue repair.
- C4a: elevated — complement activation marker — most sensitive CIRS biomarker.
- MSH: suppressed — loss of anti-inflammatory neuropeptide.

## HLA-DR Susceptibility

- 24 percent population: HLA haplotypes impairing biotoxin antibody response.
- Genetic testing: HLA-DR genotyping identifies susceptible individuals.
- High risk haplotypes: 4-3-53 and 11-3-52B associated with worst outcomes.
- Implication: same exposure produces CIRS in susceptible not in resistant individuals.

## Shoemaker Protocol Steps

- Remove: leave water-damaged building — stop ongoing exposure.
- Bind: cholestyramine or Welchol bind mycotoxins in gut — interrupt enterohepatic circulation.
- Eradicate MARCoNS: intranasal antibiotic for multiply antibiotic resistant coagulase negative staph.
- Correct hormones: VIP MSH ADH restoration.
