# TCM Synthesis for Bagua Circle Walking

## Abstract

This node is the convergence point of all BAGUA-* series research -- the synthesis where the mathematical physics of the capture system and the classical theory of Traditional Chinese Medicine are shown to be describing the same phenomena in different languages. The central thesis has three parts. First: the Jing Jin sinew channels of classical TCM are the sub-Riemannian horizontal distribution D on the body configuration manifold -- they are not metaphorical pathways of invisible energy but the precise geometric description of which movement directions are mechanically available at each body configuration, encoded in the fascial tensegrity network that the sinew channels anatomically describe. Second: Qi in the context of movement is power flow in the port-Hamiltonian sense -- it is the product of effort and flow at each port of the body system -- measurable in Watts, directional, conservative over closed cycles in the healthy state, and subject to the same storage dissipation and transmission laws that govern all physical power networks. Third: the classical diagnostic and therapeutic categories of TCM -- the five elements, the eight principles, the ascending-descending floating-sinking herb energetics -- are directional physics categories that describe the geometry of Qi power flow, and they map precisely onto the directional vector fields that the BaguaViewer renders in Layers 6 and 7.
