# Endocannabinoid System

## Abstract

The endocannabinoid system ECS is a retrograde lipid signaling system that modulates synaptic transmission throughout the central and peripheral nervous system. The two primary endocannabinoids are anandamide AEA discovered by Devane and colleagues in 1992 and 2-arachidonoylglycerol 2-AG. Both are synthesized on demand from membrane phospholipids in the postsynaptic neuron and released retrogradely — traveling backward across the synapse to activate CB1 receptors on the presynaptic terminal. CB1 receptors are the most abundant G-protein coupled receptors in the brain concentrated in the cortex, hippocampus, basal ganglia, and cerebellum. CB2 receptors are primarily expressed in immune cells and peripheral tissues. Depolarization-induced suppression of inhibition DSI and depolarization-induced suppression of excitation DSE are the primary retrograde endocannabinoid mechanisms enabling postsynaptic neurons to regulate their own synaptic input. The ECS modulates pain, appetite, mood, memory, stress response, immune function, and sleep. The ECS provides a biological substrate for the TCM concept of Qi flow modulation — the dynamic self-regulating adjustment of energy flow between organs and channels corresponding to the on-demand retrograde modulation of the endocannabinoid system.

## Retrograde Signaling

- Postsynaptic depolarization or receptor activation triggers synthesis and release of endocannabinoids.
- Released endocannabinoids travel retrogradely across the synapse to CB1 receptors on the presynaptic terminal.
- CB1 activation inhibits presynaptic calcium channels and activates potassium channels reducing neurotransmitter release.
- This retrograde feedback enables postsynaptic neurons to dynamically regulate the strength of their synaptic input.

## Pain Modulation

- The ECS provides endogenous pain modulation through CB1 activation in spinal and supraspinal pain circuits.
- Anandamide activates CB1 in the periaqueductal gray activating descending pain inhibitory pathways.
- Peripheral CB1 and CB2 activation reduces inflammatory pain through local immune modulation.
- Exercise-induced analgesia is partially mediated by exercise-induced endocannabinoid release.

## Stress and Mood

- The ECS provides a stress buffer — endocannabinoid release during stress dampens HPA axis activation.
- Anandamide in the amygdala reduces fear and anxiety through CB1 activation.
- Chronic stress depletes endocannabinoid tone corresponding to the anxiety and anhedonia of stress-related disorders.
- Antidepressant effects of exercise and meditation are partially mediated through ECS enhancement.
