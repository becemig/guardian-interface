# Yoga Traditions and Somatic Practice

## Abstract

Yoga is an ancient Indian system of somatic, breath, and contemplative practices whose physical branch Hatha Yoga shares deep structural correspondences with Chinese internal arts. Pranayama breath control practices regulate prana vital force through specific breathing techniques corresponding to Qigong breath regulation. Bandhas energetic locks at the perineum, abdomen, and throat engage the internal pressure and energy management system corresponding to Dan Tian and San Jiao functions. The nadi energy channel system of 72000 channels with Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna as primary channels corresponds to the meridian system with Conception, Governing, and Chong vessels as primary extraordinary vessels. The chakra system of seven energy centers along the spine corresponds broadly to the Dan Tian system.

## Pranayama

- Nadi Shodhana alternate nostril breathing balances Ida and Pingala channels corresponding to Yin-Yang balancing.
- Kapalabhati skull shining breath uses rapid diaphragmatic pumping to clear channels and energize the system.
- Ujjayi victorious breath uses mild glottic constriction to extend exhalation and create internal resonance.
- Kumbhaka breath retention builds internal pressure and Qi corresponding to Dan Tian compression practices.

## Bandhas

- Mula Bandha root lock engages the pelvic floor muscles corresponding to lower Dan Tian root activation.
- Uddiyana Bandha abdominal lock draws the abdomen up and in on exhalation corresponding to reverse abdominal breathing.
- Jalandhara Bandha chin lock lengthens the posterior neck corresponding to the governing vessel cervical alignment.
- Maha Bandha combines all three locks simultaneously for maximum internal pressure and energy concentration.

## Nadi System

- Ida nadi is the left lunar cooling channel corresponding to Conception Vessel and Yin energy circulation.
- Pingala nadi is the right solar warming channel corresponding to Governing Vessel and Yang energy circulation.
- Sushumna nadi is the central channel corresponding to the Chong Vessel as the central thoroughfare vessel.
- Kundalini Shakti rising through Sushumna corresponds to the Microcosmic Orbit cultivation in Taoist practice.

## TCM Correspondence

- Prana and Qi are functionally equivalent as the vital animating force governing physiological and energetic processes.
- Pranayama and Qigong breath practices regulate the vital force through similar breath-retention-movement principles.
- The chakra-Dan Tian correspondence provides a cross-cultural framework for energy center cultivation.
- Both systems recognize the central channel as the primary axis of spiritual cultivation and energy refinement.
