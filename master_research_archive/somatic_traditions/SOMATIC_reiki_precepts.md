# Reiki Five Precepts

## Abstract

The five precepts Gokai are the ethical and spiritual heart of Mikao Usui's Reiki Ryoho system — not secondary guidelines but the primary practice that Usui considered the foundation of Reiki and the direct path to healing through the clearing of mental and emotional Ki obstruction. Usui wrote in his handbook that the secret of inviting happiness through many blessings is found in the five principles — placing the Gokai at the center of his system rather than at its periphery. The just for today framing of each precept is itself a teaching — rather than demanding permanent change which creates resistance and failure, the just for today approach invites a moment-to-moment practice of returning to the precept without self-judgment when one inevitably forgets. This corresponds to the mindfulness meditation instruction to simply return to the breath without judgment when the mind wanders. The five precepts map directly onto the TCM five emotion-organ correspondences creating a unified psychophysiological practice system. Just for today do not anger corresponds to releasing Liver Qi stagnation — anger is the emotion that most directly blocks Liver Ki flow and the precept is a practice of releasing this block repeatedly throughout each day. Just for today do not worry corresponds to releasing Spleen Qi depletion — excessive worry and rumination is the emotion that most directly taxes Spleen Ki and the precept is a practice of returning to present-moment grounded awareness. Be humble and grateful corresponds to cultivating Heart Shen through the opening quality of gratitude — gratitude directly opens the Heart and produces the psychophysiological coherence that HeartMath research documents. Work diligently and honestly corresponds to expressing Kidney Zhi through the commitment and integrity that manifest the constitutional will. Be compassionate toward yourself and others corresponds to Lung Po releasing grief and Pericardium opening to receive and give appropriate connection.

## Five Precepts and Five Elements

- Just for today do not anger: Liver Wood — releasing Qi stagnation through present-moment acceptance.
- Just for today do not worry: Spleen Earth — releasing depletion through grounded present awareness.
- Be humble and grateful: Heart Fire — cultivating Shen through the opening quality of genuine gratitude.
- Work diligently and honestly: Kidney Water — expressing Zhi through integrity and constitutional commitment.
- Be compassionate toward yourself and others: Lung Metal and Pericardium — releasing grief and opening to connection.

## Just for Today Practice

- The just for today framing invites moment-to-moment practice without demanding permanent change.
- Each time the precept is forgotten and then remembered is a complete practice cycle — not a failure.
- This corresponds to the mindfulness instruction to return to the present moment without self-judgment.
- The accumulation of just for today moments produces genuine transformation without forcing.

## Gokai as Ki Medicine

- Working with the precepts directly clears the emotional Ki obstructions that produce physical disease.
- Anger clearing opens Liver Qi flow reducing the headaches, eye problems, and tendon issues of Liver stagnation.
- Worry releasing restores Spleen Qi reducing the fatigue, digestive issues, and overthinking of Spleen depletion.
- Gratitude cultivation opens Heart Shen producing the psychophysiological coherence that improves all system function.

## TCM Correspondence

- Five precepts corresponding to five emotion-organ pairs connects Reiki ethics directly to TCM psychosomatic medicine.
- Just for today framing corresponds to the Taoist present-moment cultivation of attending to what is rather than what should be.
- Gokai as Ki medicine corresponds to the TCM understanding that emotional clearing is the most fundamental level of healing.
- Precept practice as daily cultivation corresponds to the TCM concept of preventive medicine through maintaining emotional-Qi balance.
