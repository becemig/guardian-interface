# Reiki Foundation

## Abstract

Reiki is a Japanese healing and self-cultivation system developed by Mikao Usui in the early twentieth century following a transformative experience on Mount Kurama during an intensive meditation and fasting retreat. The word Reiki combines Rei meaning universal spiritual or cosmic and Ki meaning life force energy — the system is therefore a practice of working with universal life force energy for healing, self-cultivation, and spiritual development. The original Usui Reiki Ryoho system as practiced in Japan was primarily a self-cultivation practice centered on the five precepts Gokai, regular hands-on self-treatment, meditative practices including Joshin Kokyu Ho breath purification, and the transmission lineage from teacher to student through attunement Reiju. The Western Reiki tradition that spread globally from the 1970s through Hawayo Takata emphasized the healing application dimension more than the self-cultivation dimension — creating a somewhat different emphasis from the original Japanese system. Tanden is the Reiki term for the energy center below the navel corresponding to the lower Dan Tian in TCM — the primary source of Ki in the body and the foundation from which all Reiki practice flows. The five precepts Gokai are the ethical and spiritual foundation of Usui's system: Just for today do not anger, Just for today do not worry, Be humble and grateful, Work diligently and honestly, Be compassionate toward yourself and others. These five precepts are not merely ethical guidelines but active meditation practices — Usui taught that working with the precepts clears the Ki pathways and opens the practitioner to the universal Reiki energy. The transmission lineage through attunement Reiju is the mechanism by which a Reiki master opens the student's Ki pathways to channel Reiki energy — corresponding to the empowerment transmission concept found across multiple Asian spiritual traditions.

## Mikao Usui and Origins

- Usui received his Reiki transmission on Mount Kurama following a twenty-one day meditation and fasting retreat.
- The original system combined hands-on healing with meditative self-cultivation and ethical precepts.
- Usui's Memorial Stone in Tokyo documents his life and the founding of the Usui Reiki Ryoho Gakkai.
- The Western Reiki tradition modified the original system emphasizing healing application over self-cultivation.

## Tanden and Hara

- Tanden is the primary Ki center located three finger-widths below the navel — the lower Dan Tian.
- All Reiki practice is rooted in the tanden — the practitioner works from the hara not from the hands or mind.
- Developing tanden awareness and strength is the foundation of authentic Reiki practice.
- This corresponds directly to TCM lower Dan Tian cultivation as the root of all Qi practice.

## Five Precepts

- Just for today do not anger: releasing the Liver Qi stagnation of anger and frustration.
- Just for today do not worry: releasing the Spleen Qi depletion of excessive worry and rumination.
- Be humble and grateful: cultivating Heart Shen through gratitude and openness.
- Work diligently and honestly: expressing Kidney Zhi through integrity and commitment.
- Be compassionate toward yourself and others: the Heart-centered ethic that opens Ki flow.

## TCM Correspondence

- Tanden as lower Dan Tian corresponds to the TCM primary source of constitutional Yuan Qi.
- Five precepts addressing anger, worry, and compassion corresponds to TCM five emotion-organ correspondences.
- Attunement transmission opening Ki pathways corresponds to the TCM concept of opening the extraordinary vessels.
- Reiki as universal Ki corresponds to the TCM understanding of Qi as the universal organizing energy of life.
