# Longevity Science

## Abstract

Longevity science has matured from observational epidemiology to mechanistic geroscience, identifying conserved molecular pathways that regulate the rate of aging across species and developing pharmacological and lifestyle interventions that extend both lifespan and healthspan. The Blue Zones — five geographic regions with exceptional longevity concentrations (Sardinia, Okinawa, Loma Linda, Nicoya, Ikaria) — share nine lifestyle commonalities including plant-predominant diet, daily movement, purpose, stress management, moderate alcohol, community, and faith. At the molecular level, the three primary longevity pathways are mTOR (inhibited by rapamycin and caloric restriction), sirtuins (activated by NAD+ and fasting), and AMPK (activated by exercise and metformin). Hormesis — the principle that mild stressors activate adaptive responses that strengthen the organism — underlies the longevity benefits of exercise, fasting, cold exposure, and phytonutrients. The Interventions Testing Program (ITP) has rigorously validated rapamycin, acarbose, and 17-alpha-estradiol as extending mouse lifespan when initiated in middle age. TCM longevity cultivation (Yang Sheng) directly parallels these mechanisms through Jing conservation, Qi circulation, and seasonal alignment.

## Blue Zones

- Sardinia: highest concentration of male centenarians - mountainous community - strong family bonds
- Okinawa: women longest-lived - hara hachi bu (eat to 80 percent full) - moai social groups
- Loma Linda: Seventh-day Adventists - plant-based diet, Sabbath rest, community
- Nicoya: Costa Rica - strong purpose (plan de vida), beans and corn, family
- Ikaria: Greek island - Mediterranean diet, afternoon naps, strong social ties, low stress
- Common factors: movement built into daily life - plant-predominant diet - purpose - community
- TCM parallel: Yang Sheng (nourishing life) practices - seasonal alignment, moderation, community

## mTOR and Caloric Restriction

- mTOR: mechanistic target of rapamycin - master growth and metabolism regulator
- mTORC1: activated by amino acids and insulin - drives protein synthesis - inhibits autophagy
- Aging: mTOR hyperactivation suppresses autophagy and repair - accelerates aging
- Caloric restriction: reduces mTOR activity - extends lifespan in yeast worms flies mice
- Rapamycin: mTOR inhibitor - extends median lifespan 9-14 percent in mice even started at age 20 months
- Intermittent fasting: periodic mTOR suppression - activates autophagy and repair
- Protein restriction: methionine restriction extends lifespan - reduces mTOR activation

## Sirtuins and NAD+

- Sirtuins: NAD-dependent deacylases - SIRT1-7 - regulate metabolism, DNA repair, inflammation
- SIRT1: activates PGC-1 alpha (mitochondrial biogenesis) and FOXO (stress resistance)
- SIRT3: mitochondrial sirtuin - reduces ROS - maintains ETC function
- NAD+ decline: NAD+ falls 50 percent from age 20 to 60 - impairs all sirtuin activity
- NMN and NR: NAD+ precursors - restore NAD+ levels - under active clinical investigation
- Fasting: increases NAD+ via NAMPT upregulation
- Exercise: raises NAD+ in muscle - activates SIRT1 and SIRT3

## AMPK and Metformin

- AMPK: energy sensor - activated when AMP-ATP ratio rises - low energy signal
- AMPK actions: activates autophagy, mitochondrial biogenesis, fatty acid oxidation
- Exercise: most potent AMPK activator - muscle energy depletion triggers AMPK
- Metformin: biguanide diabetes drug - activates AMPK via complex I inhibition
- TAME trial: targeting aging with metformin - first FDA-approved aging trial
- Berberine: plant compound - similar AMPK activation to metformin - TCM Huang Lian

## Hormesis

- Definition: mild stressor activates adaptive response that strengthens the organism
- Exercise: muscle damage triggers repair - stronger and more resilient than before
- Fasting: metabolic stress activates autophagy, AMPK, sirtuins, heat shock proteins
- Cold exposure: activates brown adipose tissue, norepinephrine, cold shock proteins
- Heat: sauna activates heat shock proteins, BDNF, growth hormone
- Phytochemicals: plant defensive compounds (resveratrol, sulforaphane) activate NRF2
- TCM: Yang Sheng practice includes controlled mild hardship - cold water, fasting, exertion

## Exercise as Longevity Intervention

- Most validated: exercise is the most evidence-based longevity intervention available
- VO2max: strongest single predictor of longevity - each MET increase reduces mortality 13 percent
- Mechanisms: AMPK, PGC-1 alpha, BDNF, telomerase, anti-inflammatory myokines, IGF-1
- Type: combination of aerobic and resistance training - both necessary
- Zone 2: low-intensity aerobic training - maximizes mitochondrial biogenesis
- Resistance: preserves muscle mass and strength - prevents sarcopenia
- TCM parallel: Tai Chi and Qigong as longevity movement practices - gentle and sustained
