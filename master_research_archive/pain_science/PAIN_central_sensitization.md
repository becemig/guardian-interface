# Central Sensitization

## Abstract

Central sensitization — the amplification of neural signaling within the central nervous system that elicits pain hypersensitivity through the activity-dependent increase in the excitability of nociceptive neurons in the spinal dorsal horn and brain, producing the clinical features of allodynia in which normally innocuous stimuli produce pain, hyperalgesia in which noxious stimuli produce exaggerated pain responses, and the expansion of the pain area beyond the original site of injury that characterize chronic pain conditions including fibromyalgia, chronic low back pain, temporomandibular disorder, and irritable bowel syndrome. Spinal dorsal horn plasticity — the synaptic and cellular changes in the dorsal horn of the spinal cord that underlie central sensitization, including the NMDA receptor-dependent long-term potentiation of C fiber synapses on dorsal horn neurons that amplifies synaptic transmission, the insertion of AMPA receptors at previously silent synapses that increases the number of functional excitatory synapses, the loss of inhibitory interneuron function through excitotoxicity and reduced GABA and glycine signaling, and the activation of spinal microglia and astrocytes that release pro-inflammatory cytokines further amplifying nociceptive transmission. Wind-up and long-term potentiation — the two temporal forms of central sensitization, with wind-up representing the progressive increase in action potential discharge from dorsal horn neurons during repetitive low-frequency C fiber stimulation, dependent on NMDA receptor and NK1 substance P receptor activation, and with long-term potentiation of spinal nociceptive synapses representing a more durable form of synaptic strengthening analogous to hippocampal LTP that may underlie the persistent pain hypersensitivity of chronic pain conditions. Glial contribution to pain amplification — the recognition that spinal microglia and astrocytes are activated by nociceptive input and play critical roles in central sensitization through the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, and IL-6, brain-derived neurotrophic factor, and ATP that collectively amplify excitatory synaptic transmission, reduce inhibitory tone, and contribute to the maintenance of chronic pain states. The Xu Pain empty pain chronic pattern correspondence in TCM describes the classical category of deficiency pain arising not from excess obstruction but from insufficient Qi and Blood to nourish the channels and tissues, producing the dull aching empty pain of chronic deficiency — a correspondence with the central sensitization understanding that chronic pain involves not just peripheral pathology but a central nervous system that has been recalibrated to amplify pain signals.

## Dorsal Horn Plasticity

- NMDA: voltage-dependent magnesium block removed by sustained depolarization — LTP.
- AMPA insertion: silent synapses become active — increased excitatory drive.
- Inhibitory loss: reduced GABA glycinergic interneuron function — disinhibition.
- Glial activation: microglia astrocytes release cytokines BDNF ATP — amplification.

## Wind-Up

- Repetitive C fiber: low frequency 0.3-3 Hz stimulation — progressive dorsal horn firing increase.
- NMDA dependent: blocked by ketamine — pain research tool.
- NK1 dependent: substance P tachykinin receptor 1 — sustained depolarization.
- Clinical: wind-up correlates with temporal summation in clinical pain assessment.

## Fibromyalgia as Central Sensitization

- Widespread pain: above and below waist bilateral — central not peripheral.
- Allodynia: light touch painful — central amplification.
- Hyperalgesia: reduced pressure pain thresholds at all body sites.
- Treatment: central-acting drugs — duloxetine pregabalin — not NSAIDs.
