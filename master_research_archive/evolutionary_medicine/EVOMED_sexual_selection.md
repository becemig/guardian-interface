# Sexual Selection and Health

## Abstract

Sexual selection — Charles Darwin's framework for understanding the evolution of traits that enhance reproductive success through mate competition and mate choice rather than survival — provides an evolutionary explanation for the relationship between health, genetic quality, and physical attractiveness, with the immunocompetence hypothesis and handicap principle establishing why health and constitutional vitality are honest signals of genetic quality that mate choice has been selecting for throughout human evolution. The handicap principle — proposed by Amotz Zahavi in 1975 — resolves the paradox of costly and apparently maladaptive sexual ornaments by demonstrating that only genuinely high-quality individuals can bear the fitness cost of elaborate displays without suffering lethal consequences: the peacock's tail is an honest signal of genetic quality precisely because it is costly and only the healthiest individuals can afford it. The immunocompetence hypothesis — proposed by Folstad and Karter in 1992 — applies the handicap principle to testosterone-dependent secondary sexual characteristics: testosterone enhances secondary sexual trait expression including muscle mass, bone density, facial masculinity, and behavioral dominance while simultaneously immunosuppressing the organism through glucocorticoid-mediated immune suppression, creating a genuine trade-off in which only immunocompetent individuals with robust disease resistance can afford high testosterone expression without succumbing to infection. Fluctuating asymmetry — the degree of random deviation from perfect bilateral symmetry in normally symmetric traits — is a sensitive biomarker of developmental stability and genetic quality: low fluctuating asymmetry indicates that the organism maintained precise developmental buffering despite genetic and environmental perturbations during growth, and is associated with higher attractiveness ratings, better health outcomes, higher reproductive success, and higher HRV. HLA mate choice — the preference for mates with dissimilar major histocompatibility complex MHC genes, detected through body odor — maximizes offspring immune diversity and corresponds to the TCM constitutional compatibility assessment in partner selection. The constitutional vitality display correspondence describes the TCM understanding that the Shen brightness of the eyes, the luster of the complexion, the vitality of the voice, and the quality of movement display the constitutional health of the Jing-Qi-Shen triad — the same information that sexual selection has been reading through mate choice for millions of years.

## Handicap Principle

- Costly honest signals can only be maintained by genuinely high-quality individuals.
- Secondary sexual traits are reliable quality indicators because low-quality individuals cannot afford them.
- Human application: physical fitness, facial symmetry, voice quality, skin clarity as quality signals.
- Constitutional health as the honest signal underlying all human attractiveness cues.

## Immunocompetence Hypothesis

- Testosterone enhances secondary sexual traits while immunosuppressing through glucocorticoids.
- Only immunocompetent individuals can afford high testosterone expression without infection.
- High testosterone males: higher pathogen load but stronger immunocompetence offsetting it.
- The testosterone-immunity trade-off makes male secondary sexual traits honest health signals.
